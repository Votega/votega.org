Using this data

These datasets are provided free for public reuse. The underlying facts come from public and government sources, cleaned and joined into machine-readable files.

  • Attribution: please credit both VoteGA.org and the original source named on each dataset (e.g. Open States, Congress.gov, Oyez, the Georgia Secretary of State).
  • Verify upstream terms for anything you redistribute — some sources (e.g. Oyez) set their own reuse conditions that flow through to derived data.
  • Freshness & schema: each dataset lists its update cadence above; field definitions and methodology live in About the Data and each repository's README.
  • No warranty: data is provided as-is. Spotted an error? Open an issue on the relevant repository, or email admin@votega.org.

Have a dataset you'd like to see here? Let us know at admin@votega.org.
