Federal Elected Officials

Georgia is represented in Congress by 2 U.S. Senators and 14 U.S. Representatives. Senators serve six-year terms and represent the entire state. Representatives serve two-year terms and represent individual congressional districts.

Find Your U.S. Legislator

Select a Chamber (House or Senate), search your address, ZIP code, or select a county filter to view your federally elected representatives.

Select a member to view details about their term(s) in office.

A street address is sent only to the U.S. Census geocoder to look up your districts. You may search by ZIP only, which shows every district touching that ZIP code. VoteGA does not store any personal information.

OR pick your county (shows every district that touches the county)

Data is sourced from the Congress.gov API
