Find My Representatives

Georgia voters are represented at three levels of government — federal, state, and local*. Enter your address to see your federal and state representatives, or browse by category below.

* Not all localities in Georgia have been added to the site yet.

Your address is sent only to the U.S. Census geocoder to look up your districts. VoteGA does not store it.

{%- comment -%} The county fallback is what the address path degrades to. The Census geocoder misses new construction, rural routes and some apartment formats, and a ZIP alone can never resolve a district — without this the page's only remaining path was the browse cards, which hand the voter off to the Secretary of State. It also makes the geocoder's own error messages true: they say "pick your county above", which they do only because this control sits above the status line. Mirrors sample-ballot.html's county row. {%- endcomment -%}
