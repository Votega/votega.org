2026 Georgia Ballot Measures

This page contains statewide measures that Georgia voters will decide. Constitutional amendments and statewide referendums are placed on the ballot by the General Assembly and, once approved by voters, become part of Georgia law or the state constitution. In the General Election, your ballot may contain more measures than the statewide items listed as many local and county governments include measures/referendums on the ballot.
