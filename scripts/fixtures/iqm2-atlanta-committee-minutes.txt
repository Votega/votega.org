26-O-1474 (28) AN ORDINANCE BY COUNCILMEMBERS ANTONIO LEWIS, WAYNE
MARTIN, LILIANA BAKHTIARI, MATT WESTMORELAND, BYRON
D. AMOS, AND MICHAEL JULIAN BOND REQUIRING DATA
CENTERS LOCATED WITHIN THE CITY OF ATLANTA, AND DATA
CENTERS SERVED BY THE CITY OF ATLANTA'S MUNICIPAL
WATER SYSTEM THROUGH WHOLESALE OR
INTERGOVERNMENTAL WATER SERVICE AGREEMENTS, TO
TRANSITION TO CLOSED-LOOP WATER-COOLING SYSTEMS AND
PROHIBIT THE USE OF POTABLE MUNICIPAL WATER FOR
ROUTINE DATA CENTER COOLING OPERATIONS; ESTABLISHING
REQUIREMENTS FOR NEW AND EXISTING DATA CENTERS;
DIRECTING THE DEPARTMENT OF WATERSHED MANAGEMENT
TO INCORPORATE SUCH REQUIREMENTS INTO APPLICABLE
PERMITS, SERVICE AGREEMENTS, DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENTS,
AND WATER CONTRACTS; REQUESTING COORDINATION WITH
MUNICIPALITIES RECEIVING CITY OF ATLANTA WATER;
PROVIDING FOR EXEMPTIONS IN LIMITED CIRCUMSTANCES;
AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.
