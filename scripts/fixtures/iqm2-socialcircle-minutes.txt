1. 2026 Millage Rate Public Hearing
City Manager Eric Taylor presented the proposed 2026 Millage Rate at 7.756 which is a zero
increase from last year. He stated that the budget was adopted based on last year's mill rate.
Since there is an assessment increase which is higher than last year the state deems staying at
7.756 as an increase. In order to adopt a higher rate than the roll back rate of 7.183 three public
hearings are required. This being the third. City Manager Taylor recommends consideration of
7.756.
Mayor Keener opened a public hearing giving both sides 10 minutes. There being no one present
to speak for or against the public hearing was closed.
RESULT: COMPLETED
Resolution to Adopt the 2026 Millage Rate
After some discussion Council Member Shelton made a motion, seconded by Mayor Pro tempore
Price to adopt a Resolution to set the 2026 Property Tax Millage Rate at 7.6. Opportunity for
discussion. All were in favor except Council Member Jackson voted against.

2. REZ-2026-006 - Request to Rezone from Agriculture (AG) to Residential-15 (R-15) as Part
of a Proposed Subdivision Development at 0 Vine Circle; SC150155A00, SC150155B00,
SC170075A00, & SC170075.
Council Member Shelton made a motion, seconded by Mayor Pro tempore Price to adopt the request to
rezone from AG to R15 with the following conditions:
1. Developer shall provide easement(s) and or dedicate a minimum 10' wide path for the Social Circle
Trail Network in accordance with the adopted Parks and Recreation Master Plan.
2. There shall be a 15' undisturbed buffer backing to the Conner Springs development and a buffer
mirroring the existing sewer easement backing to the Dove Landing development.
3. There shall be an amenity added to the development.
4. There shall be a decel lane on E Hightower leading into the development.
Voting in favor were Mayor Pro tempore Price and Council Member Shelton. Voting against were
Council Member Jackson and Council Member Conavay. Mayor Keener voted in favor to break the tie.
Motion passed.
RESULT: ADOPTED [UNANIMOUS]
MOVER: Steve Shelton, Council Member (4)
SECONDER: Traysa Cody Price, Mayor Pro tempore (1)
AYES: Mayor Keener, Mayor Pro tempore (1) Price, Council Member (2) Jackson, Council
Member (3) Conavay, Council Member (4) Shelton
