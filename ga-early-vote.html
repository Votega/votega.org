The pace of early voting

Ballots accepted each day, lined up by the number of days before election day. Hover or tap the chart to read a day.

Days are counted back from election day, so a given day lines up across years but not always by weekday. Mail ballots keep arriving through election day; early in-person voting ends Friday, October 30.
