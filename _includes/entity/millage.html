Property tax rates

What {{ e.name }} property owners are charged, in mills, by each government that levies on them. One mill is $1 of tax per $1,000 of assessed value, and Georgia assesses at 40% of fair market value — so a mill costs about $0.40 per $1,000 of market value. Rates are maintenance-and-operations (M&O).
