{%- comment -%} Zero-based bars for the stat cards on /ga-early-vote/. Two bars when `vb` is given (a past year's value at its comparison point, and this year's at the same point), one when it is not. Every bar on one card shares one scale, so the same quantity is never drawn at two lengths (per-pair scaling drew 2026's 21.0% full width against 2022 and 62% against 2024). The page works the card's largest value out before the loop and passes it as `mx`. {% include ev-bars.html ya="2022" va=825378 vy="2026" vb=839209 mx=2619705 point="at 17 days out" n=true %} {% include ev-bars.html ya="2022" va=64.1 mx=76.7 unit="%" point="Governor" %} `ya`/`va` are the past year and its value, `vy`/`vb` this year and its value at the same point. `mx` is the largest value anywhere on the card; it falls back to the larger of this pair. `cls` (optional) overrides the first bar's color class, which is otherwise ev-bar-y - the current year has no year class of its own, so a lone bar for it passes cls="ev-bar-now". `point` (optional) is a small note beside both year labels - the comparison day, say, so a card showing this year twice says why the two differ, or the contest a share belongs to. `n=true` formats thousands separators (the page's data-n script); otherwise the value is printed with `unit`.
