{%- comment -%} Visible breadcrumb trail. Reads the same `breadcrumb:` front matter that structured-data.html turns into a schema.org BreadcrumbList, so the on-page trail and the trail Google may show in a result come from one source and cannot drift apart. Entity pages get the same front matter from scripts/build_entity_pages.py (see breadcrumbs()). Renders nothing for a page with no trail, or a trail of one — a lone "Home" crumb tells a visitor nothing. The final crumb is the current page: never a link, and marked aria-current so a screen reader says where it is. Separators are CSS ::before, not markup, to keep punctuation out of the accessibility tree. Mobile truncation is in beautifuljekyll.css. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if page.breadcrumb and page.breadcrumb.size > 1 -%}
