{%- comment -%} "What's on my ballot" entry point. The home page previously went straight from the two rep-lookup cards to "Latest Updates", so the elections journey — half the site — was reachable only from a navbar dropdown. Dates come from ga-election-calendar.json, so this never needs hand-editing as a cycle advances; the strip hides itself once no upcoming election remains. See CODEBASE-REVIEW-2026-08-18.md finding 4.10. The results link is a second, independent read of election-status.json — the live status overlay Jekyll rebuilds from the results pages' own front matter and vote totals. It points at the most recent election that has actually been counted, and stays hidden when none has, so the home page can never offer a results page that is still all zeros. The two fetches are deliberately not chained: a missing or stale calendar must not take the results link down with it, and vice versa. {%- endcomment -%}
