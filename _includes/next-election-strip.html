{%- comment -%} "What's on my ballot" entry point and its companion "Latest Results" card. The home page previously went straight from the two rep-lookup cards to "Latest Updates", so the elections journey — half the site — was reachable only from a navbar dropdown. See CODEBASE-REVIEW-2026-08-18.md finding 4.10. Two peer cards share a flex row (#electionCards): - Next Election (left) is driven by ga-election-calendar.json: it shows the soonest election dated today-or-later and hides itself once none remains, so it never needs hand-editing as a cycle advances. - Latest Results (right) is driven by election-status.json — the live status overlay Jekyll rebuilds from each results page's own front matter AND the votes in its data file. It surfaces the most recent election that has actually been counted, within a 30-day window after election day (see RESULTS_WINDOW_DAYS), so a just-decided race is one click from the home page while interest is high and then ages out on its own. "upcoming" there means "nothing counted yet" no matter what a page's front matter says, so a staged page of zeros can never be offered. The two feeds are read independently (Promise.allSettled): a missing or stale calendar must not take the results card down, nor the reverse. The results card enriches its title from the calendar when available and falls back to a generic label when it is not. The row hides entirely only when neither card has anything to show. {%- endcomment -%}
