| Source | URL | Likely covers |
|---|---|---|
| Georgia Municipal Association (GMA), member cities | https://www.gacities.com/gma-cities-districts/gma-member-cities | City list, for city officials such as mayors and councils |
| Georgia Sheriffs' Association, sheriffs by county | https://georgiasheriffs.org/resources/sheriffs-by-county/ | One sheriff per county (159 counties) |

## Before building on these
- Check each site's terms of use before scraping or republishing, and credit the source on the generated pages.
- Both are third-party lists that can lag elections; verify names against the Secretary of State's certified results once the November 2026 sheriff and local races are certified.
- Join counties on FIPS code (see `assets/data/ga-counties.json`) so the sheriff pages line up with the county map.
