Legislator Voting Scorecard

Two numbers published for every member of Congress and, until now, for no one in the Georgia General Assembly: how often a lawmaker sides with their own party on contested votes, and how often they miss the vote entirely. Ranked across all sitting members, from the same roll-call record behind each member's page.

Party unity

Of the party-line roll calls — where a majority of one caucus voted against a majority of the other — the share on which the member sided with their own party. Lopsided and bipartisan votes are excluded, because they say nothing about a member's independence.

Participation

Of the passage roll calls held in the member's chamber, the share on which they cast a Yea or Nay. Absences, excused absences, and "not voting" count against it. A floor-vote participation rate, not full attendance.
