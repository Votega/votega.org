> **See the final numbers:** The [Early Voting Turnout page]({{ '/ga-early-vote/' | relative_url }}) is now an archive of this election's early vote, with every county, the daily pace, and the mail ballot pipeline.

Early voting for the **November 3, 2026** general election began on October 13. Through **October 30**, the last day of early voting, Georgians had cast **2,825,220** early in-person and accepted mail ballots, about 37.5% of the state's active voters.

A raw count means little without a comparison, so the page lines 2026 up against past elections at the same number of days before election day.

## What the numbers show

- **Against 2022, the last midterm:** at 17 days out, 2026 was about 1.7% ahead in ballots, and level when measured against the estimated number of citizen adults (10.8% in both years). Early in-person voting was up about 8%, while accepted mail ballots were down by more than half.
- **Against 2024:** at 4 days out, 2026 was roughly 29% behind. 2024 was a presidential year, which draws far more voters, so a midterm was expected to trail it.

Early in-person voting ended on **Friday, October 30**. Mail ballots kept arriving until the polls closed on election day, so the final mail figures differ from the October 30 snapshot.

## What else is on the page

- **A county table** with ballots cast, turnout as a share of active voters and of estimated citizen adults, and mail share. Each county carries small tags showing which party carried it for Governor in 2022 and President in 2024, and you can filter by party.
- **The daily pace** of early voting, lined up by days before election day, for 2026, 2022, and 2024.
- **The mail ballot pipeline** by county: how many were accepted, still outstanding, rejected, or cancelled.
- **Turnout and education**, a scatter plot of all 159 counties. Counties with more college graduates voted at higher rates. That is a pattern across counties, not something that says anything about an individual voter.

## What to keep in mind

{: .box-note}
**The 2022 comparison stops at 17 days out.** The Secretary of State's 2022 absentee file is missing most ballots from Fulton and Gwinnett counties after October 22, 2022. We compare 2022 only up to the last complete day instead of guessing at the rest.

**Citizen adults is an estimate.** It is adults 18 and over minus non-citizen residents, from Census Bureau estimates compiled by [Georgia Civic Data](https://georgiacivicdata.org). It runs slightly low, so treat it as a rough yardstick. Active voters is the more precise rate.

Where the numbers come from, and how each is calculated, is documented on the [About the Data]({{ '/about-the-data' | relative_url }}#early-voting-turnout) page.
