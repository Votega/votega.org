VoteGA brings Georgia voter information together in one place: who represents you, who is on your ballot, and how elected officials have voted. The site is still growing, and the tools below are the ones available now.

## Find who represents you

- [Find Your Georgia State Legislator]({{ '/ga-state-reps' | relative_url }}) looks up your state House and Senate members, with committees and contact information.
- [Find Your U.S. Representative]({{ '/federal-reps' | relative_url }}) does the same for Georgia's members of Congress.
- [Find My Reps]({{ '/find-my-reps' | relative_url }}) finds all of your representatives from your address.

## Know your ballot

- [Georgia Elections]({{ '/elections/' | relative_url }}) lists every race and candidate on the 2026 ballot.
- [Your Sample Ballot]({{ '/sample-ballot' | relative_url }}) shows the contests on your own ballot.
- [Voter Access & Election Calendar]({{ '/ga-voter-access.html' | relative_url }}) has registration deadlines, early voting dates, and ways to vote.
- [Ballot Measures]({{ '/ga-ballot-measures.html' | relative_url }}) explains the statewide questions voters will decide.

## Follow the record

- [Bills & Resolutions]({{ '/ga-bills.html' | relative_url }}) tracks legislation in the General Assembly, with passage votes by party.
- [Legislator Voting Scorecard]({{ '/ga-party-unity.html' | relative_url }}) compares legislators on participation and party-line voting.
- [Legislature Majority Tracker]({{ '/ga-majority-tracker.html' | relative_url }}) shows the current party balance in each chamber.

## Where the data comes from

Figures come from official sources, including the Georgia General Assembly, the Georgia Secretary of State, Congress.gov, and campaign finance filings, and every page notes when its data was last updated. [About the Data]({{ '/about-the-data' | relative_url }}) explains the sources and methods, and the [open data catalog]({{ '/open-data' | relative_url }}) lets you download the same datasets.
