Georgia's **2026 General Election is Tuesday, November 3, 2026**. Every statewide office is on the ballot, along with all 14 U.S. House seats, all 236 seats in the Georgia General Assembly, and three statewide constitutional amendments. Whether you vote early, by mail, or in person on Election Day, a few deadlines decide whether your vote counts and they arrive sooner than most people expect.

Here is what you need to know, and where to look it up on VoteGA.

## The dates that matter

{: .box-note}
**Voter registration deadline: Monday, October 5, 2026.** If you are not registered by this date, you cannot vote in the November election. Check your status in two minutes at the [Georgia My Voter Page](https://mvp.sos.ga.gov){:target="_blank"}.

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---:|
| Voter registration deadline | October 5, 2026 |
| Advance (early) in-person voting | October 13 – October 30, 2026 |
| **Election Day** | **November 3, 2026** |
| General Election runoff (if needed) | December 1, 2026 |

Dates are maintained on our [Voter Access & Election Calendar]({{ '/ga-voter-access.html' | relative_url }}), which also links directly to check your registration, find your polling place, and track your absentee ballot.

## Step 1: Confirm you're registered (and where you vote)

Registration in Georgia is not "set it and forget it." People move, get flagged on list maintenance, or change names. Before anything else, confirm your status and polling place on the [My Voter Page](https://mvp.sos.ga.gov){:target="_blank"}. If you need to register or update your address, the October 5 deadline is firm.

## Step 2: See exactly what's on your ballot

The November ballot is long, and the races at the bottom such as: state legislature, local offices, and ballot measures are the ones most likely to be decided by a handful of votes. Our [Sample Ballot tool]({{ '/sample-ballot' | relative_url }}) lets you enter your information and preview the contests you'll actually see, so you're not making decisions for the first time in the booth. We do not track local elections however, so the best method to see your full ballot is the [My Voter Page](https://mvp.sos.ga.gov){:target="_blank"}.

Want the bigger picture first? The [2026 Elections hub]({{ '/elections' | relative_url }}) lists every race and the candidates running, and the [Candidates directory]({{ '/elections/candidates/' | relative_url }}) lets you browse who's on the ballot statewide.

## Step 3: Know who you're voting for

It's easy to fill in the top of the ticket and guess the rest. VoteGA is built to make the rest easy too:

- **[Find My Reps]({{ '/find-my-reps' | relative_url }})** — enter your address and see everyone who represents you, from your U.S. Senators down to your state House member.
- **[Federal delegation]({{ '/federal-reps' | relative_url }})** — Georgia's U.S. Senators and all 14 U.S. House members, with their records.
- **Incumbent records** — for the people already in office, you can see how they've actually voted and what they've done. 

## Step 4: Don't skip the amendments

Three statewide constitutional amendments are certified for the November ballot. Constitutional amendments are written in dense legal language and easy to skip but they change the state's founding document, the Georgia Constitution. Our [Georgia Ballot Measures tracker]({{ '/ga-ballot-measures.html' | relative_url }}) explains each measure in plain language, links the enabling legislation, and will show results after the election.
