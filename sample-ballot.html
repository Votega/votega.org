Your Sample Ballot

Enter your address to see the federal, statewide, state-legislative, and judicial contests on your Georgia ballot with a link to research on every candidate and measure. Or, select your county to see all districts that touch the selected county.

This is a preview of the state and federal portion of your ballot. Your official ballot also includes county and city races and local questions that VoteGA doesn't track. For your precinct-exact official sample ballot and polling place, use the GA Secretary of State My Voter Page.

Your address is sent only to the U.S. Census geocoder to look up your districts; VoteGA does not store it.
