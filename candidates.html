Claim your candidate profile

VoteGA.org already has a page for every candidate on Georgia's 2026 general election ballot, built from public records. If you're a candidate, or you work for one, this is how you fill in the part only you can write.

✅ What you can add

  • A biography, in your own words
  • A photo
  • Campaign, donation, volunteer, and social links
  • Endorsements, labeled as claimed by your campaign
  • Answers to the questionnaire everyone in your race gets

🔒 What you can't change

  • Your name, party, and office on the ballot
  • Whether you're the incumbent
  • Your voting record, if you've held office
  • Your campaign finance data

These come from public records and stay as they are. If something in the public-record part is wrong, we'll correct it.

How it works

  1. Find your profile. Every general-election candidate already has one. If you received an email from us, the link in it opens your profile's claim form directly.
  2. Fill it in. A biography, a photo, and the questionnaire, about five minutes. Everything except the first section is optional.
  3. We confirm it's really you. We reach out using contact information already on file for your campaign, from your qualifying paperwork or your campaign website. Nothing publishes until you confirm.
  4. It goes live. Your submission appears on your profile, labeled as submitted by your campaign and dated. Need to change it later? Submit again.

The questionnaire

Every candidate in a race gets the same questions, and we publish the answers side by side so voters can compare. Answers are capped in length so the comparison stays readable and the limits apply to everyone equally. Any skipped questions show as "No response," the same for every candidate.

Judicial candidates get a different, restricted set of questions. We've written them to avoid pledges or commitments about issues that could come before your court, consistent with Rule 4.1 of the Georgia Code of Judicial Conduct.

What this costs: nothing

VoteGA is a nonpartisan Georgia voter information site. We charge candidates nothing, and we don't accept payment from candidates, campaigns, or committees for anything. Not for a profile, not for placement, not for a feature. Claiming your profile gives you no advantage over a candidate who doesn't. Your page stays up either way.

How we handle what you submit

We label it.
Everything you submit is published as campaign-submitted content, clearly distinguished from the public-record parts of your profile, and dated.
We don't verify your claims and we say so.
Endorsements and biographical statements are published as you submit them, under a note saying VoteGA has not independently verified them.
We may decline some content.
We may decline or remove material that is defamatory, that targets an opponent by name, or that isn't about the office you're seeking. If we do, we'll tell you why.
What we don't ask for.
We don't collect your date of birth, home address, any government ID number, donor information, or anything about your opponents.

Ready to claim yours?
