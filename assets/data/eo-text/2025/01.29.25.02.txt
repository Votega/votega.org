EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On November 14, 2024, Mrs. Audrey Gibbons, member of the Glynn County
Board of Education, was indicted by the United States Attorney for the
District of Delaware on one count of Conspiracy to Commit Money
Laundering; and

Acertified copy of the indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant
to Code Section 45-5-6(b), and received on January 14, 2025; and

The fourteen-day period following receipt of the indictment as prescribed
by Code Section 45-5-6(e) has passed.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Melissa Hodge of the Camden
County Board of Education, and Roger Moss of the Savannah-Chatham
County Board of Education are hereby appointed as the review commission
to determine whether the above-mentioned indictment relates to and
adversely affects the administration of duties by Mrs. Gibbons such that the
rights and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby, and to make
a written report to the Governor within fourteen days with a
recommendation as to whether Mrs. Gibbons should be suspended from
office as required by Code Section 45-5-6(c).
