ORDERED:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 102.53 acres of improved real
property (the “Property”), by virtue of a Warranty Deed dated January 9, 1956,
from Empire Land Company being recorded in Deed Book 140, Pages 389-390, in
the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Clayton County; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Agriculture (the “Department”)
and was acquired as part of the Atlanta State Farmers Market; and

The Property is improved with sheds identified as numbers 3 and 4 (BLLIP #7627),
which is approximately 8,522 square feet, respectively (the “Structures”), as shown
in that aerial photograph attached hereto as Exhibit A, and incorporated herein by
reference; and

Said Structures are identified by the Department to be demolished due to significant
damage caused by a fire; and

The Commissioner of Agriculture on March 20, 2025, recommended the demolition
of the Structures and requested that the State Properties Commission seek an
Executive Order from the Governor to allow for the razing, demolition, and disposal
of the Structure as in the best interest of the State of Georgia; and

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Agriculture is hereby authorized and directed to provide for the
razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structures under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interests of the State of Georgia, in accordance
with applicable laws, policies, and procedures, including any requirements under
O.C.G.A. § 12-3-55 regarding historic properties that may pertain to the Structure.
