EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

House District 121 in the Georgia House of Representatives has become
vacant due to the resignation of Representative Marcus Wiedower.

Therefore, pursuant to GA. ConsT, Art. V, § II, 9 VIII and Code Section 21-
2-544, a writ of election is hereby issued to the Secretary of State for a
special election to be held on Tuesday, December 9, 2025, to fill the District
121 seat in the Georgia House of Representatives.

Bf

GOVERNOR

This 30th day of October 2025.
