EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On June 11, 2024, Mrs. Stephanie Lindsey, County Commissioner for
Newton County, was indicted by the United States Attorney for the
Northern District of Georgia on one count of Money Laundering
Conspiracy, one count of Transactional Money Laundering, one count of
Concealment Money Laundering, and two counts of Making and
Subscribing a False Tax Return; and

Acertified copy of the indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant
to Code Section 45-5-6(b), and received on January 2, 2025; and

The fourteen-day period following receipt of the indictment as prescribed
by Code Section 45-5-6(e) has passed.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS

HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Clinton Johnson of the Dougherty
County Board of Commissioners, and Bruce Henry of the Jasper County
Board of Commissioners are hereby appointed as the review commission to
determine whether the above-mentioned indictment relates to and
adversely affects the administration of duties by Mrs. Lindsey such that the
rights and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby, and to make
a written report to me within fourteen days with a recommendation as to
whether Mrs. Lindsey should be suspended from office as required by Code

Section 45-5-6(c).
