EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED:

There currently exist at Gordon State College, Lamar County, Barnesville, Georgia,
a pool and surrounding structure known as the Aquatic Center (the “Structures”)
located at 419 College Drive; and

The Board of Regents’ Policy 9.8.2.3 DEMOLITION authorizes the Chancellor or
Vice Chancellor for Facilities to declare buildings, structures and other
improvements no longer advantageously useful to the University System of
Georgia. Said Structures are included within this policy; and

It has been determined by Ms. Sandra Lynn Neuse, Vice Chancellor for Real Estate
and Facilities of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, that said
Structures can no longer be advantageously used by Gordon State College or the
University System of Georgia and should be removed.

NOW, THEREFORE, BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE
OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia is hereby authorized
to demolish and remove the Structures from Gordon State College, Lamar County,
Barnesville, Georgia.

IT IS HEREBY

That the appropriate staff of Gordon State College, acting under the approval of
the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, is hereby authorized to
demolish the Structures and to have all Structure materials removed from the
premises; all said demolition and removal to be performed as expeditiously as

possible.
