FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN DEKALB COUNTY

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Officer David Rose, 33, with the DeKalb County Police Department, died in
the line of duty on Friday, August 8, 2025; and

With a heartfelt desire to serve, Officer Rose lived out his goals to protect
the vulnerable, stand for justice, and being the person who ran toward
danger. His life was the epitome of trust, duty, and sacrifice; and

Prior to his service with the DeKalb County Police Department, Officer Rose
honorably served in the United States Marine Corps, including a
deployment in Afghanistan; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Officer Rose’s service to the citizens of
DeKalb County by lowering the flags on the State Capito] building and in
DeKalb County to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF OFFICER DAVID ROSE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be
flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in DeKalb County on
Friday, August 22, 2025, the day of Officer Rose’s public memorial, until
