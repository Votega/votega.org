THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN COBB AND FULTON COUNTIES

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Officer Jeremy R. Labonte, 24, with the Roswell Police Department died in
the line of duty on February 7, 2025; and

Known for his dedication to public service, Officer Labonte began his career
in law enforcement in 2021 with the Roswell Police Department; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Officer Labonte’s service to the citizens
of Roswell by lowering the flags on the State Capitol building and in Cobb
and Fulton Counties to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF OFFICER JEREMY R. LABONTE, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be
flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Cobb and Fulton
counties on Wednesday, February 12, 2025, the day of Officer Labonte’s

interment, until sunset.
