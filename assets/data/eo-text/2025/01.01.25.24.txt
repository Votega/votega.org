THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Byron Andrew Smith of
Lee County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a
member of the Georgia Commission on the
Deaf and Hard of Hearing, for a term of office
expiring January 1, 2028, to succeed the
Honorable Deshonda Washington, whose term
expired December 28, 2028.
