ORDERED:

On November 14, 2024, Mrs. Audrey Gibbons, member of the Glynn County
Board of Education, was indicted by the United States Attorney for the
District of Delaware on one count of Conspiracy to Commit Money
Laundering; and

Acertified copy of the indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant
to Code Section 45-5-6(b) and received on January 14, 2025; and

Executive Order 01.29.25.02 was issued on January 29, 2025, appointing
members to the Review Commission to determine whether the indictment
relates to and adversely affects the administration of the Glynn County
Board of Education such that the rights and interests of the public are
adversely affected; and

Code Section 45-5-6(c) provides that additional time may be granted by the
Governor for a review commission to make its written report; and

On February 7, 2025, the above-mentioned review commission requested a
fourteen-day extension to make its written report.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the period for the above-mentioned review commission to make its
written report is extended to February 26, 2025.

Bil

GOVERNOR

This 12th day of February 2025.
