EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Elizabeth H. Reynolds of
Fulton County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a
member of the Board of Directors of the Georgia
Emergency Communications Authority, for a term
of office expiring April 11, 2026, to succeed the
Honorable Richard Allan Johnson, Jr., who
resigned.

This 9th day of September 2025

GOVERNOR
