The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 320 acres of improved real property
(hereinafter the “Property”) in Walker County, Georgia filed with the State
Properties Commission Real Property Record as Property BLLIP ID# 003971; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Corrections and is commonly
known as Walker State Prison; and

The Property is improved with a structure identified as the “Lawrence House”
(BLLIP #6960) (hereinafter, the “Structure”), as shown in the aerial photograph
attached hereto as Exhibit A; and

The Department of Corrections is desirous of demolishing the Structure due to the
state of severe disrepair; and

By official action dated April 3, 2025, the Georgia Board of Corrections requested
authority to demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Corrections is authorized and directed to provide for the razing,
demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions which are
deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia, in accordance with applicable
laws, policies, and procedures, including any requirements under O.C.G.A. § 12-3-55
regarding historic properties that may pertain to the Structure.
