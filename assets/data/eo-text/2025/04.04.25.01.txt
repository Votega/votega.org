EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

Upon the death of Labor Commissioner Bruce Thompson, the office
of the Commissioner of Labor is now vacant; and

In accordance with the Georgia Constitution, Art. V, § II, Para. VIII
(a), in the event of a vacancy in the office of Commissioner of Labor,
the Governor shall fill the vacancy for the remainder of the unexpired
term of that office.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Honorable Barbara Rivera Holmes is hereby appointed
Commissioner of Labor for the State of Georgia, for the remainder of
the unexpired term of that office.

This 4% day of April 2025.
