The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 48.75 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) in Thomson-McDuffie County, Georgia filed
with the State Properties Commission Real Property Record as Property BLLIP
ID# 08709; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Technical College System of Georgia and is
commonly known as the Thomson-McDuffie Site of Augusta Technical College; and

The Property is improved with structures identified as the “Greenhouse-Thomson”
(BLLIP #48095) and “Greenhouse-Thomson Small Lab” (BLLIP #64549)
(hereinafter, collectively the “Structures”), as shown in the aerial photograph
attached hereto as Exhibit A; and

The Technical College System of Georgia is desirous of demolishing the Structures for
the construction of a new CDL Driving Range, project TCSG-407; and

By official action dated September 4, 2025, the Technical College System of Georgia
Board requested authority to demolish the Structures.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Technical College System of Georgia is authorized and directed to provide for the
razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structures under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia, in accordance with
applicable laws, policies, and procedures, including any requirements under O.C.G.A
§ 12-3-55 regarding historic properties that may pertain to the Structures. .
