ORDERED:

On June 2, 2025, Mr. Theodore W. Hamby, III, Councilman on the Guyton City
Council, was indicted on three counts of Financial Transaction Card Fraud and two
counts of Making a False Statement; and

The indictment was nolle prossed, and on June 4, 2025, Mr. Hamby elected to
proceed under accusation for the same charges; and

Certified copies of the indictment and accusation were transmitted to the Governor
pursuant to Code Section 45-5-6(b) and received on June 23, 2025; and

Executive Order 07.17.25.01 was issued on July 17, 2025, appointing members to
the review commission to determine whether the charges relates to and adversely
affects the administration of the Guyton City Council such that the rights and
interests of the public are adversely affected; and

Executive Order 07.31.25.01 was issued on July 31, 2025, extending the date by
which the review commission was to submit its report and recommendation; and

On August 14, 2025, the review commission found that the indictment of Mr.
Hamby does relate to and adversely affect the administration of the Guyton City
Council such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected
thereby, and recommended that Mr. Hamby be suspended from office pursuant to
the provisions of Code Section 45-5-6.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mr. Theodore W. Hamby, III, is hereby suspended from office immediately
and without further action pending the final disposition of the case or until the
expiration of his term of office, whichever occurs first, as provided by Code Section

45-5-6(c).
