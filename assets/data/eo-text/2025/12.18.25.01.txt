EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That the Honorable Susan S. Camp is hereby appointed Judge
of the State Court of Douglas County to fill the seat created by
the enactment of House Bill 1236 (Act Number 516) during
the 2024 session of the Georgia General Assembly, for a term
of office ending December 31, 2028.
