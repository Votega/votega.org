ORDERED:

On June 11, 2024, Mrs. Stephanie Lindsey, Newton County District 3
Commissioner, was indicted by the United States Attorney for the Northern
District of Georgia on one count of Money Laundering Conspiracy, one count of
Transactional Money Laundering, one count of Concealment Money Laundering,
and two counts of Making and Subscribing a False Tax Return; and

A certified copy of the indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to
Code Section 45-5-6(b), and received on January 2, 2025; and

Executive Order 01.23.25.02 was issued on January 23, 2025, appointing members
to the review commission to determine whether the indictment relates to and
adversely affects the administration of the office of Newton County District 3
Commissioner such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely
affected; and

On February 7, 2025, the above-mentioned review commission found that the
indictment of Mrs. Lindsey does relate to and does adversely affect the
administration of the office of Newton County District 3 Commissioner such that
the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby and
recommended that Mrs. Lindsey be suspended from office pursuant to the
provisions of Code Section 45-5-6.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mrs, Lindsey is hereby suspended from office immediately and without
further action pending the final disposition of the case or until expiration of her
term of office, whichever occurs first, as provided in Code Section 45-5-6(c).
