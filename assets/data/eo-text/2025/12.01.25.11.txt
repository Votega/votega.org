THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Harold “Hal” James Ragan, Jr.
of Fulton County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as
a member of the Georgia Development Authority,
for a term of office expiring December 1, 2029, to
succeed the Honorable Steve Stipe, whose term
expired July 1, 2022.

This 1st day of December 2025

GOVERNOR
