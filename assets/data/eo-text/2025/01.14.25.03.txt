THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Maria “Kristi” Christine Garrett
of Carroll County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as
the Third Congressional District Representative on
the State Board of Education, for a term of office
expiring January 1, 2032, to succeed the
Honorable Helen O. Rice, whose term expired
January 1, 2025.

This 14 day of January 2025

Bil he

GOVERNOR
