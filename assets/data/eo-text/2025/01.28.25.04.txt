THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Seth Austin Stowers of Dawson
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a member
of the State Board of Veterinary Medicine, for a term
of office expiring January 28, 2030, to succeed the
Honorable Beckey E. Malphus, whose term expired
January 13, 2025.
