EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

House District 106 in the Georgia House of Representatives has become
vacant due to the resignation of Representative Rachelle “Shelly”
Hutchinson,

Therefore, pursuant to GA. CONST. Art. V, § II, VIII and Code Section 21-
2-544, a writ of election is hereby issued to the Secretary of State for a
special election to be held on Tuesday, November 4, 2025, to fill the District
106 seat in the Georgia House of Representatives.

Df

GOVERNOR

This 8th day of September 2025.
