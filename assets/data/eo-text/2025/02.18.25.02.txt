ORDERED:

On November 12, 2024, Mr. Royce Reeves, Vice Chairman of the Cordele City
Commission, was indicted on three counts of Violation of the Georgia Racketeer
Influenced Corrupt Organization Act (RICO), four counts of Illegal Use of
Communication Facility, six counts of Violation of Street Gang Terrorism and
Prevention Act, and two counts Distribution of Marijuana; and

A certified copy of the indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to
Code Section 45-5-6(b), and received on November 14, 2024; and

Executive Order 01.23.25.03 and Executive Order 01.29.25.01 were issued on
January 23, 2025, and January 29, 2025, respectively, appointing members to the
Review Commission to determine whether the indictment relates to and adversely
affects the administration of the Office of the Cordele City Commission such that
the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected; and

On February 10, 2025, the above-mentioned review commission found that the
indictment of Mr. Reeves does relate to and does adversely affect the
administration of the Office of the Cordele City Commission such that the rights
and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby; and recommended that
Mr. Reeves be suspended from office pursuant to the provisions of Code Section

45-5-6.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mr. Reeves is hereby suspended from office immediately and without further
action pending the final disposition of the case or until expiration of his term of
office, whichever occurs first, as provided in Code Section 45-5-6(c).
