ORDERED:

FLAGS ON STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF STAFF

On Wednesday, August 27, 2025, in Minneapolis, Minnesota, an incomprehensible
act of violence was committed when a very troubled gunman approached the
Church of the Annunciation and its affiliated parochial school and opened fire
through the stained-glass windows during a school-wide Mass service; and

Two students were killed, and seventeen children and adults were injured; and

The people of Georgia share the grief of the parents, families, and friends of the
victims, and we pray for their comfort and healing in the days to come; and

Such an act of violence against innocent children in a house of worship is abhorrent
and indefensible; and

To honor the victims of the Minneapolis shooting, the President of the United
States has ordered that the flag of the United States be flown at half-staff on federal
government buildings and grounds.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THOSE
AFFECTED BY THIS TRAGEDY, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That in honor of the victims and in conjunction with the Proclamation of the
President of the United States of America, the flag of the United States and the flag
of the State of Georgia shall be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds
from this date until sunset on Sunday, August 31, 2025.

Bilt

GOVERNOR

This 287 day of August 2025.
