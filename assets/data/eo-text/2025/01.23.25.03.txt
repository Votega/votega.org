EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On November 12, 2024, Mr. Royce Reeves, Vice Chairman of the Cordele
City Commission, was indicted on three counts of Violation of the Georgia
Racketeer Influenced Corrupt Organization Act (RICO), four counts of
Illegal Use of Communication Facility, six counts of Violation of Street Gang
Terrorism and Prevention Act, and two counts of Distribution of Marijuana;
and

Acertified copy of the indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant
to Code Section 45-5-6(b), and received on November 14, 2024; and

The fourteen-day period following receipt of the indictment as prescribed
by Code Section 45-5-6(e) has passed.

Now, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS

HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Rodney McCord of the Griffin City
Commission, and Chad Warbington of the Albany City Commission are
hereby appointed as the review commission to determine whether the
above-mentioned indictment relates to and adversely affects the
administration of duties by Mr. Reeves such that the rights and interests of
the public are adversely affected thereby, and to make a written report to
me within fourteen days with a recommendation as to whether Mr. Reeves
should be suspended from office as required by Code Section 45-5-6(c).

Bill

GOVERNOR

This 23" day of January 2025.
