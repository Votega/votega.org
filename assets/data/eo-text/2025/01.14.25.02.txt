There currently exists at Georgia Southern University, Bulloch County,
Statesboro, Georgia, a baseball facility building identified as #510 (the
“Building”), located at 2480 Southern Drive; and

The Board of Regents’ Policy 9.8.2.3 DEMOLITION authorizes the
Chancellor or Vice Chancellor for Facilities to declare buildings, structures
and other improvements no longer advantageously useful to the University
System of Georgia. Said Building is included within this policy; and

It has been determined by Ms. Sandra Lynn Neuse, Vice Chancellor for Real
Estate and Facilities of the Board of Regents of the University System of
Georgia, that said Building can no longer be advantageously used by
Georgia Southern University or the University System of Georgia and
should be removed.

Now, THEREFORE, BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia is hereby
authorized to demolish and remove the Building from Georgia Southern
University, Bulloch County, Statesboro, Georgia.

IT IS FURTHER

That the appropriate staff of Georgia Southern University, acting under the
approval of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, is
hereby authorized to demolish the Building and to have all building
materials removed from the premises by public works contract; all said
demolition and removal to be performed as expeditiously as possible.

GOVERNOR
