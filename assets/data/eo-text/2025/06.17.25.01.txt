ORDERED:

On May 28, 2025, Mr. Kelvin M. Owens, Mayor of Camilla, Georgia, was
indicted on one count of Interference with Elections and two counts of
Conspiracy to Commit Election Fraud; and

Acertified copy of the indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant
to Code Section 45-5-6(b) and received on June 2, 2025; and

The fourteen-day period following receipt of the indictment as prescribed
by Code Section 45-5-6(e) has passed.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Mayor Garnett Johnson of
Augusta, Georgia, and Mayor LaRhonda Patrick of Warner Robins, Georgia
are hereby appointed as the review commission to determine whether the
above-mentioned indictment relates to and adversely affects the
administration of duties by Mayor Owens such that the rights and interests
of the public are adversely affected thereby, and to make a written report to
me within fourteen (14) days with a recommendation as to whether Mayor
Owens should be suspended from office as required by Code Section 45-5-
