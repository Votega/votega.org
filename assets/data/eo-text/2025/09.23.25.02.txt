WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

' There currently exists at Georgia State University, Fulton County,

Atlanta, Georgia, a building known as Sparks Hall (the “Building”)
located at 33 Gilmer Street; and

The Board of Regents, at its meeting on January 14, 2025, declared
that said Building could no longer be advantageously used by
Georgia State University or the University System of Georgia and
should be removed.

Now, THEREFORE, BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF
THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia is
hereby authorized to demolish and remove the Building from
Georgia State University, Fulton County, Atlanta, Georgia.

IT IS FURTHER

That the appropriate staff of Georgia State University, acting under
the approval of the Board of Regents of the University System of
Georgia, is hereby authorized to demolish the Building and to have
all building materials removed from the premises; all said demolition
and removal to be performed as expeditiously as possible.
