WHEREAS:

The State of Georgia holds title to and is in possession of certain
recently created dredge spoil islands named Brunswick Bird Island
and Cumberland Dividings (the “premises”) as described in the
attached EXHIBIT “A,” incorporated herein by reference, due to
their creation from state-owned water bottoms; and

Brunswick Bird Island was created with dredge spoil from the
Brunswick Harbor deepening project and Cumberland Dividings was
created with dredge spoil from the maintenance dredging of the
Atlantic Intracoastal Waterway; and

These premises were created with dredge spoil for the express
purpose of providing valuable nesting sites for State-protected
beach-nesting and colonial-nesting bird species; and

The Department of Natural Resources has determined that the
premises contain some of the best nesting habitats on the Georgia
Coast for beach-nesting and colonial-nesting bird species; and

Colonial nesting seabird species nest in large dense colonies that are
extremely vulnerable to disturbance by people and dogs; and

The Department of Natural Resources desires to gain custody of the
premises to protect habitat for beach-nesting and colonial-nesting
bird species by supervising and controlling visitor use and access
seasonally; and

I have determined that it is in the best interest of the State of Georgia
to transfer custody of the premises from the State of Georgia to the
Department of Natural Resources to protect habitat for beach-
nesting and colonial-nesting bird species.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, PARTICULARLY BY VIRTUE
OF THE PROVISION OF CODE SECTION 50-16-61, IT IS HEREBY

ORDERED: That custody of the premises is hereby transferred from the State of
Georgia to the Department of Natural Resources for the purposes

above states.

EXHIBIT “A”

Cumberland Dividings is all the land above mean low water which comprises a 5-acre
dredge spoil island in Camden County created by the Army Corp of Engineers in the
Summer of 2024. It was constructed during maintenance dredging of the Atlantic
Intracoastal Waterway from Cabin Bluff to Kings Bay Submarine Base in Camden County
and was designed to provide habitat for beach-nesting and colonial-nesting bird species.
It is located adjacent to Cabin Bluff, West of Cumberland Island National Seashore.
(Approximate location 30.8868, -81.5111). Cumberland Dividings is illustrated on the
attached Map # 1, which is incorporated herein by reference.

Brunswick Bird Island is all the land above mean low water which comprises a 14-acre
dredge spoil island created by the Army Corp of Engineers in 2007. It is located in St.
Simons Sound, Glynn County, approximately 1 mile west of the Jekyll Island Fishing Pier.
It was constructed to provide habitat for beach-nesting and colonial-nesting bird species
and has become one of the most important seabird colonies in the southeast, regularly
hosting over 10,000 nesting pairs of birds. (Approximate location 31.1141, -81.4351).
Brunswick Bird Island is illustrated on the attached Map # 2, which is incorporated herein
by reference.
