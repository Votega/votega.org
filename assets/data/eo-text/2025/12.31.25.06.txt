THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Karen Addams of Gwinnett
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a member
of the Georgia State Rehabilitation Council, for a
term of office expiring April 17, 2026, to succeed the
Honorable Deborah C. Hibben, who resigned.
