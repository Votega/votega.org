THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

Senate District 18 in the Georgia State Senate has become vacant due to the
resignation of Senator John F. Kennedy.

Therefore, pursuant to GA. CONST. Art. V, § II, { VIII and Code Section 21-
2-544, a writ of election is hereby issued to the Secretary of State for a

special election to be held on Tuesday, January 20, 2026, to fill the District
18 seat in the Georgia State Senate.

This 10 day of December 2025.

Bf

GOVERNOR
