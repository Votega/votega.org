ORDERED:

On July 31, 2025, Ms. Connie Mae Taylor, Clerk of Cobb County Superior
Court, was indicted on two counts of Destruction, Alteration, and
Corruption of Public Records and two counts of Violation of Oath of Office;
and

A certified copy of the indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant
to Code Section 45-5-6(b) and received on August 1, 2025; and

The fourteen-day period following receipt of the indictment as prescribed
by Code Section 45-5-6(e) has passed; and

Pursuant to Code Section 45-5-6(b), since the Attorney General of Georgia
brought the indictment against Ms. Connie Mae Taylor, Clerk of Cobb
County Superior Court, the Attorney General shall not serve on the
commission, and a retired Supreme Court Justice or retired Court of
Appeals Judge shall serve in place of the Attorney General.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the Honorable Keith Blackwell, retired Justice of the Supreme Court
of Georgia; Tim Harper, Clerk of Banks County Superior Court; and Rhett
Walker, Clerk of Dodge County Superior Court are hereby appointed as the
review commission to determine whether the above-mentioned indictment
relates to and adversely affects the administration of duties by Ms. Taylor
such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected
thereby, and to make a written report to me within fourteen (14) days with
a recommendation as to whether Ms. Taylor should be suspended from

office, as required by Code Section 45-5-6(c).
