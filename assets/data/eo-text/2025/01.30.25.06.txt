THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Haynes Maier Studstill of Lanier
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the Eight
Congressional District Representative on the Board of
Regents of the University of Georgia, for a term of
office expiring January 1, 2032, to succeed the
Honorable Allen Gudenrath, whose term expired
January 1, 2025.
