ORDERED:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 0.827 of an acre of improved real
property located in Land Lot 274, 14th District, 3rd Section, Gordon County
(“Property”) as shown on the survey attached hereto as Exhibit “A”; and

Said Property is a portion of the 3.663 acres of improved real property located in Land
Lot 274, 14th District, 3rd Section, 132 Old Fairmount Hwy, SE, City of Calhoun,
acquired by virtue of a Warranty Deed dated May 1971, being recorded in Deed Book
102, Page 203, in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Gordon County, and on
file with the State Properties Commission Real Property Records as RPR # 05192
which Property is under the custody of the Georgia Bureau of Investigation for their
Regional 1 Office; and

By Official Action dated May 8, 2025, the State Board of Public Safety declared the
Property surplus and approved the transfer of custody and control from the
Georgia Bureau of Investigation to the Department of Public Safety; and

By Official Action dated May 8, 2025, the State Board of Public Safety requested
custody of the Property from the Georgia Bureau of Investigation to the
Department of Public Safety for their Specialized Collision Reconstruction Team
A”.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR
OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That custody of the above-defined Property located in Gordon County, Georgia is
hereby transferred from the Georgia Bureau of Investigation to the Department of
Public Safety.
