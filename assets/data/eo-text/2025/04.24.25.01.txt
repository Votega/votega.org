The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 71.837 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) in Murray County, Georgia filed with the State
Properties Commission Real Property Record as Property BLLIP ID# 001022; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Natural Resources and is
commonly known as Fort Mountain State Park; and

The Property is improved with a structure identified as the “Group Pavilion”
(BLLIP #24536) (hereinafter, the “Structure”), as shown in the aerial photograph
attached hereto as Exhibit A; and

The Department of Natural Resources is desirous of demolishing the Structure due
to significant deficiencies that compromise safety, functionality, and compliance with
current standards; and

By official action dated February 25, 2025, the Department of Natural Resources
Board requested authority to demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Natural Resources is authorized and directed to provide for the
razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia, in accordance with
applicable laws, policies, and procedures, including any requirements under O.C.G.A.
§ 12-3-55 regarding historic properties that may pertain to the Structure.

This 24 day of April 2025.
