ORDERED:

On June 2, 2025, Mr. Theodore W. Hamby, III, Councilman on the Guyton City
Council, was indicted on three counts of Financial Transaction Card Fraud and two
counts of Making a False Statement; and

The indictment was nolle prossed, and on June 4, 2025, Mr. Hamby elected to
proceed under accusation for the same charges; and

Certified copies of the indictment and accusation were transmitted to the Governor
pursuant to Code Section 45-5-6(b) and received on June 23, 2025; and

The fourteen-day period following receipt of the indictment and accusation as
prescribed by Code Section 45-5-6(e) has passed.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Dan Peterson of the Perry City Council,
and Tommy Roberts of the Fitzgerald City Council are hereby appointed as the
review commission to determine whether the above-mentioned charges relate to
and adversely affect the administration of duties by Mr. Hamby such that the rights
and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby, and to make a written
report to me within fourteen (14) days with a recommendation as to whether Mr.
Hamby should be suspended from office as required by Code Section 45-5-6(c).
