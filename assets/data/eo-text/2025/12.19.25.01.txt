ORDERED:

On October 27, 2025, Mr. Kenneth Henderson, Sheriff of Towns County, was
indicted on three counts of Violation of Oath of Office by Public Officer, one count
of False Imprisonment Under Color of Law, one count of False Imprisonment, one
count of Simple Battery on a Police Officer, and one count of Simple Battery; and

A certified copy of the indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to
Code Section 45-5-6(b) and received on November 10, 2025; and

On December 2, 2025, Executive Order 12.02.25.01 was issued appointing
members to the review commission to determine whether the above-mentioned
indictment relates to and adversely affects the administration of duties by Mr.
Henderson such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected
thereby; and

By report dated December 16, 2025, the review commission found that the
indictment of Mr. Henderson does relate to and does adversely affect the
administration of duties by Mr. Henderson, and that the rights and interests of the
public are adversely affected thereby; and recommended that Sheriff Henderson
be suspended from office pursuant to the provisions of Code Section 45-5-6.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mr. Henderson is hereby suspended from office immediately and without
further action pending the final disposition of the case or until the expiration of his
term of office, whichever occurs first, as provided in Code Section 45-5-6(c).
