THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED: That the Honorable Luis Manuel Solis, Jr. of
Gwinnett County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as
the Ninth Congressional District Representative on
the Board of Corrections, for a term of office expiring
July 1, 2026, to succeed the Honorable Stacy M.
Jarrard who is now an At-Large Representative on
the Board of Corrections, as a result of the Georgia
House of Representative Redistricting Act of 2023,
adopted December 8, 2023.

This 30 day of January 2025
