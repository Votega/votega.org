UPDATING THE GEORGIA EMERGENCY OPERATIONS PLAN

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

. The State of Georgia may be subjected to emergencies and disasters

of various kinds and of different magnitudes; and

The State of Georgia will plan and prepare to implement effective
responses to mitigate the effects of such emergencies and disasters,
to protect public peace, health, and safety, and to preserve the lives
and property of the people; and

The Georgia Emergency Management Act of 1981, Code Section 38-
3-1, et seg., sets forth the authority and responsibility of local
governments, state agencies, and the undersigned in prevention of,
preparation for, response to, and recovery from natural or man-
made emergencies or hostile military or paramilitary actions; and

Code Section 38-3-2, provides that it is the responsibility of the State
to ensure that preparations are adequate to deal with emergencies or
disasters resulting from manmade or natural causes or enemy attack
and it is necessary to provide for the common defense, to protect the
public peace, health, and safety, and to preserve the lives and
property of the people of this State; and

Code Section 38-3-20 tasks the Governor with appointing the
Director of the Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland
Security Agency, who is responsible for coordinating the activities of
all organizations for emergency management and homeland security
within the State, including leading the public safety response to
natural disasters, homeland security activities, and other
emergencies within the State; and

Code Section 38-3-22, authorizes and empowers the Governor to
exercise general direction and control over all or any part of the
emergency management functions within this State, to prepare a
comprehensive plan and program for emergency management, and

to delegate any authority vested under the Georgia Emergency
Management Act; and

The Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland Security
Agency shall establish and maintain, in collaboration with all
appropriate state agencies and volunteer organizations a
standardized, verifiable, performance-based, unified incident
command system, to be utilized in response to emergencies and
disasters; and

The Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland Security
Agency will coordinate planning and lead emergency operations of
all agencies, associations, authorities, boards, departments, or
institutions within the State of Georgia; and

The Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland Security
Agency will coordinate emergency mitigation, preparedness,
prevention, response, and recovery activities in a manner consistent
with the National Response Framework and the principles of the
National Incident Management System; and,

The Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland Security
Agency has developed and submitted to the undersigned for review
and approval the 2024 Georgia Emergency Operations Plan (the
“2024 GEOP”), which sets forth the policies and procedures
governing the State’s preparation for and coordinated response to
any disasters or all-hazard events that may occur in or otherwise
impact the State of Georgia.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 38-3-22 AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the 2024 GEOP, referenced hereto as “Exhibit 1”, and on file at
the Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland Security
Agency, is hereby approved.

IT IS FURTHER

That Director of the Georgia Emergency Management and
Homeland Security Agency, or the Director’s designee, is delegated
the power and authority to exercise overall direction and
coordination of emergency and disaster planning and operations, as
authorized by the Georgia Emergency Management Act of 1981,
O.C.G.A. § 38-3-1, et seq., and stated in the 2024 GEOP.

IT IS FURTHER

That every department or agency of the State shall appoint an
Emergency Coordinator and Alternate to serve during emergencies
or disasters, and as a planning liaison to the Georgia Emergency
Management and Homeland Security Agency.

IT IS FURTHER

That each department or agency of the State shall be responsible for
its Emergency Support Functions as assigned in the 2024 GEOP.

IT IS FURTHER

That each department or agency of the State assigned Emergency
Support Function coordination responsibilities shall aid in
preparation and revision of such functions, collaborate with primary
and support agencies, and maintain comprehensive standard
operating procedures for executing its assigned emergency services.

IT IS FURTHER

That each department or agency of the State assigned primary
and/or support responsibilities shall assign personnel to serve in the
State Operations Center and/or other state command and control
facilities, maintain a twenty-four (24) hour response capability, keep
required reporting systems in compliance with state and federal laws
and regulations, and prepare updates to the 2024 GEOP, as
necessary.

IT IS FURTHER

That each department or agency of the State assigned primary
and/or support responsibilities shall participate in scheduled
exercises and shall conduct the requisite training of personnel
essential to the implementation of all assigned Emergency Support
Functions.

ITIS FURTHER

That each department or agency of the State assigned primary
and/or support responsibilities shall participate in all after action
discussions following activations of 2024 GEOP to identify and
complete necessary improvement actions.

IT IS FURTHER

That each department or agency of the State shall execute, without
delay, the emergency functions so designated in the 2024 GEOP, or
as further ordered or otherwise directed by the undersigned, during
any emergency or disaster through the initial use of existing
department or agency appropriations and all necessary department
or agency personnel, regardless of normal duty assignment.

IT IS FURTHER

That all other agencies, associations, authorities, boards,
departments, or institutions of the State not assigned a primary or
support role in the 2024 GEOP will carry out whatever duties or
services that may be required during an emergency or disaster as
specified or directed by the Governor or the Director of the Georgia
Emergency Management and Homeland Security Agency.

IT IS FURTHER

That when the Governor declares a State of Emergency, the
Department of Public Safety, with assistance from other law
enforcement agencies, including the Georgia Department of Defense,
shall at the direction of the Governor, be authorized to enforce
criminal laws and assist with evacuations in the State, if required.

IT IS FURTHER

That if one or more of the provisions contained in this Order shall be
held to be invalid, in violation of the Georgia Constitution, in
violation of Georgia law, or unenforceable in any respect, such
invalidity, violation, or unenforceability shall not affect any other
provisions of this Order, but, in such case, this Order shall be

IT IS FURTHER

That no provision of this Order shall limit, infringe, suspend, or
supplant any judicial order, judgment, or decree issued pursuant to
the laws or Constitution of this State or the laws or Constitution of
the United States, nor shall any person use any provision of this
Order as a defense to an action in violation of a judicial order,
judgment, or decree by any court created pursuant to the laws or
Constitution of this State or the laws or Constitution of the United
States.

IT IS FURTHER

That this Order does not suspend operation of any state or federal
law or regulation, except as specifically described herein.

IT IS FURTHER

The Director of the Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland
Security Agency shall make necessary changes to the 2024 GEOP
with appropriate coordination and shall similarly promulgate
additional annexes and appendices as required.

IT IS FURTHER
