EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Stacy M. Jarrard of Lumpkin
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as an At-Large
Representative on the Board of Corrections, for a
term of office expiring January 30, 2030, to succeed
the Honorable Luis Manuel Solis who is now an
At-Large Representative on the Board of Corrections,
as a result of the Georgia House of Representative
Redistricting Act of 2023, adopted December 8, 2023.
