THE STATE OF GEORGIA
                             EXECUTIVE ORDER


BY THE GOVERNOR:




     WHEREAS:   On March 7, 2025, Executive Order 03.07.25.01 was issued suspending
                Mr. Marcello Banes, Newton County Board of Commissioners
                Chairman, from office; and

     WHEREAS:   Pursuant to Code Section 45-5-6(d)(1), there being no general or local
                law, ordinance, or resolution governing the appointment of a
                replacement officer, the Governor is authorized to appoint, for the
                duration of the suspension, a replacement for the Newton County Board
                of Commissioners Chairman.

                NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6(D)(1), AND
                THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
                GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

     ORDERED:   That the Honorable Linda Hays of Newton County, Georgia, is hereby
                appointed to fill the temporary vacancy in the office of Newton County
                Board of Commissioners Chairman.

                This 7th day of March 2025.




                                                 __________________________
                                                          GOVERNOR
