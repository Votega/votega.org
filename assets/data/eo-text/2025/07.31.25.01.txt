ORDERED:

On June 2, 2025, Mr. Theodore W. Hamby, III, Councilman on the Guyton
City Council, was indicted on three counts of Financial Transaction Card
Fraud and two counts of Making a False Statement; and

The indictment was nolle prossed, and on June 4, 2025, Mr. Hamby elected
to proceed under accusation for the same charges; and

Certified copies of the indictment and accusation were transmitted to the
Governor pursuant to Code Section 45-5-6(b) and received on June 23,
2025; and

Executive Order 07.17.25.01 was issued on July 17, 2025, appointing
members to the review commission to determine whether the indictment
and accusation relate to and adversely affect the administration of duties by
Mr. Hamby such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely
affected; and

Code Section 45-5-6(c) provides that additional time may be granted by the
Governor for a review commission to make its written report; and

On July 22, 2025, the above-mentioned review commission requested a
fourteen-day extension to make its written report.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the period for the above-mentioned review commission to make its
written report is extended to August 14, 2025.
