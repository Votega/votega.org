THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

Senate District 21 in the Georgia State Senate has become vacant due to the
resignation of Senator Brandon Beach.

Therefore, pursuant to GA. CONST. Art. V, § II, § VII and Code Section 21-
2-544, a writ of election is hereby issued to the Secretary of State for a
special election to be held on Tuesday, August 26, 2025, to fill the District
21 seat in the Georgia State Senate.

This 22 day of May 2025.
