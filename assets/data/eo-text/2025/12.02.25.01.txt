EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On October 27, 2025, Mr. Kenneth Henderson, Sheriff of Towns County,
was indicted on three counts of Violation of Oath by Public Officer, one
count of False Imprisonment Under Color of Law, one count of False
Imprisonment, one count of Simple Battery on a Police Officer, and one
count of Simple Battery; and

A certified copy of the indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant
to Code Section 45-5-6(b), and received on November 10, 2025; and

The fourteen-day period following receipt of the indictment as prescribed
by Code Section 45-5-6(e) has passed.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Sheriff Lenn Wood of Coweta
County, and Sheriff Ezell Brown of Newton County are hereby appointed as
the review commission to determine whether the above-mentioned
indictment relates to and adversely affects the administration of duties by
Mr. Henderson such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely
affected thereby, and to make a written report to me within fourteen days
with a recommendation as to whether Mr. Henderson should be suspended
from office as required by Code Section 45-5-6(c).

GOVERNOR
