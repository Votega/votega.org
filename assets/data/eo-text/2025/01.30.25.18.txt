THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Chris Clark of Forsyth County,
Georgia, is appointed to serve as the Seventh
Congressional District Representative on the Board of
Corrections, for a term of office expiring July 1, 2026,
to succeed the Honorable Janelle Lashaun King, who
shall no longer served as the Seventh Congressional

District Representative on the Board of Corrections in
accordance with O.C.G.A. § 21-1-3.

This 30th day of January 2025

Bil he

GOVERNOR
