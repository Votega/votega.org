THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Kelly Martin Layton of
Glynn County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as
a member of the Georgia Council for the Arts,
for a term of office expiring September 9, 2028,
to succeed the Honorable Gina H. Jeffords,
whose term expired June 30, 2024.

This 9th day of September 2025

GOVERNOR
