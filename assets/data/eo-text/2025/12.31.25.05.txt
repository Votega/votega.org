THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Oretta “Sissee” Kirkland of
Forsyth County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a
member of the Council on American Indian Concerns,
for a term of office expiring December 31, 2028, to
succeed the Honorable Donald R. Kirland, whose term
expired July 1, 2024.

This 31st day of December 2025

Beh

GOVERNOR
