ORDERED:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 148.386 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) in Houston County, Georgia filed with the
State Properties Commission Real Property Record as Property BLLIP ID# 09836;
and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Natural Resources and is
commonly known as Flat Creek Public Fishing Area; and

The Property is improved with structures identified as the “Judge Richardson
Home” (BLLIP #30041), “J. Richardson Wellhouse” (BLLIP #30044), and
“Storage Building” (BLLIP #30042) (hereinafter, the “Structures”), as shown in
the aerial photograph and location map attached hereto as Exhibit A; and

The Department of Natural Resources is desirous of demolishing the Structures due
to significant deficiencies that compromise safety, functionality, and compliance with
current standards; and

By official action dated June 24, 2025, the Georgia Board of Natural Resources
requested authority to demolish the Structures.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Natural Resources is authorized and directed to provide for the
razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structures under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia, in accordance with
applicable laws, policies, and procedures, including any requirements under Code
Section 12-3-55 regarding historic properties that may pertain to the Structure.
