EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Laraleigh Windom of Forsyth
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a member
of the Georgia Council on Developmental Disabilities,
for a term of office expiring July 1, 2026, to succeed
the Honorable Elana Alessandra Taylor, who
resigned.

This 29th day of October 2025

GOVERNOR
