There currently exists at the Armstrong campus of Georgia Southern University,
Chatham County, Savannah, Georgia, a women’s fieldhouse (the “Building”),
located at 108D Library Drive; and

The Board of Regents’ Policy 9.8.2.3 DEMOLITION authorizes the Chancellor or
Vice Chancellor for Facilities to declare buildings, structures and other
improvements no longer advantageously useful to the University System of
Georgia. Said Building is included within this policy; and

It has been determined by Mr. J. Frank Smith, Vice Chancellor for Real Estate and
Facilities of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, that said
Building can no longer be advantageously used by Georgia Southern University or
the University System of Georgia and should be removed.

Now, THEREFORE, BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE
OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia is hereby authorized
to demolish and remove the Building from the Armstrong campus of Georgia
Southern University, Chatham County, Savannah, Georgia.

ITIS FURTHER
That the appropriate staff of Georgia Southern University, acting under the
approval of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, is hereby

authorized to demolish the Building and to have all building materials removed
from the premises by public works contract; all said demolition and removal to be

performed as expeditiously as possible.
