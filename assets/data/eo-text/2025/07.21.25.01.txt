There currently exist at Georgia Southern University, Chatham County, Savannah,
Georgia, two maintenance storage buildings known as MSC and MSC1 (the
“Structures”) located on the Armstrong campus; and

The Board of Regents’ Policy 9.8.2.3 DEMOLITION authorizes the Chancellor or
Vice Chancellor for Facilities to declare buildings, structures and other
improvements no longer advantageously useful to the University System of
Georgia. Said Structures are included within this policy; and

It has been determined by Ms. Sandra Lynn Neuse, Vice Chancellor for Real Estate
and Facilities of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, that said
Structures can no longer be advantageously used by Georgia Southern University
or the University System of Georgia and should be removed.

NOW, THEREFORE, BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE
OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia is hereby authorized
to demolish and remove the Structures from Georgia Southern University,
Chatham County, Savannah, Georgia.

IT IS FURTHER
That the appropriate staff of Georgia Southern University, acting under the
approval of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, is hereby

authorized to demolish the Structures and to have all Structure materials removed
from the premises; all said demolition and removal to be performed as

expeditiously as possible.
