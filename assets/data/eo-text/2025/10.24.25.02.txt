The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 1.35 acres of real property located in
Land Lots 88 and 89, 15th Land District, DeKalb County (“Property”) as shown on
the drawing attached hereto as Exhibit “A”; and

Said Property is a portion of the 6.22 acres of real property located in Land Lots 88
and 89, 15th Land District, DeKalb County acquired by virtue of a Quitclaim Deed
dated June 16, 1988, being recorded in Deed Book 6204, Pages 390-392 in the office
of the Clerk of Superior Court of DeKalb County, and under the custody of the
Department of Corrections for the benefit of the Georgia Correctional Industries
Administration and on file with the State Properties Commission Real Property
Records as RPR # 007588 and attached hereto as Exhibit “B” ; and

By Official Action dated September 4, 2025, the Department of Corrections
requested that custody of the Property be transferred to the Georgia Bureau of
Investigations; and

By Official Action dated August 14, 2025, the Georgia Bureau of Investigation
requested custody of the Property for the construction of a new entrance to the
Georgia Bureau of Investigation’s State Medical Examiners Annex Building.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR
OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That custody of the above-defined Property located in DeKalb County, Georgia is
hereby transferred from the Department of Corrections to the Georgia Bureau of

Investigations.
