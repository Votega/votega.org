THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN COBB AND DEKALB COUNTIES

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Master Firefighter Preston Fant, 53, with the DeKalb County Fire Rescue
Department was killed in the line of duty on Monday, September 8, 2025;
and

Mr. Fant served the DeKalb County Fire Rescue Department for 21 years at
Fire Station #24; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Master Firefighter Fant’s service to
DeKalb County and the State of Georgia by lowering the flags on the State
Capitol building and in Cobb and DeKalb Counties to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF MASTER FIREFIGHTER PRESTON FANT, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be

flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Cobb and DeKalb
Counties on Thursday, September 18, 2025, the day of Master Firefighter

Fant’s interment, until sunset.
6 fh

GOVERNOR

This 17 day of September 2025.
