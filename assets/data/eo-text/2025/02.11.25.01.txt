EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That the Honorable Mason B. Rountree is hereby appointed
Judge of the State Court of Paulding County to fill the seat
created by the enactment of House Bill 1466 (Act Number 517)
during the 2024 session of the Georgia General Assembly, for
a term of office ending December 31, 2026.

THIS 117 DAY OF FEBRUARY 2025.

Zi.

GOVERNOR
