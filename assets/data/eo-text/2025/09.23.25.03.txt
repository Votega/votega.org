EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED:

_ There currently exist at Valdosta State University, Lowndes County,

Valdosta, Georgia, two buildings located at 219 West Moore Street
and 223 West Moore Street (collectively, the “Buildings”); and

The Board of Regents, at its meeting on August 12, 2025, declared
that said Buildings could no longer be advantageously used by
Valdosta State University or the University System of Georgia and
should be removed.

NOW, THEREFORE, BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF
THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia is
hereby authorized to demolish and remove the Buildings from
Valdosta State University, Lowndes County, Valdosta, Georgia.

IT IS FURTHER

That the appropriate staff of Valdosta State University, acting under
the approval of the Board of Regents of the University System of
Georgia, is hereby authorized to demolish the Buildings and to have
all building materials removed from the premises; all said demolition
and removal to be performed as expeditiously as possible.
