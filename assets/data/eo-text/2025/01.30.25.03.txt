THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Courtney Underwood Dove of
Cobb County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Eleventh Congressional District Representative on the
State Board of Education, for a term of office expiring
January 1, 2032, to succeed the Honorable Scott
Sweeney, whose term expired January 1, 2025.

This 30th day of January 2025

Bh

GOVERNOR
