THE STATE OF GEORGIA
                             EXECUTIVE ORDER


BY THE GOVERNOR:




     WHEREAS:   On March 7, 2025, Executive Order 03.07.25.02 was issued suspending
                Mrs. Stephanie Lindsey, Newton County District 3 Commissioner, from
                office; and

     WHEREAS:   Pursuant to Code Section 45-5-6(d)(1), there being no general or local
                law, ordinance, or resolution governing the appointment of a
                replacement officer, the Governor is authorized to appoint, for the
                duration of the suspension, a replacement for the Newton County
                District 3 Commissioner.

                NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6(D)(1), AND
                THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
                GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

     ORDERED:   That the Honorable Bill Andre Cooper of Newton County, Georgia, is
                hereby appointed to fill the temporary vacancy in the office of Newton
                County District 3 Commissioner.

                This 7th day of March 2025.




                                                 __________________________
                                                          GOVERNOR
