There currently exist at the University of Georgia’s Dairy Cattle Research Farm,
Clarke County, Winterville, Georgia, four (4) silo structures identified as Buildings
#3127, 3128, 3129, and 3130 (collectively, the “Buildings”), located at 1001 Walter
Sams Road; and

The Board of Regents’ Policy 9.8.2.3 DEMOLITION authorizes the Chancellor or
Vice Chancellor for Facilities to declare buildings, structures and other
improvements no longer advantageously useful to the University System of
Georgia. Said Buildings are included within this policy; and

It has been determined by Mr. J. Frank Smith, Vice Chancellor for Real Estate and
Facilities of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, that said
Buildings can no longer be advantageously used by the University of Georgia or the
University System of Georgia and should be removed.

NOW, THEREFORE, BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE
OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia is hereby authorized
to demolish and remove the Buildings from the University of Georgia, Clarke
County, Winterville, Georgia.

IT IS FURTHER

That the appropriate staff of the University of Georgia, acting under the approval
of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, is hereby authorized
to demolish the Buildings and to have all building materials removed from the
premises by public works contract; all said demolition and removal to be

performed as expeditiously as possible.
