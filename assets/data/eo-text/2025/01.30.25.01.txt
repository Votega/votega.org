THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

ORDERED: That the Honorable Russell Clyde Fleming of Floyd

County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a member
of the Georgia Commission for the Deaf and Hard of
Hearing, for a term of office expiring January 1, 2028,

expired December 28, 2024.
