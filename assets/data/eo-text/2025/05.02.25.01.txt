ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Deputy Brandon Ray Sikes, 31, with the Columbia County Sheriff's Office,
died in the line of duty on April 26, 2025; and

With a heartfelt desire to make his community a safer place, Deputy Sikes
served with the Columbia County Sheriffs Office for more than six years;
and

Prior to his service with the Columbia County Sheriff's Office, Deputy Sikes
honorably served in the United States Army as a combat medic; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Deputy Sikes’ service to the citizens of
Columbia County by lowering the flags on the State Capitol building and in
Columbia County to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF DEPUTY BRANDON RAY SIKES, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be

flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Columbia County on
Monday, May 5, 2025, the day of Deputy Sikes’ interment, until sunset.
