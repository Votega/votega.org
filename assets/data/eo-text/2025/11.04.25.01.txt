THE STATE OF GEORGIA
                                    EXECUTIVE ORDER


BY THE GOVERNOR:


                        FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
                            ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF


   WHEREAS: Richard “Dick” Bruce Cheney, 46th Vice President of the United States of America,
            passed away on November 3, 2025; and

   WHEREAS: Vice President Cheney began his political career in 1969 and devoted his life to
            public service; and

   WHEREAS: In addition to serving as Vice President from 2001-2009, Vice President Cheney
            also served as the U.S. Secretary of Defense from 1989-1993, a member of the U.S.
            House of Representatives from 1979-1989, White House Chief of Staff from 1975-
            1977, and White House Deputy Chief of Staff from 1974-1975; and

   WHEREAS: It is fitting and proper to remember and honor Vice President Cheney’s service to
            the United States of America by lowering flags throughout Georgia to half-staff.

               NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
               MEMORY OF THE HONORABLE RICHARD “DICK” BRUCE CHENEY, PURSUANT TO
               THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
               HEREBY

   ORDERED:    That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia shall be flown
               at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds from this date until sunset on the
               day of his interment.

               This 4th day of November 2025.




                                                          _______________________
                                                                   GOVERNOR
