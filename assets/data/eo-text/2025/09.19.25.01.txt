EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

Senate District 35 in the Georgia State Senate has become vacant due to the
resignation of Senator Jason F. Esteves.

Therefore, pursuant to GA. Const. Art. V, § I, VIII and Code Section 21-
2-544, a writ of election is hereby issued to the Secretary of State for a
special election to be held on Tuesday, November 18, 2025, to fill the
District 35 seat in the Georgia State Senate.

Bill

GOVERNOR

This 19 day of September 2025.
