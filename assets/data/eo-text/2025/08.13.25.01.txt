House Bill 819 (“the local act”) enacted in 2001 by the General Assembly provides
for the establishment and governance of the Haralson County Board of Elections
and Registration (“Board”); and

The local act further provides that if any appointing authority fails to certify an
appointment to the Haralson County Board of Elections and Registration within
30 days after the beginning of a term of office, the Governor is authorized to fill
such vacancy by appointment; and

The appointing authorities failed to certify such appointments to the Haralson
County Board of Elections and Registration within 30 days after the beginning of
the terms of office; and

On July 30, 2025, the Governor received a letter from Brian L. Walker, Chairman
of the Haralson County Board of Commissioners, stating that such vacancies exist
and requesting gubernatorial appointment as members to the Haralson County
Board of Elections and Registration of Ronald V. Gable, Aletha Smith, and Mark
Stich, for terms of office expiring December 31, 2028, and Scotty Long and Carolyn
Bentley, for terms of office expiring December 31, 2026, under the authority of the
local act; and

It is necessary to fill said vacancies for the remainder of the unexpired terms to
ensure the continued lawful operation of the Haralson County Board of Elections
and Registration.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO HOUSE BILL 819 ENACTED IN 2001, AND
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT
IS HEREBY

That Ronald V. Gable, Aletha Smith, Mark Stich, Scotty Long, and Carolyn Bentley
are hereby appointed as members of the Haralson County Board of Elections and
Registration, for the remainder of the unexpired terms created by the above-

mentioned vacancies.
