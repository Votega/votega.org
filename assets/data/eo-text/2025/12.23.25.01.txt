The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 102.53 acres of improved real
property (the “Property”), by virtue of a Warranty Deed dated January 9, 1956,
from Empire Land Company being recorded in Deed Book 140, Pages 389-390, in
the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Clayton County; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Agriculture (the “Department”)
and was acquired as part of the Atlanta State Farmers Market; and

The Property is improved with various structures identified as Sheds 33 through
40, former recycling center, former incinerator house, and metal-framed buildings
(collectively, the “Structures”), and as shown in that aerial photograph attached
hereto as Exhibit A, and incorporated herein by reference; and

Said Structures are identified by the Department to be demolished due to an
upcoming underground utility construction project; and

The Commissioner of Agriculture, on November 6, 2025, recommended the
demolition of the Structures and requested the State Properties Commission seek an
Executive Order from the Governor to allow for the razing, demolition, and disposal
of the Structures; and

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Agriculture is hereby authorized and directed to provide for the
razing, demolition and disposal of said Structures under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interests of the State of State of Georgia, in
accordance with applicable laws, policies, and procedures, including any
requirements under Code Section 12-3-55 regarding historic properties that may
pertain to the Structures.
