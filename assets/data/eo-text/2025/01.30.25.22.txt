THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Ester Fleming, Jr. of Newton
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Thirteenth Congressional District Representative on
the Board of Corrections, for a term of office expiring
April 19, 2025, to succeed the Honorable Barry H.
Babb who is now the an At-Large Representative on
the Board of Corrections, as a result of the Georgia
House of Representative Redistricting Act of 2023,
adopted December 8, 2023.

This 30 day of January 2025
