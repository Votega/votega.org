THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

- On September 19, 2025, I appointed a committee consisting of

Attorney General Christopher Carr, Sheriff Lenn Wood of Coweta
County, and Sheriff Ezell Brown of Newton County to investigate
Sheriff Kenneth Henderson of Towns County, after the Georgia
Sheriffs’ Association requested such appointment due to allegations
of misconduct by Sheriff Henderson, by issuing Executive Order
09.19.25.02; and

On October 17, 2025, the committee delivered its recommendation
to me that Sheriff Henderson be suspended pursuant to Code Section
15-16-26(c).

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 15-16-26 AND
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Sheriff Henderson is hereby suspended from the Office of
Sheriff of Towns County for a period of sixty (60) days, effective

GOVERNOR

This 21st day of October 2025.
