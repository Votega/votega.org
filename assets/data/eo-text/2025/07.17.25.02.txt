ORDERED:

On May 28, 2025, Mr. Kelvin M. Owens, Mayor of Camilla, Georgia, was indicted
on one count of Interference with Elections and two counts of Conspiracy to
Commit Election Fraud; and

A certified copy of the indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to
Code Section 45-5-6 (b) and received on June 2, 2025; and

Executive Order 06.17.25.01 was issued on June 17, 2025, appointing members to
the review commission to determine whether the indictment relates to and
adversely affects the administration of the Office of the Mayor of Camilla such that
the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected; and

Executive Order 07.01.25.02 was issued on July 1, 2025, extending the date by
which the review commission was to submit its report and recommendation; and

On July 15, 2025, the review commission found that the indictment of Mr. Owens
does relate to and adversely affect the administration of the Office of the Mayor of
Camilla such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected
thereby, and recommended that Mr. Owens be suspended from office pursuant to
the provisions of Code Section 45-5-6.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mr. Kelvin M. Owens is hereby suspended from office immediately and
without further action pending the final disposition of the case or until the
expiration of his term of office, whichever occurs first, as provided by Code Section
