EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

House District 23 in the Georgia House of Representatives has become
vacant due to the passing of Representative Mandi Ballinger.

Therefore, pursuant to GA. ConsT. Art. V, § II, { VIII and Code Section 21-
2-544, a writ of election is hereby issued to the Secretary of State for a
special election to be held on Tuesday, December 9, 2025, to fill the District
23 seat in the Georgia House of Representatives.

Bil

GOVERNOR

This 224 day of October 2025.
