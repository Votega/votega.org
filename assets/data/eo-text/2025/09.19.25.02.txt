ORDERED:

By letter dated September 19, 2025, the Georgia Sheriffs’ Association has
requested the appointment of a committee under Code Section 15-16-26 to
investigate alleged misconduct by Mr. Kenneth Henderson, Sheriff of
Towns County; and

Per its request, the Georgia Sheriffs’ Association has been informed that
Sheriff Henderson is not performing the duties required of his office due
such misconduct; and

Pursuant to Code Section 15-16-26(a), upon allegations of misconduct in
office against a sheriff, the Governor has the authority to appoint an
investigative committee consisting of the Attorney General and two sheriffs
who are members of the Georgia Sheriffs’ Association.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 15-16-26, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Sheriff Lenn Wood of Coweta

County, and Sheriff Ezell Brown of Newton County are hereby appointed as
the committee to investigate the alleged misconduct of Sheriff Henderson.

IT IS FURTHER

That the committee shall make a report of its investigation to me within

thirty (30) days of the date of this Order.
