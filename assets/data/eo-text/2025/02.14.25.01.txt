FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALE-STAFF

Staff Sergeant Ryan Austin O’Hara, U.S. Army, of Lilburn, Georgia, honorably
served our country in the United States Army as a UH-60 Blackhawk Crew Chief
and Non-Rated Flight Instructor; and

Staff Sergeant O’Hara paid the ultimate sacrifice during a routine training mission
while performing crew duties when his UH-60 Blackhawk crew crashed, and there
were tragically no survivors; and

Staff Sergeant O’Hara enlisted in the Army in 2013 as a UH-60 Helicopter Repairer
(15T), and had previously deployed to Europe in support of Operation Atlantic
Resolve and to Afghanistan in support of Operation Resolute Support; and

Staff Sergeant O’Hara’s awards and decorations include the Senior Aviation Badge,
Aircraft Crewmember Standardization Instructor, Meritorious Service Medal,
Army Commendation Medal with “C” Device for combat, NATO Medal, and
Afghanistan Campaign Medal; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize the sacrifices made by Staff Sergeant O’Hara
by lowering the flags on all State buildings and grounds to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE MEMORY OF
STAFF SERGEANT RYAN AUSTIN O’HARA, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED
IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be flown at

half-staff on all state buildings and grounds on Monday, February 17, 2025, in
honor and memory of Staff Sergeant Ryan Austin O’Hara.
