ORDERED:

On May 28, 2025, Mr. Kelvin M. Owens, Mayor of Camilla, Georgia, was indicted
on one count of Interference with Elections and two counts of Conspiracy to
Commit Election Fraud; and

A certified copy of the indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to
Code Section 45-5-6 (b) and received on June 2, 2025; and

Executive Order 06.17.25.01 was issued on June 17, 2025, appointing members to
the review commission to determine whether the indictment relates to and

adversely affects the administration of duties by Mayor Owens such that the rights
and interests of the public are adversely affected; and

Code Section 45-5-6(c) provides that additional time may be granted by the
Governor for a review commission to make its written report; and

On June 26, 2025, the above-mentioned review commission requested a fourteen-
day extension to make its written report.

Now, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the period for the above-mentioned review commission to make its written

report is extended to July 15, 2025.
