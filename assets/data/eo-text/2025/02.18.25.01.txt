ORDERED:

On November 14, 2024, Mrs. Audrey Gibbons, member of the Glynn County Board
of Education, was indicted by the United States Attorney for the District of
Delaware on one count of Conspiracy to Commit Money Laundering; and

A certified copy of the indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to
Code Section 45-5-6(b) and received on January 14, 2025; and

Executive Order 01.29.25.02 was issued on January 29, 2025, appointing
members to the Review Commission to determine whether the indictment relates
to and adversely affects the administration of the Glynn County Board of Education
such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected; and

Executive Order 02.12.25.01 was issued on February 12, 2025, extending the date
for the Review Commission to submit its report and recommendation; and

On February 14, 2025, the Review Commission found that the indictment of Mrs.
Gibbons does relate to and does adversely affect the administration of the Glynn
County Board of Education such that the rights and interests of the public are
adversely affected; and recommended that Mrs. Gibbons be suspended from office
pursuant to the provisions of Code Section 45-5-6.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mrs. Audrey Gibbons is hereby suspended from office immediately and
without further action pending the final disposition of the case or until expiration
of her term of office, whichever occurs first, as provided in Code Section 45-5-6(c).
