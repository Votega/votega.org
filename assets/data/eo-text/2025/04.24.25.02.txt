ORDERED:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 191.03 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) in Chattooga County, Georgia filed with the
State Properties Commission Real Property Record as Property BLLIP ID# 75050;
and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Corrections and is commonly
known as Hays State Prison; and

The Property is improved with structures identified as the “Annex Building”
(BLLIP #7317) and the “Library Building” (BLLIP #7213) (hereinafter, the
“Structures”), as shown in the aerial photograph attached hereto as Exhibit A; and

The Department of Corrections is desirous of demolishing the Structures for the
construction of new buildings; and

By official action dated February 6, 2025, the Department of Corrections Board
requested authority to demolish the Structures.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Corrections is authorized and directed to provide for the razing,
demolition, and disposal of said Structures under the terms and conditions which are
deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia, in accordance with applicable
laws, policies, and procedures, including any requirements under O.C.G.A. § 12-3-55
regarding historic properties that may pertain to the Structures.

This 24 day of April 2025.
