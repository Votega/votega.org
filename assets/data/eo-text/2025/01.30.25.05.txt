THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED: That the Honorable Thomas Christie “Chris” Cannon
of Dougherty County, Georgia, is appointed to serve
as the Second Congressional District Representative
on the Board of Regents of the University System of
Georgia, for a term of office expiring January 1, 2032,
to succeed the Honorable Barbara Holmes, whose
term expired January 1, 2025.

This 30 day of January 2025
