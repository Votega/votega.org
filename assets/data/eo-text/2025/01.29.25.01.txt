EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On January 23, 2025, I issued Executive Order 01.23.25.03 appointing Mr.
Rodney McCord of the Griffin City Commission, among others, to the
review commission to determine whether the indictment of Mr. Royce
Reeves, Vice Chairman of the Cordele City Commission, relates to and
adversely affects the administration of duties by Mr. Reeves such that the
rights and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby, and to make
a written report to me within fourteen days with a recommendation as to
whether Mr. Reeves should be suspended from office as required by Code
Section 45-5-6(c);

On January 27, 2025, I received a letter from Mr. McCord requesting that
he be recused from serving on the above-mentioned review commission;
and

I accepted Mr. McCord’s request that he be recused from the above-
mentioned review commission.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Lisa Clarke Hill of the Moultrie City Commission is hereby appointed
to the above-mentioned review commission.

pil

‘GOVERNOR

This 29th day of January 2025.
