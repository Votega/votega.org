The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 170.037 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) in Decatur County, Georgia filed with the
State Properties Commission Real Property Record as Property BLLIP ID# 12155;
and

Said Property is in the custody of the Technical College System of Georgia and is
commonly known as the Bainbridge Campus of Southern Regional Technical College;
and

The Property is improved with structures identified as the “Maple Center” (BLLIP
#33226) (hereinafter, the “Structure”), as shown in the aerial photograph attached
hereto as Exhibit A; and

The Technical College System of Georgia is desirous of demolishing the Structure due
to roof damage from Hurricane Michael; and

By official action dated December 5, 2024, the Technical College System of Georgia
Board requested authority to demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT, TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Technical College System of Georgia is authorized and directed to provide for the
razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia, in accordance with
applicable laws, policies, and procedures, including any requirements under O.C.G.A
12-3-55 regarding historic properties that may pertain to the Structure.
