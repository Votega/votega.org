THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That the Honorable Kevin W. Drummond is hereby appointed
Judge of the State Court of Carroll County to fill the seat
created by the enactment of House Bill 160 (Act Number 197)
during the 2025 session of the Georgia General Assembly, for
a term of office ending December 31, 2026.

THIS 177 DAY OF SEPTEMBER 2025.
