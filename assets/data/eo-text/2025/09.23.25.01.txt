ATTEST:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 3.0 acres of improved real property
(hereinafter the “Property”) in Floyd County, Georgia filed with the State Properties
Commission Real Property Record as Property BLLIP ID# 04331; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Public Safety and, by
intergovernmental agreement, is leased to the Department of Driver Services; and

The Property is improved with structures identified as the “Department of Driver
Services Rome Customer Service Center” (BLLIP #42335) (hereinafter, the
“Structure”), as shown in the aerial photograph outlined in yellow attached hereto
as Exhibit A; and

The Department of Public Safety is desirous of demolishing the Structure in order to
allow for the construction of improvements which will serve the new customer service
center that is being constructed on the Property; and

By official action dated August 14, 2025, the Department of Public Safety Board
requested authority to demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Public Safety is authorized and directed to provide for the razing,
demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions which are
deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia, in accordance with applicable
laws, policies, and procedures, including any requirements under O.C.G.A. § 12-3-55
regarding historic properties that may pertain to the Structure.
