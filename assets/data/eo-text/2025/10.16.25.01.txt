FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

The State of Georgia mourns the loss of State Representative Mandi
Ballinger, who passed away on October 12, 2025; and

Representative Ballinger was first elected to the Georgia House of
Representatives in November 2012, where she honorably served the
citizens of the 23" District and held the role of chairwoman of the
House Judiciary Juvenile Committee; and

Representative Ballinger was a longtime public servant, valued
friend, and beloved wife, mother, and grandmother.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF REPRESENTATIVE MANDI BALLINGER, PURSUANT
TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia

shall be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds
throughout Georgia on Friday, October 17, 2025, the day of

GOVERNOR

This 16th day of October 2025.
