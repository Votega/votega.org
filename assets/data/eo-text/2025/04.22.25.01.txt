EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF STAFF

The people of the State of Georgia mourn the passing of His
Holiness Pope Francis; and

Pope Francis’ heart for all of God’s creation encourages each of us
to live out the Great Commandment to love our neighbor; and

Our citizens share the grief of millions of Catholics and people of
faith around the world for whom the Pope served as a spiritual
leader and source of inspiration.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF POPE FRANCIS, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of
Georgia be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds
from this date until sunset on Saturday, April 26, 2025, the day of
Pope Francis’ interment.

This 224 day of April 2025.

8h)

GOVERNOR
