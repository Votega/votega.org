THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF AND AUTHORIZING
SPEAKER DAVID RALSTON TO LIE IN STATE IN THE GEORGIA STATE CAPITOL

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

On Wednesday, November 16, 2022, David Ralston, the second
longest serving Speaker of the Georgia House of Representatives in
state history and the longest currently serving state house speaker in
the nation, passed away; and

As a native of Gilmer County, he was proud to serve his community
as the first state house speaker from North Georgia in more than 150
years; and

Speaker David Ralston devoted his life to serving the people of
Georgia, always putting the interests of the state first through his
years in both the Georgia State Senate and Georgia House of
Representatives; and

As speaker, David Ralston championed measures that promoted
economic opportunity for all Georgians, as well as bipartisan
legislation such as adoption reform and, during his final session
leading the House, the groundbreaking Mental Health Parity Act;
and

Throughout his many years as a statewide leader, Speaker Ralston
remained committed to his hometown of Blue Ridge and his family,
including his wife, Sheree, and two children, Elizabeth and Matt; and

As we mourn his death, we will also honor the monumental legacy he
leaves by striving to always put service above self and bring honor
and decency into our public lives.

ORDERED:

ORDERED:

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF SPEAKER DAVID RALSTON, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States of America and the flag of the State
of Georgia shall be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and
grounds from the date of this order through sunset on the day of his
interment.

IT IS FURTHER

That Speaker David Ralston shall lie in state in the Rotunda of the

Georgia State Capitol.
