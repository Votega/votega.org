There currently exists at Georgia State University, Fulton County, Atlanta,
Georgia, a structure known as Parking Deck Y #0106 (the “Structure”),
located at 60 Peachtree Center Avenue, NE; and

The Board of Regents’ Policy 9.8.2.3 DEMOLITION authorizes the
Chancellor or Vice Chancellor for Facilities to declare buildings, structures,
and other improvements no longer advantageously useful to the University
System of Georgia. Said Structure is included within this policy; and

It has been determined by Ms. Sandra Lynn Neuse, Vice Chancellor for
Facilities of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, that
said Structure can no longer be advantageously used by Georgia State
University or the University System of Georgia and should be removed.

NOW, THEREFORE, BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia is hereby
authorized to demolish and remove the Structure from Georgia State
University, Fulton County, Atlanta, Georgia.

IT 1S FURTHER

That the appropriate staff of Georgia State University, acting under the
approval of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, are
hereby authorized to demolish the Structure and to have all Structure
materials removed from the premises by public works contract; all said
demolition g"° removal to be performed as expeditiously as possible.
