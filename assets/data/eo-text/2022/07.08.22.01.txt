ORDERED:

APPOINTING AN INVESTIGATIVE COMMITTEE FOR
THE OFFICE OF PULASKI COUNTY SHERIFF

By letter dated June 29, 2022, the Georgia Sheriffs’ Association has
requested the appointment of a committee under Code Section
15-16-26 to investigate Pulaski County Sheriff Danny Brannen; and

Per its request, the Georgia Sheriffs’ Association has been informed
that Sheriff Brannen is not performing all the duties required by his
office due to incapacity; and

The Executive Committee of the Georgia Sheriffs’ Association
believes an investigation is warranted after inquiring into Sheriff
Brannen’s capacity to perform the functions of his office; and

Pursuant to the Georgia Sheriffs’ Association Executive Committee’s
request, as authorized by Code Section 15-16-26(a), I have
determined that an investigation of Sheriff Brannen should be made
due to the alleged incapacity of Sheriff Brannen to perform the
functions of his office; and

Code Section 15-16-26(a) provides that two sheriffs who are
members of the Georgia Sheriffs’ Association shall be appointed to
conduct the investigation along with the Attorney General.

NOw, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 15-16-26 AND
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS THE GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Sheriff Ezell Brown of
Newton County, and Sheriff Clay Whittle of Columbia County are
hereby appointed as the committee to investigate the alleged
incapacity of Sheriff Brannen to perform the functions of the Pulaski
County Sheriff's Office.

IT IS FURTHER

ORDERED: That the committee shall make a report of its investigation to me
within thirty (30) days of the date of this Order.
