FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED TO HALF STAFF

The Honorable Madeleine Korbel Albright passed away on March 23,
2022; and

Mrs. Albright served as the 64 U.S. Secretary of State from 1997 to
2001. She was the first female Secretary of State in U.S. history; and

In addition to her service as Secretary of State, she also served on the
National Security Council under President Jimmy Carter, and as an
Ambassador to the United Nations; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Secretary Albright’s service to
citizens of the United States of America by lowering the flags
throughout Georgia to half-staff.

NOw, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF THE HONORABLE MADELEINE KORBEL
ALBRIGHT, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia

shall be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds from this
date until sunset on Sunday, March 27, 2022.

Bh

"GOVERNOR

This 24TH day of March 2022.
