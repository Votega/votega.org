FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

During the most recent legislative session, the Georgia General Assembly
posthumously recognized the life and memory of the following citizens of the
great State of Georgia; and

The State mourns the loss of these men and women, of all ages and races, and
respectfully extends condolences to the families, friends, and communities
affected by the passing of these individuals; and

It is appropriate that the State honor these men and women who are
remembered by those who knew and loved them; and

The State honors the life and memory of Dorothy Mae Lawyer Johnson, who
passed on April 23, 2012; and

The State honors the life and memory of Deborah Bailey Marshall, who
passed on July 26, 2013; and

The State honors the life and memory of Lieutenant General Robert G.
“Sam” Wetzel, who passed on January 20, 2020; and

The State honors the life and memory of Matthew “Matt” Oliver, who passed on
March 31, 2020; and

The State honors the life and memory of Reverend Jimmy Rogers, who
passed on March 9, 2021; and

The State honors the life and memory of Rudolph Bullard, who passed on
May 9, 2021; and

The State honors the life and memory of Miller Peterson “Pete” Robinson, who
passed on July 1, 2021; and

ORDERED:

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND RESPECT FOR THESE INDIVIDUALS
RECOGNIZED BY THE GEORGIA GENERAL ASSEMBLY, AND PURSUANT TO
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA,
IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States of America and the flag of the great State
of Georgia be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds on
Wednesday, August 31, 2022, until sunset.

This 30th day of August 2022.
