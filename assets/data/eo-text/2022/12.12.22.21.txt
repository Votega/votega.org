THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Francis “Fran” R. Millar of
DeKalb County, Georgia, is appointed to serve
as the Fourth Congressional District
Representative on the State Board of the
Technical College System of Georgia, for a
term of office expiring June 30, 2025, to
succeed the Honorable Baoky Vu, who no
longer represents the Fourth Congressional
District on the State Board of the Technical
College System of Georgia, as a result of

reapportionment, adopted December 30,
2021.

This 12 day of December 2022

BL

GOVERNOR
