THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Tim Williams of Douglas
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as an
At-Large member on the State Board of the
Technical College System of Georgia, for a
term of office expiring June 30, 2027, to
succeed the Honorable Robert W. “Buzz” Law,
who is now the Seventh Congressional District
Representative on the State Board of the
Technical College System of Georgia.

This 122 day of December 2022

Beh

GOVERNOR
