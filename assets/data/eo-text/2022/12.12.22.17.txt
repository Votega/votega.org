THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Penny Penn of Forsyth
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Sixth Congressional District Representative on
the Board of Juvenile Justice, for a term of
office expiring July 6, 2026, to succeed the
Honorable Gary A. Yandura, who no longer
represents the Sixth Congressional District on
the Board of Juvenile Justice, as a result of
reapportionment, adopted

December 30, 2021.
