THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED: That the Honorable Emanuel “Manny”
Stephen Fialkow of Fulton County, Georgia,
is appointed to serve as a member of the
Board of Directors of the Georgia Regional
Transportation Authority, for a term of
office expiring June 1, 2027, to succeed the
Honorable Bernie Tokarz, whose term
expired June 1, 2019.

This 1st day of July 2022

Bil

GOVERNOR
