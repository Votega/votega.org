EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Dennis Chastain of
Gwinnett County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as the Ninth Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Economic
Development, for a term of office expiring
August 13, 2024, to succeed the Honorable
Stephen Syfan, who shall no longer serve as
the Ninth Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Economic
Development in accordance with

O.C.G.A. § 21-1-3.

This 12th day of December 2022

Bil

GOVERNOR
