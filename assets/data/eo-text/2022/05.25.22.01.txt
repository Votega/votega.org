THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

FLAGS ON STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF STAFF

On Tuesday, May 24, 2022, in Uvalde, Texas, an incomprehensible act of
violence was committed when a depraved gunman entered Robb
Elementary School and opened fire; and

Nineteen students and two teachers were killed, and others sustained
serious injuries; and

The people of Georgia share the grief of the parents, families, and friends of
those who were injured or killed, and we pray for their comfort and healing
in the days to come; and

The President of the United States has ordered that the flag of the United
States be flown at half-staff on federal government buildings and grounds.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THOSE
AFFECTED BY THIS TRAGEDY, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME
AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That in honor of the victims and in conjunction with the Proclamation of
the President of the United States of America, the flag of the United States
and the flag of the State of Georgia shall be flown at half-staff on all state
buildings and grounds from this date until sunset on Saturday, May 28,

Bille

~ GOVERNOR

This 25! day of May 2022.
