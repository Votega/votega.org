THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED: That the Honorable Richard T. Evans Sr. of
Fulton County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as the Sixth Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Regents of
the University System of Georgia, for a term
of office expiring January 1, 2029, to
succeed the Honorable Kessel D. Stelling,
whose term expired January 1, 2022.

This 9th day of January 2022

Bil
