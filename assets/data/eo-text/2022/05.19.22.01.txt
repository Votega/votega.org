The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 3.572 acres of real property
(“Property”) as shown on the survey attached hereto as Exhibit “A”, which Property
is a portion of the Southwestern State Hospital; and

Said Property is a portion of approximately 207.852 acres of improved State property
lying and being in Land Lots 5, 6, 41, and 42, 13'* Land District, Thomas County,
Georgia, acquired by virtue of a Quitclaim Deed recorded in Deed Book 27, Pages
625-645, and Release of Conditions Subsequent, recorded and Deed Book 1954,
pages 362-367, in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Thomas County, and
filed with the State Properties Commission Real Property Records as RPR# 03432,
and RPR # 003432.01 respectively; and

By Resolution dated August 18, 2016, the Georgia Department of Behavioral Health
and Developmental Disabilities declared the Property surplus to the needs of the
Department; and

Said Property is adjacent to 1.92 acres of property under the custody of the Georgia
Bureau of Investigation and used as the Investigative Division Regional Office; and

By Resolution dated April 14, 2022, the Board of Public Safety requested the
transfer of custody of the Property to the Georgia Bureau of Investigation for the
purposes of expanding the current Investigative Division Regional Office.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That custody of the above-defined Property located in Thomas County, Georgia is

hereby transferred from the Georgia Department of Behavioral Health and
Developmental Disabilities to the Georgia Bureau of Investigation.
