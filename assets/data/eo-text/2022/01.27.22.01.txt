THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF STAFF

Lance Corporal Jonathan Gierke, 19, of Lawrenceville, Georgia, honorably
served our country in the United States Marine Corps; and

Lance Corporal Gierke and another Marine were tragically killed when the
military vehicle they were traveling in overturned near their base at Camp
Lejeune, North Carolina; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize the sacrifices made by Lance Corporal

Jonathan Gierke by lowering the flags on all State buildings and grounds to
half-staff.

Now, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF LANCE CORPORAL JONATHAN GIERKE, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be
flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds on Saturday, February
5, 2022, the day of Lance Corporal Gierke’s interment.
