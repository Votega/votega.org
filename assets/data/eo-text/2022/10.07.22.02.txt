FLAGS ON STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

The 41st Annual National Fallen Firefighters Memorial Service will be held Sunday,
October 9, 2022, to honor firefighters who have died in the line of duty; and

The United States Congress, through joint resolution 107 P.L. 51, 115 Stat. 267,
designated the annual National Fallen Firefighters Memorial Service as a day to
honor firefighters and emergency services personnel who have sacrificed their lives
to save others by lowering the American flag on all federal buildings to half-staff;
and

The events of September 11, 2001, brought national attention to the duties,
responsibilities, hazards, and sacrifices faced by fire and emergency services
personnel on a daily basis; and

Firefighters and emergency services personnel play an essential role in the
protection of lives and property in our local communities; and

The National Fallen Firefighters Memorial Service marks the beginning of the
annual Fire Prevention Week observance.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the state of Georgia be flown at

half-staff on all state buildings and grounds on Sunday, October 9, 2022, in
observance of the National Fallen Firefighters Memorial Service and the beginning
