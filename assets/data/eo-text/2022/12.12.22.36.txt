THE STATE OF GEORGIA

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Rossie Charles James Ross of
Stewart County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Second Congressional District Representative on the
Board of Corrections, for a term of office expiring
August 6, 2025, to succeed the Honorable Alton
Russell, who is now an At-Large representative on the
Board of Corrections, as a result of reapportionment,
adopted December 30, 2021.
