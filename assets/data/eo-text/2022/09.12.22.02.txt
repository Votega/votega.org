ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Deputy Marshall Samuel Ervin, Jr., 38, with the Cobb County
Sheriff's Office, was killed in the line of duty on Thursday, September
8, 2022, while attempting to serve a warrant; and

Deputy Ervin honorably served with the Cobb County Sheriff's Office
for seven years; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Deputy Marshall Samuel Ervin,
Jr.’s service to Cobb County and the State of Georgia by lowering the
flags on the State Capitol building and in Cobb County to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF DEPUTY MARSHALL SAMUEL ERVIN, JR.,
PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia

be flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Cobb
County on Thursday, September 15, 2022, the day of Deputy Ervin’s

GOVERNOR
