ORDERED:

FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF STAFF

Her Majesty Queen Elizabeth II passed away on Thursday, September 8, 2022; and

Having reigned for more than 70 years, Queen Elizabeth II was Great Britain’s
longest reigning monarch; and

In her ascension to the throne, Queen Elizabeth II demonstrated maturity and wit
beyond her years to meet the challenges of her time, and in the decades that
followed, Her Majesty provided a steady hand to her nation and its people through
both times of crises and prosperity; and

During this solemn time, the people of Georgia stand with the people of the United
Kingdom and join them in celebrating the life of Queen Elizabeth II; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize the life and reign of Her Majesty Queen
Elizabeth II by lowering the flags throughout Georgia to half-staff.

Now, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE MEMORY
OF HER MAJESTY QUEEN ELIZABETH II, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia shall be flown
at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds on Monday, September 19, 2022,

the day of Her Majesty’s interment, until sunset.
