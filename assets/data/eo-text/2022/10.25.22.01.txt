ORDERED LOWERED TO HALE-STAFF

Mr. Charles Louis “Charley” Trippi, 100, a legendary former University of
Georgia football player, passed away on October 19, 2022; and

Mr. Trippi is admired as one of the greatest football players in the University
of Georgia’s storied history; and

Mr. Trippi earned a scholarship and played football for the University of
Georgia from 1942 to 1946 and helped take his Bulldogs to the Rose Bowl,
winning the 1942 National Championship; and

Mr. Trippi took leave from his collegiate career to serve in the United States
Army Air Corps during World War II where he played for the Third Air
Force Gremlins football team; and

In 1947, the University of Georgia retired Mr. Trippi’s jersey number, #62,
one of only four UGA football players to receive this honor; and

Mr. Trippi played football professionally with the National Football League
and was inducted into the national College Football Hall of Fame, the
national Pro Football Hall of Fame, the Rose Bowl Hall of Fame, and the
Georgia Sports Hall of Fame; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Charley Trippi’s life by lowering the flags
on the State Capitol Building, in Athens-Clarke County, and on all buildings
and campuses of the University of Georgia to half-staff.

ORDERED:

Now, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF MR. CHARLES LOUIS “CHARLEY” TRIPPI, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be
flown at half-staff on the State Capitol Building, in Athens-Clarke County,
and on all buildings and campuses of the University of Georgia on

This ZOt= day of October 2022.
