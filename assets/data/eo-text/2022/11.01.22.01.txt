FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Mr. Vincent Joseph Dooley, the iconic former University of Georgia football
coach and athletic director, passed away on Friday, October 28, 2022; and

With 201 wins during his tenure between the hedges, including six
Southeastern Conference Championships and the 1980 National
Championship, Coach Dooley is the winningest football coach in the history
of the University of Georgia; and

Coach Dooley’s distinguished mark on collegiate football has been
recognized by his enshrinement in the Georgia Sports Hall of Fame in 1978,
the Alabama Sports Hall of Fame in 1984, and the College Football Hall of
Fame in 1994; and

In 2010, Coach Dooley received the Paul “Bear” Bryant Lifetime
Achievement Award, cementing his place among the all-time coaching
greats and recognizing his excellence in coaching on and off the field; and

Under Coach Dooley’s leadership as athletic director, University of Georgia
athletic programs achieved 23 national championships, 78 conference
championships, and had numerous finishes in the top 10 for best overall
athletic departments in the nation, including a second-place finish in 1998-
1999; and

Coach Dooley’s dedication to public service extended far beyond the football
field and the University of Georgia, as he served as the state chairman for
the Georgia Easter Seals Society for over 30 years, including being named
National Volunteer of the Year in 1987, and contributed selfless charitable
work for organizations such as The Heart Fund and Boy Scouts of America,
and issues including multiple sclerosis, juvenile diabetes, and
homelessness; and

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

In 2011, Coach Dooley was named a Georgia Trustee, the highest honor
given in the State of Georgia, in recognition of his historic accomplishments
and community service; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Coach Dooley’s life by lowering the flags
throughout Georgia to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF MR. VINCENT JOSEPH DOOLEY, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be
flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds on November 5, 2022.
