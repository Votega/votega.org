THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Neil L. Pruitt, Jr., of
Fulton County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as an At-Large Representative on the
Board of Regents of the University System
of Georgia, for a term of office expiring
January 1, 2027, to succeed the Honorable
Joe Cade Joiner, who is now the Fourth
Congressional District Representative on
the Board of Regents of the University
System of Georgia.

This 27th day of January 2022
