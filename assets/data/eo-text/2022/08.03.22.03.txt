THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN SPALDING COUNTY

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Deputy Jamie Reynolds with the Spalding County Sheriffs Office,
was killed in the line of duty on Sunday, July 31, 2022, when a tree
fell and crushed the passenger side of his vehicle; and

Deputy Reynolds began his career in law enforcement in 1995 and
joined the Spalding County Sheriffs Office in 2021; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Deputy Reynolds’ service to
Spalding County and the State of Georgia by lowering the flags on the
State Capitol building and in Spalding County to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF DEPUTY JAMIE REYNOLDS, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia

be flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Spalding
County on Saturday, August 6, 2022, the day of Deputy Reynolds’

interment, until sunset.
