EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Chris Clark of Forsyth
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Sixth Congressional District Representative on
the Board of Corrections, for a term of office
expiring July 1, 2026, to succeed the
Honorable Janelle Lashaun King, who is now
the Seventh Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Corrections, as
a result of reapportionment, adopted
December 30, 2021.

This 12th day of December 2022

Bh

GOVERNOR
