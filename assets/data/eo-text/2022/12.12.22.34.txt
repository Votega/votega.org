THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Alton Russell of Muscogee
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as an
At-Large representative on the Board of
Corrections, for a term of office expiring July
1, 2026, to succeed the Honorable William
Daniel “W.D.” Strickland, who is now the First
Congressional District Representative on the
Board of Corrections, as a result of the
resignation of Tommy M. Rouse.

This 12th day of December 2022

Bil he

GOVERNOR
