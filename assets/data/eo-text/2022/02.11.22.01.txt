EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

House District 45 in the Georgia House of Representatives has become
vacant due to the resignation of Representative Matt Dollar.

Therefore, pursuant to GA. Const. Art. V, § II, { VIII and Code Section 21-
2-544, a writ of election is hereby issued to the Secretary of State for a
special election to be held on Tuesday, April 5, 2022, to fill the District 45
seat in the Georgia House of Representatives.

This 11TH day of February, 2022.

Be

GOVERNOR
