EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Timothy Perryman of
Forsyth County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as the Sixth Congressional District
Representative on the State Board of the
Technical College System of Georgia, for a
term of office expiring June 30, 2026, to
succeed the Honorable Francis “Fran”
Millar who is now the Fourth
Congressional District Representative on
the State Board of the Technical College
System of Georgia.

This 12th day of December 2022

Bh

GOVERNOR
