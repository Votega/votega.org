EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Caitlin Maureen Flatley
of Fulton County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as a member of the Georgia Board of
Examiners of Licensed Dietitians, for a term
of office expiring June 30, 2025, to succeed
the Honorable Susan Chapman, whose term
expired June 30, 2021.

This 10th day of February 2022

Bf.

GOVERNOR
