EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On April 8, 2022, Thomas Richard Hawk, Sr., Evelyn Hawk,
and Luke Hawk, were killed during a robbery at their gun
range business in Grantville, Coweta County, Georgia; and

The Grantville Police Department, with the assistance of the
Georgia Bureau of Investigation and other agencies, are
actively investigating the crime; and

Section 45-12-35 of the Official Code of Georgia Annotated
provides that the Governor of the State of Georgia is
authorized, in his discretion, to offer and cause to be paid a
reward for information leading to the _ identification,
apprehension, and conviction of a person(s) who has
committed a felony.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED
IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That a reward in the amount of $10,000.00 is hereby offered
for information leading to the identification, arrest, and
conviction of the individual(s) responsible for the murders of
the Hawk family.
