ORDERED:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 2.957 acres of real property
(“Property”) lying and being in Land Lot 176, 1oth Land District, Telfair County,
Georgia as shown on the survey attached hereto as Exhibit “A”, which Property is
commonly known as Georgia State Patrol Post 16 (“GSP Post 16 — Helena); and

Said Property was acquired by virtue of two Deeds recorded in Deed Book 3-R,
Pages 216-217, and Deed Book 4-R, Pages 455-456, in the office of the Clerk of
Superior Court of Telfair County, and filed with the State Properties Commission
Real Property Records as RPR# 04620 and RPR # 04621, respectively; and

By Resolution dated October 28, 2021, the Georgia Department of Public Safety
declared the Property surplus, less and except the chain linked fenced Radio Tower
area, to the needs of the Department; and

By Resolution dated December 16, 2021, the Georgia Board of Community
Supervision requested the transfer of custody of the Property to the Department of
Community Supervision.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That custody of the above-defined Property, less and except the chain linked fenced

Radio Tower, located in Telfair County, Georgia is hereby transferred from the
Department of Public Safety to the Department of Community Supervision.
