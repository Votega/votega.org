BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Sgt. Jean-Harold Louis Astree, 54, with the City of Fairburn Police
Department, passed away on Thursday, July 28, 2022, after the
vehicle he was driving was involved in a four-vehicle wreck; and

Sgt. Astree began his law enforcement career with the Atlanta Police
Department, serving from 2005-2008, and began working with the
Fairburn Police Department in 2018; and

Sgt. Astree was also a veteran of the U.S. Air Force; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Sgt. Astree’s service to the City of
Fairburn, the City of Atlanta, and the State of Georgia by lowering
the flags on the State Capitol building and in Fulton County to half-
staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF SGT. JEAN-HAROLD ASTREE, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia

be flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Fulton
County on Thursday, August 4, 2022, the day of Sgt. Astree’s

interment, until sunset.
