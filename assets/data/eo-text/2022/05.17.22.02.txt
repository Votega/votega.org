EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN ROCKDALE COUNTY

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALE-STAFF

Deputy Walter Jenkins, Jr., 54, with the Rockdale County Sheriff's
Office, was killed in the line of duty on Wednesday, May 11, 2022,
while directing traffic; and

Deputy Jenkins began his career in law enforcement in 1995 as a
corrections officer at Phillips State Prison, he served with the College
Park Police Department from 1999-2021 and joined the Rockdale
County Sheriff's Office in 2021; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Deputy Jenkins’ service to
Rockdale County and the State of Georgia by lowering the flags on
the State Capitol building and in Rockdale County to half-staff.

NOw, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF DEPUTY WALTER D. JENKINS, JR., PURSUANT TO
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia

be flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Rockdale
County on Thursday, May 19, 2022, the day of Deputy Jenkins’
