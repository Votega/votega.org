THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED: That the Honorable Richard J. Valladares of
Fulton County, Georgia, is appointed to serve
as the Sixth Congressional District
Representative on the State Board of
Education, for a term of office expiring
January 1, 2025, to succeed the Honorable
Scott Sweeney, who is now the Eleventh
Congressional District Representative on the
State Board of Education, as a result of
reappointment, adopted December 30, 2021.

This 12th day of December 2022

The

GOVERNOR
