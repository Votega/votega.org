EXECUTIVE SECRETARY

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 307.41 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) located at 1500 GA-197, in Land Lots 83, 84,
and 85, 11th Land District, Habersham County, Georgia, acquired by virtue of a
deed dated December 7, 1943, recorded in Deed A-42, pages 463-467, in the office
of the Clerk of Superior Court of Habersham County, and filed with the State
Properties Commission Real Property Record as RPR# 02804.01; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Technical College System of Georgia and is
commonly known as the North Georgia Technical College; and

The Property is improved with a structure known as Building L Gymnasium
(BLLIP ID # 8041), totaling 27,440 square feet as shown in the aerial photograph
attached hereto as Exhibit A (hereinafter the “Structure”); and

Said Structure needs to be demolished for the construction of the new Dr. Mark
Ivester Center for Living and Learning (TCSG-377); and

By Resolution dated October 25, 2022, the State Board of the Technical College
System of Georgia requested authority to demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Technical College System of Georgia is authorized and directed to provide for the

razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
