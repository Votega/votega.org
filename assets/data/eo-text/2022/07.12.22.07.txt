EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED: That the Honorable Jeffrey Daniel Schultz of
Coweta County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as a member of the Georgia Board of
Dentistry, to fill a seat created by the
enactment of House Bill 1219 (Act 840)
during the 2022 session of the Georgia
General Assembly, for a term of office
expiring July 1, 2027.
