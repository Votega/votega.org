THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

DESIGNATING THE TECHNICAL COLLEGE SYSTEM OF GEORGIA AS
ADMINISTRATOR OF THE STATE EMPLOYMENT SERVICE PROGRAM

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED:

The federal Wagner-Peyser Act, 29 U.S.C. § 49, as amended,
established a nationwide system of public employment offices known
as the Employment Service. The State Employment Service seeks to
improve the functioning of the State’s labor markets by bringing
together individuals seeking employment with employers seeking
workers; and

Code Section 34-8-77(a) vests the Governor with the authority to
designate an agency to administer the State Employment Service
program on behalf of the State of Georgia.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
THE GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Technical College System of Georgia is hereby appointed as
the Governor’s designee, as defined in Code Section 34-8-52, and
shall administer the State Employment Service program accordingly.

IT IS FURTHER

That this Order shall be effective upon signature.

Bh

GOVERNOR

This 1st day of July 2022.
