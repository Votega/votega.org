FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED TO HALF STAFF

Mrs. Sandra Dunagan Deal, former First Lady of Georgia, passed away on Tuesday,
August 23, 2022; and

As First Lady of Georgia from 2011 to 2019, and alongside Georgia’s 82 Governor
Nathan Deal, she promoted community service, education, and literacy initiatives;
and

During her time as First Lady, and as a passionate advocate for literacy and student
achievement, she read to students in all of Georgia’s 159 counties and 181 school
districts, including 800 schools and Pre-K programs; and

For generations to come, the Sandra Dunagan Deal Center for Early Language and
Literacy, located on the campus of her alma mater, Georgia College & State
University, will continue her good work in bringing the gift of reading to Georgia’s
children; and

During her time in the Governor’s Mansion, while improving its beauty and
preserving its memories for futures generations, Mrs. Deal co-authored a book,
“Memories of the Mansion,” which covered the rich history of the Governor's
Mansion and experiences of Georgia’s first families; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Mrs. Sandra Deal’s life and service to citizens of
the State of Georgia by lowering the flags throughout Georgia to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE MEMORY
OF MRS. SANDRA DUNAGAN DEAL, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN
ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be flown at
half-staff on all State buildings and grounds on Saturday, August 27, 2022, the day

of Mrs. Deal’s interment.
