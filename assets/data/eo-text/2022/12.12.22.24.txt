THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Charles Kim DePriest of
Douglas County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as the Thirteenth Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Natural
Resources, for a term of office expiring
December 12, 2029, to succeed the Honorable
Billy New, Jr., whose term expired

March 16, 2022.

This 12th day of December 2022
