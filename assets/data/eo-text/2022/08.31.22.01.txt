THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED: That the Honorable Tommy Dean Black,
Jr. of Chatham County, Georgia, is
appointed to serve as a member of the
Georgia Composite Board of Professional
Counselors, Social Workers, and Marriage
and Family Therapists, for a term of office
expiring August 31, 2025, to succeed the
Honorable Bonita Barker, whose term
expired April 17, 2022.

This 31st day of August 2022

Bil

GOVERNOR
