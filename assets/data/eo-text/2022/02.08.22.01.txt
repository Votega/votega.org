EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Douglas Ray Aldridge,
Jr. of Fulton County, Georgia, is appointed
to serve as the Thirteenth Congressional
District Representative on the Board of
Regents of the University System of Georgia,
for a term of office expiring January 1,
2028, to succeed the Honorable Sachin
Shailendra, who no longer represents the
Thirteenth Congressional District on the
Board of Regents of the University System
of Georgia as a result of reapportionment,
adopted December 30, 2021, thereby
creating a vacancy in the Thirteenth
Congressional District.

This 8th day of February 2022

pith

GOVERNOR
