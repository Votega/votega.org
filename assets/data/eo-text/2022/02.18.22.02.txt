WHEREAS:

Mr. Royce Reeves, Ward 2 Commissioner on the Cordele City
Commission, was indicted on August 2, 2021, on two counts of
Obstruction of an Officer, one count of Violation of Oath by a Public
Officer, one count of Criminal Trespass, and one count of Disorderly
Conduct; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to
O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(b), and the certified copy was received on August
11, 2021; and

On September 7, 2021, I appointed Attorney General Christopher
Carr, Barnesville City Councilmember/Mayor Pro Tem Christopher
Hightower, and McDonough City Councilmember Benjamin Pruett
as the Review Commission to determine whether the indictment
related to and adversely affected the administration of the office of
Commissioner Reeves such that the rights and interests of the public
were adversely affected thereby, and to issue a written report within
fourteen days with a recommendation as to whether said
commissioner should be suspended from office as required by
O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(e): and

On October 21, 2021, pursuant to a request from the Review
Commission, I extended the deadline for the Review Commission’s
report and recommendation to December 14, 2021; and

On January 20, 2022, the Review Commission issued its report
finding that the indictment of Royce Reeves does relate to and
adversely affect the administration of the office of Commissioner,
and that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected
thereby; and recommending that Royce Reeves be suspended from
office pursuant to the provisions of O0.C.G.A. § 45-5-6; and

On January 21, 2022, pursuant to the recommendation of the Review
Commission, I issued Executive Order 01.21.22.01 suspending Mr.
Reeves from his office as Ward 2 Commissioner on the Cordele City
Commission; and

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

Mr. Reeves, through counsel, on February 3, 2022, petitioned for a
review of his suspension as Ward 2 Cordele City Commissioner
under O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(f).

NOw, THEREFORE, BY VIRTUE OF THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(f), Attorney General Christopher
Carr, Barnesville City Councilmember/Mayor Pro Tem Christopher
Hightower, and McDonough City Councilmember Benjamin Pruett
are hereby re-appointed as the Review Commission to review the
suspension of Mr. Royce Reeves as Ward 2 Cordele City
Commissioner and to make a written report within 14 days.
