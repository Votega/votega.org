THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

House District 119 in the Georgia House of Representatives has become
vacant due to the resignation of Representative-elect Danny Rampey.

Therefore, pursuant to Ga. Const. Art. V, § II, { VIII and Code Section 21-
2-544, a writ of election is hereby issued to the Secretary of State for a
special election to be held on Tuesday, January 31, 2023, to fill the District
119 seat in the Georgia House of Representatives.

This 31st day of December 2022.
