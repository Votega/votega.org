ORDERED:

The Georgia Department of Community Affairs has been charged with the
responsibility of administering the affairs of the Georgia Housing and Finance
Authority, including the administration of the federal low-income housing tax
credit program in Georgia; and

The Georgia Department of Community Affairs, acting on behalf of the Georgia
Housing and Finance Authority, published a draft 2023 Georgia Qualified
Allocation Plan on the Department’s website on October 7, 2022, and gave due
notice of the availability of the draft 2023 Georgia Qualified Allocation Plan and of
the public hearings on the Department’s public website, and by sending notices to
industry participants; and

The Georgia Department of Community Affairs held three public hearings
pursuant to due and reasonable notice, one in Dublin, Georgia on October 18,
2022, and two virtual, public hearings on October 20, 2022, and October 31, 2022;
and

Following the hearings and public comment period, the Board of Directors of the
Georgia Housing and Finance Authority adopted the 2023 Georgia Qualified
Allocation Plan on November 9, 2022.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That, solely for the purpose of satisfying the requirements for such approval under
Section 42(m)(1) of the Internal Revenue Code, as amended by the Omnibus
Budget Reconciliation Act of 1989, the 2023 Georgia Qualified AHocation Plan, in
the form approved by the Board of Directors for the Georgia Housing and Finance
Authority on November 9, 2022, is approved.

This 5 day of December 2022.
