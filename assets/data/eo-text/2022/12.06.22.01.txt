FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

On August 23, 1994, the United States Congress, by Joint Resolution, passed Public
Law 103-308 regarding the observance of National Pearl Harbor Remembrance
Day; and

On December 7, 1941, the Imperial Japanese Navy and Air Force attacked units of
the armed forces of the United States stationed at Pearl Harbor, Hawaii; and

More than 2,000 U.S. citizens were killed and more than 1,000 were wounded in
the attack on Pearl Harbor; and

The attack on Pearl Harbor marked the entry of the United States into World War
II; and

Commemoration of the attack on Pearl Harbor should instill a greater
understanding and appreciation of the selfless sacrifice of the individuals who
served in the armed forces of the United States during World War II.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND COMMEMORATION OF THE ATTACK ON PEARL
HARBOR AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE MEMORY OF THOSE WHO
PERISHED, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be flown at

half-staff on all state buildings and grounds on National Pearl Harbor
Remembrance Day, Wednesday, December 7, 2022, until sunset.

GOVERNOR

This 6th day of December 2022.
