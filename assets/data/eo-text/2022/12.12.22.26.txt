THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable David “Penn” Pendleton
Hodge of Fulton County, Georgia, is appointed
to serve as the Sixth Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Natural
Resources, for a term of office expiring
January 1, 2024, to succeed the Honorable
Dwight Davis, who is now the Fourth
Congressional District Representative on the
Board of Natural Resources.

This 12th day of December 2022
