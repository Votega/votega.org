FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED TO HALF STAFF

The Honorable Clyde L. Reese, Judge of the Court of Appeals of the State of
Georgia, passed away on Saturday, December 17, 2022; and

Judge Reese honorably served the citizens of Georgia first as an Assistant Attorney
General in the Georgia Department of Law, and later as counsel for the Georgia
Department of Insurance, the Subsequent Injury Trust Fund, and the State Health
Planning Agency; and

Judge Reese also served as Commissioner of the Department of Community Health
and the Department of Human Services before his 2016 appointment to the Court
of Appeals of the State of Georgia; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Judge Reese’s service to citizens of the State of
Georgia by lowering the flags throughout Georgia to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE MEMORY
OF THE HONORABLE CLYDE L. REESE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN
ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be flown at
half-staff on all State buildings and grounds on Monday, December 19, 2022, and

until sunset on the day of Judge Reese’s interment.
