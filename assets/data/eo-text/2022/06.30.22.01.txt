THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That effective July 1, 2022, the Honorable Angela R. O’Conor
is hereby appointed Judge of the State Court of Paulding
County to fill the seat created by the enactment of House Bill
1119 (Act Number 339) during the 2020 session of the Georgia
General Assembly, for a term of office ending January 1, 2025.

THIS 30TH DAY OF JUNE, 2022.

Bil he

GOVERNOR
