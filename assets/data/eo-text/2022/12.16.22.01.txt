THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN GWINNETT COUNTY

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Gwinnett County Senior Corrections Officer Scott Ozburn Riner
passed away on December 13, 2022, after being shot in the parking
lot of the Gwinnett County Comprehensive Correctional Complex as
he prepared to begin his shift; and

Officer Riner served Gwinnett County as a correctional officer since
June 2010; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Officer Riner’s service to
Gwinnett County and the State of Georgia by lowering the flags on
the State Capitol building and in Gwinnett County to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF OFFICER SCOTT OZBURN RINER, PURSUANT TO
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia

be flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Gwinnett
County on Tuesday, December 20, 2022, the day of Officer Riner’s

interment, until sunset.
