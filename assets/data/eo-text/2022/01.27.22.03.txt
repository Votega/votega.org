THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Joe Cade Joiner of
Dekalb County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as the Fourth Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Regents of
the University System of Georgia, for a term
of office expiring January 1, 2026, to
succeed the Honorable Rachel Blackstone
Little, who no longer represents the Fourth
Congressional District on the Board of
Regents of the University System of Georgia
as a result of reapportionment, adopted
December 30, 2021, thereby creating a
vacancy in the Fourth Congressional
District.

This 27th day of January 2022

Bh

GOVERNOR
