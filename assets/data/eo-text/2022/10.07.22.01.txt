THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN COOK COUNTY

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Captain Terry “Turtle” Randall Arnold, 59, with the Cook County
Sheriff's Office, passed away on October 3, 2022, after responding to
a fight between two students; and

Captain Arnold served with the Cook County Sheriffs Office for 28
years and previously served with the Remerton Police Department
for two years and the Adel Police Department for five years; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Captain Arnold’s service to Cook
County and the State of Georgia by lowering the flags on the State
Capitol building and in Cook County to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF CAPTAIN TERRY RANDALL ARNOLD, PURSUANT
TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia
be flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Cook
County on Monday, October 10, 2022, the day of Captain Arnold’s
interment, until sunset.

This 7'h day of October, 2022.

Bh h-

GOVERNOR
