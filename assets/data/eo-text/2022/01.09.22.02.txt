THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable James Keller Syfan, III
of Hall County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as the Ninth Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Regents of
the University System of Georgia, for a term
of office expiring January 1, 2029, to
succeed the Honorable Philip Wilheit, whose
term expired January 1, 2022.

This 9th day of January 2022

Bil

GOVERNOR
