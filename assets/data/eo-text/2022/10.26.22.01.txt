ORDERED:

On September 28, 2022, Mr. Richard D. Morgan, Sheriff of Miller County,
was indicted on one count of Violation of Oath of Public Officer and one
count of Sexual Battery; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to Code Section
45-5-6(b), and the certified copy was received on September 29, 2022; and

An Executive Order was issued on October 18, 2022, appointing members
to the Review Commission to determine whether the indictment relates to
and adversely affects the administration of the Office of the Miller County
Sheriff such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected;
and

By report dated October 25, 2022, the Review Commission found that the
indictment of Mr. Richard D. Morgan does relate to and does adversely
affect the administration of the Office of the Miller County Sheriff, and that
the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby; and
recommended that Sheriff Morgan be suspended from office pursuant to
the provisions of Code Section 45-5-6.

NOw, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6 AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Mr. Richard D. Morgan is hereby suspended from office immediately

and without further action pending the final disposition of the case or until
the expiration of his term of office, whichever occurs first, as provided in

Code Section 45-5-6(c).
