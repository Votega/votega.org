THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

~ BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That the Honorable Ronald K. Thompson is hereby appointed
Superior Court Judge of the Ogeechee Judicial Circuit to fill
the seat created by the passage of House Bill 786 (Act Number
493) during the 2020 session of the Georgia General
Assembly, for a term of office ending December 31, 2024.

THIS 18TH DAY OF JANUARY, 2022.

! GOVERNOR
