THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:
ORDERED:

That the Honorable Steven Broadbent of
Fulton, Georgia, is appointed to serve as an
At-Large representative on the Board of
Community Affairs, for a term of office
expiring July 1, 2027, to succeed the
Honorable Albert Hodge, who is now the
Fourteenth Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Community
Affairs, as a result of reapportionment,
adopted December 30, 2021.

This 12th day of December 2022

Bil

GOVERNOR
