EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That the Honorable Benjamin A. Land is hereby appointed
Judge of the Court of Appeals of the State of Georgia to fill the
vacancy created by the appointment of the Honorable Andrew
A. Pinson to the Supreme Court of Georgia, for a term of office
ending December 31, 2024.

THIS 207H DAY OF JULY 2022.

BP

‘GOVERNOR
