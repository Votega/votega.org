EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALE-STAFF

The state of Georgia mourns the loss of State Representative Henry
“Wayne” Howard who passed away on October 13, 2022; and

Representative Howard served the citizens of Georgia’s 124th District
(formerly 121st District) in the Georgia House of Representatives
from 2007 — 2022; and

Wayne Howard was a successful businessman, and a public servant
who developed a reputation of service, reliability, and loyalty.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF REPRESENTATIVE HENRY “WAYNE” HOWARD,
PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the state of Georgia
shall be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds
throughout Georgia on Thursday, October 20, 2022, the day of
Representative Howard’s interment.

This 17th day of October 2022.

Bl

GOVERNOR
