FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED TO HALF STAFF

Mrs. Betty Foy Sanders, former First Lady of Georgia, passed away on May 29,
2022; and

As First Lady of Georgia from 1963 to 1967, and alongside Georgia’s 74t” Governor
Carl Edward Sanders, Sr., she was instrumental in the creation of the Georgia
Council for the Arts and spearheaded the committee to design, furnish and
construct the current Governor’s Mansion; and

Asa talented artist in her own right, she was devoted to helping students of the arts
further their own education and talents through the Betty Foy Sanders Department
of Art at Georgia Southern University; and

Mrs. Sanders represented so much of what makes Georgia the greatest state in our
nation to call home. With her sharp wit, class, and famous sense of humor, she was
the epitome of a southern woman; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Mrs. Betty Foy Sanders’ life and service to
citizens of the State of Georgia by lowering the flags throughout Georgia to half-
staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE MEMORY
OF MRS. BETTY FOY SANDERS, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be flown at
half-staff on all State buildings and grounds on Wednesday, June 8, 2022, the day
of Mrs. Sanders’ interment.

This 77 day of June 2022.

GOVERNOR
