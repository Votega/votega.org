EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:
That the Honorable Andrew A. Pinson is hereby appointed
Justice of the Supreme Court of Georgia to fill the vacancy

created by the resignation of the Honorable David E.
Nahmias, for a term of office ending December 31, 2024.

THIS 207TH DAY OF JULY 2022.

BY)

' GOVERNOR
