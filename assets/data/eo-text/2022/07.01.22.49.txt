THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Rachel Blackstone Little
of Gwinnett County, Georgia, is appointed
to serve as a member of the Board of
Governors of the George L. Smith II Georgia
World Congress Center Authority, for a term
of office expiring July 1, 2026, to succeed
the Honorable Jeff Payne, whose term
expired July 1, 2022.

This 1st day of July 2022

Bil

GOVERNOR
