The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 61.163 acres of real property as
shown as Tract “B” on the survey attached hereto as Exhibit “A”, which Property is a
portion of the Southwestern State Hospital; and

An 8 acre portion of the 61.163 acre tract is in the custody of the Georgia
Department of Juvenile Justice, as shown on the survey attached hereto as Exhibit
“B”, and the remaining 53.163 acres (“Property”) is in the custody of the Georgia
Department of Behavioral Health and Developmental Disabilities; and

Said Property is a portion of the 207.852 acres of real property located at 400 S.
Pinetree Blvd, in Land Lots 5 and 6, 13" Land District, Thomas County, Georgia,
acquired by virtue of a Quitclaim Deed dated June 14, 1966, recorded in Deed Book
27, Pages 625-645, in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Thomas County,
and filed with the State Properties Commission Real Property Records as RPR#
03432; and

By Resolution dated August 18, 2016, the Georgia Department of Behavioral Health
and Developmental Disabilities declared the Property surplus to the needs of the
Department; and

By Resolution dated April 13, 2022, the Georgia Department of Juvenile Justice
requested custody of the Property for the purposes of adding an additional utility
duct bank and future expansion for the campus to service the youth in the
Department’s care and custody.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That custody of the above-defined Property located in Thomas County, Georgia is

hereby transferred from the Georgia Department of Behavioral Health and
Developmental Disabilities to the Georgia Department of Juvenile Justice.
