THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Thomas Lane Bradbury
of Cobb County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as the Eleventh Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Regents of
the University System of Georgia, for a term
of office expiring January 1, 2024, to
succeed the Honorable Neil L. Pruitt, Jr.,
who is now an At-Large Representative on
the Board of Regents of the University
System of Georgia.

This 27th day of January 2022

Bl

GOVERNOR
