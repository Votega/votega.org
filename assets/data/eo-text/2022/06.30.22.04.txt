ORDERED:

On March 15, 2022, Sohail N. Butt filed a Petition for Mandamus in case number
2022CV362087 against Judge Kimberley M. Esmond Adams in the Superior Court
of Fulton County; and

On May 12, 2022, the Judge Eric K. Dunaway of the Superior Court of Fulton
County entered an Order dismissing Sohail N. Butt’s Petition for Mandamus; and

On May 23, 2022, Sohail N. Butt filed a Notice of Appeal to the Supreme Court of
Georgia appealing the Order entered by Judge Eric K. Dunaway; and

The Georgia Department of Law has declined representation of the Honorable
Kimberley M. Esmond Adams due to a potential conflict; and

Mr. Dennis T. Cathey of Cathey & Strain, LLC is an attorney duly admitted to the
State Bar of Georgia; and

Mr. Dennis T. Cathey of Cathey & Strain, LLC has agreed to represent the
Honorable Kimberley M. Esmond Adams at a rate mutually agreed upon with the
Office of the Governor.

Now, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO O.C.G.A. § 45-15-70 AND BY THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Mr. Dennis T. Cathey of Cathey & Strain, LLC is hereby appointed as Special

Counsel to assume control and responsibility for the representation of the
Honorable Kimberley M. Esmond Adams in all matters related to the above-

GOVERNOR

This 307 day of June, 2022.
