THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Benton Drew Ellenburg of
Fulton County, Georgia, is appointed to serve
as the Fifth Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Economic
Development, for a term of office expiring July
1, 2024, to succeed the Honorable Cassius F.
Butts, who is now the Eleventh Congressional
District Representative on the Board of
Economic Development, as a result of
reapportionment, adopted December 30,
2021.

This 12th day of December 2022

Bil

GOVERNOR
