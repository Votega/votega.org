ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Deputy Jonathan Randall Koleski, 42, with the Cobb County Sheriff's
Office, was killed in the line of duty on Thursday, September 8, 2022,
while attempting to serve a warrant; and

Following September 11, 2001, Deputy Koleski joined the United
States Army where he served three tours in Iraq and Afghanistan.
Upon returning home, he served with the Georgia National Guard;
and

In addition to his service to the United States Army and Georgia
National Guard, Deputy Koleski honorably served with the Cobb
County Sheriff's Office for 15 years; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Deputy Koleski’s service to Cobb
County, the State of Georgia, and the United States by lowering the
flags on the State Capitol building and in Cobb County to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF DEPUTY JONATHAN RANDALL KOLESKI,
PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia

be flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Cobb
County on Wednesday, September 14, 2022, the day of Deputy

Koleski’s interment, until sunset.
