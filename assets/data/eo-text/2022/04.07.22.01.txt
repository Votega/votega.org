ORDERED:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 1.285 acres of real property
(“Property”) as shown as Tract “D” on the survey attached hereto as Exhibit “A”,
which Property is a portion of the Southwestern State Hospital; and

Said Property is a portion of the 207.852 acres of real property located at 430 S.
Pinetree Blvd, in Land Lots 5, 6, 41, and 42, 13'" Land District, Thomas County,
Georgia, acquired by virtue of Quitclaim Deeds dated June 14, 1966, and August 8,
1973, recorded in Deed Book 27, Pages 625-645, and Deed Book 75, Pages 599-611,
in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Thomas County, and filed with the
State Properties Commission Real Property Records as RPR # 03432, RPR #
004927, and RPR # 003432.01 respectively; and

By Resolution dated August 18, 2016, the Georgia Department of Behavioral Health
and Developmental Disabilities declared the Property surplus to the needs of the
Department; and

By Resolution dated March 17, 2021, the Georgia Department of Community
Supervision requested custody of the Property for the purposes of operating a
Community Supervision Field Office and a Day Reporting Center.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That custody of the above-defined Property located in Thomas County, Georgia is

hereby transferred from the Georgia Department of Behavioral Health and
Developmental Disabilities to the Georgia Department of Community Supervision.
