The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 193.439 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) located at 3481 Mike Padgett Highway, in
123rd District, Richmond County, Georgia, acquired by virtue of a deed dated
February 12, 1931, recorded in Deed Book 12-B, pages 586-588 in the office of the
Clerk of Superior Court of Richmond County, and filed with the State Properties
Commission Real Property Record as RPR# 002026; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Georgia Department of Juvenile Justice and is
commonly known as the Augusta Youth Development Campus; and

The Property is improved with a structure formerly known as Building 16,
Visitation Building-Dining Hall (BLIPP ID # 1906), totaling 9,535 square feet as
shown in the aerial photograph attached hereto as Exhibit A (hereinafter the
“Structure”); and

Said Structure needs to be demolished for the construction of a new education
building and a 56-bed housing unit; and

By Resolution dated April 13, 2022, the Georgia Board of Juvenile Justice requested
authority to demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Georgia Department of Juvenile Justice is authorized and directed to provide for

the razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
