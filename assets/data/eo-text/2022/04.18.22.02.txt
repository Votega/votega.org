EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Sidney “Sid” Albert
Johnson, III of Forsyth County, Georgia, is
appointed to serve as a member of the
Georgia Access to Medical Cannabis
Commission, for a term of office expiring
November 22, 2023, to succeed the
Honorable Jason Michael Hockenberry, who
resigned.

IT IS FURTHER

That Sidney “Sid” Albert Johnson, II shall
serve as Chairman of the Georgia Access to
Medical Cannabis Commission.

This 18th day of April 2022

Bill

GOVERNOR
