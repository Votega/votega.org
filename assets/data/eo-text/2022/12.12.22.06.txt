THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Nicholas Francis Masino
of Gwinnett County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as the Seventh Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Economic
Development, for a term of office expiring
December 12, 2027, to succeed the Honorable
Ronald Garrard, who is now the Sixth
Congressional District Representative on the
Board of Economic Development, as a result
of reapportionment, adopted

December 30, 2021.
