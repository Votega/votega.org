ATTEST:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 3.64 acres of improved real property
(hereinafter the “Property”) located at 150 Lakemont Drive, in Land Lot 135, 7th
Land District, Henry County, Georgia, acquired by virtue of a deed dated March
10, 2021, recorded in Deed Book 17931, page 292-294, in the office of the Clerk of
Superior Court of Henry County, and filed with the State Properties Commission
Real Property Record as RPR# 12522; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Technical College System of Georgia and is
commonly known as the Center for Education and Entrepreneurship of Southern
Crescent Technical College; and

The Property is improved with a structure formerly known as Lakemont Chattels
(BLLIP ID # 119573), totaling 25,500 square feet as shown in the aerial photograph
attached hereto as Exhibit A (hereinafter the “Structure”); and

Said Structure needs to be demolished to prevent vandalism and to eliminate the
building maintenance cost; and

By Resolution dated August 4, 2022, the Technical College System of Georgia
requested authority to demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Technical College System of Georgia is authorized and directed to provide for the

razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are i to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
