THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Ronald Garrard of Forsyth
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Sixth Congressional District Representative on
the Board of Economic Development, for a
term of office expiring August 13, 2024, to
succeed the Honorable David Belle Isle, who
shall no longer serve as the Sixth
Congressional District Representative on the
Board of Economic Development in
accordance with O.C.G.A. § 21-1-3.
