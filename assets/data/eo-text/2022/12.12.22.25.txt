THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Daniel Pedro Garcia of
Cobb County, Georgia, is appointed to serve
as the Eleventh Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Natural
Resources, for a term of office expiring
January 1, 2024, to succeed the Honorable
Robert D. Leebern, Jr., who no longer
represents the Eleventh Congressional District
on the Board of Natural Resources as a result
of reapportionment, adopted December 30,
2021, thereby creating a vacancy in the
Eleventh Congressional District.

This 12th day of December 2022
