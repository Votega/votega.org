The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 35.01 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) located at 265 Bicentennial Trail, in Land Lot
121, 8th District, and 4t* Section of Walker County, Georgia, acquired by virtue of a
Warranty Deed dated January 23, 2006, recorded in Deed Book 1386, Pages 644-
647, in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Walker County, and filed with
the State Properties Commission, Real Property Record as RPR# 11388; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Technical College System of Georgia and is
commonly known as Georgia Northwestern Technical College, Swainsboro Campus;
and

The Property is improved with structures known as the Baker Property Residential
and Guest House, (BLLIP ID # 40026) and (BLLIP ID # 40027) respectively,
totaling 3,864 square feet as shown in the aerial photograph attached hereto as
Exhibit A (hereinafter the “Structures”); and

Said Structures need to be demolished to prevent vandalism, and eliminate the
building maintenance cost; and

By Resolution dated August 4, 2022, the Technical College System of Georgia
requested authority to demolish the Structures.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Technical College System of Georgia is authorized and directed to provide for the

razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structures under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
