EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On September 28, 2022, Mr. Richard D. Morgan, Sheriff of Miller
County, was indicted on one count of Violation of Oath by Public
Officer and one count of Sexual Battery; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to Code
Section 45-5-6(b), and the certified copy was received on September
29, 2022; and

The fourteen-day period following receipt of the indictment as
prescribed by Code Section 45-5-6(e) has passed.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Sheriff Rod Howell of
Colquitt County, and Sheriff Ashley Paulk of Lowndes County are
hereby appointed as the review commission to determine whether
the above-mention indictment relates to and adversely affects the
administration of the Office of the Miller County Sheriff and that the
rights and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby, and
to make a written report to me within fourteen days with a
recommendation as to whether Sheriff Morgan should be suspended
from office as required by Code Section 45-5-6(c).

This 18th day of October 2022.
