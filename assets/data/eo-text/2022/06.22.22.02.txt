WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

The Honorable Joseph “Sonny” Vickers of Valdosta, Georgia passed
away on Thursday, June 16, 2022; and

Councilman Vickers represented District 3 on the Valdosta City Council
for more than three decades; and

In addition to his extraordinary service and dedication to the Valdosta
City Council, Councilman Vickers was also the first African American to
serve as Mayor of Valdosta; and

It is fitting and proper to remember and honor Councilman Vickers for
his dedicated service to the City of Valdosta and the State of Georgia by
lowering the flags on the State Capitol Building and in Lowndes County
to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF THE HONORABLE JOSEPH “SONNY” VICKERS, PURSUANT
TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be

flown at half-staff on the State Capitol Building and in Lowndes County
on Sunday, June 26, 2022, the day of Councilman Vickers’ interment.
