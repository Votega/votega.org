EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED: That the Honorable Rhonda Barnes Wilson
of Newton County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as a member of the Board of Directors
of the State Employees’ Assurance
Department, for a term of office ending at
the pleasure of the Governor, to succeed the
Honorable Lonice Barrett, who resigned.

This 17t day of February 2022
