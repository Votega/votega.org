EXECUTIVE ORDER

“5

BY THE GOVERNOR:

INCREASING THE GEORGIANS FOR REFUGE,

ACTION, COMPASSION & EDUCATION COMMISSION MEMBERSHIP

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

Pursuant to Executive Order 02.26.19.01, the Georgians for Refuge, Action,
Compassion & Education (GRACE) Commission was created to leverage the
wealth of knowledge, community resources, and unwavering commitment
of an alliance of individuals and entities from the private and public sectors
to find solutions to the challenge of human trafficking and seek to eliminate
human trafficking in our state; and

Since its creation, the GRACE Commission has taken and continues to take
swift and comprehensive action to combat the persistent — and growing —
threat to freedom for countless victims in every region of our state; and

Given this growing threat to freedom for these victims, growth of the
GRACE Commission is also necessary in order to effectively continue to take
action to combat this threat.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the GRACE Commission membership shall be increased by one (1)
member from twenty-two (22) members to twenty-three (23) members to

be appointed by the Governor based on the recommendations of First Lady
Marty Kemp.

This | at day of February 2022.
