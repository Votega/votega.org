THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED: That the Honorable Janelle Lashaun King
of Fulton County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as the Seventh Congressional
District Representative on the Board of
Corrections, for a term of office expiring
July 1, 2026, to succeed the Honorable
Chris Clark, who is now the Sixth
Congressional District Representative on
the Board of Corrections, as a result of

reapportionment, adopted December 30,
2021.

This 12th day of December 2022

Bei

GOVERNOR
