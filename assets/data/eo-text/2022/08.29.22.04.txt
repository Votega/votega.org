ORDERED:

On August 11, 2022, I appointed a committee consisting of Attorney
General Christopher Carr, Sheriff Rod Howell of Colquitt County,
and Sheriff Ashley Paulk of Lowndes County to investigate Sheriff
Richard Morgan of Miller County after he was arrested by the
Georgia Bureau of Investigation and charged with one count of
Sexual Battery and one count of Violation of Oath by Public Officer,
by issuing Executive Order 08.11.22.03; and

On August 26, 2022, the committee delivered its recommendation to
me that, based on the committee’s investigation and findings, Sheriff
Morgan be suspended pursuant to Code Section 15-16-26(c).

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 15-16-26 AND
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Sheriff Morgan is hereby suspended from the Office of Sheriff
of Miller County for a period of sixty (60) days, effective immediately.

IT IS FURTHER

That pursuant to Code Section 15-16-26(d), the Chief Judge of the
Pataula Judicial Circuit shall appoint a person who meets the
qualifications for sheriffs pursuant to Code Section 15-16-1 to assume
the duties and responsibilities of the Office of Sheriff of Miller County
during the period of Sheriff Morgan’s suspension.
