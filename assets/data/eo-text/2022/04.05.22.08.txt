EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Lisa Cooley Davies of
Gwinnett County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as a member of the Georgia Board of
Examiners of Licensed Dietitians, for a term
of office expiring July 1, 2026, to succeed
the Honorable Rashelle Centor Berry, who
is now the Private Practice Representative
on the Georgia Board of Examiners of
Licensed Dietitians.

This 5 day of April 2022

ate

GOVERNOR
