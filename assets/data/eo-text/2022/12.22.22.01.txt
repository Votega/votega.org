EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

House District 172 in the Georgia House of Representatives has become
vacant due to the resignation of Representative Sam Watson.

Therefore, pursuant to GA. Const. Art. V, § II, § VIII and Code Section 21-
2-544, a writ of election is hereby issued to the Secretary of State for a
special election to be held on Tuesday, January 31, 2023, to fill the District
172 seat in the Georgia House of Representatives.

This 2274 day of December 2022.

Bill

‘GOVERNOR
