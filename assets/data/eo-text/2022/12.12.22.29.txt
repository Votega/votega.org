THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Scott Sweeney of Cobb
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as
the Eleventh Congressional District
Representative on the State Board of
Education, for a term of office expiring
January 1, 2025, to succeed the
Honorable Scott Johnson, who shall no
longer serve as the Eleventh Congressional
District Representative on the State Board

of Education in accordance with
O.C.G.A. § 21-1-3.

This 12t day of December 2022

Bh,

GOVERNOR
