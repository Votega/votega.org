ORDERED:

On August 2, 2021, Mr. Royce Reeves, Ward 2 Commissioner on the Cordele City
Commission, was indicted on two counts of Obstruction of an Officer, one count of
Violation of Oath by a Public Officer, one count of Criminal Trespass, and one
count of Disorderly Conduct; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to 0.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(b),
and the certified copy was received on August 11, 2021; and

An Executive Order was issued on September 7, 2021, appointing members to the
Review Commission to determine whether the indictment relates to and adversely
affects the administration of the office of City Commissioner such that the rights
and interests of the public are adversely affected; and

The Review Commission found that the indictment of Mr. Royce Reeves does relate
to and does adversely affect the administration of duties by Commissioner Reeves,
and that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby; and
recommended that Mr. Reeves be suspended from office pursuant to the
provisions of O0.C.G.A. § 45-5-6.

Now, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mr. Royce Reeves is hereby suspended from office immediately and without

further action pending the final disposition of the case or until the expiration of his
term of office, whichever occurs first, as provided in 45-5-6(c).
