THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable C. Scott Johnson of Cobb
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Eleventh Congressional District
Representative on the Georgia Student
Finance Commission Board of Commissioners,
for a term of office expiring June 1, 2025, to
succeed the Honorable David Perez, who now
serves as the Fifth Congressional District
Representative on the Georgia Student
Finance Commission Board of Commissioners,
as a result of reapportionment, adopted
December 30, 2021.

This 12 day of December 2022

Bille

GOVERNOR
