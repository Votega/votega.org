EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

ORDERED:

For the limited purpose of the Georgia Department of Transportation
assisting Bryan County with the removal of storm debris, the State of
Emergency declared by Executive Order 04.06.22.01 is hereby reinstated
for Bryan County for a period of seven (7) days, expiring at 11:59 P.M. on
Monday, April 25, 2022.

ITIS FURTHER

That the relevant terms of Executive Order 04.06.22.01 are incorporated
herein by reference.

This 18 day of April 2022.
