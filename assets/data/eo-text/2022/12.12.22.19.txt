THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Joseph Poan Hsiao of
Cobb County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as the Thirteenth Congressional
District Representative on the State Board
of the Technical College System of
Georgia, for a term of office expiring June
30, 2023, to succeed the Honorable Tim
Williams who is now an At-Large
representative on the State Board of the
Technical College System of Georgia.

This 12th day of December 2022

Bill
