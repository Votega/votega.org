THE STATE OF GEORGIA

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Garland Hunt of Fulton
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Seventh Congressional District Representative
on the Board of Juvenile Justice, for a term of

Honorable Penny Penn, who is now the Sixth
Congressional District Representative on the
Board of Juvenile Justice, as a result of
reapportionment, adopted

December 30, 2021.
