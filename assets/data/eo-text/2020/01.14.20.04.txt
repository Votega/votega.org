ORDERED:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 2 acres of improved real property
(hereinafter the “Property”) located at 564 Old Newnan Road, Carrollton, Georgia
30117, located in Land Lot 217 of the 5'* Section, Carroll County, Georgia acquired
by virtue of a General Warranty Deed dated April 6, 1977, recorded in Deed Book
358, page 113 in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Carroll County, and
filed with the State Properties Commission Real Property Records as RPR# 06344;
and

Said Property is in the custody of the Georgia Forestry Commission; and

The Property is improved with a crew/storage building (BLLIP ID # 36083) as
shown in the aerial photograph attached hereto as Exhibit “A” (“Structure”); and

Said Structure has been identified by the Georgia Forestry Commission as a liability
to the agency due to cracks in the foundation and drainage issues; and

By Resolution dated March 19, 2019, the Georgia Forestry Commission requested
authorization to demolish Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Georgia Forestry Commission is authorized and directed to provide for the

razing, demolition and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
