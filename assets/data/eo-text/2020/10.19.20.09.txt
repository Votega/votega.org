The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 3.63 acres of improved real property
located at 8463 Burton’s Ferry Highway, in the 260 G.M. District, City of Sylvania,
Screven County, Georgia (hereinafter the “Property”), acquired by virtue of a Deed
dated June 1, 1960, from A.H. Rowan and recorded in Deed Book 115, page 272, in
the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Screven County, and filed with the State
Properties Commission, Real Property Record as RPR# 05919; and

The Property is improved with a building being 2,800 square feet, commonly
known as the Georgia Welcome Center, BLIPP ID 7300; and

By Resolution dated August 26, 2020, the Board of Economic Development
requested that the Property be transferred to the Georgia Department of Natural
Resources pursuant to Resolution Act 382 (S.B. 474) of the 2020 General
Assembly; and

By Resolution dated September 22, 2020, the Board of Natural Resources
requested that custody of the Property be transferred to the Department of Natural
Resources to manage and maintain pursuant to Resolution Act 382 (S.B. 474) of
the 2020 General Assembly.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR
OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That custody of the above-defined Property located Screven County, Georgia is
hereby transferred from the Georgia Department of Economic Development to the

Georgia Department of Natural Resources.
