The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 1.483 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) located at 2970 Highway 190, in Land Lot 19,
end District, City of Pine Mountain, Harris County, commonly known as Franklin
D. Roosevelt State Park, acquired by virtue of a General Warranty Deed, dated
February 25, 2000, recorded in Deed Book 451, pages 04-09, in the office of the
Clerk of Superior Court of Harris County, and filed with the State Properties
Commission, Real Property Record as RPR# 009514; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Georgia Department of Natural Resources; and
The Property is improved with a building known as Cottage 22 (BLLIP ID # 24173)
as shown in the aerial photograph attached hereto as Exhibit A (hereinafter the

“Structure”); and

Said Structure has been identified by the Georgia Department of Natural Resources
as unsafe for guests and beyond reasonable repair due to foundation problems; and

By Resolution dated August 25, 2020, the Georgia Department of Natural Resources
requested the Structure to be demolished.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY ©

The Georgia Department of Natural Resources is authorized and directed to provide

for the razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and
conditions which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
