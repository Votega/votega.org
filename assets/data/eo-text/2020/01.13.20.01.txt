EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable James Alton Epps, Jr. of Twiggs County,
Georgia, is appointed as a member of the State Board of
Registration for Professional Geologists, for a term of office
ending January 13, 2025, to succeed the Honorable Steven
Henderson, whose term expired June 30, 2015.

The Honorable James Alton Epps, Jr.
Twiggs County, Georgia

This 13 day of January, 2020.
