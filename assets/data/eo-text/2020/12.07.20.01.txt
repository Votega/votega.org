EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

Spalding County District 5 Commissioner Donald F. Hawbaker was
indicted on October 29, 2020, on one count of Aggravated Assault, Family
Violence, one count of Possession of Firearm During Commission of a
Felony, five counts of Aggravated Assault on a Peace Officer, and one count
of Possession of Firearm During Commission of a Felony; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-
5-6(b), and the certified copy was received on November 4, 2020; and

The fourteen-day period following the receipt of the indictment as
prescribed by 0.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(e) has passed.

Now, THEREFORE, BY VIRTUE OF THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Commissioner Terry Goodger of
White County, and Commissioner Nancy Thrash of Lamar County are
hereby appointed as the review commission to determine whether the
indictment relates to and adversely affects the administration of the office
of Commissioner Hawbaker such that the rights and interests of the public
are adversely affected thereby and to make a written report to me within
fourteen days with a recommendation as to whether said commissioner
should be suspended from office as required by O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(e).

This I, ™ day of December, 2020.
