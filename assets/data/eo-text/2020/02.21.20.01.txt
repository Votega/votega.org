ORDERED:

On December 31, 2019, Johnny Isakson — a native Georgian and long-time public
servant — retired from office, creating a vacancy in the representation of this state in
the United States Senate; and

Section 21-2-542 of the Official Code of Georgia Annotated provides that whenever
a vacancy occuts in the United States Senate, such vacancy shall be filled for the
unexpired term by the voters of this state in a special election; and

Said Code Section further provides that such special election shall be held in
conjunction with the next November state-wide general election, occutring at least
forty days following such vacancy; and

Section 21-2-132 provides that each candidate shall file a notice of his or her candidacy
in the office of the Secretary of State no earlier than the date of the call of the special
election and no later than sixty days prior to such special election; and

In accordance with 1986 Op. Atty. Gen. Ga. 72, the Secretary of State has the authority
to set a qualifying petiod between the call of the special election and sixty days prior
to such special election for candidates to file their notices of candidacy in the office of
the Secretary of the State.

Now, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That a proclamation of election is hereby issued to the Secretary of State for a special

election to be held on November 3, 2020, in conjunction with the state-wide general
election to fill the unexpired term of Johnny Isakson in the United States Senate.
