THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING, IN MADISON COUNTY, AND AT ALL GEORGIA
DEPARTMENT OF NATURAL RESOURCES LOCATIONS THROUGHOUT THE STATE OF GEORGIA

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Georgia Department of Natural Resources Captain Stan Elrod was killed in the line
of duty on September 3, 2020; and

Captain Elrod honorably served the citizens of Georgia for 28 years; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Captain Elrod’s service to the State of Georgia
by lowering the flags on the State Capitol building, in Madison County, and at all

Georgia Department of Natural Resources locations throughout the state of
Georgia to half-staff.

NOw, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE MEMORY
OF CAPTAIN STAN ELROD, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be flown at
half-staff on the State Capitol building, in Madison County, and at all Georgia
Department of Natural Resources locations throughout the state of Georgia on
Tuesday, September 8, 2020, the day of Captain Elrod’s interment.

This 6T day of September 2020.
