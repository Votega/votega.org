THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

# O1

ORDERED: That the sum of $19,552,646 be transferred from
the Governor’s Emergency Fund and credited to

This 20 day of March 2020.

PP

GOVERNOR

ATTEST

Executive Secretary
B-07-01-2020


Executive Order #1
Page Two

Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland Security Agency — To provide
funds of $19,552,646 to cover costs associated with novel coronavirus disease 2019
(COVID-19). Funds are issued in response to the United States Declaration of National
Emergency for COVID-19, dated March 13, 2020, and State of Georgia Executive Order
03.14.20.01, Declaration of Public Health State of Emergency.
