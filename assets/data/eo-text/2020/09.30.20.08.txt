ORDERED:

On February 18, 2020, Hart County District 1 Commissioner R.C. Oglesby was
indicted on one count of Violation of the Racketeer Influenced and Corrupt
Organizations Act, ten counts of Theft by Shoplifting, one count of Financial
Transaction Card Theft, and two counts of Financial Transaction Card Fraud; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(b),
and the certified copy was received on July 6, 2020; and

An Executive Order was issued on August 20, 2020, appointing members to the
Review Commission to determine whether the indictment relates to and adversely
affects the administration of the office of commissioner such that the rights and
interests of the public are adversely affected; and

An Executive Order was issued on September 14, 2020, extending the date for the
Review Commission to submit its report and recommendation; and

In its report dated September 24, 2020, the Review Commission found that the
indictment of R.C. Oglesby does relate to and does adversely affect the
administration of the office of Commissioner, that the rights and interests of the
public are adversely affected thereby; and recommended that R.C. Oglesby be
suspended from office pursuant to the provisions of O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mr. R.C. Oglesby is hereby suspended from office immediately and without

further action pending the final disposition of the case, or until the expiration of
his term of office, whichever occurs first, as provided in 45-5-6(c).
