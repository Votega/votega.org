THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Kelley Luthi Claas of Oconee
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a member of
the State Board of Examiners for Speech-Language
Pathology and Audiology, for a term of office ending
October 26, 2023, to succeed the Honorable Alisa
Schultz whose term expired on July 1, 2020.

The Honorable Kelley Luthi Claas
Oconee County, Georgia

This 23 day of October, 2020.

Bile

GOVERNOR
