WHEREAS:

Hart County District 1 Commissioner R.C. Oglesby was indicted on
February 18, 2020 on one count of Violation of Racketeer Influenced
and Corrupt Organizations Act, ten counts of Theft by Shoplifting,
one count of Financial Transaction Card Theft, and two counts of
Financial Transaction Card Fraud; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to
O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(b), and the certified copy was received on July 6,
2020; and

On August 20, 2020, I appointed Attorney General Christopher Carr,
Commissioner Carvel Lewis of Georgetown-Quitman County, and
Commissioner Doug Duncan of Columbia County as the Review
Commission to determine whether the indictment relates to and
adversely affects the administration of the office of Commissioner
Oglesby such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely
affected thereby, and to make a written report to me within fourteen
days with a recommendation as to whether said commissioner
should be suspended from office as required by O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(e);
and

On September 14, 2020, pursuant to the request of the Review
Commission, I extended the deadline for the Review Commission’s
report to September 24, 2020; and

On September 24, 2020, the Review Commission issued its report
finding that the indictment of R.C. Oglesby does relate to and does
adversely affect the administration of the office of Commissioner,
and that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected
thereby; and recommending that R.C. Oglesby be suspended from
office pursuant to the provisions of O0.C.G.A. § 45-5-6.

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On September 30, 2020, pursuant to the recommendation of the
Review Commission, I issued Executive Order 09.30.30.08
suspending R.C. Oglesby from his office as Hart County District 1
Commissioner; and

On October 1, 2020, R.C. Oglesby, through Counsel, petitioned me in
writing for a review of his suspension as Hart County District 1
Commissioner under O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(f).

Now, THEREFORE, BY VIRTUE OF THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME
AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(f), Attorney General Christopher
Carr, Commissioner Carvel Lewis of Georgetown-Quitman County,
and Commissioner Doug Duncan of Columbia County are hereby re-
appointed as the Review Commission to review the suspension of
R.C. Oglesby as Hart County District 1 Commissioner, and to make a
written report within 14 days.
