THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Martha Mitchell Zoller of Hall
County, Georgia, is appointed as the Ninth
Congressional District Representative of the State
Board of Education, for a term of office ending
January 1, 2027, to succeed the Honorable Larry
Boyd, whose term expired January 1, 2020.

The Honorable Martha Mitchell Zoller
Hall County, Georgia

This 13t day of January, 2020.
