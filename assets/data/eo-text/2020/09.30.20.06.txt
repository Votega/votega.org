The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 3.032 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) located at 530 Church Street, in the 53° G.M.
District, City of Swainsboro, Emanuel County, commonly known as the
Swainsboro campus of Southeastern Technical College, acquired by virtue of a
General Warranty Deed dated December 20, 2018, recorded in Deed Book 475,
pages 28-30, in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Emanuel County, and
filed with the State Properties Commission Real Property Record as RPR# 012259;
and

Said Property is in the custody of the Technical College System of Georgia; and

The Property is improved with a building known as the Old Swainsboro High
School Gymnasium (BLLIP ID #117973) as shown in the aerial photograph
attached hereto as Exhibit A (hereinafter the “Structure”); and

Said Structure has been identified by the Technical College System of Georgia to be
the future site of the new commercial truck driving range and will need to be
demolished to accommodate the driving range; and

By Resolution dated August 6, 2020, the Technical College System of Georgia
requested the Structure to be demolished.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Technical College System of Georgia is authorized and directed to provide for the

razing, demolition and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
