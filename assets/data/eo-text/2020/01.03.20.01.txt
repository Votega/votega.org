EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Joe Cade Joiner of DeKalb County,
Georgia, is appointed as an at-large member of the Board of
Regents of the University System of Georgia, for a term of
office ending January 1, 2027, to succeed the Honorable
Chris Cummiskey, whose term expired January 1, 2020.

The Honorable Joe Cade Joiner
DeKalb County, Georgia

This 34 day of January, 2020.

PL

GOVERNOR
