EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Darryl Anthony Hicks of Fayette
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a member of
the State Board of Registration of Used Motor Vehicle
Dealers and Used Motor Vehicle Parts Dealers, Used
Car Division, for a term of office ending September 18,
2024, to succeed the Honorable Sandra Gresham,
whose term expired June 30, 2019.

The Honorable Darryl Anthony Hicks
Fayette County, Georgia

This 206 day of September, 2020.
