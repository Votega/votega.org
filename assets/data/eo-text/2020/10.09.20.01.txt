EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

Pursuant to Code Section 38-2-152, an Assistant Adjutant
General for Army of Georgia is to be appointed by and shall
serve at the pleasure of the Governor.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That effective October 9, 2020, Brigadier General Richard D.
Wilson of Cherokee County, Georgia, was appointed to serve as
Assistant Adjutant General for Army of Georgia.

This qu day of October, 2020.

Bh)

GOVERNOR
