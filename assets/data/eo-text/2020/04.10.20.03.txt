THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That effective April 10, 2020, the Honorable Verda M. Colvin
is hereby appointed Judge of the Court of Appeals of the State
of Georgia to fill the vacancy created by the appointment of
the Honorable Carla Wong McMillian to the Supreme Court
of Georgia, for a term of office ending December 31, 2022.

THIS 107TH DAY OF APRIL, 2020.
