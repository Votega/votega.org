Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland Security Agency — To
provide funds of $32,000,000 to cover costs associated with novel coronavirus disease
2019 (COVID-19). Funds are issued in response to the United States Declaration of
National Emergency for COVID-19, dated March 13, 2020 and State of Georgia
Executive Order 03.14.20.01, Declaration of Public Health State of Emergency.
