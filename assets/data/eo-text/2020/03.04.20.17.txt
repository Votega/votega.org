EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Kristie Louise Sharp of Fulton County,
Georgia, is appointed as a member of the Georgia
Commission for Service and Volunteerism, for a term of office
ending March 4, 2023, to succeed the Honorable Laurie
Chandler, whose term expired January 1, 2011.

The Honorable Kristie Louise Sharp

Fulton County, Georgia

GOVERNOR

This 4 day of March, 2020.
