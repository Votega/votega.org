EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable James Nicholas Ayers of Fulton County,
Georgia, is appointed to serve as an At-Large Representative
of the Board of Natural Resources, for a term of office ending
August 3, 2027, to succeed the Honorable Winburn E.
“Brother” Stewart, Jr., who will be appointed to serve as the
Eighth Congressional District Representative of the Board of
Natural Resources.

The Honorable James Nicholas Ayers

GOVERNOR
