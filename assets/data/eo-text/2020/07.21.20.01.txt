EXECUTIVE ORDER

ORDERED:

On Friday, July 17, our Great Nation and the State of Georgia learned of
the passing of Dr. C.T. Vivian, a giant of the civil rights movement and one
of the foremost advocates for justice in our history; and

Dr. C.T. Vivian was renowned for his wisdom, wit, and commitment to
nonviolent protest. He played an instrumental role in many defining
moments of the Civil Rights Era, serving as one of Dr. Martin Luther King,
Jr.’s most trusted friends and deputies in the fight for equality; and

Dr. C.T. Vivian leaves behind a legacy of compassion, integrity, and a fierce
belief in the cause of justice. He is an example for the ages, and the
American people mourn his death and recognize his immense
contributions to our nation’s history and collective conscience.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF Dr. C.T. VIVIAN, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN
ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Dr. C.T. Vivian shall lie in state in the Rotunda of the Georgia State
Capitol on Wednesday, July 22, 2020 from 11:30 AM to 3:30 PM.

This 215T day of July, 2020.
