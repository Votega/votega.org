THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Sally Lyndell Moore Nabors of
Douglas County, Georgia, is appointed as the
Thirteenth Congressional District Representative of the
State Board of Education, for a term of office ending

January 1, 2023, to succeed the Honorable Matthew
Krull, who resigned.

The Honorable Sally Lyndell Moore Nabors
Douglas County, Georgia

This 13 day of January, 2020.

rade

GOVERNOR
