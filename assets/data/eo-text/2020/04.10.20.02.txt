THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That effective April 10, 2020, the Honorable John A. “Trea”
Pipkin III is hereby appointed Judge of the Court of Appeals
of the State of Georgia to fill the vacancy created by the
passing of the Honorable Stephen S. Goss, for a term of office
ending December 31, 2022.

THIS 101TH DAY OF APRIL, 2020.

GOVERNOR
