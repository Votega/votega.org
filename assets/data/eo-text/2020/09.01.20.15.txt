EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

is appointed to serve as a member of the Sexual Offender
Registration Review Board, for a term of office ending August 31,

2024, to succeed the Honorable Chris Steverson, whose term
expired September 1, 2018.

The Honorable Martin Patrick Ferrell
Cobb County, Georgia

This 1st day of September, 2020.
