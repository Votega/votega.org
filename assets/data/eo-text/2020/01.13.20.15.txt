THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Phenna Rene Petty of Murray
County, Georgia, is appointed as the Fourteenth
Congressional District Representative of the State
Board of Education, for a term of office ending
January 1, 2027, to succeed the Honorable Larry
Winter, whose term expired January 1, 2020.

The Honorable Phenna Rene Petty
Murray County, Georgia

This 13 day of January, 2020.
