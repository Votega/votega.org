FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED TO FLY AT HALF-STAFF

The Honorable Jack Hill of Reidsville, Georgia passed away on Monday, April 6,
2020; and

Senator Hill represented District 4 in the Georgia State Senate for 30 years. Most
recently he served as the chairman of the Senate Appropriations Committee, vice
chairman of the Senate Rules Committee; and

In addition to his extraordinary service in the Georgia State Senate, Senator Hill
spent 37 years in the military. For 33 years he served in the Georgia Air National
Guard, and in 2004 retired from the United States Air Force Reserve as a Reserve
Forces Officer assigned to the Selective Service System; and

It is fitting and proper to remember and honor Senator Hill for his dedicated
service to the State of Georgia and the United States of America.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND MEMORY OF THE HONORABLE JACK HILL,
PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the state of Georgia be flown at
half-staff on all state buildings and grounds on Friday, April 10, 2020, the day of

Senator Hill’s interment.
