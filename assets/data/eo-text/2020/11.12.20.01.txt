EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

The Office of District Attorney of the Griffin Judicial Circuit
became vacant due to the appointment of the Honorable Ben
Coker to the Superior Court of the Griffin Judicial Circuit.
That office was subsequently filled by the appointment of the
Honorable Marie Broder pursuant to Article VI Section VIII
Paragraph I of the Constitution of the State of Georgia.

Further, pursuant to Sections 45-5-3 and 21-2-540 of the
Official Code of Georgia Annotated, a writ of election is hereby
issued to the Secretary of State for a special election to be held
on Tuesday, February 9'", 2021, to fill the District Attorney
vacancy within the Griffin Judicial Circuit.

This / Zz day of November 2020.
