THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS: The initial members of the Safe Harbor for Sexually Exploited Children Fund
Commission have been appointed pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 15-21-203; and

WHEREAS: Pursuant to the aforementioned statute, the Governor shall establish all initial
terms of office for the members of the Commission.

NOW, THEREFORE, BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE
OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

ORDERED: That the members of the Safe Harbor for Sexually Exploited Children Fund
Commission shall have initial terms of office ending on the following dates:

The Honorable Angela Rogers for a term of office ending March 1, 2023;
The Honorable Theresa Schiefer for a term of office ending March 1, 2023;
The Honorable Dianne Scoggins for a term of office ending March 1, 2022;
The Honorable Judith Fouts for a term of office ending March 1, 2022;
The Honorable Hal Jones for a term of office ending March 1, 2022;

The Honorable April Hale for a term of office ending March 1, 2022; and
The Honorable Andy Hite for a term of office ending March 1, 2021.

This 34 day of March, 2020.

Bil

GOVERNOR
