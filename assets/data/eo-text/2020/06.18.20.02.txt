EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

The Governor, on the recommendation of the Adjutant General, shall
appoint an individual to serve as the State Judge Advocate (O.C.G.A.
§ 38-2-1006(a)); and

The officer so appointed as State Judge Advocate shall be a judge
advocate, a member of the State Bar of Georgia in good standing for
not less than ten years, and have not less than five years of
continuous service in the Army or Air National Guard of this state
(0.C.G.A § 38-2-1006(a)); and

The Adjutant General, Major General Thomas M. Carden, Jr., has
recommended the appointment of Lieutenant Colonel Brian Scott
Bischoff of Fayette County, Georgia, as State Judge Advocate; and

Lieutenant Colonel Bischoff is a judge advocate, has been a member
of the bar of the Supreme Court of this state and admitted to the bar
of this state continuously and in good standing since 2000, and has
been a member of the Georgia Army National Guard continuously
since 2002.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Lieutenant Colonel Brian Scott Bischoff of Fayette County,
Georgia, is hereby appointed State Judge Advocate (O.C.G.A. § 38-2-
1006(a)) and any executive orders to the contrary are hereby
revoked.

This 16“Gay of June, 2020.
