EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

A vacancy now exists in Georgia’s Fifth Congressional District
for the United States House of Representatives due to the
death of Congressman John Lewis.

Therefore, pursuant to Code Section 21-2-543, a writ of
election is hereby issued to the Secretary of State for a special
election to be held on Tuesday, September 29, 2020, to fill the
vacancy in Georgia’s Fifth Congressional District for the
United States House of Representatives.

This 27% day of July 2020.
