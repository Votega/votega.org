EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Anne Moody Isbell of Sumter County,

Georgia, is appointed as a member of the State Board for the
Certification of Librarians, for a term of office ending January
13, 2025, to succeed the Honorable Susan Whittle, whose
term expired January 1, 2019.

The Honorable Anne Moody Isbell
Sumter County, Georgia
