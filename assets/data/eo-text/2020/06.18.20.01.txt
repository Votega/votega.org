ORDERED:

The State of Georgia leases approximately 62 acres of improved real property
(hereinafter the “Property”) located at 2910 Newton Road, Albany, Dougherty
County, Georgia, from Dougherty County pursuant to a Ninety-Nine-year lease
which commenced January 25, 1939, which lease is recorded with the State
Properties Commission Real Property Records as RPR# 002868; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Georgia Forestry Commission; and

The Property is improved with an office/shop building (BLLIP ID # 36257) which
was constructed by the Georgia Forestry Commission in 1963 and is shown in the
aerial photograph attached hereto as Exhibit “A” (“Structure”); and

Said Structure is no longer in use and has been identified by the Georgia Forestry
Commission as a liability to the agency due to cracks in the foundation and outdated
electrical systems; and

By Resolution dated February 11, 2020, the Georgia Forestry Commission requested
authorization to demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Georgia Forestry Commission is authorized and directed to provide for the

razing, demolition and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
