EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That effective April 10, 2020, the Honorable Carla Wong
MeMillian is hereby appointed Justice of the Supreme Court
of Georgia to fill the vacancy created by the retirement of the
Honorable Robert Benham, for a term of office ending
December 31, 2022.

THIS 10TH DAY OF APRIL, 2020.
