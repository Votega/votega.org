THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

The State of Georgia holds title to, and the Department of Community Health
is the custodian of, approximately 0.2576 of an acre of improved real
property, by virtue of a General Warranty Deed dated December 19, 2001,
from the Downtown Development Authority of Alma, Georgia, being
recorded in Deed Book 311, Pages 265-267, in the office of the Clerk of
Superior Court of Bacon County, and on file with the State Properties
Commission Real Property Records as RPR # 009566.01, with an address of
426 & 428 W. 12¢ Street, Alma, Georgia, and located Bacon County, Georgia,
(hereinafter the “Property”), as more fully described in that survey attached
hereto as Exhibit “A”; and

By Resolution from the Department of Community Health, dated
December 12, 2019, the Property was declared surplus; and

By Resolution dated February 12, 2020, the Department of Human
Services requested that custody of the Property be transferred from the
Department of Community Health to the Department of Human Services
for use as office space for the staff of the Department of Human Services.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That custody of the above-defined Property located in Bacon County,
Georgia is hereby transferred from the Department of Community Health to
the Department of Human Services.

This Goth day of Ap. . / , 2020.
