The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 5.71 acres of improved real property
located in Land Lots 104, 105, 120, and 121, 6th Land District, Monroe County,
Georgia (hereinafter the “Property”), which Property is a portion of the 236.872 acres
acquired by virtue of a Warranty Deed dated September 2, 1982, from the Forest
Farms, Inc., being recorded in Deed Book 147, Pages 15 to 21 in the office of the Clerk
of Superior Court of Monroe County and on file with the State Properties Commission
Real Property Records as RPR # 007087, and which Property is under the custody of
the Georgia Public Safety Training Center and is more fully described in that survey
attached hereto as Exhibit “A”; and

By letter dated June 8, 2020, the Georgia Public Safety Training Center declared
the Property surplus; and

By letter dated June 5, 2020, the Georgia Emergency Management Agency
requested that custody of the Property be transferred from the Georgia Public
Safety Training Center to the Georgia Emergency Management Agency to establish
an Alternate Care Facility in support of the COVID-19 response operations.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR
OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That custody of the above-defined Property located in Monroe County, Georgia is
hereby transferred from the Georgia Public Safety Training Center to the Georgia

Emergency Management Agency.
