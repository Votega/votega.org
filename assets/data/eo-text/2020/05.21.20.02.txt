FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF STAFF

On Memorial Day, Monday, May 25, 2020, Americans will once again join
together in honoring the brave men and women who served this Great
Nation and made the ultimate sacrifice in the name of freedom. We, a
nation of proud Americans, consider the courage of our fellow Georgians
and Americans who represent the highest ideals of our nation, as they carry
our flag into battle, knowing they may never return; and

This day marks the time of year where we mourn the fallen and count our
blessings. This country would be nothing without those who gave their lives
to preserve liberty. Because of them, we are blessed to live in a free country
where the American dream is real — where our families can live, grow, and
prosper — all because of our soldiers’ willingness to put country above self.
For their acts of valor, we owe a debt that can never be repaid; and

For families across the United States, there are difficult emotions associated
with days like today. They represent great sadness and hope, grief and
celebration, conflict and peace. Although we cannot erase your pain, please
know that we will always stand by you. We will honor your loved ones’
sacrifice. We will permanently etch their names into our hearts and minds,
and we will lift you up in our thoughts and prayers; and

This Memorial Day, as we meet with friends and family, let us all pause and
remember the fallen. Let us pray for our veterans, the brave men and
women who currently serve in our Armed Forces, and their families.
Remember that these families serve our nation by supporting their loved
ones in uniform. We must pledge to act worthy of their sacrifices year-
round. May God bless those that we have lost, and those that have served
and continue to serve.

Now, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF THOSE WHO HAVE MADE THE ULTIMATE SACRIFICE IN
DEFENSE OF OUR NATION, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

ORDERED: That on Memorial Day, Monday, May 25, 2020, in accordance with 36 USC
§ 174, the flag of the United States of America and the flag of the great state
of Georgia shall be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds until
noon, and then raised to the top of the staff.
