The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 13.27 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) located at 361 Industrial Drive, in Land Lot
260, 8th District, City of Moultrie, Colquitt County, commonly known as Southern
Regional Technical College, acquired by virtue of a Fee Deed Without Warranty,
Dated March 28, 1989, recorded in Deed Book 465, pages 756-757, in the office of
the Clerk of Superior Court of Colquitt County, and filed with the State Properties
Commission, Real Property Record as RPR#¥ 008227; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Technical College System of Georgia; and

The Property is improved with a building known as Building 900 (BLLIP ID # 192)
as shown in the aerial photograph attached hereto as Exhibit A (hereinafter the
“Structure”); and

Said Structure is 45 years old, is expensive to maintain, and has been identified by
the Technical College System of Georgia as surplus to the needs of Southern Regional
Technical College; and

By Resolution dated August 6, 2020, the Technical College System of Georgia
requested authority to demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Technical College System of Georgia is authorized and directed to provide for the

razing, demolition and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
