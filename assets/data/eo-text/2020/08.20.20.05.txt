EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Stanley Aaron McWhorter of Carroll
County, Georgia, is appointed as a member of the Board of
Governors of the George L. Smith II World Congress Center
Authority, for a term of office ending August 18, 2024, to

succeed the Honorable David Allen, whose term expired July
1, 2020.

The Honorable Stanley Aaron McWhorter
Carroll County, Georgia

This Zoth day of August, 2020.

58 hh

GOVERNOR
