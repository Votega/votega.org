EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Charlie M. Maddox of Clarke
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Tenth Congressional District Representative of
the Board of Community Affairs, for a term of
office ending July 1, 2025, to succeed the
Honorable Cliff Sheppard, whose term expired
July 1, 2020.

The Honorable Charlie M. Maddox
Clarke County, Georgia

This Cate day of September, 2020.
