THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Amy Washinger Arnold of Oconee
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a member of
the State Board of Examiners for Speech-Language
Pathology and Audiology, for a term of office ending
October 26, 2023, to succeed the Honorable Edgar
Clark whose term expired on July 1, 2018.

The Honorable Amy Washinger Arnold
Oconee County, Georgia

This 93th day of October, 2020.
