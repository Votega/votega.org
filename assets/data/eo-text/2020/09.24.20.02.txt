THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 15-1-9.2(a.1), the
Honorable Edward H. Johnson is hereby appointed
Senior Judge of the Superior Courts of Georgia.

IT IS FURTHER ORDERED:

That pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 15-3A-2, the Honorable
Edward H. Johnson is hereby appointed Senior
Appellate Court Judge of Georgia.

This 247 day of September, 2020.

Bil

GOVERNOR
