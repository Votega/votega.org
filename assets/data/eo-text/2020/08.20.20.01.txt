BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

Hart County District 1 Commissioner R.C. Oglesby was indicted on
February 18, 2020 on one count of Violation of Racketeer Influenced and
Corrupt Organizations Act, ten counts of Theft by Shoplifting, one count of
Financial Transaction Card Theft, and two counts of Financial Transaction
Card Fraud; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-
5-6(b), and the certified copy was received on July 6, 2020; and

The fourteen-day period following the receipt of the indictment as
prescribed by 0.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(e) has passed.

Now, THEREFORE, BY VIRTUE OF THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Commissioner Carvel Lewis of
Georgetown-Quitman County, and Commissioner Doug Duncan of
Columbia County are hereby appointed as the review commission to
determine whether the indictment relates to and adversely affects the
administration of the office of Commissioner Oglesby such that the rights
and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby and to make a
written report to me within fourteen days with a recommendation as to
whether said commissioner should be suspended from office as required by
O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(e).
