ATTEST:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 21.373 acres of real property
(hereinafter the “Property”) located in Land Lot 245, 5th Land District, 1714th G.M.D,
Baldwin County, Georgia, as shown on the survey attached hereto as Exhibit A, and
being a portion within the campus of Central State Hospital (Parcel “C”) and the
Milledgeville Driver Services (Parcel “B”), which Property is more particularly
described and inventoried on file in the office of the State Properties Commission as
Real Property Record numbers 051710 and 007598, respectively; and

The Department of Behavioral Health and Disabilities by resolution dated August
20, 2015, declared Parcel C to be surplus to its needs; and

The Department of Driver Services by resolution dated March 11, 2020, declared
Parcel B to be surplus to its needs; and

Department of Public Safety, by Resolution dated December 13, 2018, requested
transfer of custody of the Property for the construction of a new Post 33 facility.

Now, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR
OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That custody of the above-defined Property located in Baldwin County, Georgia is

hereby transferred from the Department of Behavioral Health and Disabilities and
the Department of Driver Services to the Department of Public Safety.
