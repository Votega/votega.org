EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable David Andrew Hamilton of Oconee
County, Georgia, is appointed as a member of the Georgia
Commission for Service and Volunteerism, for a term of office
ending March 4, 2023, to succeed the Honorable Mary
Katelyn Williams, whose term expired January 1, 2017.

The Honorable David Andrew Hamilton
Oconee County, Georgia

This 4% day of March, 2020.
