EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable William Todd Groce of Chatham
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a member of
the State Board for the Certification of Librarians, for a
term of office ending October 26, 2025, to succeed the
Honorable Julia Skinner whose term expired on
December 31, 2017.

The Honorable William Todd Groce
Chatham County, Georgia

This LB day of October, 2020.
