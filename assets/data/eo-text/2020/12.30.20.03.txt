ORDERED:

On October 29, 2020, Spalding County District 5 Commissioner Donald F. Hawbaker was
indicted on one count of Aggravated Assault, Family Violence, one count of Possession of
Firearm During Commission of a Felony, five counts of Aggravated Assault on a Peace
Officer, and one count of Possession of Firearm During Commission of a Felony; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(b), and
the certified copy was received on November 4, 2020; and

An Executive Order was issued on December 7, 2020, appointing members to the Review
Commission to determine whether the indictment relates to and adversely affects the
administration of the office of commissioner such that the rights and interests of the public
are adversely affected; and

By the report dated December 18, 2020, the Review Commission found that the
indictment of Donald F. Hawbaker does relate to and does adversely affect the
administration of the office of Commissioner, and that the rights and interests of the
public are adversely affected thereby; and recommended that Donald Hawbaker be
suspended from office pursuant to the provisions of O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6.

Now, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF
THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mr. Donald F. Hawbaker is hereby suspended from office immediately and without

further action pending the final disposition of the case or until the expiration of his term
of office, whichever occurs first, as provided in 45-5-6(c).
