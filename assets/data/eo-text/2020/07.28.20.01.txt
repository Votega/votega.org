THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On Friday, July 17, 2020, civil rights icon, the Honorable John
Robert Lewis lost a fierce battle with cancer; and

In addition to his lifelong commitment to fight oppression and
battle hatred, Congressman Lewis honorably served Georgia’s
fifth congressional district in the United States House of
Representatives for seventeen terms; and

As we mourn his death, we honor his dedication to his fellow
man and woman, and we commend his efforts to positively
change the world in profound and immeasurable ways.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT
FOR THE MEMORY OF THE HONORABLE JOHN ROBERT
LEWIS, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Congressman John Lewis shall lie in state in the
Rotunda of the Georgia State Capitol on Wednesday, July 29,
2020.

This 28TH day of July, 2020.

Bil

GOVERNOR
