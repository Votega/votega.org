THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On August 20, 2020, pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(b), an Executive
Order was issued appointing members to a committee charged with
investigating the Hart County District 1 Commissioner R.C. Oglesby;
and

Pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(c), due to scheduling difficulties
caused by COVID-19, the Attorney General’s Office requested the
deadline for the commission to issue its report be extended to
Thursday, September 24, 2020.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the extension for the review commission to issue its report in
the matter of the indictment Hart County District 1 Commissioner
R.C. Oglesby, is hereby extended to Thursday, September 24, 2020.

This 14TH day of September, 2020.

hie

GOVERNOR
