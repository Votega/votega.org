EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

FLAGS ON STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

On Friday, July 17, 2020, Georgia lost a true titan, Congressman
John Robert Lewis — an American hero, civil rights icon, freedom
fighter, and beloved Georgian — following his fierce battle with
cancer; and

Throughout his life, John Lewis fought oppression, battled hatred,
and stood against intolerance with integrity and iron resolve; and

John Lewis placed the lives of his fellow Americans above his own —
risking his all for the cause of freedom and equality; and

As we mourn his death, we must honor his legacy by striving toward
the principles he embodied to forge a stronger, brighter future for the
generations of Georgians and Americans to come.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF JOHN ROBERT LEWIS, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States of America and the flag of the State
of Georgia shall be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and
grounds from the date of this order through sunset on the day of his
interment.

This 18" day of July 2020.

B:f),

GOVERNOR
