The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 200 acres of improved real property
(hereinafter the “Property”) located in the 1201 G.M.D, Johnson County, Georgia,
by virtue of a Fee Simple deed dated June 17, 1988, from Johnson County recorded
in Deed Book 116, pages 217-220 in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of
Johnson County, which Property is more particularly described and inventoried on
file in the office of the State Properties Commission as Real Property Record #007567
and shown on the survey attached hereto as Exhibit “A”; and

The Department of Corrections was the original custodian of the Property and
operated the Property as Johnson State Prison; and

The Property was transferred from the custody of the Department of Corrections to
the Department of Children and Youth Services, now known as the Department of
Juvenile Justice, by Executive Order dated July 12, 1996, and inventoried on file in
the office of the State Properties Commission as Real Property Record #008852; and

The property is currently being used by the Georgia Department of Corrections as
the Johnson State Prison facility, and has been so used since 2002 although custody
of the Property was never officially transferred back to the Georgia Department of
Corrections; and

The Department of Juvenile Justice, by Resolution dated September 5, 2019,
requested transfer of custody of the Property to the Department of Corrections and
the Georgia Board of Corrections; and

The Property is improved with an 1,800 square foot pre-fabricated modular
building (the “Structure”) BLLIP#18240, as shown in that aerial photograph
attached hereto as Exhibit B and incorporated herein by reference; and

The Board of Corrections, by Resolution dated October 6, 2016, requested approval
to demolish the Structure since it is in poor condition with no salvage value.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

ORDERED: That custody of the above-defined Property located in Johnson County,
Georgia is hereby transferred from the Georgia Department of Juvenile
Justice to the Georgia Department of Corrections and the Georgia
Department of Corrections is authorized to provide for the razing,

demolition, and disposal of said Structure.
