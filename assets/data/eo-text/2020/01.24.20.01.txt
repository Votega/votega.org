EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

The Honorable Kimberly Childs, in her individual capacity as Judge
of the Superior Court of Cobb County, has been named as a party
respondent in the lawsuit John Hughes v. Honorable Kimberly
Childs, Civil Action Case No. 19-1-8638, Superior Court of Cobb
County; and

The Attorney General, because of conflict, has declined
representation of Judge Childs in this case; and

Mr. Dennis Cathey from Cornelia, Georgia, has agreed to represent
the Honorable Kimberly A. Childs in this matter at a rate agreed upon
by Judge Childs, Mr. Cathey, and the Office of the Governor.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO O.C.G.A. § 45-15-70 AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mr. Dennis Cathey is hereby appointed as Special Counsel to
assume control and responsibility for the representation of the
Honorable Kimberly A. Childs in all matters related to John Hughes
uv. Honorable Kimberly Childs, Civil Action Case No. 19-1-8638

Bh he

GOVERNOR

This 24th day of January, 2020.
