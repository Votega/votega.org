EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable William Kevin Bostock of Gwinnett

County, Georgia, is appointed as a member of the Georgia
Composite Medical Board, for a term of office ending January
9, 2024, to succeed the Honorable John Downey.

The Honorable William Kevin Bostock
Gwinnett County, Georgia

This 9th day of January, 2020.
