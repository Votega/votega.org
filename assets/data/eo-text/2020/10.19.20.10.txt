The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 3.47 acres of improved real property
(hereinafter the “Property”) located at 800 U.S. Highway 29 N., in the 219th G.M.
District, Athens-Clarke County, Georgia, acquired by virtue of a General Warranty
Deed dated February 14, 2012, recorded in Deed Book 3932, pages 0401-0402, in
the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Athens-Clarke County, and filed with
the State Properties Commission, Real Property Record as RPR# 011006; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Technical College System of Georgia and is
commonly known as Athens Technical College; and

The Property is improved with a fifty-four-year-old building known as Building S
— Campus 900 (BLLIP ID # 71440) as shown in the aerial photograph attached
hereto as Exhibit A (hereinafter the “Structure”); and

Said Structure is located on the site planned for the construction of the Industrial
Systems Technology Building (TCSG-365); and

By Resolution dated October 1, 2020, the State Board of the Technical College System
of Georgia requested authority to demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Technical College System of Georgia is authorized and directed to provide for the
razing, demolition and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
