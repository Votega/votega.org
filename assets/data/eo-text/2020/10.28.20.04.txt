THE STATE OF GEORGIA

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Thomas Neils Culpepper of Bartow
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a member of
the Georgia Board of Private Detectives and Security

expired on July 1, 2020.

The Honorable Thomas Neils Culpepper
Bartow County, Georgia
