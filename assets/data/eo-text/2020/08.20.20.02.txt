EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED: That the Honorable Barry H. Babb of Fayette County,
Georgia, is appointed as the Thirteenth Congressional
District Representative of the Board of Corrections, for a
term of office ending August 19, 2025, to succeed the

Honorable Hernitha Robinson, whose term expired July 1,
2020.
