EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable James E. “Trey” Allen
of Columbia County, Georgia is appointed
to serve as a member of the Board of
Directors of the Georgia Lottery
Corporation, for a term of office expiring
December 31, 2025, to succeed the
Honorable Simeon F. Deal, whose term of
office expired December 15, 2019.

This 31st day of December, 2020.

Bh

GOVERNOR
