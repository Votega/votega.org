THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Paul Harvey Matthews of Clarke County,
Georgia, is appointed as a member of the Georgia
Commission for Service and Volunteerism, for a term of office
ending March 4, 2023, to succeed the Honorable Matt
Westmoreland, whose term expired April 15, 2019.

The Honorable Paul Harvey Matthews
Clarke County, Georgia

This 4t day of March, 2020.

OVERNOR
