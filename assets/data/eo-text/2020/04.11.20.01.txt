EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

Senate District 4 in the Georgia State Senate has become
vacant due to the passing of Senator Jack Hill.

Therefore, pursuant to Article V, Section II, Paragraph VIII of
the Constitution of the State of Georgia and Section 21-2-544
of the Official Code of Georgia Annotated, a writ of election is
hereby issued to the Secretary of State for a special election to
be held on Tuesday, June 9, 2020, to fill the vacancy in
District 4 of the Georgia State Senate.

Bl

This 11th day of April, 2020.
