FLAGS ON STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS ORDERED TO HALF STAFF

The citizens of the United States of America mourn the passing of the Honorable
Ruth Bader Ginsburg; and

Appointed by President Bill Clinton, Justice Ginsburg honorably served this
nation as an Associate Justice of the Supreme Court of the United States of
America for twenty-seven years; and

Justice Ginsburg was a trailblazer, poured her heart and soul into public service,
and made a lasting, positive impact on our Great Nation; and

In accordance with U.S. Flag Code, it is fitting and proper to fly the flag of the
United States of America at half-staff in honor of and to recognize the passing of
an Associate Justice of the U.S. Supreme Court.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR
OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be flown at
half-staff on all state buildings and grounds from this date until the date of
Justice Ginsburg’s interment.

This 18TH day of September, 2020.

Bh

GOVERNOR
