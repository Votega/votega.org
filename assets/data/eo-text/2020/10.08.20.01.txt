ORDERED:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 1,480 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) located at 46 John Coffee Road, located in
Land Lot 329, 6th District, City of Nicholls, Coffee County, Georgia, acquired by
virtue of a deed dated May 28, 1970, recorded in Deed Book 168, pages 191-195, in
the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Coffee County, and filed with the State
Properties Commission, Real Property Record as RPR# 004193; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Georgia Department of Natural Resources and
is operated as General Coffee State Park; and

The Property is improved with a building known as the Pool House (BLLIP ID #
26764) as shown in the aerial photograph attached hereto as Exhibit A (hereinafter
the “Structure”); and

Said Structure is beyond reasonable repair and no longer needed since the pool has
been filled; and

By Resolution dated August 25, 2020, the Georgia Department of Natural Resources
requested authority to demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Georgia Department of Natural Resources is authorized and directed to provide

for the razing, demolition and disposal of said Structure under the terms and
conditions which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
