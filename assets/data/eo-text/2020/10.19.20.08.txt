CUTIVE SECRETARY

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 17.63 acres of improved real
property located at 1763 U.S. Highway 280, in Land Lots 79 and 82, 26 Land
District, City of Plains, Sumter County, Georgia (hereinafter the “Property”), acquired
by virtue of a Fee Simple Deed Without Warranty dated March 2, 1977, from the
Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, and filed with the State
Properties Commission, Real Property Record as RPR# 06351; and

The Property is improved with a building being 3,150 square feet, commonly known
as the Georgia Welcome Center Building, BLIPP ID 10423; and

By Resolution dated August 26, 2020, the Board of Economic Development
requested that the Property be transferred to the Georgia Department of Natural
Resources pursuant to Resolution Act 382 (S.B. 474) of the 2020 General
Assembly; and

By Resolution dated September 22, 2020, the Board of Natural Resources
requested that custody of the Property be transferred to the Department of Natural
Resources to manage and maintain pursuant to Resolution Act 382 (S.B. 474) of
the 2020 General Assembly.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR
OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That custody of the above-defined Property located in Sumter County, Georgia is

hereby transferred from the Georgia Department of Economic Development to the
Georgia Department of Natural Resources.
