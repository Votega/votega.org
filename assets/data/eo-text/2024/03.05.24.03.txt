The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 1.017 acres of unimproved real
property located in Land Lots 88 and 89, 15th Land District, DeKalb County
(“Property”) as shown on the survey attached hereto as Exhibit “A”; and

Said Property is a portion of the 6.22 acres of real property located in Land Lots 88
and 89, 15th Land District, DeKalb County acquired by virtue of a Quitclaim Deed
dated June 16, 1988, being recorded in Deed Book 6204, Pages 390-392, in the office
of the Clerk of Superior Court of DeKalb County, and on file with the State Properties
Commission Real Property Records as RPR # 007588, which Property is under the
custody of the Department of Corrections for the benefit of the Georgia Correctional
Industries Administration; and

By Official Action dated December 7, 2023, the Department of Corrections
requested that custody of the Property be transferred to the Georgia Bureau of
Investigations; and

By Official Action dated October 12, 2023, the Georgia Bureau of Investigation
requested custody of the Property for Georgia Bureau of Investigation’s State
Medical Examiners Annex Building.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR
OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That custody of the above-defined Property located in DeKalb County, Georgia is
hereby transferred from the Department of Corrections to the Georgia Bureau of
