EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable David Belle Isle of Fulton
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Seventh Congressional District Representative on
the Board of Community Affairs, for a term of
office expiring July 1, 2028, to succeed the
Honorable Tung Quang Le, who is now the Fourth
Congressional District Representative on the
Board of Community Affairs, as a result of the
Georgia House of Representatives Redistricting Act
of 2023, adopted December 8, 2023.

This 31st day of January 2024
