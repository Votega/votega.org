EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN COWETA COUNTY

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Deputy Eric Anthony Minix, 31, with the Coweta County Sheriffs
Office was killed in the line of duty on Thursday, January 4, 2024;
and

Deputy Minix served as a dedicated investigator and K-9 handler for
the Coweta County Sheriff's Office; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Deputy Minix’s service to Coweta
County and the State of Georgia by lowering the flags on the State
Capitol building and in Coweta County to half-staff.

Now, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF DEPUTY ERIC ANTHONY MINIX, PURSUANT TO
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia
be flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Coweta
County on Monday, January 8, 2024, the day of Deputy Minix’s
interment, until sunset.

This 5% day of January 2024.

BP

' GOVERNOR
