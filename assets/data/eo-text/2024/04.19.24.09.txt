THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Jose R. Perez of Gwinnett
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Fourth Congressional District Representative on
the Board of Economic Development, for a term of
office expiring July 1, 2026, to succeed the
Honorable Dwight Evans, who no longer
represents the Fourth Congressional District on
the Board of Economic Development, as a result
of the Georgia House of Representatives
Redistricting Act of 2023, adopted December 8,
2023.

This 19th day of April 2024

Bil

GOVERNOR
