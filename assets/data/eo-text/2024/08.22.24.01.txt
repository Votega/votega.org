EXECUTIVE ORDER |

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN PAULDING COUNTY

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Deputy Brandon Cunningham, 30, with the Paulding County
Sheriff's Office was killed in the line of duty on Saturday, August 17,
2024; and

Deputy Cunningham had served with the Paulding County Sheriff's
Office for just over four years; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Deputy Cunningham’s service to
Paulding County and the State of Georgia by lowering the flags on the
State Capitol building and in Paulding County to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF DEPUTY BRANDON CUNNINGHAM, PURSUANT TO
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia
be flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Paulding
County on Friday, August 23, 2024, the day of Deputy Cunningham’s
interment, until sunset.

This 22"4 day of August 2024.

Bxh-

GOVERNOR
