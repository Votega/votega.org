ORDERED:

THE STATE OF GEORGIA ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Trooper Chase Winston Redner, 31, with the Georgia State Patrol was killed
in the line of duty on Tuesday, February 20, 2024; and

Trooper Redner honorably served with the Georgia Department of Public
Safety after graduating from the 103" Trooper School with the Georgia
State Patrol in 2018; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Trooper Redner’s service to the State of
Georgia by lowering the flags on the State Capitol building, in Cherokee
County, and at all Department of Public Safety locations throughout the
State of Georgia to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF TROOPER CHASE WINSTON REDNER, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be
flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building, in Cherokee County, and at
all Department of Public Safety locations throughout the State of Georgia
on Tuesday, February 27, 2024, the day of Trooper Redner’s interment,

until sunset.
