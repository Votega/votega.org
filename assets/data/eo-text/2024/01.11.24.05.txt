EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That the Honorable Joseph W. Dent is hereby appointed
Superior Court Judge of the Dougherty Judicial Circuit to fill
the seat created by the enactment of House Bill 77 (Act
Number 58) during the 2023 session of the Georgia General
Assembly, for a term of office ending December 31, 2026.

THIS 11T™ DAY OF JANUARY 2024.
