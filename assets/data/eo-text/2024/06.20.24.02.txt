The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 23.20 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) in Muscogee County, Georgia filed
with the State Properties Commission Real Property Record as Property
BLLIP ID# 009842; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Juvenile Justice and is
commonly known as Muscogee Youth Development Campus; and

The Property is improved with structures identified as “Housing Cottages
1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6” (BLLIP ID# 32161, 32162, 32163, 32164, 32165, and
32166, respectively), (hereinafter, the “Structures”), as shown in the aerial
photographs attached hereto as Exhibit A; and

The Department of Juvenile Justice has outlined plans to demolish the
Structures to construct a new 56 bed housing unit; and

By resolution dated May 23, 2024, the Department of Juvenile Justice
requested authority to demolish the Structures.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Juvenile Justice is authorized and directed to provide for
the razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structures under the terms and
conditions which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia,
in accordance with applicable laws, policies, and procedures, including any
