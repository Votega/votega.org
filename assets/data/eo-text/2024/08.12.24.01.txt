ORDERED:

On May 31, 2024, Mr. Chad Nichols, Sheriff of Rabun County, was
arrested by the Georgia Bureau of Investigation and charged with
one count of Violation of Oath by Public Officer, one count of Sexual
Battery, and one count of Public Indecency; and

An Executive Order was issued on June 3, 2024, appointing a
committee to investigate Sheriff Nichols; and

On June 14, 2024, the committee delivered its recommendation that
Sheriff Nichols be suspended from office pursuant to Code Section
15-16-26; and

Accordingly, on June 14, 2024, I issued Executive Order 06.14.24.01
suspending Sheriff Nichols for a period of sixty days; and

Pursuant to Code Section 15-16-26(c), “in any case where a sheriff
has been suspended for [sixty] days, the Governor may extend the
period of suspension for an additional [thirty] days.”

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 15-16-26, AND
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the period of suspension for Sheriff Nichols is hereby extended
for an additional thirty days.

This 12th day of August 2024.

Bill
