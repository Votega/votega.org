THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Richard T. Evans Sr. of
Fulton County, Georgia, is appointed to serve
as the Seventh Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Regents of the
University System of Georgia, for a term of office
expiring January 1, 2026, to succeed the
Honorable Jose R. Perez, who no longer
represents the Seventh Congressional District
on the Board of Regents of the University
System of Georgia, as a result of the Georgia
House of Representatives Redistricting Act of
2023, adopted December 8, 2023.

This 19 day of April 2024

Bh

GOVERNOR
