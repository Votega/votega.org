EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

‘That the Honorable Artesius Miller of Clayton

County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Thirteenth Congressional District Representative on
the State Board of the Technical College System of
Georgia, for a term of office expiring June 30, 2028,
to succeed the Honorable Joseph Poan Hsiao, who is
now the Fifth Congressional District Representative
on the State Board of the Technical College System
of Georgia, as a result of the Georgia House of
Representatives Redistricting Act of 2023, adopted
December 8, 2023.

This 31st day of January 2024

BRL

GOVERNOR
