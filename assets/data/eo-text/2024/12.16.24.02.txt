THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That, effective January 1, 2025, the Honorable Ralph W.
Powell is hereby appointed Superior Court Judge of the Tifton
Judicial Circuit to fill the seat created by the enactment of
House Bill 906 (Act Number 503) during the 2024 session of
the Georgia General Assembly, for a term of office ending
December 31, 2026.

THIS 167 DAY OF DECEMBER 2024.
