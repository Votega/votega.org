EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Ronald Garrard of Forsyth
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Seventh Congressional District Representative
on the Board of Economic Development, for a
term of office expiring December 12, 2027, to
succeed the Honorable Nicholas Francis Masino,
who no longer represents the Seventh
Congressional District on the Board of Economic
Development, as a result of the Georgia House of
Representatives Redistricting Act of 2023,
adopted December 8, 2023.

This 19% day of April 2024

Bille

GOVERNOR
