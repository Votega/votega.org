THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED:

There currently exist at Augusta University, Richmond County, Augusta,
Georgia, buildings known as Pavilion I, Clinical Trials Office, Pavilion 2,
Community Medicine Offices, and Gorgas Road Toilet (collectively, the
“Buildings”), located at 1120 15t Street; and

The Board of Regents, at its meeting on June 14, 2024, declared that said
Buildings could no longer be advantageously used by Augusta University or
the University System of Georgia and should be removed.

Now, THEREFORE, BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE
OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia is hereby
authorized to demolish and remove the Buildings from Augusta University,
Richmond County, Augusta, Georgia.

IT IS FURTHER

That the appropriate staff of Augusta University, acting under the approval
of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, are hereby
authorized to demolish the Buildings and to have all building materials
removed from the premises by public works contract; all said demolition
and removal to be performed as expeditiously as possible.

Bil he

GOVERNOR

This 26 day of June 2024.
