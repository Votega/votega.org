THE STATE OF GEORGIA
                                     EXECUTIVE ORDER


BY THE GOVERNOR:

                           FLAGS ON STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
                              ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

   WHEREAS: Mr. Bruce Thompson, Georgia’s Commissioner of Labor, passed away on
            November 24, 2024; and

   WHEREAS: Commissioner Thompson honorably served our country for six years within the
            Tank/Armored Division of the U.S. Army National Guard; and

   WHEREAS: Commissioner Thompson represented the 14th district in the Georgia State Senate
            from December 2013 until January 2023 when he took office as Commissioner of
            Labor; and

   WHEREAS: As Georgia’s tenth Commissioner of Labor, Bruce Thompson used his work ethic,
            integrity, and legislative experience to serve Georgia’s workers and employers by
            leading an agency focused on transparency, responsiveness, and fiscal
            responsibility; and

   WHEREAS: It is fitting and proper to remember and honor Bruce Thompson’s service to the
            State of Georgia by lowering flags on all State buildings and grounds to half-staff.

                NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
                MEMORY OF BRUCE THOMPSON, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME
                AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

   ORDERED:     That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be flown at
                half-staff on all State buildings and grounds on Saturday, November 30, 2024, the
                day of Commissioner Bruce Thompson’s interment.

                This 29th day of November 2024.




                                                         __________________________
                                                                  GOVERNOR
