EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED:

There currently exists at Georgia Southwestern State University,
Sumter County, Americus, Georgia, a building known as the Griffin
Bell Conference Center #0044 (the “Building”), located at 1800
South Lee Street; and

The Board of Regents, at its meeting on January 18, 2023, declared
that said Building could no longer be advantageously used by
Georgia Southwestern State University or the University System of
Georgia and should be removed.

NOW, THEREFORE, BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF
THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia is
hereby authorized to demolish and remove the Building from
Georgia Southwestern State University, Sumter County, Americus,
Georgia.

IT IS FURTHER

That the appropriate staff of Georgia Southwestern State University,
acting under the approval of the Board of Regents of the University
System of Georgia, are hereby authorized to demolish the Building
and to have all building materials removed from the premises by
public works contract; all said demolition and removal to be
performed as expeditiously as possible.
