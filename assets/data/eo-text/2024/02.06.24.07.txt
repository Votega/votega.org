EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Penny Penn of Forsyth County,
Georgia, is appointed to serve as an At Large
Representative on the Board of Juvenile Justice, for
a term of office expiring December 19, 2027, to
succeed the Honorable Adam Kennedy, who is now
the Twelfth Congressional District Representative on
the Board of Juvenile Justice, as a result of the
Georgia House of Representatives Redistricting Act
of 2023, adopted December 8, 2023.

This 6 day of February 2024

Bill

GOVERNOR
