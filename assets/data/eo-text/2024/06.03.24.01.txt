On May 31, 2024, Mr. Chad Nichols, Sheriff of Rabun County, was
arrested by the Georgia Bureau of Investigation and charged with
one count of Violation of Oath by Public Officer, one count of Sexual
Battery, and one count of Public Indecency; and

By letter dated May 31, 2024, the Georgia Sheriffs’ Association has
requested the appointment of a committee under Code Section
15-16-26 to investigate Sheriff Nichols; and

Pursuant to Code Section 15-16-26(a), upon criminal charges against
a sheriff, the Governor has the authority to appoint an investigative
committee consisting of the Attorney General and two sheriffs who
are members of the Georgia Sheriffs’ Association.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 15-16-26 AND
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS THE GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Sheriff Robert Markley of

Morgan County, and Sheriff Janis Mangum of Jackson County are
hereby appointed as the committee to investigate Sheriff Nichols.

IT IS FURTHER

That the committee shall make a report of its investigation to me
within thirty (30) days of the date of this Order.
