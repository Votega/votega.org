EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Paul Shailendra of Fulton
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the Sixth
Congressional District Representative on the Board
of Natural Resources, for a term of office expiring
January 1, 2031, to succeed the Honorable David
“Penn” Pendleton Hodge, who is now the Seventh
Congressional District Representative on the Board
of Natural Resources, as a result of the Georgia
House of Representatives Redistricting Act of 2023,
adopted December 8, 2023.

This 6 day of February 2024

Bil

GOVERNOR
