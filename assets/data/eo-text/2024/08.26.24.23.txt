THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Cheri “Mikki” Quinones of
Houston County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as
a member of the Board of Directors of the Georgia
Emergency Communications Authority, for a term
of office expiring April 11, 2026, to succeed the
Honorable Ted Wynn, who resigned.
