EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED:

Code Section 21-2-30(a.1)(3) provides that in the event of a vacancy in the
position of chairperson of the State Election Board at a time when the
Georgia General Assembly is not in session, it shall be the duty of the
Governor and the Governor is empowered and directed to appoint a
qualified chairperson who shall serve as chairperson until the next regular
session of the General Assembly; and

The position of chairperson of the State Election Board is currently vacant
and the General Assembly is not in session.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 21-2-30(a.1)(3), AND
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA,
IT IS HEREBY

That the Honorable John Thomas Fervier of Gwinnett County, Georgia is
hereby appointed as chairperson of the State Election Board.

IT IS FURTHER
That Mr. Fervier shall serve as chairperson until the nomination and

election of a chairperson during the next regular session of the Georgia
General Assembly.

This 5% day of January 2024.
