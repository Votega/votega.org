THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Nicholas Francis Masino of
Gwinnett County, Georgia, is appointed to serve
as a member of the Lake Lanier Islands
Development Authority, for a term of office

Honorable Terri E. Jondahl, whose term expired
January 1, 2024.
