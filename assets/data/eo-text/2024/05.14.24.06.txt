EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Narasimhulu Neelagaru of
Dekalb County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as
the Fifth Congressional District Representative
on the Georgia Student Finance Commission
Board of Commissioners, for a term of office
expiring April 1, 2029, to succeed the Honorable
David Perez, who now serves as the Sixth
Congressional District Representative on Georgia
Student Finance Commission Board of
Commissioners, as a result of the Georgia House
of Representatives Redistricting Act of 2023,
adopted December 8, 2023.

This 14t day of May 2024

Bill

GOVERNOR
