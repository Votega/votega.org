The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 5,804.47 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) in Morgan County, Georgia filed with the
State Properties Commission Real Property Record as Property BLLIP ID# 51522;
and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Natural Resources and is
commonly known as Hard Labor Creek State Park; and

The Property is improved with a structure identified as “Rutledge Beach House”
(BLLIP ID# 29775), (hereinafter, the “Structure”), as shown in the aerial
photograph attached hereto as Exhibit A; and

The Department of Natural Resources vacated the Structure in 2022 as a result of
storm damage and repairs were deemed to be cost prohibitive to maintain a
structurally sound facility due to plumbing, roof, siding, and ADA compliance issues;
and

By resolution dated March 26, 2024, the Department of Natural Resources requested
authority to demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Natural Resources is authorized and directed to provide for the
razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia, in accordance with
applicable laws, policies, and procedures, including any requirements under 0.C.G.A
12-3-55 regarding historic properties that may pertain to the Structure.
