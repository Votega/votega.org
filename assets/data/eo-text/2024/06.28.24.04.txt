THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED:

There currently exists at Columbus State University, Muscogee County,
Columbus, Georgia, a building known as Woodall Hall (the “Building”,
located at 4225 University Avenue; and

The Board of Regents, at its meeting on June 14, 2024, declared that said
Building could no longer be advantageously used by Columbus State
University or the University System of Georgia and should be removed.

NOW, THEREFORE, BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia is hereby
authorized to demolish and remove the Building from Columbus State
University, Muscogee County, Columbus, Georgia.

IT IS FURTHER

That the appropriate staff of Columbus State University, acting under the
approval of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, are
hereby authorized to demolish the Building and to have all building
materials removed from the premises by public works contract; all said
demolition and removal to be performed as expeditiously as possible.

Bill

GOVERNOR

This Ze day of June 2024.
