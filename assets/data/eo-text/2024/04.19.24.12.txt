THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable T. Dallas Smith of Dekalb
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Fifth Congressional District Representative on
the Board of Regents of the University System
of Georgia, for a term of office expiring
January 1, 2031, to succeed the Honorable
Sarah-Elizabeth Langford, whose term expired
January 1, 2024.

This 19 day of April 2024

GOVERNOR
