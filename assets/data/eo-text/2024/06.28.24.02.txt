THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED:

There currently exist at Albany State University, Dougherty County, Albany,
Georgia, buildings known as Wiley Hall, Gibson Hall, Simmons (RHS), and
the ROTC/Military Science Building, (collectively, the “Buildings”), located
at 504 College Drive; and

The Board of Regents, at its meeting on June 14, 2024, declared that said
Buildings could no longer be advantageously used by Albany State
University or the University System of Georgia and should be removed.

NOW, THEREFORE BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia is hereby
authorized to demolish and remove the Buildings from Albany State
University, Dougherty County, Albany, Georgia.

IT IS FURTHER

That the appropriate staff of Albany State University, acting under the
approval of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, are
hereby authorized to demolish the Buildings and to have all building
materials removed from the premises by public works contract; all said
demolition and removal to be performed as expeditiously as possible.

Bil he

GOVERNOR

This 20 day of June 2024.
