THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Frank Turner, Jr. of Newton
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Thirteenth Congressional District Representative
on the Board of Community Affairs, for a term of
office expiring July 1, 2028, to succeed the
Honorable Vince Williams, who is now an At Large
Representative on the Board of Community
Affairs, as a result of the Georgia House of
Representatives Redistricting Act of 2023, adopted
December 8, 2023.

This 31st day of January 2024
