THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF STAFF

Staff Sergeant William Jerome Rivers, 46, of Carrollton, Georgia, honorably
served our country in the United States Army Reserve; and

Staff Sergeant Rivers and two other soldiers were tragically killed in a drone
attack at a base in Jordan on January 28, 2024; and

Staff Sergeant Rivers enlisted in the U.S. Army Reserve in August 2011 as
an Interior Electrician, and earned many awards and decorations, including
a Purple Heart; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize the sacrifices made by Staff Sergeant
Rivers by lowering the flags on all State buildings and grounds to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF STAFF SERGEANT WILLIAM JEROME RIVERS, PURSUANT TO
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA,
IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be

flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds on Tuesday, February
13, 2024, the day of Staff Sergeant Rivers’ interment.

Bil

GOVERNOR
