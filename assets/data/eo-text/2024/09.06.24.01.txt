EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN GREENE AND MORGAN COUNTIES

WHEREAS:
WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Battalion Chief Chris Eddy with the Greene County Fire Rescue Department
was killed in the line of duty on Wednesday, September 4, 2024; and

Chief Eddy began his service with the Greene County Fire Rescue
Department in 2019 as a firefighter; and

Prior to his service with the Greene County Fire Rescue, Chief Eddy was
dedicated to firefighting by serving as a volunteer with the Woodville
Volunteer Fire Department and Fire Station 4 in Buckhead, as a part-time
firefighter in Morgan County, and as a paid firefighter with the Putnam
County Fire Department and the Old Salem Fire Rescue Department; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Chief Eddy’s service to Greene County
and the State of Georgia by lowering the flags on the State Capitol building
and in Greene and Morgan Counties to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF BATTALION CHIEF CHRIS EDDY, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be
flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Greene and Morgan
Counties on Sunday, September 8, 2024, the day of Battalion Chief Eddy’s

interment, until sunset.
e
e

GOVERNOR

This 6th day of September 2024.
