EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Laura Valentina Montoya
of Hall County, Georgia, is appointed to serve
as a member of the State Board of
Registration of Used Motor Vehicle Dealers
and Used Motor Vehicle Parts Dealers, for a
term of office expiring June 30, 2025, to
succeed the Honorable Tyler Marion Wood,
who now serves as a public member on the
State Board of Registration of Used Motor
Vehicle Dealers and Used Motor Vehicle
Parts Dealers.

This 5 day of March 2024

Bill

GOVERNOR
