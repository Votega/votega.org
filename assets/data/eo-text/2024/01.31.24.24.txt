EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Timothy Perryman of Forsyth
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as an At
Large Representative on the State Board of the
Technical College System of Georgia, for a term of
office expiring June 30, 2028, to succeed the
Honorable Corey Lebron Ferguson, who is now the
Sixth Congressional District Representative on the
State Board of the Technical College System of
Georgia, as a result of the Georgia House of
Representatives Redistricting Act of 2023, adopted
December 8, 2023.

This 31st day of January 2024

Bh h-

GOVERNOR
