EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Randy L. Dellinger of Gwinnett
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Thirteenth Congressional District Representative on
the Board of Natural Resources, for a term of office
expiring December 12, 2029, to succeed the
Honorable Charles Kim DePriest, who is now an At
Large Representative on the Board of Natural
Resources, as a result of the Georgia House of
Representatives Redistricting Act of 2023, adopted
December 8, 2023.

This 6 day of February 2024

Bil

GOVERNOR
