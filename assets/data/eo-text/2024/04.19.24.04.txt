EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Cassius F. Butts of Fulton County,
Georgia, is appointed to serve as an At-Large
Representative on the Board of Economic
Development, for a term of office expiring July 1, 2025,
to succeed the Honorable Mulham Shbeib, who is now
the Eleventh Congressional District Representative on
the Board of Economic Development, as a result of the
Georgia House of Representatives Redistricting Act of
2023, adopted December 8, 2023.

This 19 day of April 2024

Bil

GOVERNOR
