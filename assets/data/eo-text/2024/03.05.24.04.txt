The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 5 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) in Baldwin County, Georgia filed with
the State Properties Commission Real Property Record as Property BLLIP
ID# 68089; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Corrections and is
commonly known as the Central Region Office-Arnall Building tract; and

The Property is improved with a structure identified as “Arnall Building”
(BLLIP ID # 1263), totaling approximately 252,184 square feet
(hereinafter, collectively, the “Structure”), as shown in the aerial
photograph attached hereto as Exhibit A; and

By resolution dated December 7, 2023, the Board of Corrections requested
authority to demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Corrections is authorized and directed to provide for the
razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and
conditions which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia,
in accordance with applicable laws, policies, and procedures, including any
requirements under O.C.G.A. § 12-3-55 regarding historic properties that

may pertain to the Structure.
