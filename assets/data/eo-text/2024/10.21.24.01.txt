BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN HOUSTON COUNTY

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Sheriff Henry Cullen Talton, Jr. with the Houston County Sheriffs Office
passed away on Saturday, October 19, 2024; and

Sheriff Talton was the longest-tenured sheriff in America; and

Sheriff Talton was first elected as Houston County Sheriff in 1972, and was
committed to serving and protecting the people of his community for nearly
52 years; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Sheriff Talton’s service to Houston
County and the State of Georgia by lowering the flags on the State Capitol
building and in Houston County to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF SHERIFF HENRY CULLEN TALTON, JR., PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be

flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Houston County on
Wednesday, October 23, 2024, the day of Sheriff Talton’s interment, until

5h 1-

" GOVERNOR

This 21st day of October 2024.
