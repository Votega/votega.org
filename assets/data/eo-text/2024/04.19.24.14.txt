EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable David B. Dove of Cobb
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Eleventh Congressional District Representative
on the Board of Regents of the University
System of Georgia, for a term of office expiring
January 1, 2031, to succeed the Honorable
Thomas Lane Bradbury, who is now the Sixth
Congressional District Representative on the
Board of Regents of the University System of
Georgia, as a result of the Georgia House of
Representatives Redistricting Act of 2023,
adopted December 8, 2023.
