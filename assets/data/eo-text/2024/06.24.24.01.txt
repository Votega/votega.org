EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On June 3, 2024, Mr. Elvis Davis, a member of the Dodge County Board of
Education, was indicted on two counts of Child Molestation and two counts
of Sexual Contact by a Foster Parent in the first degree; and

A certified copy of the indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant
to Code Section 45-5-6(b), and received on June 6, 2024; and

The fourteen-day period following receipt of the indictment as prescribed
by Code Section 45-5-6(e) has passed.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Ms. Pamela Brown of the Rockdale
County Board of Education, and Mr. David Chastain of the Cobb County
Board of Education are hereby appointed as the review commission to
determine whether the above-mentioned indictment relates to and
adversely affects the administration of duties by Mr. Davis such that the
rights and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby, and to make
a written report to me within fourteen days with a recommendation as to
whether Mr. Davis should be suspended from office as required by Code

Section 45-5-6(c).
