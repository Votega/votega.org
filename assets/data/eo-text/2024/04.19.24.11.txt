EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Thomas Lane Bradbury of
Cobb County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as
the Sixth Congressional District Representative
on the Board of Regents of the University
System of Georgia, for a term of office expiring
January 1, 2029, to succeed the Honorable
Richard T. Evans Sr., who is now the Seventh
Congressional District Representative on the
Board of Regents, as a result of the Georgia
House of Representatives Redistricting Act of
2023, adopted December 8, 2023.

This 19t day of April 2024

Bill

GOVERNOR
