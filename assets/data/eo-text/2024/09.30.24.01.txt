ORDERED:

On September 24, 2024, due to the potential negative impacts of
Tropical Storm Helene on the State of Georgia, I issued Executive
Order No. 09.24.24.01, declaring a State of Emergency for Tropical
Storm Helene; and

Executive Order 09.24.24.01 authorized a call of up to five hundred
(500) Georgia National Guard troops for state active duty to be used
in response to this State of Emergency; and

On September 27, 2024, due to need for additional support in the
wake of the storm’s impact across Georgia, I issued Executive Order
No. 09.24.24.01, authorizing an additional one thousand (1,000)
Georgia National Guard troops to be called up for state active duty to
be used in response to this State of Emergency; and

Continued assistance from the Georgia National Guard is necessary
to ensure the necessary response and recovery efforts of the State
from the negative impacts of Helene; and

Georgia law vests the Governor in Code Section 38-3-51 with the
authority to bring emergency situations under control by issuing
orders, rules, and regulations to protect the safety and welfare of the
public.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 38-3-51, AND
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Georgia Department of Defense provide an additional one
thousand (1,000) Georgia National Guard troops to be used in
response to the State of Emergency for Tropical Storm Helene,
bringing the total authorization to two thousand five hundred
(2,500) troops.

ORDERED:

ORDERED:

ORDERED:

IT IS FURTHER

That these Georgia National Guard troops be called up to State Active
Duty as necessary by the Adjutant General at the request of the
Director of the Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland
Security Agency.

IT IS FURTHER

That if one or more of the provisions contained in this Order shall
be held to be invalid, in violation of the Georgia Constitution, in
violation of Georgia law, or unenforceable in any respect, such
invalidity, violation, or unenforceability shall not affect any other
provisions of this Order, but, in such case, this Order shall be
construed as if such invalid, illegal, or unenforceable provision
had not been included in the Order.

IT IS FURTHER

That all provisions of this Order shall remain effective until the State
of Emergency for Tropical Storm Helene declared by Executive
Order 09.24.24.01 is terminated or ceases to be renewed by the
Governor or until this Order is otherwise terminated via subsequent
Executive Order.

This 30 day of September 2024.

Bh

GOVERNOR
