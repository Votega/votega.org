THE STATE OF GEORGIA
                                      EXECUTIVE ORDER


BY THE GOVERNOR:


                 STATE OF EMERGENCY FOR THE PASSING OF PRESIDENT CARTER


   WHEREAS: The nation and our state lost a great leader with the passing of President James
            Earl Carter, Jr. on December 29, 2024; and

   WHEREAS: President Carter’s final farewell will attract visitors, domestically and
            internationally, wishing to pay their respects and mourn with other likeminded
            individuals; and

   WHEREAS: President Carter’s hometown of Plains, Georgia is a rural community and the
            number of expected visitors during President Carter’s final farewell will exceed the
            management capacity of the local governments; and

   WHEREAS: The influx of persons to Plains, Georgia, and the surrounding communities, if not
            carefully curated with additional transportation and public safety personnel, will
            impact the citizens of Georgia living in or traveling through the area; and

   WHEREAS: Code Section 38-3-51 vests the Governor with the authority to issue orders, rules,
            and regulations to protect the safety and welfare of the public in certain situations.

                NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 38-3-51, AND THE AUTHORITY
                VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

   ORDERED:     That all resources of the state be made available to assist with the final farewell and
                interment of President Carter.

                IT IS FURTHER

   ORDERED:     That a State of Emergency exists in Sumter County for the limited purpose of
                allowing state resources to meet the personnel and logistical challenges presented
                in executing President Carter’s final farewell and interment.
           IT IS FURTHER

ORDERED:   That the Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland Security Agency activate
           the State Operations Center to coordinate all state assets assisting with the final
           farewell and interment of President Carter.

           IT IS FURTHER

ORDERED:   That the State of Emergency portion of this Executive Order shall be valid for a
           period of fourteen (14) days, beginning on December 29, 2024, and ending at 11:59
           pm on January 12, 2025.

           This 29th day of December 2024.
