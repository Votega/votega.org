ORDERED:

On August 14, 2024, Mr. Benjamin Cary Cranford, Jr., Mayor of Thomson,
Georgia, was indicted on one count of Furnishing Prohibited Items to Inmates and
one count of Criminal Attempt to Commit a Felony; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to Code Section
45-5-6(b), and the certified copy was received on August 20, 2024; and

An Executive Order was issued on September 9, 2024, appointing members to the
Review Commission to determine whether the indictment relates to and adversely
affects the administration of duties by Mayor Cranford; and

Additional time was granted for the Review Commission to make its written report
pursuant to Code Section 45-5-6(c); and

By report received October 1, 2024, the Review Commission found that the
indictment of Mr. Benjamin Cary Cranford, Jr. does relate to and does adversely
affect the administration of duties by Mayor Cranford, and that the rights and
interests of the public are adversely affected thereby; and recommended that Mr.
Benjamin Cary Cranford, Jr. be suspended from office pursuant to the provisions
of Code Section 45-5-6.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Mr. Benjamin Cary Cranford, Jr. is hereby suspended from office immediately

and without further action pending the final disposition of the case or until the
expiration of his term of office, whichever occurs first, as provided in Code Section
