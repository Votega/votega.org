THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On June 3, 2024, I appointed a committee consisting of Attorney
General Christopher Carr, Sheriff Robert Markley of Morgan County,
and Sheriff Janis Mangum of Jackson County to investigate Sheriff
Chad Nichols of Rabun County after he was arrested by the Georgia
Bureau of Investigation and charged with one count of Violation of
Oath by Public Officer, one count of Sexual Battery, and one count of
Public Indecency, by issuing Executive Order 06.03.24.01; and

On June 14, 2024, the committee delivered its recommendation to
me that Sheriff Nichols be suspended pursuant to Code Section 15-
16-26.

NOw, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 15-16-26, AND
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Sheriff Nichols is hereby suspended from the Office of Sheriff of
Rabun County for a period of sixty (60) days, effective immediately.

Bil he

GOVERNOR

This 14th day of June 2024.
