EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On August 14, 2024, Mr. Chad Nichols, Sheriff of Rabun County, was
indicted on one count of Violation of Oath by Public Officer; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to Code Section
45-5-6(b), and the certified copy was received on August 15, 2024; and

The fourteen-day period following receipt of the indictment as prescribed
by Code Section 45-5-6(e) has passed.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Sheriff Robert Markley of Morgan
County, and Sheriff Janis Mangum of Jackson County are hereby appointed
as the review commission to determine whether the above-mentioned
indictment relates to and adversely affects the administration of the Office
of the Rabun County Sheriff and that the rights and interests of the public
are adversely affected thereby, and to make a written report to me within
fourteen days with a recommendation as to whether Sheriff Nichols should
be suspended from office as required by Code Section 45-5-6(c).
