EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

‘WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

Mr. Reginald “Reggie” Loper, District Four Commissioner of the
Effingham County Board of Commissioners, was indicted on March
18, 2024, on one count of Sexual Battery Against a Child Under 16
and one count of Sexual Battery; and

A certified copy of the indictment was transmitted to the Governor
pursuant to Code Section 45-5-6(b), and received on March 20,
2024; and

Pursuant to Code Section 45-5-6(e), Mr. Loper has voluntarily
authorized his suspension from office as District Four Commissioner
of the Effingham County Board of Commissioners.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mr. Reginald “Reggie” Loper, District Four Commissioner of
the Effingham County Board of Commissioners, is hereby suspended
from office immediately and without further action pending the final
disposition of the case or until the expiration of his term of office,
whichever occurs first, as provided in Code Section 45-5-6(c).
