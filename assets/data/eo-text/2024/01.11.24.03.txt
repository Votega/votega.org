THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That the Honorable Mark A. Hendrix is hereby appointed
Superior Court Judge of the Atlantic Judicial Circuit to fill the
seat created by the enactment of Senate Bill 66 (Act Number
76) during the 2023 session of the Georgia General Assembly,
for a term of office ending December 31, 2026.
