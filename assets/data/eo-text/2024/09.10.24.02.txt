EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On August 14, 2024, Mr. Chad Nichols, Sheriff of Rabun County, was
indicted on one count of Violation of Oath by Public Officer; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to Code Section
45-5-6(b), and the certified copy was received on August 15, 2024; and

An Executive Order was issued on September 3, 2024, appointing members
to the Review Commission to determine whether the indictment relates to
and adversely affects the administration of the Office of the Rabun County
Sheriff such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected;
and

By report dated September 9, 2024, the Review Commission found that the
indictment of Mr. Chad Nichols does relate to and does adversely affect the
administration of the Office of the Rabun County Sheriff, and that the rights
and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby; and
recommended that Sheriff Nichols be suspended from office pursuant to the
provisions of Code Section 45-5-6.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6 AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Mr. Chad Nichols is hereby suspended from office immediately and

without further action pending the final disposition of the case or until the
expiration of his term of office, whichever occurs first, as provided in Code

Section 45-5-6(c).
