THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

National Fentanyl Prevention Awareness Day is recognized annually on
August 21st; and

Fentanyl-laced opioids and counterfeit pills, which can look identical to
safe prescription medications, constitute a growing threat to young
people; and

Overdose deaths among adolescents 10-19 years old across the country
more than doubled between 2019 and 2021; and

In recognition of National Fentanyl Prevention Awareness Day, August
21, 2024, to remember those lost to fentanyl overdose, show support for
grieving families, and increase awareness about the risks of fentanyl, it
is fitting and proper to lower the flags on all State buildings and grounds
to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN MEMORY OF THOSE LOST TO FENTANYL
OVERDOSE, AND PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States of America and the flag of the great State

of Georgia be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds on
Wednesday, August 21, 2024, until sunset.

Bil

This 20th day of August 2024.

GOVERNOR
