EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Sandra Veronica Mullings of
Clayton County, Georgia, is appointed to serve
as a member of the Georgia Composite Board of
Professional Counselors, Social Workers, and
Marriage and Family Therapists, for a term of
office expiring January 31, 2027, to succeed the
Honorable Andrew Paul Carrier, whose term
expired December 31, 2023.
