THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Scotty Eugene Hancock of
Floyd County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as
the Fourteenth Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Juvenile Justice,
for a term of office expiring July 6, 2026, to
succeed the Honorable Timothy Dewayne
McCulley, who is now the Eleventh
Congressional District Representative on the
Board of Juvenile Justice, as a result of the
Georgia House of Representatives Redistricting
Act of 2023, adopted December 8, 2023.

This 6t day of February 2024

Bil

GOVERNOR
