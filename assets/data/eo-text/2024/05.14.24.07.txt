EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable John David Loud of Cobb
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Eleventh Congressional District Representative
on the Georgia Student Finance Commission
Board of Commissioners, for a term of office
expiring June 1, 2025, to succeed the Honorable
C. Scott Johnson, who no longer represents the
Eleventh Congressional District on the Georgia
Student Finance Commission Board of
Commissioners, as a result of the Georgia House
of Representatives Redistricting Act of 2023,
adopted December 8, 2023.
