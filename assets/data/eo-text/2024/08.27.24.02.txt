EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN CARROLL COUNTY

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Investigator Taylor Jaimeson Bristow, 30, a deputy and investigator
for the Carroll County Sheriffs Office, on Friday, August 23, 2024,
succumbed to injuries received in the line of duty; and

Investigator Bristow had served with the Carroll County Sheriff's
Office for six years; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Investigator Bristow’s service to
Carroll County and the State of Georgia by lowering the flags on the
State Capitol building and in Carroll County to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF INVESTIGATOR TAYLOR JAIMESON BRISTOW,
PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia
be flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Carroll
County on Thursday, August 29, 2024, the day of Investigator
Bristow’s funeral services, until sunset.

GOVERNOR

This 27 day of August 2024.
