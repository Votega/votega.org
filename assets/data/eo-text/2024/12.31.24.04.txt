EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Delos Harley Yancey, IV of Floyd
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a member
of the North Georgia Mountains Authority, for a term
of office expiring December 31, 2028, to succeed the
Honorable Ray Lambert, Jr., whose term expired
December 11, 2024.

This 31st day of December 2024

Bh he

GOVERNOR
