The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 2.9 acres of improved real property
(hereinafter the “Property”) in Franklin County, Georgia filed with the State
Properties Commission Real Property Record as Property BLLIP ID# 004431; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Georgia Forestry Commission and is commonly
known as the Franklin County Unit; and

The Property is improved with a structure identified as “Foresters’ Office” (BLLIP
ID# 36163), (hereinafter, the “Structure”), as shown in the aerial photograph
attached hereto as Exhibit A; and

The Georgia Forestry Commission vacated the Structure in 2019 and repairs were
deemed cost prohibitive to maintain a structurally sound facility due to moisture
issues; and

By resolution dated February 7, 2022, the Board of Commissioners of the Georgia
Forestry Commission requested authority to demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Georgia Forestry Commission is authorized and directed to provide for the
razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia, in accordance with
applicable laws, policies, and procedures, including any requirements under O.C.G.A.
§ 12-3-55 regarding historic properties that may pertain to the Structure.
