THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS: Pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 38-5-152, an Assistant Adjutant General for
Army of Georgia is to be appointed by and shall serve at the pleasure
of the Governor.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

ORDERED: That effective May 3, 2024, Brigadier General Jason W. Fryman of

Cherokee County, Georgia, is hereby appointed to serve as Assistant
Adjutant General for Army for the Georgia Army National Guard.
