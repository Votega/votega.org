ORDERED:

On June 3, 2024, Mr. Elvis Davis, a member of the Dodge County Board of
Education, was indicted on two counts of Child Molestation and two counts
of Sexual Contact by a Foster Parent in the first degree; and

A certified copy of the indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant
to Code Section 45-5-6(b), and received on June 6, 2024; and

An Executive Order was issued on June 24, 2024, appointing members to
the Review Commission to determine whether the indictment relates to and
adversely affects the administration of the Office of the Dodge County Board
of Education; and

By report dated July 1, 2024, the Review Commission found that the
indictment of Mr. Elvis Davis does relate to and does adversely affect the
administration of the Office of the Dodge County Board of Education, and
that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby; and
recommended that Mr. Elvis Davis be suspended from office pursuant to
the provisions of Code Section 45-5-6.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Mr. Elvis Davis is hereby suspended from office immediately and
without further action pending the final disposition of the case or until the
expiration of his term of office, whichever occurs first, as provided in Code

Section 45-5-6(c).
