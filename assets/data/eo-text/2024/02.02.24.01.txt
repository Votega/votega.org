THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

‘WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

The State of Georgia mourns the loss of State Representative Richard
Smith who passed away on January 30, 2024; and

Representative Smith was first elected to the Georgia House of
Representatives in November 2004, where he honorably served the
citizens of the 139th District, and held the role of chairman of the
House Rules Committee since 2020; and

Richard Smith was a longtime public servant, valued friend, and
beloved husband, father, and grandfather.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF REPRESENTATIVE RICHARD SMITH, PURSUANT TO
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the state of Georgia
shall be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds
throughout Georgia on Monday, February 5, 2024, the day of
Representative Smith’s interment.

This 2"¢ day of February 2024.

Bil

~ GOVERNOR
