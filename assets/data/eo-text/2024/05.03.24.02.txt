THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS: Pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 38-2-150, an Adjutant General of Georgia is
to be appointed by and shall serve at the pleasure of the governor.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

ORDERED: That effective May 4, 2024, Major General Richard D. Wilson of
Cherokee County, Georgia, is hereby appointed to serve as Adjutant

General of Georgia to serve a term of office concurrent with the
Governor.

This 3" day of May 2024.
