THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED: That the Honorable Kwanza Hall of Fulton
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Fifth Congressional District Representative
on the Board of Community Affairs, for a
term of office expiring July 1, 2025, to
succeed the Honorable David James Burge,
who is now the Sixth Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Community
Affairs, as a result of the Georgia House of
Representatives Redistricting Act of 2023,
adopted December 8, 2023.

This 31st day of January 2024

Bil

GOVERNOR
