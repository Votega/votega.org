THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED:

There currently exists at South Georgia State College, Coffee County,
Douglas, Georgia, a building known as Floyd Hall (the “Building”), located
at 100 West College Park Drive; and

The Board of Regents, at its meeting on June 14, 2024, declared that said
Building could no longer be advantageously used by South Georgia State
College or the University System of Georgia and should be removed.

NOW, THEREFORE, BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia is hereby
authorized to demolish and remove the Building from South Georgia State
College, Coffee County, Douglas, Georgia.

IT IS FURTHER

That the appropriate staff of South Georgia State College, acting under the
approval of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, are
hereby authorized to demolish the Building and to have all building
materials removed from the premises by public works contract; all said
demolition and removal to be performed as expeditiously as possible.

Bill

GOVERNOR

This Zor day of June 2024.
