THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Colt Douglas Chambers of
Polk County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as
the chairperson of the Georgia Council for the
Arts, for a term of office expiring June 30, 2025,
to succeed the Honorable Gladys P. Wyant,
whose term expired June 30, 2024.

This 30 day of June 2024

Bill

GOVERNOR
