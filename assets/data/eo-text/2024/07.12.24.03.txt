Senate Bill 333 was passed by the Georgia General Assembly during the
2024 Legislative Session and signed into law on February 13, 2024; and

Senate Bill 333 provided for the incorporation of the City of Mulberry in
Gwinnett County, Georgia, if approved by referendum; and

During the May 2024 General Primary, Nonpartisan Election, 56.96% of
voters approved the referendum to incorporate of the City of Mulberry; and

Pursuant to Code Section 36-31-8(g), “the Governor is authorized to
appoint five persons to serve as interim representatives of [a] newly
incorporated municipality until the election of the municipality’s first
governing authority”; and

Code Section 36-31-8(g) provides that the function of such interim
representatives “shall be to facilitate the provision of municipal services and
facilities, the collection of taxes and fees, and the negotiation of
intergovernmental agreements in preparation of the establishment of the
new municipality.”

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 36-31-8 AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY
