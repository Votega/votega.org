ORDERED:

On October 9, 2024, the Muscogee County Board of Elections and
Registration filed a Verified Petition seeking declaratory and injunctive
relief (the “Petition”) against the Georgia State Election Board in the
Superior Court of Muscogee County, civil action file mumber
SU2024CV002288; and

The Georgia Department of Law declined representation of the Georgia
State Election Board due to a conflict of interest; and

The Petition seeks equitable relief against the Georgia State Election
Board; and

Mr. William “Chip” Collins, Jr. of Burr & Forman LLP is an attorney duly
admitted to the State Bar of Georgia; and

Mr. Collins has agreed to represent the Georgia State Election Board
pursuant to Code Section 45-15-70 at a rate mutually agreed upon with the
Office of the Governor.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-15-70 AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Mr. William “Chip” Collins, Jr. of Burr & Forman LLP is hereby

appointed as Special Counsel to assume control and responsibility for the
representation of the Georgia State Election Board in all matters related to

the Petition.
