EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Maureen Elizabeth Meyers
of Dekalb County, Georgia, is appointed to

Indian Concerns, for a term of office expiring

Alice Fazlollah Gooding, whose term expired
October 28, 2023.
