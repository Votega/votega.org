ATTEST:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 0.017 of an acre of improved real
property located in the 1825" District, Spalding County (“Property”) as shown on the
survey attached hereto as Exhibit “A”; and

Said Property is a portion of the 2.287 acres of real property located in the 1825"
District, Spalding County, 427 North Expressway, City of Griffin, acquired by virtue
of a Quitclaim Deed dated December 30, 2019, being recorded in Deed Book 4577,
Pages 132-136 in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Spalding County, and on
file with the State Properties Commission Real Property Records as RPR # 012358,
which Property is under the custody of the Technical College System of Georgia for
the benefit of Southern Crescent Technical College; and

By Official Action dated December 7, 2023, the Technical College System of
Georgia declared the Property surplus; and

By Official Action dated April 11, 2024, the Department of Public Safety requested
custody of the Property for the custody and control of a 200-foot communications
tower and the tower’s radio equipment shelter.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR
OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That custody of the above-defined Property located in Spalding County, Georgia is
hereby transferred from the Technical College System of Georgia to the Department
of Public Safety.

This OX aay of April 2024.

GOVERNOR
