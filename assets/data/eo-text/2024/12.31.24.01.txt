THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Charles Kim DePriest of Douglas

County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a member
of the North Georgia Mountains Authority, for a term
of office expiring December 31, 2028, to succeed the
Honorable Joe Hatfield, whose term expired
December 11, 2024.
