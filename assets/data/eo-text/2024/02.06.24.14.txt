THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Brent Davis Layton of Fulton
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the Fifth
Congressional District Representative on the
Board of Natural Resources, for a term of office
expiring January 1, 2025, to succeed the
Honorable Paul Shailendra, who is now the Sixth
Congressional District Representative on the
Board of Natural Resources, as a result of the
Georgia House of Representatives Redistricting Act
of 2023, adopted December 8, 2023.

This 6 day of February 2024

Bh

GOVERNOR
