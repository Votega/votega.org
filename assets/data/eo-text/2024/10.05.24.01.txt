THE STATE OF GEORGIA
                                    EXECUTIVE ORDER


BY THE GOVERNOR:

                        FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
                            ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

    WHEREAS: Nearly one year ago, on Saturday, October 7, 2023, during the Jewish holiday
             of Shemini Atzeret, militant Hamas terrorists launched a coordinated attack
             on the country of Israel by land, sea, and air, killing over 1,200 people; and

    WHEREAS: Among those killed were over 40 American citizens, with at least 7
             Americans still unaccounted for or being held hostage; and

    WHEREAS: As we observe the oneyear anniversary of this tragedy, the people of Georgia
             continue to mourn the American lives lost in these unwarranted attacks and
             join our close ally Israel in grieving all those who were taken from their
             families or injured; and

    WHEREAS: It is appropriate that the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of
             Georgia be flown at half-staff in memory of those killed during these brutal
             assaults.

                 NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
                 MEMORY OF THOSE KILLED IN THESE UNWARRANTED ATTACKS, PURSUANT
                 TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
                 GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

    ORDERED:     That the flag of the United States of America and the flag of the State of
                 Georgia shall be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds on
                 Monday, October 7, 2024, until sunset.


                 This 5th day of October 2024.




                                                        ___________________________
                                                                 GOVERNOR
