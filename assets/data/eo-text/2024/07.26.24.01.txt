ORDERED:

On June 18, 2024, Ms. Stephanie Woodard, Solicitor General of Hall
County, was indicted by the Attorney General of Georgia on thirteen counts
of False Statements and Writings and eleven counts of Theft by Taking; and

On July 12, 2024, pursuant to Code Section 45-5-6(b), an Executive Order
was issued appointing members to a review commission charged with
determining whether the above-mentioned indictment relates to and
adversely affects the administration of duties by Ms. Woodard such that the
rights and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby; and

Code Section 45-5-6(c) provides that additional time may be granted by the
Governor for a review commission to make its written report; and

On July 22, 2024, the review commission requested a fourteen-day
extension for the commission to make its written report.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the time period for the above-mentioned review commission to make
its written report is extended to August 9, 2024.

Bille

GOVERNOR

This 26th day of July 2024.
