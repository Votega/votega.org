THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

Senate District 30 in the Georgia State Senate has become vacant due to the
resignation of Senator Mike Dugan.

Therefore, pursuant to GA. Const. Art. V, § II, { VIII and Code Section 21-
2-544, a writ of election is hereby issued to the Secretary of State for a
special election to be held on Tuesday, February 13, 2024, to fill the District
30 seat in the Georgia State Senate.
