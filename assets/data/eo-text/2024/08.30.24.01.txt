EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On August 1, 2024, Executive Order 08.01.24.02 was issued suspending
Mr. Marcello Banes, Chairman of the Newton County Board of
Commissioners, from office; and

Pursuant to Code Section 45-5-6(d)(1), there being no general or local
law governing the appointment of a replacement officer, the Governor is
authorized to appoint, for the duration of the suspension, a replacement
for the Newton County Board of Commissioners Chairman.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA,
IT IS HEREBY

That the Honorable Lanier Sims of Newton County, Georgia, is hereby

appointed to fill the temporary vacancy in the office of the Chairman of
the Newton County Board of Commissioners.
