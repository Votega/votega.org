EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Donna Endsley Real of
Dekalb County, Georgia, is appointed to serve
as a member of the Georgia Composite Board of
Professional Counselors, Social Workers, and
Marriage and Family Therapists, for a term of
office expiring January 31, 2027, to succeed the
Honorable Kay Gresham, whose term expired
April 30, 2022.

This 31st day of January 2024

— BRL

GOVERNOR
