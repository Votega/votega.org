EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable David “Penn” Pendleton Hodge
of Fulton County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as
the Seventh Congressional District Representative
on the Board of Natural Resources, for a term of
office expiring January 1, 2028, to succeed the
Honorable Randy L. Dellinger, who is now the
Thirteenth Congressional District Representative on
the Board of Natural Resources, as a result of the

Georgia House of Representatives Redistricting Act
of 2023, adopted December 8, 2023.

This 6 day of February 2024

Bh

GOVERNOR
