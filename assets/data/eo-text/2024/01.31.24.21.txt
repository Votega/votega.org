THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Tung Quang Le of Gwinnett
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Fourth Congressional District Representative on
the Board of Community Affairs, for a term of office
expiring July 1, 2028, to succeed the Honorable
Frank Turner, Jr., who is now the Thirteenth
Congressional District Representative on the Board
of Community Affairs, as a result of the Georgia
House of Representatives Redistricting Act of 2023,
adopted December 8, 2023.
