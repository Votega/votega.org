ORDERED:

On March 11, 2024, Mr. Brandon Holt, Mayor of Pineview, Georgia, was
indicted on multiple felony counts of Theft by Taking; and

A certified copy of the indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant
to Code Section 45-5-6(b), and received on March 13, 2024;

The fourteen-day period following receipt of the indictment as prescribed
by Code Section 45-5-6(e) has passed.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Attorney General Chris Carr, Mayor Lester Miller of Macon-Bibb, and
Mayor LaRhonda Patrick of Warner Robins are hereby appointed as the
review commission to determine whether the above-mentioned indictment
relates to and adversely affects the administration of duties by Mayor Holt
such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected
thereby, and to make a written report to me within fourteen (14) days with
a recommendation as to whether Mayor Holt should be suspended from

office as required by Code Section 45-5-6(c).
