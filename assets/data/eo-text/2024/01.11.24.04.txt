EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That the Honorable C. Jephson Bendinger is hereby
appointed Superior Court Judge of the Coweta Judicial Circuit
to fill the seat created by the enactment of House Bill 243 (Act
Number 62) during the 2023 session of the Georgia General
Assembly, for a term of office ending December 31, 2026.

THIS 117 DAY OF JANUARY 2024.
