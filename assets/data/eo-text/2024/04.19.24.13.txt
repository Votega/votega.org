EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Daniel Vincent Murphy of
Newton County, Georgia, is appointed to serve
as the Thirteenth Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Regents of the
University System of Georgia, for a term of office
expiring January 1, 2028, to succeed the
Honorable Douglas Ray Aldridge, Jr., who no
longer represents the Thirteenth Congressional
District on the Board of Regents of the
University System of Georgia, as a result of the
Georgia House of Representatives Redistricting
Act of 2023, adopted December 8, 2023.
