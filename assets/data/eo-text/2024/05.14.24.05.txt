THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable James C. Weidner of
Habersham County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as the Ninth Congressional District
Representative on the Georgia Student Finance
Commission Board of Commissioners, for a term
of office expiring December 12, 2028, to succeed
the Honorable Eric Burrell, who no longer
represents the Ninth Congressional District on
the Georgia Student Finance Commission Board
of Commissioners, as a result of the Georgia
House of Representatives Redistricting Act of
2023, adopted December 8, 2023.
