The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 1,175 acres of improved real property
(hereinafter the “Property”) in Taliaferro County, Georgia, acquired by virtue of
various deeds and filed in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Taliaferro
County, and filed with the State Properties Commission Real Property Record as
Property BLLIP ID# 51525; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Natural Resources and is
commonly known as A.H. Stephens State Park; and

The Property is improved with a structure identified as the “Group Shelter” (BLLIP
ID #12665) being approximately 1,865 square feet (hereinafter, collectively called,
the “Structure”), as shown in the aerial photograph and map attached hereto as
Exhibit “A”; and

Said Structure was condemned for structural integrity as the cost to repair and
bring up to ADA compliance was determined to cost more than it is worth; and

The Office of the State Archaeologist determined the Structure to be historic, and
Photographic Permanent Archival Record was completed, reviewed and approved
by Historic Preservation Division and will not impact the Georgia/National
Register of Historic Places; and

By resolution dated October 24, 2023, the Board of Natural Resources requested
authority to demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Natural Resources is authorized and directed to provide for the
razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia, in accordance with
applicable laws, policies, and procedures, including any requirements under O.C.G.A
12-3-55 regarding historic properties that may pertain to the Structure.

GOVERNOR
