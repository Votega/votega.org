THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Lee Chapman of Hall County,
Georgia, is appointed to serve as the Ninth
Congressional District Representative on the State
Board of the Technical College System of Georgia, for
a term of office expiring June 30, 2028, to succeed
the Honorable Daren Wayne Culbreath, who is now
an At Large Representative on the State Board of the
Technical College System of Georgia, as a result of
the Georgia House of Representatives Redistricting
Act of 2023, adopted December 8, 2023.

This 31st day of January 2024

Bil

GOVERNOR
