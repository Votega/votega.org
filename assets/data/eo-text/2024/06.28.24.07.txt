WHEREAS:

There currently exist at Georgia Southwestern State University, Sumter
County, Americus, Georgia, buildings known as Jordan Hall and Duncan
Hall (collectively, the “Buildings”), located at 800 GSW State University
Drive; and

The Board of Regents, at its meeting on June 14, 2024, declared that said
Buildings could no longer be advantageously used by Georgia Southwestern
State University or the University System of Georgia and should be
removed.

NOW, THEREFORE, BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia is hereby
authorized to demolish and remove the Buildings from Georgia
Southwestern State University, Sumter County, Americus, Georgia.

IT IS FURTHER

That the appropriate staff of Georgia Southwestern State University, acting
under the approval of the Board of Regents of the University System of
Georgia, are hereby authorized to demolish the Buildings and to have all
building materials removed from the premises by public works contract; all
said demolition and removal to be performed as expeditiously as possible.
