THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Benton Drew Ellenburg of
Fulton County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as
the Sixth Congressional District Representative
on the Board of Economic Development, for a
term of office expiring August 13, 2024, to
succeed the Honorable Ronald Garrard, who is
now the Seventh Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Economic
Development, as a result of the Georgia House of
Representatives Redistricting Act of 2023,
adopted December 8, 2023.

This 19 day of April 2024

Bil

GOVERNOR
