ORDERED:

THE STATE OF GEORGIA ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Trooper Jimmy Cenescar with the Georgia State Patrol was killed in the line
of duty on Sunday, January 28, 2024; and

Trooper Cenescar honorably served with the Atlanta Police Department
before joining the Georgia Department of Public Safety in January 2023 and
graduating with the 114'» Trooper School; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Trooper Cenescar’s service to the State
of Georgia by lowering the flags on the State Capitol building, in Fulton and
Gwinnett Counties, and at all Department of Public Safety locations
throughout the State of Georgia to half-staff.

NOw, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF TROOPER JIMMY CENESCAR, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be
flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building, in Fulton and Gwinnett
Counties, and at all Department of Public Safety locations throughout the
State of Georgia on Friday, February 9, 2024, the day of Trooper Cenescar’s
interment, until sunset.
