BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN MUSCOGEE COUNTY

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF STAFF

Colonel Ralph Puckett, Jr., U.S. Army (retired), of Columbus, Georgia,
honorably served our country in the United States Army; and

Colonel Puckett was the last living person to receive the Medal of Honor for
heroism during the Korean War; and

Colonel Puckett was a decorated war hero having received two
Distinguished Service Crosses, two Silver Stars, five Purple Hearts, and two
Bronze Star Medals with the “V” device for valor; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize the service Colonel Puckett by lowering
the flags on the State Capitol building and in Muscogee County to half-staff.

NOw, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF COLONEL RALPH PUCKETT, JR., PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be
flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Muscogee County on

Saturday, April 20, 2024, the day of the public ceremony to honor Colonel
Puckett.

This 18 day of April 2024.

Bh

GOVERNOR
