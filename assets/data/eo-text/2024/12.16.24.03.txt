EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That, effective January 1, 2025, the Honorable Sarah Stimac
Japour is hereby appointed District Attorney of the new West
Georgia Judicial Circuit created by the enactment of Senate
Bill 424 (Act Number 615) during the 2024 session of the
Georgia General Assembly, for a term of office ending
December 31, 2026.
