THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Edward Pease of Fulton
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Seventh Congressional District Representative on
the Georgia Student Finance Commission Board
of Commissioners, for a term of office expiring
June 1, 2025, to succeed the Honorable Linwood
H. Thompson, IV, who now serves as the Fourth
Congressional District Representative on Georgia
Student Finance Commission Board of
Commissioners, as a result of the Georgia House
of Representatives Redistricting Act of 2023,
adopted December 8, 2023.

This 14th day of May 2024
