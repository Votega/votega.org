THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED: That the Honorable Nancy Beach Denson of
Clarke County, Georgia, is appointed to serve
as a member of the State Ethics Commission,
for a term of office expiring July 14, 2025, to
succeed the Honorable Darryl Anthony Hicks,
who resigned.

This 1st day of August 2024
