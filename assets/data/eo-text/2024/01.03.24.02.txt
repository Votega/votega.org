EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN SPALDING COUNTY

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Sergeant Mare Andrew McIntyre, 55, with the Spalding County
Sheriff's Office was killed in the line of duty on Friday, December 29,
2023; and

Sergeant McIntyre was a veteran of the U.S. Army, and served with
the Spalding County Sheriff's Office for eleven years; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Sergeant MclIntyre’s service to
Spalding County, the State of Georgia, and the United States of
America by lowering the flags on the State Capitol building and in
Spalding County to half-staff.

NOw, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF SERGEANT MARC ANDREW MCINTYRE,
PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia
be flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Spalding
County on Friday, January 5, 2024, the day of Sergeant McIntyre’s
interment, until sunset.

This 3" day of January 2024.
