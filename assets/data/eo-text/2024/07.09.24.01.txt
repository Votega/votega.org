THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That the Honorable John Blake Cunningham is hereby
appointed Judge of the State Court of Coweta County to fill
the seat created by the enactment of House Bill 615 (Act
Number 157) during the 2023 session of the Georgia General
Assembly, for a term of office ending December 31, 2026.
