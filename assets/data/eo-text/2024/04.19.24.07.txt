EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable David Richard Tyndall of
Fulton County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as
the Fifth Congressional District Representative
on the Board of Economic Development, for a
term of office expiring July 1, 2024, to succeed
the Honorable Benton Drew Ellenburg, who now
serves as the Sixth Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Economic
Development, as a result of the Georgia House of
Representatives Redistricting Act of 2023,
adopted December 8, 2023,

This 19 day of April 2024

Bh

GOVERNOR
