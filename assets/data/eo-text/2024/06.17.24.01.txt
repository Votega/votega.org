EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ATTEST

During the 2024 legislative session, the Georgia General Assembly passed
House Bill 1267 which provides, in part, that the Georgia State-wide
Business Court assignment for administrative purposes shall be transferred
from the Court of Appeals to the Administrative Office of the Courts; and

House Bill 916, the Appropriations Act for Fiscal Year 2025, enacted as Act
Number 709, provides for appropriations for the Georgia State-wide
Business Court through the Court of Appeals.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-12-73 AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS

HEREBY

That effective July 1, 2024, the Governor’s Office of Planning and Budget
shall approve any budgetary amendments submitted by the Court of
Appeals or Administrative Office of the Courts to reflect the State-wide
Business Court as an agency attached for administrative purposes only to
the Judicial Council, consistent with Code Section 15-54-16, as amended by
House Bill 1267, enacted as Act Number 601.
