FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF STAFF

Sergeant Kennedy Landon Sanders, 24, of Waycross, Georgia, honorably
served our country in the United States Army Reserve; and

Sergeant Sanders and two other soldiers were tragically killed in a drone
attack at a base in Jordan on January 28, 2024; and

Sergeant Sanders enlisted in the U.S. Army Reserve in 2019 as a Horizontal
Construction Engineer; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize the sacrifices made by Sergeant Sanders
by lowering the flags on all State buildings and grounds to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF SERGEANT KENNEDY LANDON SANDERS, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be
flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds on Saturday, February
17, 2024, the day of Sergeant Sanders’ interment.
