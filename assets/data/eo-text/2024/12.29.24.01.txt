THE STATE OF GEORGIA
                             EXECUTIVE ORDER


BY THE GOVERNOR:


                   FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
                       ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF


     WHEREAS:      Today we celebrate the life of the late James E. “Jimmy” Carter, Jr.,
                   former Governor of Georgia, and President of the United States from
                   1977 to 1981; and

     WHEREAS:      A man from humble roots, Jimmy Carter was born in Plains, Georgia,
                   as the oldest sibling of three children to James and Bessie Carter,
                   owners of a local peanut farm, warehouse, and store in the
                   surrounding community; and

     WHEREAS:      As a young man, Jimmy Carter was admitted to the Naval Academy
                   in Annapolis, Maryland, where he excelled academically and
                   eventually earned his bachelor’s degree. He subsequently served
                   seven years as a naval officer, rising to the rank of Lieutenant; and

     WHEREAS:      After his father’s death, Carter resigned his commission as an officer
                   in the armed forces to return to his childhood home and manage the
                   family business, becoming a successful peanut farmer in South
                   Georgia and well-respected member of his community and church;
                   and

     WHEREAS:      His desire to serve others and love of politics inspired Jimmy Carter
                   to successfully run for state office as a two-term Georgia State
                   Senator; and

     WHEREAS:      On January 12, 1971, Jimmy Carter was sworn in as the 76th Governor
                   of the state of Georgia. During his gubernatorial tenure, Carter was
                   a fierce advocate for civil rights, education reform, and more efficient
                   government processes; and
WHEREAS:   As the first Georgia native to ever serve in the nation’s highest
           political office, Jimmy Carter became the 39th President of the United
           States on January 20, 1977; and

WHEREAS:   In 1982, Jimmy Carter opened the Carter Center in Atlanta, Georgia
           to advance international human rights, promote democracy, and
           improve health; and

WHEREAS:   Across the globe, former President Jimmy Carter is widely respected
           for his work in human rights and health education in developing
           countries, and he was awarded with the Nobel Peace Prize in 2002
           for his efforts as a world-renowned humanitarian and
           philanthropist; and

WHEREAS:   On this day, December 29, 2024, the date of his death, we honor the
           memory of Georgia native, Jimmy Carter, as a revered community
           activist, veteran, statesman, author, former Governor of Georgia, and
           former President of the United States.

           NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF FORMER PRESIDENT OF THE
           UNITED STATES OF AMERICA JIMMY CARTER, AND PURSUANT TO
           THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
           GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

ORDERED:   That, pursuant to 4 U.S.C. Chapter 1, Section 7, subsection (m), the
           flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia should
           be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds for thirty
           days from December 29, 2024, the day of his death, until sunset on
           January 28, 2025.

           This 29th day of December 2024.
