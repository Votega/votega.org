EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Daren Culbreath Wayne of Hall
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as an At Large
Representative on the State Board of the Technical
College System of Georgia, for a term of office expiring
June 30, 2028, to succeed the Honorable Lee
Chapman, who is now the Ninth Congressional
District Representative on the State Board of the
Technical College System of Georgia, as a result of
the Georgia House of Representatives Redistricting
Act of 2023, adopted December 8, 2023.

This 31st day of January 2024

Bh

GOVERNOR
