EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED:

There currently exist at Valdosta State University, Lowndes County,
Valdosta, Georgia, buildings known as 1526 N. Oak Street, 1504 N. Oak
Street, 304 Baytree Road, 1506 N. Oak Street, and Parking Control Office
(collectively, the “Buildings”), located at 1500 N. Patterson Street; and

The Board of Regents, at its meeting on June 14, 2024, declared that said
Buildings could no longer be advantageously used by Valdosta State
University or the University System of Georgia and should be removed.

NOW, THEREFORE, BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia is hereby
authorized to demolish and remove the Buildings from Valdosta State
University, Lowndes County, Valdosta, Georgia.

IT IS FURTHER

That the appropriate staff of Valdosta State University, acting under the
approval of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, are
hereby authorized to demolish the Buildings and to have all building
materials removed from the premises by public works contract; all said
demolition and removal to be performed as expeditiously as possible.

This VOL day of June 2024.
