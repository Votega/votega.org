EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Vince Williams of Fulton
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as an At
Large Representative on the Board of Community
Affairs, for a term of office expiring July 1, 2028,
to succeed the Honorable Kwanza Hall, who is
now the Fifth Congressional District
Representative on the Board of Community
Affairs, as a result of the Georgia House of
Representatives Redistricting Act of 2023, adopted
December 8, 2023.

This 31st day of January 2024
