The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 26.815 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) in Troup County, Georgia filed with the State
Properties Commission Real Property Record as Property BLLIP ID# 07574; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Technical College System of Georgia and is
commonly known as the LaGrange Campus of West Georgia Technical College; and

The Property is improved with a structure identified as “Building B” (BLLIP
#19139) (hereinafter, the “Structure”), as shown in the aerial photograph attached
hereto as Exhibit A; and

The Technical College System of Georgia desires to demolish the Structure to
construct the new Building A WAM Renovation (TCSG-422); and

By official action dated October 2, 2024, the State Board of the Technical College
System of Georgia requested authority to demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Technical College System of Georgia is authorized and directed to provide for the
razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia, in accordance with
applicable laws, policies, and procedures, including any requirements under O.C.G.A.
§ 12-3-55, regarding historic properties that may pertain to the Structure.
