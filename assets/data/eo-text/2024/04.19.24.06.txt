THE STATE OF GEORGIA

That the Honorable Mulham Shbeib of Fulton
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the
Eleventh Congressional District Representative

At-Large Representative on the Board of
Economic Development, as a result of the
Georgia House of Representatives Redistricting
Act of 2023, adopted December 8, 2023.
