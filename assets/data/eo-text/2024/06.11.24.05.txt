EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED: That the Honorable Maurizio Centis of Cobb
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a
member of the State Board of Registration for
Professional Geologists, for a term of office
expiring April 13, 2026, to succeed the
Honorable Edward Rooks, who now serves as
the Commissioner of Natural Resources’
designee on the State Board of Registration for
Professional Geologists.

This 11 day of June 2024

Bh

GOVERNOR
