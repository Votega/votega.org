BY THE GOVERNOR:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 20.0 acres of improved real property
(hereinafter the “Property”) in Bibb County, Georgia filed with the State Properties
Commission Real Property Record as Property BLLIP ID# 51732; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Education and is commonly
known as the Georgia Academy for the Blind; and

The Property is improved with structures identified as “Cottage 1” (BLLIP #3930),
and “Cottage 4” (BLLIP #4519) (hereinafter, the “Structures”), as shown in the
aerial photograph attached hereto as Exhibit A; and

The Department of Education is desirous of demolishing the Structures due to age-
related issues and non-compliance with current construction codes, occupancy
standards, and the ability to adequately accommodate the necessary spaces for
student services; and

The demolition of the Structures will create space for the proposed Hendrix building
expansion, playground relocation, and potential future growth; and

By official action dated August 23, 2024, the Superintendent of the Department of
Education requested authority to demolish the Structures.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Education is authorized and directed to provide for the razing,
demolition, and disposal of said Structures under the terms and conditions which are
deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia, in accordance with applicable
laws, policies, and procedures, including any requirements under O.C.G.A. § 12-3-55
regarding historic properties that may pertain to the Structures.

This GQ day of September 2024.
