THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Joseph Poan Hsiao of Dekalb
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as the Fifth
Congressional District Representative on the State
Board of the Technical College System of Georgia,
for a term of office expiring June 1, 2024, to
succeed the Honorable Artesius Miller, who is now
the Thirteenth Congressional District
Representative on the State Board of the Technical
College System of Georgia, as a result of the
Georgia House of Representatives Redistricting Act
of 2023, adopted December 8, 2023.
