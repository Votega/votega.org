ORDERED:

On February 16, 2026, Waseem Daker filed an Amended Original Petition
for Writ of Mandamus (the “Petition”) against the Honorable Kimberly A.
Childs, in her official capacity as the Judge of the Superior Court of Cobb
County, in case number $2600792; and

The Georgia Department of Law declined representation of Judge Childs
due to a conflict; and

The Petition seeks equitable relief against Judge Childs in her official
capacity as a Superior Court Judge of this State; and

Mr. Dennis T. Cathey of Cathey & Strain, LLC is an attorney duly admitted
to the State Bar of Georgia; and

Mr. Cathey has agreed to represent Judge Childs at a rate mutually agreed
upon with the Office of the Governor.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-15-70 AND BY THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Mr. Dennis T. Cathey of Cathey & Strain, LLC is hereby appointed as

Special Counsel to assume control and responsibility for the representation
of the Honorable Kimberly A. Childs in all matters related to the Petition.
