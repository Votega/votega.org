The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 1.5 acres of improved real property
(the “Property”) by virtue of a Quitclaim Deed dated September 13, 1954, from the
Mayor and City Council of Statesboro, and Board of Commissioners of Roads and
Revenues of Bulloch County, Georgia, and recorded in Deed Book 197, Pages 514-
515, in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Bulloch County, being
inventoried and recorded as Real Property Record number 03573; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Georgia Forestry Commission and was acquired
as part of the Statesboro Area Forestry Office; and

The Property is improved with various structures identified as “Conference/Storage
Building” (BLLIP ID# 36031), and “Fuel Shed” (BLLIP ID# 36701) (collectively, the
“Structures”), and as shown in that aerial map attached hereto as Exhibit A, and
incorporated herein by reference; and

The Georgia Forestry Commission has ceased to utilize the Structures due to termite
damage and mold concerns and desires to demolish the Structures; and

The Georgia Forestry Commission Board, on August 25, 2026, resolved to request the
State Properties Commission seek an Executive Order from the Governor to allow for
the razing, demolition, and disposal of the Structures; and

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Georgia Forestry Commission is hereby authorized and directed to provide for
the razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structures under the terms and
conditions which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia, in
accordance with applicable laws, policies, and procedures, including any
requirements under Code Section 12-3-55 regarding historic properties that may

pertain to the Structures.
