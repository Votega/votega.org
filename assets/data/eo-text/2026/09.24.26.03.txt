ORDERED:

On August 11, 2026, Mr. Marcello Banes, Chairman of the Newton County Board
of Commissioners, was indicted on one count of Aiding and Assisting the
Preparation of a False Tax Return, one count of Wire Fraud Conspiracy, ten counts
of Wire Fraud, and two counts of Theft of Government Property; and

This indictment alleges facts not contained in the previous indictment considered
by the review commission appointed on January 23, 2025; and

After the fourteen-day period following receipt of the indictment passed, I
appointed members to the review commission on September 4, 2026, to determine
whether the indictment relates to and adversely affects the administration of the
Office of Chairman of the Newton County Board of Commissioners such that the
rights and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby and to make a
written report to me within 14 days with a recommendation as to whether Mr.
Banes should be suspended from office; and

By report dated September 14, 2026, the review commission found that the
indictment relates to and adversely affects the administration of the Office of
Chairman of the Newton County Board of Commissioners such that the rights and
interests of the public are adversely affected thereby and recommended Mr. Banes
be suspended from office in accordance with Code Section 45-5-6.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mr. Banes is hereby suspended from the Office of Chairman of the Newton
County Board of Commissioners immediately and without further action pending
the final disposition of the case or until the expiration of his term of office,
whichever occurs first, as provided by Code Section 45-5-6(c).
