FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF STAFF

1st Lt. Tyler James Feehan, 25, 2¢ Battalion, 55th Air Defense Artillery
Regiment, 32"4 Army Air and Missile Defense Command, honorably served
our country in the United States Army; and

1st Lt. Feehan and three others were killed in action on July 18 during an
enemy attack at Muwaffaq Salti Air Base, Jordan; and

His awards and decorations include the Army Achievement Medal, National
Defense Service Medal, Army Service Ribbon, Combat Action Badge, and in
recognition of his ultimate sacrifice, 1st Lt. Feehan was posthumously
promoted to Captain and will receive the Bronze Star and Purple Heart; and

1st Lt. Feehan was raised in Bryan County, Georgia, and will be laid to rest
in the Georgia National Cemetery in Canton; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize the sacrifices made by 15t Lt. Feehan by
lowering the flags on all State buildings and grounds to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF FIRST LIEUTENANT TYLER JAMES FEEHAN, PURSUANT TO
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA,
IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be
flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds on Friday, August 7,
2026, the day of 1st Lt. Feehan’s interment.
