There currently exists at Albany State University, Dougherty County, Albany,
Georgia, one (1) building identified as ALSU Facility Inventory No. 33114, Peace
Hall (the “Building”); located at 504 College Drive; and

The Board of Regents’ Policy 9.8.2.3 DEMOLITION authorizes the Chancellor or
Vice Chancellor for Real Estate & Facilities to declare buildings, structures and
other improvements no longer advantageously useful to the University System of
Georgia. Said Building is included within this policy; and

It has been determined by Mr. J. Frank Smith, Vice Chancellor for Real Estate and
Facilities of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, that said
Building can no longer be advantageously used by Albany State University or the
University System of Georgia and should be removed.

NOW, THEREFORE, BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE
OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia is hereby authorized
to demolish and remove the Building from Albany State University, Dougherty
County, Albany, Georgia.

IT IS FURTHER

That the appropriate staff of Albany State University, Dougherty County, Albany,
Georgia, acting under the approval of the Board of Regents of the University
System of Georgia, is hereby authorized to demolish the Building and to have all

building materials removed from the premises by public works contract; all said
demolition and removal to be performed as expeditiously as possible.
