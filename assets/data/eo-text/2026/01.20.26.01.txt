THE STATE OF GEORGIA
                                EXECUTIVE ORDER


BY THE GOVERNOR:




           On January 13, 2026, Senator Colton Moore qualified for the Special
           Election to fill the vacancy in Georgia’s 14th Congressional District for the
           United States House of Representatives. By operation of law under GA.
           CONST. Art. II, § II, ¶ V, a vacancy now exists in Senate District 53 in the
           Georgia State Senate.

           Therefore, pursuant to GA. CONST. Art. V, § II, ¶ VIII and Code Section 21-
           2-544, a writ of election is hereby issued to the Secretary of State for a
           special election to be held on Tuesday, March 10, 2026, to fill the District 53
           seat in the Georgia State Senate.

           This 20th day of January 2026.
