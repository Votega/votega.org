THE STATE OF GEORGIA
                                EXECUTIVE ORDER


BY THE GOVERNOR:




           Senate District 7 in the Georgia State Senate has become vacant due to the
           resignation of Senator Nabilah Parkes.

           Therefore, pursuant to GA. CONST. Art. V, § II, ¶ VIII and Code Section 21-
           2-544, a writ of election is hereby issued to the Secretary of State for a
           special election to be held on Tuesday, May 19, 2026, to fill the District 7
           seat in the Georgia State Senate.

           This 23rd day of March 2026.
