ORDERED:

Congressman David Scott, a longtime public servant, passed away
on Wednesday, April 21, 2026; and

Congressman Scott served the State of Georgia for more than 50
years, first as a member of the Georgia General Assembly and
later as a member of the United States House of Representatives
representing Georgia’s Thirteenth Congressional District; and

As we mourn his death, we also honor the legacy he leaves behind
and the impact he had on his loved ones, constituents, and the
State of Georgia.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT
FOR THE MEMORY OF THE HONORABLE DAVID SCOTT,
PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF
THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Congressman David Scott shall lie in state in the Rotunda of
the Georgia State Capitol on Friday, May 1, 2026.
