THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That the Honorable James A. Kiger is hereby appointed
Superior Court Judge of the Douglas Judicial Circuit to fill the
seat created by the enactment of Senate Bill 88 (Act Number
25) during the 2025 session of the Georgia General Assembly,
for a term of office ending December 31, 2028.

THIS 382 DAY OF FEBRUARY 2026.
