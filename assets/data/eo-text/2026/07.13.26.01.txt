THE STATE OF GEORGIA
                                     EXECUTIVE ORDER


BY THE GOVERNOR:


                         FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
                             ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF


   WHEREAS: Senator Lindsey Graham, a faithful public servant and patriot, passed away on
            Saturday, July 11, 2026; and

   WHEREAS: Sen. Graham began his political career in 1992, serving in the South Carolina
            House of Representatives, followed by the United States House of Representatives
            and the United States Senate; and

   WHEREAS: In addition to his political career, Sen. Graham honorably served his nation in the
            Judge Advocate General’s Corps of the United States Air Force and United States
            Air Force Reserve; and

   WHEREAS: Sen. Graham’s love for his nation, unyielding belief in its possibilities, and defense
            of its values made him a true force to be reckoned with in Washington; and

   WHEREAS: It is fitting and proper to remember and honor Sen. Graham’s service to the United
            States of America by lowering flags throughout Georgia to half-staff.

                NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
                MEMORY OF SENATOR LINDSEY GRAHAM, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY
                VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

   ORDERED:     That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia shall be flown
                at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds from the date of this order until
                sunset on Saturday, July 18, 2026.

                This 13th day of July 2026.




                                                           _______________________
                                                                    GOVERNOR
