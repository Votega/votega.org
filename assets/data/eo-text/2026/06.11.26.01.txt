The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 51.17 acres of
improved real property located in the 1660th G.M.D., Augusta-
Richmond County (“Property”) as shown on the survey attached hereto
as Exhibit “A”; and

Said Property is a portion of the real property commonly known as East
Central Regional Hospital in the custody of the Department of
Behavioral Health and Developmental Disabilities located in the
1660th G.M.D., Augusta-Richmond County, and acquired by virtue of
a Warranty Deed dated November 19, 1955, being recorded in Deed
Book 23-B, Pages 87-88, Warranty Deed dated October 1, 1920, being
recorded in Deed Book 9-J, Pages 388-391, Warranty Deed dated
August 30, 1960, being recorded in Deed Book 27-A, Pages 410-411,
Warranty Deed dated September 29, 1958, being recorded in Deed
Book 25-M, Pages 307-308 in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court
of Richmond County, and on file with the State Properties Commission
Real Property Records as RPR # 01129, 02717, 02732 and 02736
(respectively); and

By Official Action dated February 26, 2026, the Department of
Behavioral Health and Developmental Disabilities requested that
custody of the Property be transferred to the Department of Veterans
Service; and

The Property is improved with structures identified as Staff
Training-99 (BLLIP ID #2227), Building-100 (BLLIP ID #2228),
Building-101 (BLLIP ID #2229), and Building-102 (BLLIP ID
#2230) (collectively, the “Structures”), and as shown on that aerial
attached hereto as Exhibit “B”, and incorporated herein by reference;
and

By Official Action dated June 8, 2026, the Veterans Service Board
requested the State Properties Commission to seek an Executive
Order from the Governor to transfer custody of the Property to the

ORDERED:

ATTEST:

Department of Veterans Service and to allow for razing, demolition
and disposal of the Structures for the construction of a new veterans
cemetery; and

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That custody of the above-defined Property located in Richmond
County, Georgia is hereby transferred from the Department of
Behavioral Health and Developmental Disabilities to the Department
of Veterans Service and is hereby authorized and directed to provide
for the razing, demolition and disposal of said Structures under the
terms and conditions which are deemed to be in the best interests of
the State of Georgia, in accordance with applicable laws, policies, and
procedures, including any requirements under Code Section 12-3-55
regarding historic properties that may pertain to the Structures.
