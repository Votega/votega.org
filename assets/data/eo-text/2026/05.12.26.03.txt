WHEREAS:

House Bill 297, known as the Georgia Transportation Efficiency Authority
Act, was passed by the Georgia General Assembly and is effective upon
signature; and

House Bill 297 dissolves the Georgia Regional Transportation Authority
(GRTA) and all its responsibilities from state law and renames Atlanta-
region Transit Link Authority (ATL) as the Georgia Transportation
Efficiency Authority (GTEA), transferring all assets, obligations, employees,
and responsibilities to GTEA; and

House Bill 297 retains a limited set of activities and responsibilities from
GRTA and ATL, which have been effective in implementing state policy and
efficiently utilizing state taxpayer funds for transportation outcomes for
Georgians; and

Subject to Code Section 50-39-5, the GTEA Board of Directors will manage
the business and affairs of the authority; and

The 13-member GTEA board is comprised of eight members appointed by
the Governor, at least five of which must reside within a compliance zone
county, two by the President of the Senate, at least one of which must reside
within a compliance zone county, two by the Speaker of the House, at least
one of which must reside within a compliance zone county, and the
Commissioner of the Georgia Department of Transportation; and

House Bill 297 authorizes the Governor to designate a county as a
compliance zone county for the purposes of the initial appointment of
members of the board, and until any board action is taken; and

The jurisdictional boundaries that applied to GRTA and ATL were

Cherokee, Clayton, Cobb, Coweta, DeKalb, Douglas, Fayette, Forsyth,
Fulton, Gwinnett, Henry, Paulding, and Rockdale Counties; and
