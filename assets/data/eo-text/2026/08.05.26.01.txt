The State of Georgia leases approximately 1,775.74 acres of improved real property
in Bartow County (hereinafter the “Property”) from the United States Secretary of
the Army pursuant to a 25-year lease which commenced September 15, 2006, which
lease is recorded in the State Properties Commission Real Property Records as RPR#
010293; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Natural Resources (hereinafter
the “Department”) and is operated as Red Top Mountain State Park; and

The Property is improved with a well house consisting of 80 square feet (BLLIP
#29026) (hereinafter the “Structure”), which is owned by the State of Georgia and
as shown in that location map attached hereto as Exhibit A and incorporated
herein by reference; and

The Department is desirous of demolishing the Structure due to significant disrepair;
and

By official action dated January 30, 2026, the Board of Natural Resources requested
the authority from the State Properties Commission to seek an Executive Order from
the Governor to allow for the razing, demolition, and disposal of the Structure; and

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Natural Resources is hereby authorized and directed to raze,
demolish and dispose of said Structure under the terms and conditions which are
deemed to be in the best interests of the State of Georgia, in accordance with
applicable laws, policies, and procedures, including any requirements under O.C.G.A.
§ 12-3-55 regarding historic properties that may pertain to the Structure.
