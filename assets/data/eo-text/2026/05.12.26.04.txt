WHEREAS:

Under Code Section 50-8-7.1 and the Department of Community Affairs’
(DCA) Development of Regional Impact (DRI) rules, see Ga. Comp. R. &
Regs. 110-12-3.01, large-scale developments in Georgia must undergo a
review to evaluate potential effects on public infrastructure and services;
and

DCA has promulgated rules and regulations that govern DRIs throughout
the state, which include the Georgia Regional Transportation Authority
(GRTA) as the reviewer of certain transportation aspects of DRIs within its
13-county jurisdiction; and

House Bill 297, known as the Georgia Transportation Efficiency Authority
Act, was passed by the Georgia General Assembly and is effective upon
signature; and

House Bill 297 dissolves GRTA and renames the Atlanta-region Transit
Link Authority (ATL) as the Georgia Transportation Efficiency Authority
(GTEA), transferring all of both agencies’ assets, obligations, employees,
and certain responsibilities to GTEA; and

House Bill 297 authorizes GTEA to continue the DRI review framework for
the metro Atlanta region, continuing GRTA’s historical role in evaluating
large-scale developments for transportation impacts; and

DRI review plays a critical role in identifying and mitigating traffic impacts
while enabling adequate site access, and coordinating transportation
considerations essential to the safe and efficient movement of people and
goods in the metro Atlanta region; and

House Bill 297 does not specify that GTEA’s role in reviewing DRI projects

is only related to transportation purposes and to projects within its
jurisdiction.

Now, THEREFORE, BY THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF
THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

GTEA is hereby authorized to conduct and approve DRI reviews for the
geographic area over which the authority has jurisdiction, including the
transportation components of such reviews previously performed by GRTA.
IT IS FURTHER

All processes, criteria, and thresholds previously applied by GRTA for
transportation-related DRI determinations, including land transportation
services or access to such DRIs, shall remain in effect unless or until
modified by GTEA pursuant to its rulemaking and statutory authority.

Ir IS FURTHER

All prior Executive Orders relating to GRTA’s DRI review authority are

hereby superseded by this Order.
