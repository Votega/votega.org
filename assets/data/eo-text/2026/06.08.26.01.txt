ORDERED:

On April 7, 2026, Mr. Charles Brandon Fletcher, Coroner of Irwin County, was
indicted on nine counts of Theft by Taking, six counts of Financial Transaction
Card Fraud, four counts of Criminal Use of an Article with an Altered Identification
Mark, three counts of Making a False Statement, and three counts of Fraudulent
Use of Exemption Certificate to Avoid Taxes; and

A certified copy of the indictment was transmitted to me pursuant to Code Section
45-5-6(b) and received on April 27, 2026; and

Executive Order 05.12.26.02 was issued on May 12, 2026, appointing members to
the review commission to determine whether the indictment relates to and
adversely affects the administration of the Office of Coroner of Irwin County such
that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby; and

Executive Order 05.26.26.01 was issued on May 26, 2026, extending the period for
the review commission to make its written report; and

By report dated June 2, 2026, the review commission found that the indictment
relates to and adversely affects the administration of the Office of Coroner of Irwin
County and that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected
thereby, and recommended that Mr. Fletcher be suspended from office pursuant
to the provisions of Code Section 45-5-6.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Mr. Fletcher is hereby suspended from the Office of Coroner of Irwin County

immediately and without further action pending the final disposition of the case or
until the expiration of his term of office, whichever occurs first, as provided in Code

Section 45-5-6(c).
