THE STATE OF GEORGIA

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Albert King Rocker of Jenkins
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a member
of the Board of Directors of the Georgia Emergency
Communications Authority, for a term of office

Warren Samuel Couvillon, who resigned.
