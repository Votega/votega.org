THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

‘WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

When a vacancy occurs on the State Election Board pursuant to Code
Section 21-2-30 (c), such state executive committee of each political
party shall select a nominee within 30 days after a vacancy occurs in
such office, and such nominee shall be immediately appointed by the
Governor; and

The Honorable Janice Johnston, the state executive committee of the
Georgia Republican Party’s nominee to the State Election Board,
resigned from office on June 4, 2026; thereby creating a vacancy in
such office; and

By letter dated June 9, 2026, the state executive committee of the
Georgia Republican Party indicated its nomination of the Honorable
Carolyn Roddy to the State Election Board.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 21-2-30, AND
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That effective June 10, 2026, the Honorable Carolyn Roddy is
appointed as a member of the State Election Board, for a term of
office ending June 10, 2028, to succeed the Honorable Janice
Johnston.

This 10' day of June 2026.
