EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That the Honorable Erin H. Fowler is hereby appointed Judge
of the State Court of Forsyth County to fill the seat created by
the enactment of House Bill 750 (Act 520) during the 2026
session of the Georgia General Assembly, for a term of office
ending December 31, 2028.

THIS 267 DAY OF OCTOBER 2026.
