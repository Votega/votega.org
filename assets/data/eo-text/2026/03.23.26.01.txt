THE STATE OF GEORGIA
                                EXECUTIVE ORDER


BY THE GOVERNOR:




           House District 177 in the Georgia House of Representatives has become
           vacant due to the resignation of Representative Dexter Sharper.

           Therefore, pursuant to GA. CONST. Art. V, § II, ¶ VIII and Code Section 21-
           2-544, a writ of election is hereby issued to the Secretary of State for a
           special election to be held on Tuesday, May 12, 2026, to fill the District 177
           seat in the Georgia House of Representatives.

           This 23rd day of March 2026.
