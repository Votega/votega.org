EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

A vacancy now exists in Georgia’s Thirteenth Congressional
District for the United States House of Representatives due to
the death of Congressman David Scott.

Therefore, pursuant to Code Section 21-2-543, a writ of
election is hereby issued to the Secretary of State for a special
election to be held on Tuesday, July 28, 2026, to fill the
vacancy in Georgia’s Thirteenth Congressional District for the
United States House of Representatives.

Bel

This 1st day of May 2026.

GOVERNOR
