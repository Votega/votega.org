ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Sergeant Christopher Durran Fisher, 36, with the Warm Springs Police
Department, died in the line of duty on September 2, 2026; and

With the heart of a public servant, Sgt. Fisher was passionate about his service as
a law enforcement officer and was a great representative of the public safety
community; and

Prior to his service with the Warm Springs Police Department, Sgt. Fisher worked
for the Meriwether County Sheriffs Office, Fayette County Sheriffs Office, and
Heard County Sheriffs Office; and

Sgt. Fisher also proudly served his country for five years in the United States Army
as a Transportation Specialist; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Sgt. Fisher’s service to his fellow Georgians by
lowering the flags on the State Capitol building and in Meriwether, Fayette, and
Heard Counties to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF SGT. CHRISTOPHER DURRAN FISHER, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be flown at

half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Meriwether, Fayette, and Heard
Counties on Tuesday, September 8, 2026, the day of Sgt. Fisher’s interment, until
