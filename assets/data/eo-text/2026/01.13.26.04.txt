THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That the Honorable Robert D. “Bobby” Wolf is hereby
appointed Judge of the State Court of Fulton County to fill the
seat created by the enactment of House Bill 625 (Act Number
208) during the 2025 session of the Georgia General
Assembly, for a term of office ending December 31, 2028.

THIS 137 DAY OF JANUARY 2026.

PPL

/ GOVERNOR
