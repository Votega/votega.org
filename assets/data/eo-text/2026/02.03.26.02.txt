THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That the Honorable Kristi G. Connell is hereby appointed
Superior Court Judge of the Augusta Judicial Circuit to fill the
seat created by the enactment of Senate Bill 145 (Act Number
26) during the 2025 session of the Georgia General Assembly,
for a term of office ending December 31, 2028.

THIS 38° DAY OF FEBRUARY 2026.
