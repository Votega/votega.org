AMENDING THE STATE OF EMERGENCY FOR
PETROLEUM SUPPLY DISRUPTIONS

On September 28, 2026, due to ongoing disruptions to global
petroleum supply chains, I issued Executive Order No. 09.28.26.01,
declaring a State of Emergency for Petroleum Supply Disruptions
and suspending the collection of the state motor fuel taxes; and

On October 5, 2026, President Donald J. Trump signed an executive
order directing the Secretary of the Treasury to facilitate the use of
dyed diesel on highways by providing penalty relief and deferring
federal tax obligations; and

Dyed diesel is critical for agricultural and forestry operations,
particularly in the fall harvest season, and the State of Georgia should
take every action to facilitate the implementation of President

Trump’s order; and

Code Section 38-3-51 vests the Governor with the power to renew any
state of emergency for a period not to exceed thirty (30) days; and

Code Section 38-5-51(d) vests the Governor with the power to
suspend any statute prescribing the procedures for conduct of state
business or the orders, rules, or regulations of any state agency if
strict compliance with any statute, order, rule, or regulation would in
any way prevent, hinder, or delay necessary action in coping with the
emergency or disaster; and

Code Section 48-2-43 authorizes the State Revenue Commissioner to
waive, in whole or in part, the collection of any amount due to the
state as a penalty under any revenue law when the Commissioner
determines such waiver is reasonable; and

In consultation with state economic officials, I have determined that
the following actions are necessary and appropriate to protect the
continued strength of Georgia’s economy, particularly the
agricultural and timber sectors, and to provide for the health, safety,
and welfare of Georgia’s residents and visitors.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 38-3-51, AND
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the State Revenue Commissioner shall waive the enforcement
of the penalties incurred for the selling, delivery, and consumption
of dyed fuel oil for highway use set forth in Code Section 48-9-16(e).

IT IS FURTHER

That the State Revenue Commissioner shall issue guidance as
necessary to facilitate the implementation of this waiver and of the
President’s executive order.

IT IS FURTHER

In addition to the provisions of this Order, the terms of Executive
Order No. 09.28.26.01 are hereby adopted by reference.

ITIS FURTHER

That the State of Emergency for Petroleum Supply Disruptions
declared by Executive Order No. 09.28.26.01 shall be renewed for
thirty (30) days from the date of this Order, and shall terminate on
November 5, 2026, at 11:59 P.M., unless otherwise renewed or
terminated by a subsequent executive order.

IT IS FURTHER

That if one or more of the provisions contained in this Order shall be
held to be invalid, in violation of the Georgia Constitution, in
violation of Georgia law, or unenforceable in any respect, such
invalidity, violation, or unenforceability shall not affect any other
provisions of this Order, but, in such case, this Order shall be
construed as if such invalid, illegal, or unenforceable provision had
never been contained within the Order.
