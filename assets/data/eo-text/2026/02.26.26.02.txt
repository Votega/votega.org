EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN LOWNDES COUNTY

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Officer Caleb Abney, with the Hahira Police Department, died in the line of
duty on February 19, 2026; and

Known for his dedication to public service, Office Abney worked for
Lowndes County Fire and Rescue before joining the Hahira Police
Department; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Officer Abney’s service to the citizens of
Hahira by lowering the flags on the State Capitol building and in Lowndes
County to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF OFFICER CALEB ABNEY, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be
flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Lowndes County on
Sunday, March 1, 2026, the day of Officer Abney’s interment, until sunset.

GOVERNOR
