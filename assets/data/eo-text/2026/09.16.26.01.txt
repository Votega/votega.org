The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 1.4 acres of improved real property
(the “Property”) by virtue of a Warranty Deed dated August 6, 1954, from Mose
Gordon and recorded in Deed Book 147, Page 309, in the office of the Clerk of
Superior Court of Hall County being inventoried and recorded as Real Property
Record number 00736; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Georgia Forestry Commission and was acquired
as part of the Hall County Fire Tower; and

The Property is improved with a structure identified as the “Residence” (BLLIP
ID# 36176) (the “Structure”), and as shown in that aerial map attached hereto as
Exhibit A, and incorporated herein by reference; and

The Georgia Forestry Commission has ceased to utilize the Structure due to
deterioration and desires to demolish the Structure; and

The Georgia Forestry Commission Board, on August 25, 2026, resolved to request the
State Properties Commission seek an Executive Order from the Governor to allow for
the razing, demolition, and disposal of the Structure; and

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Georgia Forestry Commission is hereby authorized and directed to provide for
the razing, demolition and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia, in accordance with
applicable laws, policies, and procedures, including any requirements under Code
Section 12-3-55, regarding historic properties that may pertain to the Structure.
