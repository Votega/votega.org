THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Susan K. Treadaway of Cherokee
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a member
of the State Commission on Family Violence, for a
term of office expiring November 17, 2029, to
succeed the Honorable Bradford Lee Rigby, whose
term expired September 26, 2026.

This 17th day of November 2026

GOVERNOR
