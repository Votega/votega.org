ORDERED:

House Resolution 1008, Act No. 425 (2026), was passed by the Georgia
General Assembly and ratified amendments to the Georgia Comprehensive
State-wide Water Management Plan (the “State-wide WMP”); and

These amendments revise the composition of the Regional Water Planning
Councils, necessitating termination of existing gubernatorial appointments
to facilitate reconstitution pursuant to the State-wide WMP.

NOW, THEREFORE, BY THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF
THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

All existing gubernatorial appointments to the Regional Water Planning
Councils established under the State-wide WMP, including alternate
appointments and any continued service in a holdover capacity, are hereby
terminated. This Order does not affect appointments made by the
Lieutenant Governor or the Speaker of the House of Representatives.

IT IS FURTHER

This Order shall not affect appointments to the Metropolitan North Georgia
Water Planning District Governing Board established by Code Section 12-5-
575 or any other council, commission, board, or entity except those
established by the State-wide WMP.

IT IS FURTHER

That this Order shall be effective upon signature.

This 20th day of October 2026.
