ORDERED:

On July 9, 2026, Mr. Alfonzo Williams, Sheriff of Burke County, was indicted on
five counts of Hobbs Act Extortion Under Color of Official Right and one count of
Transfer of a Firearm to a Prohibited Person; and

Acertified copy of the indictment was transmitted to me pursuant to Code Section
45-5-6(b) and received on July 16, 2026; and

On July 31, 2026, I appointed members to the review commission to determine
whether the indictment relates to and adversely affects the administration of the
Office of Sheriff of Burke County such that the rights and interests of the public are
adversely affected thereby and to make a written report to me within 14 days with
a recommendation as to whether Mr. Williams should be suspended from office;
and

By report dated August 10, 2026, the review commission found that the indictment
relates to and adversely affects the administration of the Office of Sheriff of Burke
County such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected
thereby and recommended that Mr. Williams be suspended from office in
accordance with Code Section 45-5-6.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mr. Williams is hereby suspended from the Office of Sheriff of Burke County
immediately and without further action pending the final disposition of the case or
until the expiration of his term of office, whichever occurs first, as provided by
Code Section 45-5-6(c).
