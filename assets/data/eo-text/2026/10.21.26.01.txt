THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Richard Manning Kramer, Jr.
of Fulton County, Georgia, is appointed to serve
as a member of the Georgia State Board of
Architects and Interior Designers, for a term of
office expiring October 21, 2031, to succeed the
Honorable David M.G. Maschke, whose term
expired March 5, 2025.
