THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On March 6, 2026, I issued Executive Order 03.06.26.01, appointing
a committee to investigate Sheriff Gerald Couch of Hall County
pursuant to Code Section 15-16-26; and

Code Section 15-16-26(c) authorizes the Governor, upon the
recommendation of the investigative committee, to suspend a sheriff
for a period of up to 60 days; and

On March 16, 2026, the committee delivered its recommendation to
me that Sheriff Couch be suspended from office.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 15-16-26, AND
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Sheriff Couch is hereby suspended from the Office of Sheriff of
Hall County for a period of 60 days, effective immediately.

Bil

GOVERNOR

This 17th day of March 2026.
