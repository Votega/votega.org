By virtue of a General Warranty Deed dated July 23, 2004, from Melvin Hugh
Howard, Randall C. Howard, Brenda H. Wallace, Charlotte H. Satterfield, and Vera
H. Green, being recorded in Deed Book 3434, Pages 433-435, in the office of the Clerk
of Superior Court of Forsyth County, and on file with the State Properties
Commission Real Property Records as RPR 010017, the State of Georgia holds title
to approximately 8.74 acres of improved real property situated in Land Lot 226 of
the 2nd District of the ist Section of Forsyth County, Georgia, as more fully
described in the legal description and depicted in that “Department of
Transportation, State of Georgia, Plan and Profile of Proposed Cumming Park and
Ride Lot” attached hereto collectively as Exhibit “A” and incorporated herein by
reference (the “Property”),; and

Said Property is in the custody of the State Road and Tollway Authority; and

By resolution dated June 9, 2026, the State Road and Tollway Authority declared
the Property to be surplus to its needs and requested that the Property be
transferred to the Georgia Transportation Efficiency Authority; and

By resolution dated June 16, 2026, the Georgia Transportation Efficiency Authority
approved the acceptance of the Property from the State Road and Tollway
Authority.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR
OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That custody of the above-defined Property located in Forsyth County, Georgia is
hereby transferred from the State Road and Tollway Authority to the Georgia

Transportation Efficiency Authority.
