THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Lenese Michelle Colson of Lowndes
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a member of
the State Board for the Certification of Librarians, for a

the Honorable Anne Moody Isbell, whose term expired
January 13, 2025.
