ORDERED:

On April 7, 2026, Mr. Charles Brandon Fletcher, Coroner of Irwin County,
was indicted on nine counts of Theft by Taking, six counts of Financial
Transaction Card Fraud, four counts of Criminal Use of an Article with an
Altered Identification Mark, three counts of Making a False Statement, and
three counts of Fraudulent Use of Exemption Certificate to Avoid Taxes; and

A certified copy of the above-mentioned indictment was transmitted to me
pursuant to Code Section 45-5-6(b) and received on April 27, 2026; and

Executive Order 05.12.26.02 was issued on May 12, 2026, appointing
members to the review commission to determine whether the above-
mentioned indictment relates to and adversely affects the administration of
the Office of Coroner of Irwin County such that the rights and interests of
the public are adversely affected; and

Code Section 45-5-6(c) provides that additional time may be granted by the
Governor for a review commission to make its written report; and

On May 26, 2026, the above-mentioned review commission requested a 30-
day extension to make its written report.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the period for the above-mentioned review commission to make its

written report is extended to June 25, 2026.
