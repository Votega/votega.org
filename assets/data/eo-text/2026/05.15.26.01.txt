On March 6, 2026, I issued Executive Order 03.06.26,01, appointing
a committee to investigate Sheriff Gerald Couch of Hall County
pursuant to Code Section 15-16-26; and

On March 16, 2026, the committee delivered its recommendation to
me that Mr. Couch be suspended from office; and

Accordingly, on March 17, 2026, I issued Executive Order
03.17.26.01 suspending Mr. Couch for 60 days; and

Pursuant to Code Section 15-16-26(c), “[i]n any case where a sheriff
has been suspended for 60 days, the Governor may extend the period
of suspension for an additional 30 days.”

NoW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 15-16-26, AND

THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the period of suspension for Mr. Couch is hereby extended for

an additional 30 days.
