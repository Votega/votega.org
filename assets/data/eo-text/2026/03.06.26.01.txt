On February 27, 2026, Mr. Gerald Couch, Sheriff of Hall County, was
arrested by the Georgia State Patrol and charged with one count of Driving
Under the Influence, Failure to Maintain Lane, and Possession of Open
Container of Alcoholic Beverage; and

Pursuant to Code Section 15-16-26(a), the Governor has the authority to
appoint an investigative committee consisting of the Attorney General and
two sheriffs who are members of the Georgia Sheriffs’ Association when the
Governor “determines that an investigation of a sheriff of this state should
be made as a result of criminal charges, alleged misconduct in office, or
alleged incapacity of the sheriff to perform the functions of his office[;]”
and

By letter dated March 3, 2026, the Georgia Sheriffs’ Association has
requested the appointment of a committee under Code Section 15-16-26 to
investigate Sheriff Couch, as a result of his arrest.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 15-16-26, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Sheriff Donnie Harrison of Greene
County, and Sheriff Jeremy Kelley of Glasscock County are hereby
appointed as the committee to investigate Sheriff Gerald Couch.

ITIS FURTHER

That the committee shall make a report of its investigation to me within 30

days of the date of this Order.
