THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

A vacancy now exists in Georgia’s 14th Congressional District
for the United States House of Representatives due to the
resignation of Congresswoman Marjorie Taylor Greene.

Therefore, pursuant to Code Section 21-2-543, a writ of
election is hereby issued to the Secretary of State for a special
election to be held on Tuesday, March 10, 2026, to fill the
vacancy in Georgia’s 14th Congressional District for the United
States House of Representatives.
