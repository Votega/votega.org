THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN GWINNETT COUNTY

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Officer Pradeep Tamang, 25, with the Gwinnett County Police Department,
died in the line of duty on Sunday, February 1, 2026; and

Officer Tamang joined the Gwinnett County Police Department in July
2024 and graduated from the 1215t Police Academy in May 2025; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Officer Tamang’s service to the citizens
of Gwinnett County by lowering the flags on the State Capitol building and
in Gwinnett County to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF OFFICER PRADEEP TAMANG, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be

flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Gwinnett County on
Saturday, February 7, 2026, the day of Officer Tamang’s memorial service,

until sunset.
