ORDERED:

On December 2, 2025, Ms. Sharon Henderson, Representative of House
District 113 in the Georgia House of Representatives, was indicted on two
counts of Theft of Government Funds and ten counts of Making False
Statements; and

A certified copy of the indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant
to GA. ConsT, Art. H, § III, I (b) and received on December 18, 2025; and

The fourteen-day period following receipt of the indictment as prescribed
by Ga. Constr, Art, I, § III, #1 (d) has passed.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO GA. CONST. ART. II, § III, § 1, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Senator Harold Jones, II, of
Senate District 22 in the Georgia State Senate, and Representative Jan
Jones of House District 47 in the Georgia House of Representatives are
hereby appointed as the review commission to determine whether the
above-mentioned indictment relates to and adversely affects the
administration of duties of Representative Henderson such that the rights
and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby, and to make a
written report to me within fourteen (14) days of this Order with a
recommendation as to whether she should be suspended from office, as

required by GA. CONST, Art. II, § II, I (e).
