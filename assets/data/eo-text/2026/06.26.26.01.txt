DEPARTMENT OF NATURAL RESOURCES

Department of Natural Resources — To provide funds of $5,500,000 to the Stone

Department of Public Safety — To provide funds of $1,192,869 to cover costs
associated with crime suppression efforts.

OFFICE OF THE GOVERNOR

Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland Security Agency — To provide
funds of $5,852,798 for the disaster response expenses related to recent wildfire
outbreaks and other natural disasters.

Office of the Governor — To provide funds of $2,016,374 to cover costs associated with
disputes over water usage in the Apalachicola-Chattahoochee-Flint River Basin and the
Alabama-Coosa-Tallapoosa River Basin, counsel fees, and litigation expenses.
