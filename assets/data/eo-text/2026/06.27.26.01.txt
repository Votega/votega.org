ORDERED:

On June 26, 2026, allegations of misconduct in office by Mr. Kenneth
Henderson, Sheriff of Towns County, were brought to my attention; and

These allegations are separate and distinct from the allegations previously
investigated pursuant to Executive Order 09.19.25.02, or the charges in the
indictment reviewed pursuant to Executive Order 12.02.25.01; and

Code Section 15-16-26(a) grants the Governor the authority to appoint an
investigative committee consisting of the Attorney General and two sheriffs
who are members of the Georgia Sheriffs’ Association whenever the
Governor determines that an investigation of a sheriff should be conducted
as a result of criminal charges, alleged misconduct in office, or alleged
incapacity to perform the functions of his or her office.

Now, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 15-16-26, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Sheriff Lenn Wood of Coweta

County, and Sheriff Ezell Brown of Newton County are hereby appointed as
the committee to investigate the alleged misconduct of Sheriff Henderson.

IT IS FURTHER

That the committee shall make a report of its investigation to me within 30

days of the date of this Order.

GOVERNOR
