THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That the Honorable Patrick M. Shepherd is hereby appointed
Judge of the State Court of Spalding County to fill the seat
created by the enactment of House Bill 798 (Act Number 233)
during the 2025 session of the Georgia General Assembly, for
a term of office ending December 31, 2028.
