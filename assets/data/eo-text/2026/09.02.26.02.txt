Code Section 20-2-73 provides that when a local school system or school
has been placed on the level of accreditation immediately preceding loss of
accreditation, the State Board of Education shall conduct a hearing to
determine whether to recommend the suspension of members of a local
board of education; and

The State Board of Education, following such a hearing, recommended to
me the suspension of all eligible members of the Dublin City Board of
Education; and

On August 17, 2026, I issued Executive Order 08.17.26.01, suspending all
eligible members of the Dublin City Board of Education and creating the
Dublin City Board of Education Nominating Committee, pursuant to Code
Section 20-2-73; and

The Dublin City Board of Education Nominating Committee sent its
nominations to me for temporary replacements to appoint to the Dublin
City Board of Education on August 31, 2026; and

Code Section 20-2-73 provides that, upon suspension, the Governor shall
“appoint temporary replacement members who shall otherwise be qualified
to serve” as members of the local board of education; and

Such temporary replacement members shall become permanent members
of the local board of education upon the conclusion of the appeal process set
forth in Code Section 20-2-73(b) and (c).

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 20-2-73, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS

HEREBY

That, effective immediately, Teresa Holliday is appointed to the Dublin City
Board of Education, District 1 seat.
