THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That the Honorable Torrey D. Smith is hereby appointed
Judge of the State Court of Fulton County to fill the seat
created by the enactment of House Bill 625 (Act Number 208)

aterm of office ending December 31, 2028.
