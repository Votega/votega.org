ORDERED:

The labor contract between the Metropolitan Atlanta Rapid Transit
Authority (“MARTA”) and Amalgamated Transit Union, Local 732
(“the Union”) expired on December 31, 2025; and

MARTA and the Union are unable to agree upon the terms and
conditions of a new labor agreement; and

On October 23, 2026, MARTA requested my appointment of a
neutral fact finder to investigate and explore all unresolved
bargaining issues, pursuant to the Metropolitan Atlanta Rapid
Transit Authority Act of 1965, as amended (“the MARTA Act”).

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE MARTA ACT, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Honorable Harold D. Melton is hereby appointed as a
neutral fact finder to investigate and explore all unresolved collective
bargaining issues.

IT IS FURTHER

That the fact finder shall make a report, which shall recommend as
to all unresolved collective bargaining issues, including appropriate
wages, hours, and other terms and conditions of employment for
represented employees, and shall set forth supporting factual
findings determined after due consideration of the following factors,
as set forth in the MARTA Act:

(i) The financial ability of MARTA to pay wages and
provide benefits, whether or not increased, while
adhering to all legal requirements governing MARTA’s
expenditure of public funds and revenues and
maintaining levels of transit service sufficient to serve
the metropolitan area;

(ii) |The amount, if any, of any fare increase which would
be necessary to afford a wage or salary increase or
improvement in fringe benefits or extension of
vacation, holiday, or excused time and the ability of the
public to bear a fare increase, with consideration of the
per capita income of those persons in the service area;

(iii) A comparison between the overall wage and salary
levels and fringe benefit levels and vacation, holiday,
and excused time allowances of MARTA’s represented
employees and other workers in the public and private
sectors of the metropolitan area who perform work
requiring similar skills in other major ground
transportation services;

(iv) A comparison of the hours and working conditions of
MARTA’s represented employees and other workers in
the public and private sectors of the metropolitan area
who perform work requiring similar skills in other
major ground transportation services; and

(v) The cost of consumer goods and services within the
metropolitan area.

IT IS FURTHER

ORDERED: That the report shall be issued within 30 days of this Order and
distributed to MARTA, the Union, and the public.
IT IS FURTHER

ORDERED: That the fact finder shall cause the summary of findings to be

published once in the newspaper having the largest circulation in the
metropolitan area.
