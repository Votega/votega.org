THE STATE OF GEORGIA
                                   EXECUTIVE ORDER


BY THE GOVERNOR:


   WHEREAS:   On June 26, 2026, allegations of misconduct by Mr. Kenneth Henderson, Sheriff
              of Towns County, were brought to my attention; and

   WHEREAS:   These allegations are separate and distinct from the allegations previously
              investigated pursuant to Executive Order 09.19.25.02 or the indictment reviewed
              pursuant to Executive Order 12.02.25.01; and

   WHEREAS:   On June 27, 2026, I appointed Attorney General Christopher Carr, Sheriff Lenn
              Wood of Coweta County, and Sheriff Ezell Brown of Newton County as the
              committee to investigate the alleged misconduct of Sheriff Henderson; and

   WHEREAS:   By report dated June 27, 2026, the committee recommended that Sheriff
              Henderson be suspended pursuant to Code Section 15-16-26(c).

              NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 15-16-26, AND THE
              AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
              HEREBY

   ORDERED:   That Sheriff Henderson is hereby suspended from the Office of Sheriff of Towns
              County for a period of 60 days, effective immediately.


              This 28th day of June 2026.




                                                         __________________________
                                                                  GOVERNOR
