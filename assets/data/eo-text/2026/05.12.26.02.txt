ORDERED:

On April 7, 2026, Mr. Charles Brandon Fletcher, Coroner of Irwin County,
was indicted on nine counts of Theft by Taking, six counts of Financial
Transaction Card Fraud, four counts of Criminal Use of an Article with an
Altered Identification Mark, three counts of Making a False Statement, and
three counts of Fraudulent Use of Exemption Certificate to Avoid Taxes; and

A certified copy of the indictment was transmitted to me pursuant to Code
Section 45-5-6(b) and received on April 27, 2026; and

The fourteen-day period following receipt of the indictment as prescribed
by Code Section 45-5-6(e) has passed.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Coroner Kenneth Cooper of
Barrow County, and Coroner Richard Stanley of Laurens County are hereby
appointed as the review commission to determine whether the above-
mentioned indictment relates to and adversely affects the administration of
the Office of Coroner of Irwin County such that the rights and interests of
the public are adversely affected thereby, and to make a written report to
me within fourteen days with a recommendation as to whether Mr. Fletcher
should be suspended from office, as required by Code Section 45-5-6(c).
