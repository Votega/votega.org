ORDERED:

On December 2, 2025, Representative Sharon Henderson of House District 113 in
the Georgia House of Representatives was indicted on two counts of Theft of
Government Funds and ten counts of Making False Statements; and

Acertified copy of the indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to GA.
Const. Art. II, § II, 7 I (b) and received on December 18, 2025; and

On January 8, 2026, Executive Order 01.08.26.01 was issued appointing members
to a review commission to determine whether the above-mentioned indictment
relates to and adversely affects the administration of duties by Representative
Henderson such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected
thereby; and

The review commission submitted its report on January 22, 2026; and

In its report, the review commission found that the above-mentioned indictment
does relate to and adversely affect the administration of duties by Representative
Henderson such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected
thereby and recommended that Representative Henderson be suspended from
office pursuant to GA. CONST. Art. II, § III, 4 I (b).

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO GA. CONST. ART. II, § ITI, {I (B), AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Representative Henderson is hereby suspended from office immediately and
without further action pending the final disposition of the case or until the
expiration of her term of office, whichever occurs first.
