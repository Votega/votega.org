STATE OF EMERGENCY FOR
SEVERE FLOODING

From Wednesday, November 4, through Thursday, November 5,
2026, heavy showers and thunderstorms moved over North Georgia,
with some areas receiving up to ten inches of rainfall; and

The National Weather Service issued a Flash Flood Warning for
Fannin, Gilmer, Murray, and Union Counties on November 4, and
subsequently issued Flash Flood Emergencies for portions of the
affected areas later that evening and into the morning; and

The Toccoa River reached historic flood levels early this morning,
cresting at slightly over 18 feet; and

Reports from local emergency management agencies and law
enforcement indicate mudslides, water rescues, water entering
homes, and evacuations along the Toccoa River; and

On September 28, 2026, due to the petroleum supply disruptions
and the resulting volatile prices for gas and diesel fuel, I issued
Executive Order No. 09.28.26.01, which was amended on October 6,
2026, by Executive Order No. 10.06.26.01, and extended on October
24, 2026, by Executive Order No. 10.24.26.01; and

This Order shall create a coexisting state of emergency in the State of
Georgia with the State of Emergency for Petroleum Supply
Disruptions described in the immediately preceding paragraph; and

Due to the severe flooding, assistance from the State of Georgia is
necessary to provide for the public’s safety, protect public and private
property, and restore the social and economic welfare of the
impacted areas; and

The Governor is vested with the emergency powers cited herein as
the Chief Executive of this State; and

As Chief Executive, the Governor is tasked with declaring a state of
emergency “[i]n the event of [an] actual or impending emergency or
disaster of natural or human origin . . . within or affecting this
[S]tate” under Code Section 38-3-51(a); and

Code Section 38-3-51(c) vests the Governor with the authority to
bring emergency situations under control by issuing orders, rules,
and regulations to protect the safety and welfare of the public; and

Code Section 38-3-28 provides that “[a]ll orders, rules, and
regulations promulgated by the Governor” have the force and effect
of law; and

In consultation with state emergency preparedness officials, I have
determined that the following actions are necessary and appropriate
to protect the continued strength of Georgia’s economy and provide
for the health, safety, and welfare of Georgia’s residents and visitors.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTIONS 38-3-28 AND
38-3-51, AND THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That a State of Emergency exists in the following Georgia counties
due to severe flooding: Fannin, Gilmer, Murray, and Union Counties.

IT IS FURTHER

That the State of Emergency declared by this Order shall coexist with
the State of Emergency for Petroleum Supply Disruptions declared
by Executive Order 09.28.26.01, amended by Executive Order
10.06.26.01, and extended by Executive Order 10.24.26.01. The
existence or termination of one shall not impinge the others.

IT IS FURTHER

That any orders derivative of or appurtenant to this Order addressing
this State of Emergency shall not infringe, overturn, or in any way
amend any orders that have been issued for the purpose of
responding to the State of Emergency for Petroleum Supply
Disruptions declared by Executive Order 09.28.26.01, amended by

IT IS FURTHER

That all resources of the State of Georgia be made available to assist
in preparation, response, and recovery activities throughout the
State, and the Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland
Security Agency shall activate the Georgia Emergency Operations
Plan and the Georgia State Operations Center.

IT IS FURTHER

That state agencies shall coordinate all public and emergency
information, activities, releases, and response efforts related to this
emergency with the Georgia Emergency Management and
Homeland Security Agency.

IT IS FURTHER

That the Georgia Department of Transportation and Georgia
Department of Public Safety shall take all necessary action to ensure
the expeditious movement of utility vehicles, equipment, and
personnel through the State to eliminate power outages.

ITIS FURTHER

That state agencies shall provide sufficient personnel required for the
staffing of the Georgia State Operations Center or other command,
control, and coordination points as may be designated by the
Director of the Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland
Security Agency and shall provide such personnel, vehicles,
equipment, and other resources needed to protect life and property
and to ensure continuation, restoration, and recovery of essential
public services.

IT IS FURTHER

That, pursuant to Code Section 10-1-393.4, price gouging related to
goods and services necessary for preparation, response, and recovery
activities for this State of Emergency, including water, motor fuel,
diesel fuel, and other petroleum products, would be detrimental to
the social and economic welfare of the citizens of this State and is
therefore prohibited.

IT IS FURTHER

That if one or more of the provisions in this Order shall be held to be
invalid, in violation of the Georgia Constitution, in violation of
Georgia law, or unenforceable in any respect, such invalidity,
violation, or unenforceability shall not affect any other provisions of
this Order, but, in such case, this Order shall be construed as if such
invalid, illegal, or unenforceable provision had never been contained
within the Order.

IT IS FURTHER

That no provision of this Order shall limit, infringe, suspend, or
supplant any judicial order, judgment, or decree issued pursuant to
the laws or constitution of this State or the laws or constitution of the
United States, nor shall any person use any provision this Order as a
defense to an action in violation of a judicial order, judgment, or
decree by any court created pursuant to the laws or constitution of
this State or the laws or constitution of the United States.

IT IS FURTHER

That this Order does not suspend the operation of any state or
federal law or regulation, except as specifically described herein.

IT IS FURTHER

That the Office of the Governor may continue to issue guidance on
the scope of this Order as needed through communication media,
including social media, without need for further Executive Orders.

IT IS FURTHER

That all provisions of the Order shall become effective upon
signature and shall be valid for a period of seven (7) days, expiring
Thursday, November 12, 2026, at 11:59 P.M. unless this State of
Emergency is renewed by the Governor.

This 5th day of November 2026.

OVERNOR
