EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That the Honorable Matthew B. Bennett is hereby appointed
Superior Court Judge of the Alapaha Judicial Circuit to fill the
seat created by the enactment of House Bill 55 (Act Number
171) during the 2025 session of the Georgia General Assembly,
for a term of office ending December 31, 2028.

THIS 137 DAY OF JANUARY 2026.
