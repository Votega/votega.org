ORDERED:

On August 11, 2026, Mr. Marcello Banes, Chairman of the Newton County
Board of Commissioners, was indicted on one count of Aiding and Assisting
the Preparation of a False Tax Return, one count of Wire Fraud Conspiracy,
ten counts of Wire Fraud, and two counts of Theft of Government Property;
and

This indictment alleges new facts and charges not contained in the
indictment considered by the review commission appointed on January 23,
2025, per Executive Order 01.23.25.01; and

A certified copy of the indictment was transmitted to me pursuant to Code
Section 45-5-6(b) and received on August 19, 2026; and

The fourteen-day period following receipt of the indictment as prescribed
by Code Section 45-5-6(e) has passed.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS

HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Clinton Johnson of the Dougherty
County Board of Commissioners, and Bruce Henry of the Jasper County
Board of Commissioners are hereby appointed as the review commission to
determine whether the indictment relates to and adversely affects the
administration of the Office of Chairman of the Newton County Board of
Commissioners such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely
affected thereby and to make a written report to me within 14 days of this
Order with a recommendation as to whether Mr. Banes should be
suspended from office, as required by Code Section 45-5-6(c).
