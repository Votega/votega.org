The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 181.096 acres of improved
real property (the “Property”) by virtue of a Warranty Deed dated January
3, 1899, from Tallulah O. McComb and recorded in Deed Book Z, Page 40
in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Baldwin County and a
Warranty Deed dated January 3, 1899, from H.E. Hendrix, Executor of the
last will and testament of Isabella A. Hendrix, and recorded in Deed Book
Z, Page 9, each deed being inventoried and recorded as Real Property
Record numbers 000043 and 000044, respectively; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Juvenile Justice and was
acquired as part of the Bill Ireland Youth Detention Center; and

The Property is improved with various structures identified as “Living Unit
#1” (BLLIP ID# 1079), “Living Unit #2” (BLLIP ID# 1080), “Living Unit #3”
(BLLIP ID# 1081), “Living Unit #4” (BLLIP ID# 1082), “Living Unit #5”
(BLLIP ID# 1083), “Living Unit #6” (BLLIP ID# 1312), “Living Unit #7”
(BLLIP ID# 1313), “Living Unit #8” (BLLIP ID# 1314), “Living Unit #9”
(BLLIP ID# 1315), “Living Unit #10” (BLLIP ID# 1316), “Academic Building
#24” (BLLIP ID# 1348), “School Annex #25” (BLLIP ID# 1349),
“Administration #26” (BLLIP ID# 1350), “Old Dining Hall #28” (BLLIP
ID# 1352), Boot Camp Classroom #29” (BLLIP ID# 72361) and Steam
Plant/Tunnel #32 (BLLIP ID# 1355) (collectively, the “Structures”), and as
shown in that campus map attached hereto as Exhibit A, and incorporated
herein by reference; and

The Department of Juvenile Justice has ceased to utilize the Structures due to
their deterioration and desires to demolish the Structures in preparation for
the construction of a new 56-bed youth development campus; and

The Board of Juvenile Justice, on October 23, 2025, resolved to request the
State Properties Commission seek an Executive Order from the Governor to
allow for the razing, demolition, and disposal of the Structures; and

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

ORDERED: The Department of Juvenile Justice is hereby authorized and directed to
provide for the razing, demolition and disposal of said Structures under the
terms and conditions which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State
of Georgia, in accordance with applicable laws, policies, and procedures,
including any requirements under Code Section 12-3-55 regarding historic
properties that may pertain to the Structures.
