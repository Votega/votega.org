ORDERED:

On July 9, 2026, Mr. Alfonzo Williams, Sheriff of Burke County, was
indicted on five counts of Hobbs Act Extortion Under Color of Official Right
and one count of Transfer of a Firearm to a Prohibited Person; and

A certified copy of the indictment was transmitted to me pursuant to Code
Section 45-5-6(b) and received on July 16, 2026; and

The 14-day period following receipt of the indictment as prescribed by Code
Section 45-5-6(e) has passed.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Sheriff Billy Hancock of Crisp
County, and Sheriff Tim Watkins of Thomas County are hereby appointed
as the review commission to determine whether the indictment relates to
and adversely affects the administration of the Office of Sheriff of Burke
County such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected
thereby and to make a written report to me within 14 days with a
recommendation as to whether Mr. Williams should be suspended from

office, as required by Code Section 45-5-6(c).
Bel

GOVERNOR
