THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

House District 94 in the Georgia House of Representatives has become
vacant due to the resignation of Representative Karen Bennett.

Therefore, pursuant to GA. CoNsT. Art. V, § II, § VIII and Code Section 21-
2-544, a writ of election is hereby issued to the Secretary of State for a
special election to be held on Tuesday, March 10, 2026, to fill the District
94 seat in the Georgia House of Representatives.
