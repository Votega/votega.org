EXECUTIVE SECRETARY

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 8.510 acres of improved real
property located at 935 United Avenue S.E. (formerly known as East Confederate
Ave.), Atlanta, Georgia 30316 (hereinafter the “Property”) by virtue of a deed dated
November 26, 1901, from The Confederate Soldiers’ Home of Georgia and being
recorded in Deed Book 201, page 651, in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of
Fulton County, which Property is more particularly described on the survey attached
hereto as Exhibit “A”; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Georgia Department of Defense by virtue of an
Executive Order dated December 21, 1953, and being inventoried and recorded as
Real Property Record number 000036; and

By letter dated October 17, 2025, the Georgia Department of Defense declared the
Property to be surplus to its present and future needs and requested that custody
of the Property be transferred to the Georgia Emergency Management and
Homeland Security Agency; and

By letter dated February 24, 2026, the Georgia Emergency Management and
Homeland Security Agency requested that custody of the Property be transferred
from the Georgia Department of Defense to the Georgia Emergency Management
and Homeland Security Agency to allow for more timely responses, strategic
equipment staging, and a hub of logistics during all phases of emergency
management.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR
OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That custody of the above-defined Property located in Fulton County, Georgia is

hereby transferred from the Georgia Department of Defense to the Georgia
Emergency Management and Homeland Security Agency.
