On September 11, 2001, our nation was forever marked by tragedy when nearly
3,000 men, women, and children were murdered as a result of terrorist acts; and

A quarter century has passed since those acts of terror shook our nation, but the
impact of that day remains; and

On the 25" anniversary of this event, we honor those who perished by reflecting
on their lives and the patriotism and passion that drove their sacrifice; and

We remember the brave men and women of our Armed Forces for their vigilance
in protecting our nation from future acts of terror; and

To memorialize this day in our nation’s history, Congress enacted 36 U.S.C. § 144
on December 18, 2001, which designates the 11» day of September as “Patriot Day.”

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the State of Georgia recognize Friday, September 11, 2026, as Patriot Day by
flying the flag of the United States of America and the flag of the State of Georgia
at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds.

IT IS FURTHER

That all Georgians shall observe a moment of silence at 8:46 A.M. on September

11, 2026, to honor the lives lost 25 years ago.
