On May 12, 2026, the Governor signed House Bill 297, creating the Georgia
Transportation Efficiency Authority; and

The Georgia Transportation Efficiency Authority (GTEA) has the authority
to approve all transportation projects within areas under non-attainment as
prescribed by EPA regulation; and

Pursuant to Code Section 50-39-13, the Governor may delegate to the
GTEA, by executive order, his power under applicable federal
transportation planning laws and regulations; and

Pursuant to Code Section 50-39-14, the Governor may delegate to the
GTEA, by executive order, powers under federal laws and regulations for
approval, review, and modification(s) of state-wide transportation
improvement programs.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That all powers vested in the Office of the Governor of the State of Georgia

_for approval, review, and modification(s) of Atlanta Regional

Transportation Improvement Programs are hereby delegated to the Georgia
Transportation Efficiency Authority.

ITIS FURTHER

All prior delegations related to transportation improvement program
approval within the Atlanta Metropolitan Planning Organization region are

superseded by this Order.
