Pursuant to Code Section 20-2-73(a)(1)(A), the Dublin City Board of
Education notified the State Board of Education of its placement on the level
of accreditation immediately preceding loss of accreditation; and

Upon such notification, the State Board of Education conducted a hearing
on August 12, 2026, and recommended to me the suspension of all eligible
members of the Dublin City Board of Education, as prescribed by Code
Section 20-2-73(a)(1)(A); and

A nominating committee composed of education professionals and
advocates from the community in which the local board of education at
issue is located best ensures the highest quality of temporary replacements
to such board.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 20-2-73, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the following eligible members of the Dublin City Board of Education
are hereby suspended: Ms. Amanda Smith, Mr. John Bell, Mr. Jeff Davis,
Ms. Joanna Glover, Ms. Peggy Johnson, Mr. James Lanier, and Mr. Kenny
Walters.

IT IS FURTHER

That there is created the Dublin City Board of Education Nominating
Committee (hereinafter “Committee”), The Committee shall make
nominations to me for temporary replacements to appoint to the Dublin
City Board of Education in accordance with Code Section 20-2-73(a)(2).

IT IS FURTHER

That the Committee shall consist of Mr. Matt Donaldson, the member of the
State Board of Education from the congressional district in which the local

board of education at issue is located; Senator Larry Walker of the 20%
District of the Georgia State Senate, the state senator representing the area
in which the local board of education at issue is located; Representative
Matt Hatchett of the 155" District of the Georgia House of Representatives,
the state representative representing the area in which the local board of
education at issue is located; Mr. Phil Best, former mayor of the city of
Dublin; and Mr. Edwin May, member of the community in which the local
board of education at issue is located. Mr. Donaldson shall serve as

chairman of the Committee.
