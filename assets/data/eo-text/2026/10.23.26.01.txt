THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On September 24, 2026, I suspended Mr. Marcello Banes from the Office of
Chairman of the Newton County Board of Commissioners, as provided by Code
Section 45-5-6(c); and

Pursuant to Code Section 45-5-6(d)(1), there being no applicable general or local
law governing the filling of the temporary vacancy, I am authorized to appoint, for
the duration of the suspension, a replacement officer for the Office of Chairman of
the Newton County Board of Commissioners.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Honorable Tommy Davis of Newton County, Georgia, is hereby appointed
to fill the temporary vacancy in the Office of Chairman of the Newton County Board
of Commissioners.

This 23" day of October 2026.
