EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN PIERCE COUNTY
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Private Bennett Hubert Waters, U.S. Army, of Blackshear, Georgia,
honorably served our country in the United States Army Air Forces in the
17h Bombardment Squadron, 27 Bombardment Group during World War
II; and

Private Waters was captured by the Japanese military on April 9, 1942, and
died during captivity on January 9, 1945; and

Over 80 years later, the Prisoner of War/Missing in Action Accounting
Agency of the U.S. Department of Defense identified Private Waters’
remains, which were returned to Blackshear, Georgia, on March 28, 2026;
and

It is fitting and proper to recognize the sacrifices made by Private Waters by
lowering the flags on the State Capitol building and in Pierce County to half-
staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF PRIVATE BENNETT HUBERT WATERS, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY .

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be
flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Pierce County on
Saturday, April 4, 2026, the day of Private Waters’ internment.
