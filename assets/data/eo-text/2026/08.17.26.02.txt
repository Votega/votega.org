ORDERED:

On July 2, 2026, Hannah Kass filed a Petition for Writ of Mandamus (the
“Petition”) against the Honorable Sonja N. Brown, in her official capacity as
the Judge of the Superior Court of Cobb County, and the Honorable Robert
E. Flournoy, III, in his official capacity as Senior Judge serving by
designation on the Superior Court of Cobb County, in case number
26CV05486; and

The Georgia Department of Law declined representation of Judge Brown
and Judge Flournoy due to a conflict; and

The Petition seeks equitable relief against Judge Brown in her official
capacity as a Superior Court Judge of this State and against Judge Flournoy
in his official capacity as a Senior Judge of this State; and

Mr. Dennis T. Cathey of Cathey & Strain, LLC is an attorney duly admitted
to the State Bar of Georgia; and

Mr. Cathey has agreed to represent Judge Brown and Judge Flournoy at a
rate mutually agreed upon with the Office of the Governor.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-15-70 AND BY THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Mr. Dennis T. Cathey of Cathey & Strain, LLC is hereby appointed as

Special Counsel to assume control and responsibility for the representation
of the Honorable Sonja N. Brown and the Honorable Robert E. Flournoy,

III, in all matters related to the Petition.
