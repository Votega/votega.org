ORDERED:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 146 acres of improved real property
(the “Property”), by virtue of a Quitclaim Deed dated June 23, 2009, from the
Georgia Building Authority and recorded in Deed Book 9694, Pages 597-603, in
the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Clayton County; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Agriculture and is part of the
Atlanta State Farmer’s Market; and

The Property is improved with a structure identified as Wholesale Building O
(BLLIP ID # 8873), (the “Structure”), and as shown in that aerial photograph
attached hereto as Exhibit A, and incorporated herein by reference; and

Said Structure is in disrepair and has far exceeded its serviceable life, and the
Department of Agriculture has deemed the Structure to be a hazard; and

The Department of Agriculture seeks to demolish the Structure to clear space for a
new 45,000 square foot Regulatory Laboratory in alignment with the department’s
long-term master plan; and

By letter dated May 26, 2026, the Commissioner of the Department of Agriculture
requested the State Properties Commission seek an Executive Order from the
Governor to allow for the razing, demolition, and disposal of the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Agriculture is hereby authorized and directed to provide for the
razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interests of the State of Georgia, in accordance
with applicable laws, policies, and procedures, including any requirements under
Code Section 12-3-55 regarding historic properties that may pertain to the Structure.
