ORDERED:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 471.51 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) located at 2620 Highway 155 SW, in Land
Lots 222, 227 and 254, 11‘ District, Rockdale County, and in Land Lot 222 11!
District of Henry County, Georgia, acquired by virtue of a deed dated May 10, 1971,
recorded in Deed Book 97, page 360 in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of
Rockdale County, and filed with the State Properties Commission Real Property
Record as RPR# 05176; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Georgia Department of Natural Resources and
is commonly known as Panola Mountain State Park; and

The Property is improved with several lake structures known as cabin #1 (BLLIP
ID # 25337), lake cabin #2 (BLLIP #25199), lake cabin #3 (BLLIP #25333), storage
shed (BLLIP#25334), and pole barn (not listed in BLLIP) totaling 2,901 square
feet as shown in the aerial photograph attached hereto as Exhibit A (hereinafter
the “Structures”); and

Said Structures are no longer needed for Department operations, are in poor
condition, and beyond reasonable repair; and

By Resolution dated February 23, 2021, the Georgia Department of Natural
Resources requested authority to demolish the Structures.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Georgia Department of Natural Resources is authorized and directed to provide

for the razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structures under the terms and
conditions which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
