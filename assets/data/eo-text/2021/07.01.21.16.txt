EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Dwight Evans of Dekalb
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a
member of the North Georgia Mountains
Authority, for a term of office expiring June
30, 2025, to succeed the Honorable William
Bagwell, Jr., whose term expired February
1, 2021.
