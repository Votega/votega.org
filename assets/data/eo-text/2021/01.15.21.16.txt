EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Bryan Vincent Hunter
of Peach County, Georgia is appointed to
serve as a member of the Georgia Peace
Officer Standards and Training Council, for
a term of office expiring April 7, 2021, to
succeed the Honorable Dolly A. Johnson,
who resigned.

This 15th day of January, 2021.
