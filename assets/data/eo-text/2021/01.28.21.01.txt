WHEREAS:

On October 29, 2020, Spalding County District 5 Commissioner
Donald F. Hawbaker was indicted on one count of Aggravated
Assault, Family Violence, two counts of Possession of a Firearm
During the Commission of a Felony, and five counts of Aggravated
Assault on a Peace Officer; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to
O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(b), and the certified copy was received on
November 4, 2020; and

On December 7, 2020, Executive Order 12.07.20.01 was issued
appointing members to a Review Commission to determine whether
the indictment related to and adversely affected the administration
of the office of Commissioner Hawbaker such that the rights and
interests of the public are adversely affected, and to make a
recommendation of whether Commissioner Hawbaker should be
suspended from office; and

By areport dated December 18, 2020, the Review Commission found
that the indictment of Donald F. Hawbaker does relate to and does
adversely affect the administration of the office of Commissioner,
and that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected
thereby, and recommended that Donald Hawbaker be suspended
from office pursuant to the provisions of O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6; and

On December 30, 2020, Executive Order 12.30.20.03 was issued
suspending Mr. Donald F. Hawbaker from office pending the final
disposition of the case or until the expiration of his term of office,
whichever occurs first, as provided in O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(c); and

Pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(c)(4), there being no general or local
law, ordinance, or resolution governing the appointment of a

ORDERED:

replacement officer, the Governor is authorized to appoint, for the
duration of the suspension, a replacement for the Spalding County
District 5 Commissioner.

NOw, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(c)(), Mr. Claiborne (Clay) W.
Davis III is hereby appointed to fill the temporary vacancy in the
office of Spalding County District 5 Commissioner pending the final
disposition of the charges against Mr. Donald Hawbaker or until the
expiration of his term of office, whichever occurs first.
