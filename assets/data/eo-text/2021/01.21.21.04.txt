THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Eric Ray Burrell of
Franklin County, Georgia is appointed to
serve as the Ninth Congressional District
Commissioner of the Georgia Student
Finance Commission, for a term of office
effective March 15, 2021 and expiring
March 15, 2022, to succeed the Honorable
William Wade, who resigned.

This 21st day of January, 2021.
