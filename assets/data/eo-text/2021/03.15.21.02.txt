The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 21 acres of improved real property
(hereinafter the “Property”) located at 2895 Vineville Avenue, in Land Lot 29 of the
Macon Reserve East, Bibb County, Georgia, acquired by virtue of an Indenture
dated February 16, 1952, recorded in Deed Book 633, page 200 in the office of the
Clerk of Superior Court of Bibb County, and filed with the State Properties
Commission, Real Property Record as RPR# 00093; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Georgia Department of Education and is
commonly known as Georgia Academy for the Blind; and

The Property is improved with two buildings known as Maintenance Engineer
Residence (BLLIP ID # 4618) and Maintenance Building (BLLIP ID # 4616) as
shown in the aerial photograph attached hereto as Exhibit A (hereinafter the
“Structures”); and

The Maintenance Engineer Residence is in poor condition has been abandoned due
to health risks posed by asbestos, mold, and lead paint, and the Maintenance Building
is over sixty years old and is in poor condition; and

By official action dated February 10, 2021, the Georgia Department of Education
requested authority to demolish the Structures.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Georgia Department of Education is authorized and directed to provide for the

razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structures under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
