THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

Mr. Donald Richard “Dick” Donovan, District Attorney of the
Paulding Judicial Circuit, was indicted on February 17, 2021, on one
count of Bribery, one count of Violation of Oath by Public Officer,
and two counts of False Swearing; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Office of the Governor
pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(b), and the certified copy was received
on February 17, 2021; and

Pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(e), Mr. Donovan has voluntarily
authorized his suspension from office as District Attorney of the
Paulding Judicial Circuit.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mr. Donald Richard “Dick” Donovan, District Attorney of the
Paulding Judicial Circuit, is hereby suspended from office
immediately and without further action pending the final disposition
of the case or until the expiration of his term of office, whichever
occurs first, as provided in O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(c).

Bill

GOVERNOR

This 24TH day of February, 2021.
