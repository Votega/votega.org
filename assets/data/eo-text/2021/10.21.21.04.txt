The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 0.4838 of an acre of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) located at 1375 Constitution Road, in Land
Lot 79, 15 District, DeKalb County, Georgia, acquired by virtue of a Warranty
Deed dated December 20, 1995, recorded in Deed Book 8809, Pages 12-14, anda
Quitclaim Deed dated December 20, 1995, recorded in Deed Book 8820, Pages
423-425, in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Dekalb County, and filed
with the State Properties Commission Real Property Record as RPR# 08754.01
and 08754.02; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Georgia Department of Corrections and is
commonly known as Fleet Maintenance Facility- Atlanta; and

The Property is improved with structures known as the Auto Garage (BLLIP ID #
713), and Radio/Body Shop (BLLIP ID # 716), totaling 6,162 square feet as shown
in the aerial photograph attached hereto as Exhibit A (hereinafter the
“Structures”); and

Said Structures are in disrepair, no longer needed for Department operations, and
must be demolished to make space for new construction; and

By Resolution dated September 2, 2021, the Georgia Department of Corrections
requested authority to demolish the Structures.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Georgia Department of Corrections is authorized and directed to provide for the

razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structures under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
