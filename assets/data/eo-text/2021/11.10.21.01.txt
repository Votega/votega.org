THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN JACKSON COUNTY

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Deputy Lena Nicole Marshall, with the Jackson County Sheriff's
Office, passed away on Monday, November 8, 2021, after being shot
in the line of duty on Friday, November 5, 2021; and

In addition to her service with the Jackson County Sheriff's Office,
Deputy Marshall also served with the Winder Police Department, the
Barrow County Sheriffs Office, the Helen Police Department, and
the Habersham County Sheriff's Office; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Deputy Marshall’s service to
Jackson County and the State of Georgia by lowering the flags on the
State Capitol building and in Jackson County to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF DEPUTY LENA NICOLE MARSHALL, PURSUANT TO
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia
be flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Jackson
County on Monday, November 15, 2021, the day of Deputy Marshall’s
interment, until sunset.

This 10T day of November, 2021.
