ATTEST:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 75 acres located at 4602
Fulton Mill Road, in Land Lot 30 of the 4% District, Bibb County, Georgia, by
virtue of a deed dated March 5, 1974, from Bibb County, Georgia and recorded
in Deed Book 1206, Pages 467 - 471 in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court
of Bibb County, and filed with the State Properties Commission as Real
Property Record # 005840; and

Said Property is in custody of the Georgia Department of Corrections and is
operated as Central State Prison; and

The Property is improved with a building identified as Central State Prison
Greenhouse, BLLIP ID# 4432, as shown in the aerial photo attached hereto
as Exhibit A (hereinafter the “Structure”); and

By Resolution dated April 18, 2019, the Georgia Board of Corrections
requested authority to demolish the Structure since it is no longer in use and
is in disrepair.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Georgia Department of Corrections is authorized and directed to provide

for the razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and
conditions which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
