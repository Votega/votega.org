ORDERED:

On July 13, 2020, the Honorable Terrinee L. Gundy filed a lawsuit against The Honorable
Louisa Abbot, James Balli, Richard Hyde, the Honorable Stacey K. Hydrick, William Pope
Langdale, the Honorable Robert McBurney, Jamala S. McFadden, Michael Register,
Warren Selby, and Edward Tolley, all individually and as members of the Judicial
Qualifications Commission (“JQC”) in the Superior Court of Fulton County, Civil Action
No. 2020CV338280; and

On May 10, 2021, a Final Order was entered in the Superior Court of Fulton County in
Civil Action No. 2020CV338280 denying the Honorable Terrinee L. Gundy’s Quo
Warranto Petition and the Honorable Terrinee L. Gundy subsequently filed an Appeal of
the Fulton County Superior Court’s Final Order with the Georgia Court of Appeals; and

JQC Director, Chuck Boring, because of a conflict, has declined representation of the
Honorable Robert McBurney, Richard Hyde, and Jamala S. McFadden in this case; and

The Georgia Department of Law has declined representation of the Honorable Robert
McBurney, Richard Hyde, and Jamala S. McFadden; and

Mr. William D. Barwick is an attorney duly admitted to the State Bar of Georgia; and

Mr. William D. Barwick has agreed to represent the Honorable Robert McBurney, Richard
Hyde, and Jamala S. McFadden in this action at a rate mutually agreed upon by the Office
of the Governor and Mr. Barwick.

Now, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO O.C.G.A. § 45-15-70 AND BY THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That William D. Barwick of Ichter Davis, LLC is hereby appointed as Special Counsel to
assume control and responsibility for the representation of the Honorable Robert
McBurney, Richard Hyde, and Jamala S. McFadden; all individually and as members of
the JQC, in all matters related to Judge Gundy’s Appeal, Case No. A21A1763.
