EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Randy L. Dellinger of
Gwinnett County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as a member of the North Georgia
Mountains Authority, for a term of office
expiring June 30, 2025, to succeed the
Honorable Delos Yancey, whose term
expired February 1, 2021.

This 1st day of July, 2021

GOVERNOR
