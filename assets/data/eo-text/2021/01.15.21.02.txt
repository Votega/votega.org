EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED: That the Honorable David Joseph Cyr of
Houston County, Georgia is appointed to
serve as a member of the Georgia Ports
Authority, for a term of office expiring June
30, 2024, to succeed the Honorable James

Walters, whose term expired June 30,
2020.

This 15th day of January, 2021.

rade

GOVERNOR
