FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALE-STAFE

The state of Georgia mourns the loss of former state Senator Ross
Tolleson who passed away on November 5, 2021; and

Senator Tolleson served the citizens of Georgia’s 18th Senate District
(later becoming the 20TH Senate District) in the Georgia State Senate
from 2002 — 2015; and

Ross Tolleson was a smart businessman, and a tireless public servant
who devoted himself to “working for the people.”

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF SENATOR ROSS TOLLESON, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the state of Georgia

shall be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds
throughout Georgia on Friday, November 12, 2021, the day of

Senator Tolleson’s interment.
