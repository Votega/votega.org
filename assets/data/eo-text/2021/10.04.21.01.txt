ORDERED:

On September 7, 2021, Mr. Mark Preston Jones, District Attorney of the
Chattahoochee Judicial Circuit was indicted on two counts of Influencing
Witnesses, one count of Attempted Subornation of Perjury, two counts of
Violation of Oath By Public Officer, two counts of Bribery, and two counts
of Attempted Violation of Oath by Public Officer; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-
5-6(b), and the certified copy was received on September 7, 2021; and

An Executive Order was issued on September 22, 2021, appointing
members to the Review Commission to determine whether the indictment
relates to and adversely affects the administration of the office of District
Attorney such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely
affected; and

The Review Commission found that the indictment of Mr. Mark Preston
Jones does relate to and does adversely affect the administration of the
office of District Attorney, and that the rights and interests of the public are
adversely affected thereby; and recommended that Mr. Jones be suspended
from office pursuant to the provisions of O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mr. Mark Preston Jones is hereby suspended from office immediately

and without further action pending the final disposition of the case or until
the expiration of his term of office, whichever occurs first, as provided in
