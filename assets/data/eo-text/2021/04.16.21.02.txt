EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS: The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 4.446 acres located at 935 United
Avenue S.E. (formerly known as East Confederate Ave.), in Land Lot 10 of the 14"
District, Fulton County, Georgia, by virtue of a Quitclaim Deed dated June 20, 2019,
from the Georgia Building Authority and recorded in Deed Book 60192, Pages 54 -
62 in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Fulton County, and filed with the
State Properties Commission as Real Property Record # 012287; and

WHEREAS: _ Said Property is in custody of the Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland
Security Agency by an Executive Order dated March 25, 2021, and filed as Real
Property Record #012520; and

WHEREAS: | The Property is improved with a building identified as Building #3 as shown in the
aerial photo attached hereto as Exhibit A (hereinafter the “Structure”); and

WHEREAS: By Memorandum dated April 12, 2021, the Georgia Emergency Management and
Homeland Security requested authority to demolish the Structure to improve the
security of the campus and provide additional parking space for the State Operations
Center.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

ORDERED: The Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland Security Agency is authorized

and directed to provide for the razing, demolition and disposal of said Structure
under the terms and conditions which are deemed to be in the best interest of the

GOVERNOR
