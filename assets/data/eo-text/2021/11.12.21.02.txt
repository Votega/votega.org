THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN HENRY COUNTY

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Officer Paramhans Desai, 38, with the Henry County Police
Department, passed away on Monday, November 8, 2021, after being
shot in the line of duty on Thursday, November 4, 2021; and

In addition to his service with the Henry County Police Department,
Officer Desai previously served with the DeKalb County Police
Department and the Georgia Department of Corrections; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Officer Desai’s service to Henry
County and the State of Georgia by lowering the flags on the State
Capitol building and in Henry County to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF OFFICER PARAMHANS DESAI, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia

be flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Henry
County on Sunday, November 14, 2021, the day of Officer Desai’s

interment, until sunset.
e
e

GOVERNOR

This 12TH day of November, 2021.
