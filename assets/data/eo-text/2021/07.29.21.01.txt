THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:
That the Honorable Verda M. Colvin is hereby appointed
Justice of the Supreme Court of Georgia to fill the vacancy

created by the resignation of the Honorable Harold D. Melton,
for a term of office ending December 31, 2022.

THIS 29TH DAY OF JULY, 2021.

BLL

’ GOVERNOR
