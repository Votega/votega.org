FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF STAFF

The Honorable Robert “Bob” Joseph Dole, United States Senator, passed away on
Sunday, December 5, 2021; and

Bob Dole first answered his call to a lifetime of public service in 1942 when he joined
the United States Army’s Enlisted Reserve Corps to fight in World War II, where he
received two Purple Hearts and the Bronze Star with “V” Device for valor; and

In addition to his service in the United States Army, Mr. Dole served in the United
States House of Representatives for eight years and in the United States Senate for
twenty-seven years; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Senator Bob Dole’s service to citizens of the
United States of America by lowering the flags throughout Georgia to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE MEMORY
OF THE HONORABLE ROBERT “BOB” JOSEPH DOLE, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That, in conjunction with a proclamation by the President of the United States, the
flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia shall be flown at half-
staff on all state buildings and grounds from this date until sunset Thursday,
December 9, 2021.

This 67 day of December, 2021.
