EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That effective January 16, 2021, the Honorable Shawn Ellen
LaGrua is hereby appointed Justice of the Supreme Court of
Georgia to fill the vacancy created by the resignation of the
Honorable Keith R. Blackwell, for a term of office ending
December 31, 2022.

THIS 7TH DAY OF JANUARY, 2021.

Pau

” GOVERNOR
