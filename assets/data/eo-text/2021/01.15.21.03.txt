EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Don Anthony
Grantham, Sr., of Richmond County,
Georgia is appointed to serve as a member
of the Georgia Ports Authority, for a term of
office effective January 25, 2021 and
expiring June 30, 2024, to succeed the
Honorable Kevin “Catfish” Jackson whose
term expired June 30, 2020.

This 15th day of January, 2021.

SPL

GOVERNOR
