WHEREAS:

On July 6, 2021, Augusta-Richmond County Commissioner Sammie
Lee Sias was indicted in the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Georgia on one count of Destruction, Alteration,
or Falsification of Records in a Federal Investigation and one count
of False Statement or Representation Made to a Department or
Agency of the United States; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to
O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(b), and the certified copy was received on July 9,
2021; and

An Executive Order was issued on July 26, 2021, appointing
members to the Review Commission to determine whether the
indictment relates to and adversely affects the administration of the
office of Augusta-Richmond County Commissioner such that the
rights and interests of the public are adversely affected; and

On August 9, 2021, pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(c), members of the
review commission requested a 30-day extension for the commission
to issue its report; and

On August 20, 2021, Executive Order 08.20.21.01 was issued to
extend the deadline for the review commission to issue its report and
recommendation; and

By the report dated August 24, 2021, the Review Commission found
that the indictment of Sammie Lee Sias does relate to and does
adversely affect the administration of the office of Commissioner,
and that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected
thereby; and recommended that Sammie Lee Sias be suspended from
office pursuant to the provisions of O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6.

ORDERED:

NOw, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mr. Sammie Lee Sias is hereby suspended from office
immediately and without further action pending the final disposition
of the case or until the expiration of his term of office, whichever
occurs first, as provided in 45-5-6(c).

This 20 ™ day of August, 2021.
