EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Jerry Don Spillers Jr. of
Peach County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as a member of the Georgia Board of
Dentistry, for a term of office expiring
August 1, 2022, to succeed the Honorable
Bert Yeargan, who resigned.

This 29th day of December 2021

BFL

GOVERNOR
