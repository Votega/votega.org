The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 39.60 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) located at 1301 Constitution Road, in Land
Lot 79, 15'" District, DeKalb County, Georgia, acquired by virtue of a Warranty
Deed dated August 9, 1974, recorded in Deed Book 3222, page 312, in the office of
the Clerk of Superior Court of DeKalb County, and filed with the State Properties
Commission Real Property Record as RPR# 05895; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Georgia Department of Corrections and is
commonly known as Metro Reentry Facility; and

The Property is improved with structures known as the Metro State Prison
Training Storage, Block House (BLLIP ID # 694) and Pole Barn (BLLIP ID # 708),
totaling 1,193 square feet as shown in the aerial photograph attached hereto as
Exhibit A (hereinafter the “Structures”); and

Said Structures have been abandoned, contain no salvageable equipment of value and
have been identified as hazardous; and

By Resolution dated September 2, 2021, the Georgia Department of Corrections
requested authority to demolish the Structures.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Georgia Department of Corrections is authorized and directed to provide for the

razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structures under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
