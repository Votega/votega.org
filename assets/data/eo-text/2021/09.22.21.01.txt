ORDERED:

On September 7, 2021, Mr. Mark Preston Jones, District Attorney of the
Chattahoochee Judicial Circuit, was indicted on two counts of Influencing
Witnesses, one count of Attempted Subornation of Perjury, two counts of Violation
of Oath By Public Officer, two counts of Bribery, and two counts of Attempted
Violation of Oath by Public Officer; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(b),
and the certified copy was received on September 7, 2021; and

The fourteen-day period following the receipt of the indictment as prescribed by
O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(e) has passed; and

Pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(b), since the Attorney General of Georgia brought
the indictment against Mr. Jones, the Attorney General shall not serve on the
commission, and a retired Supreme Court Justice or retired Court of Appeals
Judge shall serve in place of the Attorney General.

Now, THEREFORE, BY VIRTUE OF THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT 1S HEREBY

That the Honorable Harold Melton, retired Chief Justice of the Supreme Court of
Georgia; the Honorable Joe Mulholland, District Attorney of the South Georgia
Judicial Circuit; and the Honorable Samir Patel, District Attorney of the Cherokee
Judicial Circuit, are hereby appointed as the review commission to determine
whether the above-mentioned indictment relates to and adversely affects the
administration of the duties of district attorney by Mr. Jones such that the rights
and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby, and to make a written
report to me within fourteen days with a recommendation as to whether Mr. Jones
should be suspended from office as required by O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(c).
