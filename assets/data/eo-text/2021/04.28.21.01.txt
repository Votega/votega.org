THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

House District 34 in the Georgia House of Representatives has become
vacant due to the resignation of Representative Bert Reeves.

Therefore, pursuant to Article V, Section II, Paragraph VIII of the
Constitution of the State of Georgia and Section 21-2-544 of the Official
Code of Georgia Annotated, a writ of election is hereby issued to the
Secretary of State for a special election to be held on Tuesday, June 15, 2021,
to fill the District 34 seat in the Georgia House of Representatives.

This 2% day of April, 2021.

GOVERNOR
