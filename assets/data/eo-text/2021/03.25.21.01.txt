EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS: The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 4.446 acres located at 935 United
Avenue, S.E. (formerly known as East Confederate Ave.), in Land Lot 10 of the 14'
District, Fulton County, Georgia, by virtue of a Quitclaim Deed dated June 20, 2019,
from the Georgia Building Authority and recorded in Deed Book 60192, Pages 54 -
62 in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Fulton County, and filed with the
State Properties Commission as Real Property Record #12287, which property is
depicted as “Tract C” on the attached Exhibit “A” (hereinafter “Property”); and

WHEREAS: _ Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Defense and improved with a
building identified as Building #3; and

WHEREAS: _ Said Property has been vacant since the Department of Defense relocated to Clay
National Guard Center; and

WHEREAS: The Georgia Department of Defense requested that custody of the Property be
transferred to the Georgia Emergency Management Agency and the Georgia
Emergency Management Agency has requested custody of the Property.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

ORDERED: That custody of the above-defined Property located in Fulton County, Georgia is
hereby transferred from the Georgia Department of Defense to the Georgia
Emergency Management Agency.
