EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On November 3, 2020, the Democratic Party of Georgia filed a
lawsuit against Governor Brian P. Kemp, Candice Broce, and ten
unidentified individuals in U.S. District Court, Northern District of
Georgia, 1:20-CV-04495-LMM; and

The Attorney General, because of a conflict, has declined
representation of the Governor and his co-defendants in this case;
and

Ms. Holly Pierson is an attorney duly admitted to the State Bar of
Georgia.

Now, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO O.C.G.A. § 45-15-70 AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Ms. Holly Pierson of Pierson Law LLC is hereby appointed as
Special Counsel to assume control and responsibility for the
representation of Governor Kemp in all matters related to
Democratic Party of Georgia v. Kemp et al.
