ORDERED:

On February 10, 2021, Fani T. Willis, Fulton County District Attorney
provided notice that she had “opened an investigation into attempts
to influence the administration of the 2020 Georgia General
Election”; and

The Attorney General has declined representation of the Secretary of
State in this matter; and

Mr. Jack Sharman is an attorney duly admitted to the State Bar of
Georgia; and

Mr. Jack Sharman has agreed to represent the Office of the Secretary
of State in this investigation at a rate mutually agreed upon by the
Secretary of State and Mr. Sharman.

Now, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO O.C.G.A. § 45-15-70 AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mr. Jack Sharman of Lightfoot, Franklin & White LLC is hereby
appointed as Special Counsel to assume control and responsibility
for the representation of the Office of the Secretary of State in all
matters related to District Attorney Willis’ investigation.

ZI,

GOVERNOR
