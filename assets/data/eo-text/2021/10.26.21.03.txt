The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 7.58 acres of improved real property
(hereinafter the “Property”) located at 8631 US Highway 301, in GMD 1607", Evans
County, Georgia, acquired by virtue of a Warranty Deed dated March 10, 1987,
recorded in Deed Book 91, Pages 78-80 in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court
of Evans County, and filed with the State Properties Commission Real Property
Record as RPR#¥ 07437; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Georgia Department of Corrections and is
commonly known as Smith Transition Center; and

The Property is improved with a structure known as a Radio Building (BLLIP ID #
616), totaling 80 square feet as shown in the aerial photograph attached hereto as
Exhibit “A” (hereinafter the “Structure”); and

Said Structure must be removed and rebuilt in a new location to make way for new
construction; and

By Resolution dated September 2, 2021, the Georgia Department of Corrections
requested authority to demolish the Structures.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Georgia Department of Corrections is authorized and directed to provide for the

razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
