THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

In accordance with the Georgia Constitution, Art. IJ, § III, Para. I, the
office of Commissioner of Insurance is now vacant; and

In accordance with the Georgia Constitution, Art. V, § II, Para. VIII,
in the event of a vacancy in the office of Commissioner of Insurance,
the Governor shall fill the vacancy.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Honorable John F. King is hereby appointed Commissioner
of Insurance for the State of Georgia, for a term of office ending
January 9, 2023.

This 382 day of December, 2021.
