EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Christopher Clarence
Womack of Greene County, Georgia is
appointed to serve as a member of the
Georgia Ports Authority, for a term of office
expiring June 30, 2024, to succeed the
Honorable Paul Bowers, whose term
expired June 30, 2020.

This 15th day of January, 2021.

Bib

GOVERNOR
