ORDERED:

On August 2, 2021, Mr. Royce Reeves, Ward 2 Commissioner on the
Cordele City Commission, was indicted on two counts of Obstruction
of an Officer, one count of Violation of Oath by a Public Officer, one
count of Criminal Trespass, and one count of Disorderly Conduct;
and

On September 7, 2021, pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(b), an
Executive Order was issued appointing members to a committee
charged with determining whether the above-mentioned indictment
relates to and adversely affects the administration of duties by
Commissioner Reeves such that the rights and interests of the public
are adversely affected thereby; and

On October 15, 2021, pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(c), members of
the review commission requested a 60-day extension for the
commission to issue its report.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the extension for the review commission to issue its report in

the matter of the indictment of Cordele City Commissioner Royce
Reeves is hereby extended to Tuesday, December 14, 2021.
