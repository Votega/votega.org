The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 1.82 acres of improved real property
(hereinafter the “Property”) located at o Padlock Road, in Land Lot 873, 3rd
District, Floyd County, Georgia, acquired by virtue of deeds dated November 25,
1890, March 4, 1891, and March 5, 1891, recorded in Deed Book VIII, pages 379,
380, and 381 respectively, in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Floyd
County, and filed with the State Properties Commission Real Property Record as
RPR#s 002784, 002785, and 002786 respectively; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Georgia Department of Education and is
commonly known as Georgia School for the Deaf; and

The Property is improved with a fifty-two-year-old building known as the
Talmadge Gymnasium and Swimming Facility, Building GSD — 22 (BLLIP ID #
9540) as shown in the aerial photograph attached hereto as Exhibit A (hereinafter
the “Structure”); and

Said Structure has been abandoned due to health risks posed by asbestos and mold
and is of no use to the Georgia School for the Deaf; and

By official action dated February 10, 2021, the Georgia Department of Education
requested authority to demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Georgia Department of Education is authorized and directed to provide for the

razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
