EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED: That the Honorable Bridget Coman Knowles
of Bibb County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as a member of the Georgia Composite
Board of Professional Counselors, Social
Workers, and Marriage and Family
Therapists, for a term of office expiring
October 7, 2024, to succeed the Honorable
Steve E. Livingston, whose term expired
December 31, 2017.

This 8 day of October, 2021
