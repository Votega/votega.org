THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN WHEELER COUNTY

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Officer Dylan McCauley Harrison, 26, with the Alamo Police
Department, passed away on Saturday, October 9, 2021, after being
targeted following an arrest made earlier in the day; and

In addition to his service with the Alamo Police Department, Officer
Harrison also served as a narcotics agent with the Oconee Drug Task
Force Agency; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Officer Harrison’s service to the
City of Alamo and the State of Georgia by lowering the flags on the
State Capitol building and in Wheeler County to half-staff.

Now, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF OFFICER DYLAN MCCAULEY HARRISON,
PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia
be flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Wheeler
County on Friday, October 15, 2021, the day of Officer Harrison’s
interment, until sunset.

This 14TH day of October, 2021.

rae

GOVERNOR
