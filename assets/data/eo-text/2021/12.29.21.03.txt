EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That, effective January 1, 2022, the Honorable Danielle P.
Roberts is hereby appointed Superior Court Judge of the Flint
Judicial Circuit to fill the seat created by the passage of House
Bill 786 (Act Number 493) during the 2020 session of the
Georgia General Assembly, for a term of office ending
December 31, 2024.

THIS 29TH DAY OF DECEMBER, 2021.

Aan

OVERNOR
