ORDERED:

Tracy J. BeMent, Court Administrator for the 10 Judicial Circuit has been served
with a subpoena in the matter of Willie Saunders v. Governor Brian Kemp,
Augusta, Georgia, Columbia County, Georgia, and Burke County, Georgia, Civil
Action Number 2021RCCVo0o0277 in the Superior Court of the Augusta Judicial
Circuit; and

Court Administrator BeMent has requested representation by counsel in the
above-referenced matter and the Georgia Department of Law has determined that
it cannot provide such representation due to a conflict of interest; and

Mr. Robert Homlar is an attorney duly admitted to the State Bar of Georgia; and

Mr. Robert Homlar has agreed to represent Court Administrator BeMent in this
action at a rate mutually agreed upon by the Office of the Governor and Mr.
Homlar.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO O.C.G.A. § 45-15-70 AND THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mr. Robert Homlar of Robert T. Homlar, P.C. is hereby appointed as Special
Counsel and Conflict Counsel to assume control and responsibility for the
representation of Court Administrator Tracy J. BeMent of the 10% Judicial Circuit
in all matters related to the above-referenced civil action.
