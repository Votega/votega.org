ATTEST:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 110.276 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) located at 650 Mount Vernon Alston Road, in
GMD 1343" and 1757%, Montgomery County, Georgia, acquired by virtue of a
Warranty Deed dated December 19, 1968, recorded in Deed Book 55, Pages 566-7,
in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Montgomery County, and filed with
the State Properties Commission Real Property Record as RPR# 03956; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Georgia Department of Corrections and is
commonly known as Montgomery State Prison; and

The Property is improved with several structures located on the old Egg Laying
Facility at Montgomery State Prison known as Farm Egg Cooler (BLLIP ID # 1832),
Farm Office (BLLIP ID # 2047), Layer House#1 (BLLIP ID # 6326), Layer
House#2 (BLLIP ID # 1822), Layer House#3 (BLLIP ID # 1820), Layer House#4
(BLLIP ID # 2039), Farm Storage (BLLIP ID # 2048), and Covered Storage
Buildings (BLLIP ID # 43527), totaling 56,257 square feet as shown in the aerial
photograph attached hereto as Exhibit A (hereinafter the “Structures”); and

Said Structures are in poor condition and no longer in use; and

By Resolution dated September 2, 2021, the Georgia Department of Corrections
requested authority to demolish the Structures.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Georgia Department of Corrections is authorized and directed to provide for the

razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structures under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
