EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN DECATUR COUNTY

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

On Saturday, February 27, 2021, Captain Justin Williams Bedwell of
the Decatur County Sheriff's Office died in the line of duty following
a traffic stop in Seminole County; and

Captain Bedwell honorably served the citizens of Decatur County for
19 years; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Captain Bedwell’s service to
Decatur County and the State of Georgia by lowering the flags on the
State Capitol building and in Decatur County to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF CAPTAIN JUSTIN WILLIAMS BEDWELL,
PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia

be flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Decatur
County on Thursday, March 4, 2021, the day of Captain Bedwell’s

interment, until sunset.
e
e

GOVERNOR

This 2NP day of March, 2021.
