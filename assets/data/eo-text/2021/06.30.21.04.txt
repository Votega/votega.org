EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Grant Thomas of Fulton
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as
ex-officio chairperson of the Georgia All-
Payer Claims Database Advisory Committee,
by virtue of his position as Director of
Health Strategy and Coordination, for a
term of office concurrent with his
appointment as Director of Health Strategy
and Coordination, to succeed the Honorable
Ryan Michael Loke, who resigned.

This 30 day of June, 2021
