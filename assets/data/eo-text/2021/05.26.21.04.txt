ORDERED:

The Georgia Board of Education has requested representation by counsel in the
matter of Avis King vs. Georgia Department of Education, Civil Action No.
2018CV302764, in the Superior Court of Fulton County and the Georgia
Department of Law has determined that it cannot provide such representation due
to a conflict of interest; and

By Executive Order 10.02.18.04, Mr. Vincent Toreno was appointed to represent
the Georgia Board of Education but is no longer able to provide representation in
this action; and

Ms. Holly Pierson is an attorney duly admitted to the State Bar of Georgia; and
Ms. Holly Pierson has agreed to represent the Georgia Board of Education in this
action at a rate mutually agreed upon by the Office of the Governor and Ms.
Pierson.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO O.C.G.A. § 45-10-70 AND THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Executive Order 10.02.18.04 is hereby rescinded and replaced by this Order.

IT IS FURTHER
That Ms. Holly Pierson of Pierson Law LLC is hereby appointed as Special Counsel

and Conflict Counsel to assume control and responsibility for the representation
of the Georgia Board of Education in all matters related to the above-referenced
