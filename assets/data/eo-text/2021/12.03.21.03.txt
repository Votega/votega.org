THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN CLAYTON COUNTY

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Clayton County Field Training Officer Henry Laxson, 27, passed
away on Tuesday, November 30, 2021, after being shot in the line of
duty; and

Officer Laxson joined the Clayton County Police Department in 2017
where he served as a member of the Targeted Interdiction General
Enforcement Response unit and the SWAT team; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Officer Laxson’s service to
Clayton County and the State of Georgia by lowering the flags on the
State Capitol building and in Clayton County to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF OFFICER HENRY LAXSON, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia
be flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Clayton
County on Tuesday, December 7, 2021, the day of Officer Laxson’s
interment, until sunset.

This 382 day of December, 2021.

Bll

GOVERNOR
