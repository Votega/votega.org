ORDERED:

On February 1, 2021, City of Nashville Mayor Taylor Scarbrough was
indicted on one count of Theft of Services, one count of Theft by Conversion,
one count of Theft by Deception, and one count of Criminal Damage to
Property in the Second Degree; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to O.C.G.A. 8 45-
5-6(b), and the certified copy was received on February 8, 2021; and

An Executive Order was issued on March 3, 2021, appointing members to
the Review Commission to determine whether the indictment relates to and
adversely affects the administration of the office of Mayor of the City of
Nashville such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely
affected; and

By the report dated March 16, 2021, the Review Commission found that the
indictment of Taylor Scarbrough does relate to and does adversely affect the
administration of the office of Mayor, and that the rights and interests of
the public are adversely affected thereby; and recommended that Taylor
Scarbrough be suspended from office pursuant to the provisions of O.C.G.A.

§ 45-5-6.

Now, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mr. Taylor Scarbrough is hereby suspended from office immediately

and without further action pending the final disposition of the case or until
the expiration of his term of office, whichever occurs first, as provided in
