EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That the Honorable Andrew A. Pinson is hereby appointed
Judge of the Court of Appeals of the State of Georgia to fill the
vacancy created by the appointment of the Honorable Verda
M. Colvin to the Supreme Court of Georgia, for a term of office
ending December 31, 2022.

THIS 30TH DAY OF AUGUST, 2021.

BL

GOVERNOR
