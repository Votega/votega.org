EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Gregory William Garrett
of Greene County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as a member of the Georgia Public
Telecommunications Commission, for a
term of office expiring June 30, 2025, to
succeed the Honorable John W.

Stephenson, Jr., whose term expired June
30, 2019.

This 16 day of July, 2021

Bib.

GOVERNOR
