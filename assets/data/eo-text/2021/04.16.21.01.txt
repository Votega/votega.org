ATTEST:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 2.066 acres located at 1050 Canal
Road, in the 1356" District, Glynn County, Georgia, by virtue of a deed from Glynn
County, which deed is recorded in Glynn County Superior Court Clerk’s Office in
Deed Book 32-R, folio 265, and is filed with the State Properties Commission as Real
Property Record # 07830, which property is further described on Exhibit “A”
attached hereto (hereinafter “Property”); and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Defense and is improved with
a 7,651 sq. ft. building which Property was previously leased to the Secretary of the
Air Force and used as part of the Georgia Air National Guard Site; and

The Georgia Department of Defense has declared the property surplus to its needs
and requested that custody of the Property be transferred to the Georgia
Department of Natural Resources; and

The Georgia Department of Natural Resources has requested custody of the
Property for use as its Coastal District Office of the Environmental Protection
Division since the Property is more conveniently located and better suited for its
use than its current Costal District Office.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR
OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That custody of the above-defined Property located in Glynn County, Georgia is
hereby transferred from the Georgia Department of Defense to the Georgia

Department of Natural Resources.

GOVERNOR
