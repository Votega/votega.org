EXECUTIVE SECRETARY

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 8.6609 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) located at 650 Hi Hope Lane, in Land Lot 13,
7th Land District, Gwinnett County, commonly known as the Gwinnett Regional
Youth Detention Center, acquired by virtue of a Warranty Deed dated July 27,
1978, recorded in Deed Book 1531, page 350 in the office of the Clerk of Superior
Court of Gwinnett County, and filed with the State Properties Commission Real
Property Record as RPR# 06509; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Juvenile Justice; and

The Property is improved with a building known as the Gwinnett Regional Youth
Detention Center as shown in the aerial photograph attached hereto as Exhibit A
(hereinafter the “Structure”); and

Said Structure has been identified by the Department of Juvenile Justice to be the
future site of the new Receiving Center and will need to be demolished to
accommodate the Receiving Center; and

By Resolution dated February 25, 2021, the Board of Juvenile Justice requested the
Structure to be demolished.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Juvenile Justice is authorized and directed to provide for the

razing, demolition and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are dgemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
