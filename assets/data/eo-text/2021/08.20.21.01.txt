EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On July 6, 2021, in the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Georgia, Augusta-Richmond County Commissioner
Sammie Lee Sias was indicted on one count of Destruction,
Alteration, or Falsification of Records in a Federal Investigation and
one count of False Statement or Representation Made to a
Department or Agency of the United States; and

On July 26, 2021, pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(b), an Executive
Order was issued appointing members to a committee charged with
determining whether the above-mentioned indictment relates to and
adversely affects the administration of duties by Commissioner Sias
such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected
thereby; and

On August 9, 2021, pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(c), members of the
review commission requested a 30-day extension for the commission
to issue its report.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the extension for the review commission to issue its report in
the matter of the indictment of Augusta-Richmond County
Commissioner Sammie Sias is hereby extended to Wednesday,
September 8, 2021.

This 20TH day of August, 2021.

Bh,

GOVERNOR
