THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND BIBB COUNTY
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF STAFF

Bibb County Sheriffs Deputy Christopher Knight was killed in the line of duty
on Tuesday, April 6, 2021; and

The people of Georgia are grateful for Deputy Knight’s bravery, service, and
sacrifice, and mourn his loss; and

The people of Georgia wish to honor the life of Deputy Knight as we share in the
grief of his family and friends, and pray for their comfort and healing in the days
to come.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR DEPUTY
CHRISTOPHER KNIGHT, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia shall be flown

at half-staff on the State Capitol building and grounds and throughout Bibb County
on Tuesday, April 13, 2021, the day of Deputy Knight’s interment.

Bll

GOVERNOR

This 9TH day of April, 2021.
