EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On July 13, 2020, the Honorable Terrinee L. Gundy filed a lawsuit
against The Honorable Louisa Abbot; James Balli; Richard Hyde; the
Honorable Stacey K. Hydrick; William Pope Langdale; The
Honorable Robert McBurney; Jamala S. McFadden; Michael
Register; Warren Selby; and Edward Tolley; all individually and as
members of the Judicial Qualifications Commission (“JQC”) in the
Superior Court of Fulton County, Civil Action No. 2020CV338280;
and

JQC Director, Chuck Boring, because of a conflict, has declined
representation of the Honorable Robert McBurney, Richard Hyde,
and Jamala S. McFadden in this case.

Now, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO O.C.G.A. § 45-15-70 AND BY
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Honorable Chris Carr, Attorney General of Georgia, is
hereby appointed as Special Counsel to assume control and
responsibility for the representation of the Honorable Robert
McBurney, Richard Hyde, and Jamala S. McFadden; all individually
and as members of the Judicial Qualifications Commission in all
matters related to Judge Gundy’s Quo Warranto Petition.

This IZ day of January, 2021.
