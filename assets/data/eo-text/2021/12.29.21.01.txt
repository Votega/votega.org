THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That, effective January 1, 2022, the Honorable Henry R.
Thompson is hereby appointed Superior Court Judge of the
Cobb Judicial Circuit to fill the seat created by the passage of
House Bill 786 (Act Number 493) during the 2020 session of
the Georgia General Assembly, for a term of office ending
December 31, 2024.

THIS 291TH DAY OF DECEMBER, 2021.

ZY LL

" GOVERNOR
