EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN CHEROKEE COUNTY
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

On Wednesday, June 16, 2021, Officer Joe Burson of the Holly Springs Police

Department died in the line of duty following a traffic stop in Cherokee County;
and

Officer Burson joined the Holly Springs Police Department in January 2020 where
he honorably served the citizens of his community; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Officer Burson’s service to Holly Springs and
the State of Georgia by lowering the flags on the State Capitol building and in
Cherokee County to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE MEMORY
OF OFFICER JOE BURSON, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be flown at

half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Cherokee County on Monday, June
21, 2021, the day of Officer Burson’s interment, until sunset.

Bh

GOVERNOR
