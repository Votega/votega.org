The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 60.71 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) located at 3001 Gordon Highway, in GMD
119t and 1269, Richmond County, Georgia, acquired by virtue of a Quitclaim
Deed dated October 20, 1976, recorded in Deed Book 187, Pages 718-729, in the
office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Richmond County, and on filed with the
State Properties Commission Real Property Record as RPR# 06302; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Georgia Department of Corrections and is
commonly known as Augusta State Medical Prison; and

The Property is improved with structures known as a Sanitation Warehouse
(BLLIP ID # 30175), and Ground Maintenance Equipment Room (BLLIP ID #
30189), totaling 2,336 square feet as shown in the aerial photograph attached
hereto as Exhibit “A” (hereinafter the “Structures”); and

Said Structures are no longer needed and must be demolished to make way for new
construction; and

By Resolution dated September 2, 2021, the Georgia Department of Corrections
requested authority to demolish the Structures.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Georgia Department of Corrections is authorized and directed to provide for the

razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structures under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
