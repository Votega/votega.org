ORDERED:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 3,254.157 acres of improved real property
(hereinafter the “Property”) in Jasper County and Newton County with an address of 3015 Shepherd
Road, Mansfield, Georgia and acquired by virtue of a Corrected Limited Warranty Deed dated
January 4, 1995, recorded in Deed Book 551, Pages 636-642 in the office of the Clerk of Superior
Court of Jasper County, and in Deed Book 452, Pages 505-510 in the office of the Clerk of Superior
Court of Newton County and filed with the State Properties Commission Real Property Record as
RPR# 08289; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Natural Resources and is commonly known as
Clybel WMA; and

The Property is improved with a structure known as the Clybel Farm Barn (BLLIP ID # 12589),
totaling 1,770 square feet as shown on the aerial photograph attached hereto as Exhibit A
(hereinafter the “Structure”); and

Said Structure is no longer in use, is unsafe for the public, and would be cost prohibitive to repair; and

The Structure is eligible for inclusion in the National Register of Historic Places and the Department
of Natural Resources has obtained a Photographic Permanent Archival Record in order to comply with
the Georgia Environmental Protection Act requirements to allow for demolition; and

By Resolution dated August 24, 2021, the Department of Natural Resources requested authority to
demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Natural Resources is authorized and directed to provide for the razing, demolition,
and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions which are deemed to be in the best interest
of the State of Georgia.
