ORDERED:

On August 2, 2021, Mr. Royce Reeves, Ward 2 Commissioner on the
Cordele City Commission, was indicted on two counts of Obstruction of an
Officer, one count of Violation of Oath by a Public Officer, one count of
Criminal Trespass, and one count of Disorderly Conduct; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to O.C.G.A. 8 45-
5-6(b), and the certified copy was received on August 11, 2021; and

The fourteen-day period following the receipt of the indictment as
prescribed by O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(e) has passed.

Now, THEREFORE, BY VIRTUE OF THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Barnesville City
Councilmember/Mayor Pro Tem Christopher Hightower, and McDonough
City Councilmember Benjamin Pruett are hereby appointed as the review
commission to determine whether the above-mentioned indictment relates
to and adversely affects the administration of the duties by Commissioner
Reeves such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected
thereby, and to make a written report to me within fourteen days with a
recommendation as to whether said commissioner should be suspended
from office as required by O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(c).
