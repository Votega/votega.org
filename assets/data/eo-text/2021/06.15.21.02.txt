EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On April 12, 2021, pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 15-11-742(a), a
nominating committee was appointed to consider nominees
for the position of Child Advocate; and

The nominating committee accepted nominations for the
position of Child Advocate and on May 16, 2021, pursuant to
O.C.G.A. § 15-11-742(b), submitted the names of three
finalists for consideration.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED
IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That effective June 16, 2021, the Honorable Jerry Bruce, is
hereby appointed as Georgia’s Child Advocate for a term of
office ending June 15, 2024, to succeed the Honorable Rachel
Davidson who resigned, effective April 26, 2021.

GOVERNOR
