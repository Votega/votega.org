ORDERED:

On March 8, 2021, Talbot County Commissioner Robert Lee Lanier was
indicted on one count of Possession of Methamphetamine; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-
5-6(b), and the certified copy was received on March 15, 2021; and

An Executive Order was issued on May 6, 2021, appointing members to the
Review Commission to determine whether the indictment relates to and
adversely affects the administration of the office of District 1 Commissioner
of Talbot County such that the rights and interests of the public are
adversely affected; and

By the report dated May 28, 2021, the Review Commission found that the
indictment of Robert Lee Lanier does relate to and does adversely affect the
administration of the office of Commissioner, and that the rights and
interests of the public are adversely affected thereby; and recommended
that Robert Lee Lanier be suspended from office pursuant to the provisions
of 0.C.G.A. § 45-5-6.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mr. Robert Lee Lanier is hereby suspended from office immediately
and without further action pending the final disposition of the case or until
the expiration of his term of office, whichever occurs first, as provided in

45-5-6(c).
