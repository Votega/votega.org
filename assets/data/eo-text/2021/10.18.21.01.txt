FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED TO HALF STAFF

The Honorable Colin Luther Powell, 65t* United States Secretary of State passed
away on Monday, October 18, 2021; and

General Colin Powell, USA (Ret) first answered his call to a lifetime of public service
in 1958 when he joined the United States Army where he served for 35 years and
eventually earned the rank of four-star general; and

In addition to his service in the United States Army and as the U.S. Secretary of
State, General Powell also served as the 16t U.S. National Security Advisor, the
Commander of the U.S. Army Forces Command, and the 12" Chairman of the Joint
Chiefs of Staff; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize General Colin Powell’s service to citizens of the
United States of America by lowering the flags throughout Georgia to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE MEMORY
OF THE HONORABLE COLIN LUTHER POWELL, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia shall be flown
at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds from this date until sunset Friday,

October 22, 2021.
