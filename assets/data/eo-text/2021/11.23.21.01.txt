ADDRESSING CDL DRIVER SHORTAGES
The logistics industry is a key part of Georgia’s economy; and

The economy is facing supply chain disruptions affecting the
movement of goods throughout the State; and

An expanded workforce of individuals holding commercial driver’s
licenses (“CDL.”) is critical to alleviate these disruptions; and

Many aspects of the trucking industry are federally regulated; and

I have joined the governors of fourteen other states in urging the
federal government to suspend regulations that prohibit national
economic recovery; and

In addition to federal regulatory changes, the state should regularly
review rules, regulations, and policies to identify changes that will
allow for an expanded workforce without jeopardizing public safety
on Georgia’s roadways; and

It is appropriate all divisions of the Executive Branch of the State
work together and engage nongovernmental stakeholders to address
these critical issues.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
THE GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Department of Driver Services (“DDS”), working with other
state agencies, shall draft a report addressing CDL driver shortages
that shall be provided to the Governor.

IT IS FURTHER

That the report shall identify and suggest amendments to statutes,
regulations, and policies that negatively impact the availability of

CDL drivers and unreasonably limit expansion of the commercial

IT IS FURTHER

That no recommendation shall be included that jeopardizes the
public’s safety on Georgia’s roadways.

IT IS FURTHER

That DDS, the Georgia Department of Defense, and the Department

of Veterans Service continue to promote CDL training and
certification opportunities for servicemen and servicewomen.

IT IS FURTHER

That DDS, the Department of Corrections, and the Department of
Community Supervision shall promote CDL training and
certification opportunities for formerly incarcerated individuals.

IT IS FURTHER

That DDS shall collaborate with third parties, which may include, but
are not limited to, the Department of Labor, the Board of Regents of
the University System of Georgia, the Technical College System of
Georgia, private businesses, and other stakeholders to increase the
number of training and employment opportunities for CDL drivers.

IT IS FURTHER

That all divisions of the executive branch shall cooperate with DDS
in carrying out this Order and shall provide staff support and any
other assistance as reasonably requested.

IT IS FURTHER

That the provisions of this Order shall become effective upon
