ORDERED:

FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

On Thursday, August 26, 2021, terrorists carried out an attack on
American service members and innocent civilians at the Hamid Karzai
International Airport in Kabul, Afghanistan; and

Among those killed in this horrific act of violence were members of the
armed forces of the United States; and

The people of Georgia mourn with the families and friends of the heroic
United States service members who answered the call of duty to go into
harm’s way to save American and Afghan lives; and

It is appropriate that the flag of the United States and the flag of the
State of Georgia be flown at half-staff in memory of these individuals
killed standing guard at the airport in Kabul, Afghanistan.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
UNITED STATES SERVICE MEMBERS WHO MADE THE ULTIMATE
SACRIFICE FOR OUR COUNTRY, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED
IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States of America and the flag of the State of

Georgia be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds from
this issuance of this order until sunset on Monday, August 30, 2021.

piPh

GOVERNOR

This 2h day of August 2021.
