FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF STAFF

Today we pay respects to the Honorable Joseph Maxwell “Max”
Cleland; and

Max Cleland was born in Atlanta, grew up in Lithonia, Georgia, and
received a master’s degree from Emory University; and

During the Vietnam War, Cleland served honorably in the United
States Army where he earned the Silver Star and the Bronze Star and
was also severely injured by a grenade blast that resulted in the
amputation of both his legs and right forearm; and

Cleland served in the Georgia State Senate from 1971 to 1975, as
administrator of the U.S. Veterans Administration under President
Carter from 1977 to 1981, as Secretary of State of Georgia from 1982
to 1996, and as a United States Senator from 1997 to 2003; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Max Cleland’s lifetime of public
service by lowering flags throughout Georgia to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND MEMORY OF JOSEPH MAXWELL
“MAX” CLELAND, AND PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN
ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That, the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia

should be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds on
Monday, November 15, 2021, until sunset.
