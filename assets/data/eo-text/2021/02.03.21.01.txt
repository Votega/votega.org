THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

FLAGS ON STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

On Tuesday, February 2, 2021, in Sunrise, Florida, two FBI agents
were killed and three others injured while attempting to serve a
warrant in a child exploitation case; and

Special Agent Daniel Alfin and Special Agent Laura
Schwartzenberger specialized in investigating crimes against
children; and

While we mourn the deaths of these heroes, we should honor their
memory by continually striving to put an end to child exploitation
and human trafficking.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF SPECIAL AGENT ALFIN AND SPECIAL AGENT
SCHWARTZENBERGER, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN
ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States of America and the flag of the State
of Georgia shall be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and
grounds until sunset today, Wednesday, February 3, 2021.

This 38? day of February, 2021.

GOVERNOR
