EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

FLAGS ON STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF STAFF

United States Capitol Police Officer William “Billy” Evans was killed
in the line of duty at the United States Capitol on Friday, April 2,
2021; and

The people of Georgia are grateful for Officer Evans’ bravery, service,
and sacrifice, and mourn his loss; and

The people of Georgia wish to honor the life of Officer Evans as we
share in the grief of his family and friends, and pray for their comfort
and healing in the days to come.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
OFFICER EVANS, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia
shall be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds from
this date until sunset, Tuesday, April 6, 2021.

This 3®° day of April 2021.

~ GOVERNOR
