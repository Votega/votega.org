ORDERED:

On September 9, 2021, Tatyana and Troy Ellis filed a Verified
Petition for Writ of Mandamus in case number 2021-0118399-CV
against Senior Judge Adele Grubbs in the Superior Court of Cobb
County; and

The Georgia Department of Law has declined representation of the
Honorable Adele Grubbs due to a potential conflict; and

Mr. Robert Ingram, Mr. Ryan Ingram, and Mr. David Conley of
Moore Ingram Johnson & Steele, LLP are all attorneys duly admitted
to the State Bar of Georgia; and

Mr. Robert Ingram, Mr. Ryan Ingram, and Mr. David Conley of
Moore Ingram Johnson & Steele, LLP have agreed to represent
Judge Adele Grubbs at a rate mutually agreed upon with the Office
of the Governor.

Now, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO O.C.G.A. § 45-15-70 AND BY
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That Mr. Robert Ingram, Mr. Ryan Ingram, and Mr. David Conley of
Moore Ingram Johnson & Steele, LLP are hereby appointed as
Special Counsel to assume control and responsibility for the
representation of the Honorable Adele Grubbs in all matters related
to the Verified Petition for Writ of Mandamus filed against her in
case number 2021-0118399-CV.
