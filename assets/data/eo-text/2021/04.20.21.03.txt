FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED TO HALF STAFF

Walter Frederick “Fritz” Mondale, 424 vice president of the United States of
America passed away on Monday, April 19, 2021; and

Mr. Mondale first answered his call to public service in 1951 when he joined the
United States Army and served in the Korean War; and

In addition to his service in the United States Army, Mr. Mondale served as
Attorney General of Minnesota, and as a Senator in the U.S. Senate before being
selected as the vice-presidential running mate to then presidential nominee Jimmy
Carter; and

Mr. Mondale served as vice president from 1977-1981, and as the United States
Ambassador to Japan from 1993-1996; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Vice President Mondale’s service to citizens of
the United States of America by lowering the flags throughout Georgia to half-staff.

Now, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE MEMORY
OF THE HONORABLE WALTER FREDERICK “FRITZ” MONDALE, PURSUANT TO
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia shall be flown

at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds from this date until sunset on the day
of his interment.

This 207H day of April, 2021.
