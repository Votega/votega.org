THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

EXTENSION OF THE STATE OF EMERGENCY
FOR PETROLEUM SHORTAGE

That Executive Order 05.10.21.02 declaring a State of Emergency for
Petroleum Shortage, which was most recently renewed through May
29, 2021, by Executive Order 05.21.21.01, shall be renewed for an
additional four (4) days and shall expire on Wednesday, June 2,
2021, at 11:59 P.M. unless it is otherwise renewed by the Governor.

Bil

GOVERNOR

This 28th day of May, 2021.
