EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That Executive Order 07.23.21.02 is hereby
superseded and amended to state that the
Honorable James Frank Mattison IV of
Lincoln County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as a member of the State Board of
Registration for Professional Engineers and
Land Surveyors, for a term of office expiring
June 30, 2026, to succeed the Honorable
Mark Chastain, whose term expired June 1,
2021.

This 2nd day of November, 2021

Bil

GOVERNOR
