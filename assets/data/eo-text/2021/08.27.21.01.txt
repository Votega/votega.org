FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

During the 2021 legislative session, the Georgia General Assembly posthumously
recognized the life and memory of the following citizens of the great State of
Georgia; and

The State mourns the loss of these men and women, of all ages and races, and
respectfully extends condolences to the families, friends, and communities affected
by the passing of these individuals; and

It is appropriate that the State honor these men and women who are remembered
by those who knew and loved them; and

The State honors the life and memory of individuals who passed away from drug
overdoses, including Melanie Travassos and Joey Purpura, who will be honored on
Overdose Awareness Day at the State Capitol on August 31, 2021; and

The State honors the life and memory of Viola Hart-Felton, who passed on March
31, 1933; and

The State honors the life and memory of William “Uncle Bill” Greenlee, who passed
in 1972, at the age of 102; and

The State honors the life and memory of Columbus Police Officer James Neal
Bowers, who passed on April 4, 1979, in the line of duty; and

The State honors the life and memory of Jamarion Robinson, who passed on
August 5, 2016; and

The State honors the life and memory of Billy Franklin Payne, who passed on May
22, 2017; and

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND RESPECT FOR THESE INDIVIDUALS

AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the flag of the United States of America and the flag of the great State of
Georgia be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds on Tuesday,

August 31, 2021, until sunset.
