ORDERED:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 6,385 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) in the 1645th GMD, Tattnall County, Georgia,
acquired by virtue of various deeds and filed in the office of the Clerk of Superior
Court of Tattnall County, and filed with the State Properties Commission Real
Property Record as Property BLLIP ID# 51098; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Corrections and is commonly
known as Rogers State Prison; and

The Property is improved with a structure identified as “House 163” (BLLIP ID #
8233) and totaling 1,700 square feet (hereinafter, collectively, the “Structure”) as
shown in the aerial photograph attached hereto as Exhibit A; and

Said Structure had a fire on January 9, 2022, and the Structure was a total loss. The
cost to repair the Structure far exceeded its serviceable life making the Structure
beyond reasonable repair; and

By resolution dated June 1, 2023, the Board of the Department of Corrections
requested authority to demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Corrections is authorized and directed to provide for the razing,

demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions which are
deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
