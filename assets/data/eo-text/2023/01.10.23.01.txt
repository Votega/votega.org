THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That, effective January 13, 2023, the Honorable William R.
Oliver is hereby appointed Superior Court Judge of the
Mountain Judicial Circuit to fill the seat created by the
passage of Senate Bill 395 (Act Number 792) during the 2022
session of the Georgia General Assembly, for a term of office
ending December 31, 2024.

THIS 10TH DAY OF JANUARY 2023.

~ GOVERNOR
