ATTEST;

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 1,402 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) in Baldwin County, Georgia, acquired by
virtue of various deeds and filed in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of
Baldwin County, and filed with the State Properties Commission Real Property
Record as Property BLLIP ID# 51710; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Behavioral Health and
Developmental Disabilities and is located at the campus known as Central State
Hospital; and

The Property is improved with three structures identified as 1) the “Walker
Building” (BLLIP ID # 931) being approximately 88,492 square feet; 2) the “Green
Building” (BLLIP ID # 919) being approximately 62,284 square feet; and 3) the
“Jones Building” (BLLIP ID # 922) being approximately 142,140 square feet and
hereinafter, collectively called the “Structures”, as shown in the aerial photograph
and map attached hereto as Exhibit “A”; and

By resolution dated June 29, 2023, the Board of Behavioral Health and
Developmental Disabilities requested authority to demolish the Structures.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Behavioral Health and Developmental Disabilities is authorized
and directed to provide for the razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structures
under the terms and conditions which are deemed to be in the best interest of the
State of Georgia, in accordance with applicable laws, policies, and procedures,
including any requirements under O.C.G.A 12-3-55 regarding historic properties that
may pertain to the Structures.
