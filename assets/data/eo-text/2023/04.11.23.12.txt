THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Rhonda Futch Chatham

of Gilmer County, Georgia, is appointed to serve
as a member of the Board of Directors of the
Georgia Emergency Communications Authority,

succeed the Honorable Michael Wall, whose
term expired June 30, 2020.
