THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

The state of Georgia mourns the loss of State Representative Letitia
“Tish” Naghise who passed away on March 8, 2023; and

Representative Naghise was elected to serve the citizens of Georgia’s
68th District in the Georgia House of Representatives in November
2022; and

Tish Naghise was a successful businesswoman, dedicated public
servant, and beloved wife and mother.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF REPRESENTATIVE LETITIA “TISH” NAGHISE,
PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the state of Georgia
shall be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds
throughout Georgia on Saturday, March 25, 2023, the day of
Representative Naghise’s interment.

Bilh

GOVERNOR

This 2222. day of March 2023.
