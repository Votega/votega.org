WHEREAS:

The State of Georgia holds title to, and the Georgia Department of Defense (“Georgia
DOD”) is the custodian of two tracts known as the Boland Building, totaling
approximately 6.92 acres of real property (the “Property”) situated and lying in Land
Lots 260 and 287, 1st District, 341st G.M. District, Baldwin County, as more fully
depicted as Tracts 1A and 1B, as shown on the survey attached hereto as Exhibit A;
and

Said Property is a portion of the former Central State Hospital Campus on file in the
offices of the State Properties Commission and inventoried as Real Property ID #
51710; and

By Official Action dated March 22, 2023, the Adjutant General of Georgia DOD
requested that custody of the Property be transferred to the Georgia Department
of Behavioral Health and Developmental Disabilities (““DBHDD”); and

The State of Georgia, acting by and through the Georgia State Financing and ©
Investment Commission, previously issued General Obligation Bonds shown on
Exhibit B (the “Tax Exempt Bonds”), attached hereto, for the purpose of financing
capital projects for State Agencies and a portion of such bonds were used to finance
improvements on the Property; and

By Resolution dated April 13, 2023, DBHDD requested custody of the Property for
use as a hurricane evacuation site by residents and staff of Georgia Regional
Hospital Savannah; and

DBHDD has certified that it shall not take, or fail to take, any action which would
cause such Tax Exempt Bonds to be deemed private activity bonds or arbitrage
bonds under Section 103 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended (“Tax
Code”), and further, shall not use the Property for any non-governmental
purposes, or any purpose that would give rise to private business use as provided
in the Tax Code.

Now, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR
OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

ORDERED: That custody of the above-defined Property located in Baldwin County, Georgia is
hereby transferred from the Georgia DOD to DBHDD.

IT IS FURTHER

ORDERED: That DBHDD shall not use the Property for any non-governmental purpose, or any
purpose that would give rise to private business use as provided in the Tax Code

until the Tax Exempt Bonds are retired.
