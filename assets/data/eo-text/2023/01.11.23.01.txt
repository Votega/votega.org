ORDERED:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 1,442 acres of improved real
property (hereinafter the “Property”) in Land Lots 106, 107, and 111 of the 8t Land
District, and Land Lots 245-6, 251-5, 279-281, and 284-5 of the 19th Land District,
Muscogee County, Georgia, acquired by virtue of a General Warranty Deed dated
February 9, 2001, recorded in Deed Book 5789, pages 4-10 in the office of the Clerk
of Superior Court of Muscogee County, and filed with the State Properties
Commission Real Property Record as RPR# 09602.01; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Natural Resources and is
commonly known as Standing Boy Creek State Park; and

The Property is improved with a structure formerly known as residence house
(BLLIP ID # 28046), totaling 2,400 square feet as shown in the aerial photograph
attached hereto as Exhibit A (hereinafter the “Structure”); and

Said Structure needs to be demolished as the structure is no longer needed, is in poor
condition, and is beyond reasonable repair; and

By Resolution dated December 7, 2022, the Board of Natural Resources requested
authority to demolish the Structure.

NOw, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Natural Resources is authorized and directed to provide for the

razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
