ORDERED:

On February 24, 2023, Mr. Henry Mitchell, County Commissioner for
Douglas County, was indicted on one count of Conspiracy in Restraint of
Free and Open Competition in Transactions; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to Code Section
45-5-6(b), and the certified copy was received on February 27, 2023; and

An Executive Order was issued on March 21, 2023, appointing members to
the Review Commission to determine whether the indictment relates to and
adversely affects the administration of the Office of the Douglas County
Commission such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely
affected; and

By report dated April 4, 2023, the Review Commission found that the
indictment of Mr. Henry Mitchell does relate to and does adversely affect
the administration of the Office of the Douglas County Commission, and
that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby; and
recommended that Commissioner Mitchell be suspend from office pursuant
to the provisions of Code Section 45-5-6.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Mr. Henry Mitchell is hereby suspended from office immediately and

without further action pending the final disposition of the case or until
expiration of his term of office, whichever occurs first, as provided in Code

Section 45-5-6(c).
