THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED: That the Honorable Robert Hewlett Grigsby of
Floyd County, Georgia, is appointed to serve
as the Fourteenth Congressional District
Representative on the Georgia Student
Finance Commission Board of Commissioners,
for a term of office expiring November 5, 2026,
to succeed the Honorable William Pope
Hightower, who no longer represent the
Fourteenth Congressional District on the
Georgia Student Finance Commission Board
of Commissioners, as a result of
reapportionment, adopted
December 30, 2021.

This 7 day of February 2023

Bil

GOVERNOR
