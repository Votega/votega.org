THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF AND AUTHORIZING

DR. CHRISTINE KING FARRIS TO LIE IN STATE IN THE GEORGIA STATE CAPITOL

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED:

On Thursday, June 29, 2023, Dr. Christine King Farris—a founding board member
of the King Center, beloved educator, and civil rights leader—passed away at age
95; and

Dr. King Farris was a cherished member of the historic Ebenezer Baptist Church,
a respected professor at Spelman College and educator of 64 years, and a source of
inspiration for others to join the pursuit of justice in the face of discrimination; and

The sister and last living sibling of Rev. Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr., Dr. King Farris
was revered as a servant leader by all who were blessed to know her; and

As the State of Georgia mourns her death, we also honor the remarkable legacy she
leaves behind and the enduring impact she had on her loved ones, her generations
of students, her community, our state, and the world beyond.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF DR. CHRISTINE KING FARRIS, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States of America and the flag of the State of Georgia
shall be flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and grounds and
throughout Fulton County from Friday, July 14, 2023, through sunset on Sunday,
July 16, 2023.

IT IS FURTHER

That Dr. Christine King Farris shall lie in state in the Rotunda of the Georgia State
Capitol on Friday, July 14, 2023, from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

GOVERNOR

This Oe day of July 2023.
