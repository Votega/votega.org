ORDERED:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 1.35 acres of improved real property
(hereinafter the “Property”) in Land Lot 16, 13th G.M. District, Jones County,
Georgia, acquired by virtue of a Deed dated January 19, 1956, recorded in Deed
Book 3-J, pages 223-224 in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court of Jones
County, and filed with the State Properties Commission Real Property Record as
RPR# 01934; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Georgia Forestry Commission and is commonly
known as the Jasper-Jones Forestry Unit; and

The Property is improved with structures identified as an “Office” (BLLIP ID #
36119), “Equipment Shed/Shop” (BLLIP ID # 36123) and “Storage Shed” (BLLIP
ID # 36127) (hereinafter, collectively, the “Structures”) as shown in the aerial
photograph attached hereto as Exhibit A; and

Said Structures have substantial termite and water damage, outdated electrical
systems, and are too small for modern fire suppression equipment making the
Structures unusable; and

Funding was approved to construct a new forestry unit on the land where the existing
Structures are located and upon demolition a new unit will be constructed; and

By Resolution dated May 24, 2023, the Georgia Forestry Commission requested
authority to demolish the Structures.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Georgia Forestry Commission is authorized and directed to provide for the

razing, demolition, and disposal of said Structures under the terms and conditions
which are deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.

This 29% day of June 2023.
