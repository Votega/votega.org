ORDERED:

Pursuant to Executive Order 02.26.19.01, the Georgians for Refuge, Action,
Compassion, & Education (GRACE) Commission was created to leverage
the knowledge and community resources of an alliance of members from
the private and public sectors to find solutions to the challenge of human
trafficking and seek to eliminate human trafficking in our state; and

On February 14, 2022, I issued Executive Order No. 02.14.22.01 increasing
the membership of the GRACE Commission to twenty-three (23) members
to allow the Commission to take increased action to combat the growing
threat to freedom for victims in every region of our state; and

Further increasing the Commission’s membership will allow for additional
stakeholder input to contribute to the valuable work against human
trafficking it continues to champion; and

Former co-chair of the Commission, Vic Reynolds, accepted an
appointment to the Cobb County Superior Court bench and is no longer with
the Georgia Bureau of Investigation (“GBI”); and

Given the GBI’s substantial role in fighting human trafficking in Georgia, it
is appropriate that the current GBI director, Michael Register, serve as co-
chair of the GRACE Commission.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the GRACE Commission membership shall be increased by two (2)
members, from twenty-three (23) members to twenty-five (25) members, to
be appointed by the Governor based on the recommendations of First Lady
Marty Kemp.

ORDERED:

IT IS FURTHER
That GBI Director Michael Register shall replace former GBI Director Vic
Reynolds as co-chair of the GRACE Commission and shall serve alongside

existing co-chairs First Lady Marty Kemp and State House Speaker Pro
Tempore Jan Jones.
