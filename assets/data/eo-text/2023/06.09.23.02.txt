The State of Georgia holds title to and the Georgia Department of Public Safety is the
custodian of the buildings commonly known as the 1957 Headquarters Building and
Guard House located at 959 United Avenue, SE, Atlanta, located in Land Lots 9, 10,
23, and 24 of the 14th District, Fulton County, Georgia (hereinafter the “Property”),
as more fully described in Exhibit “A” attached hereto and incorporated herein by
reference; and

The Property is improved with structures identified as the “1957 Headquarters
Building” and “Guard House” (BLLIP ID # 9379) and totaling 9,800 square feet
(hereinafter, collectively, the “Structures”) as shown in the aerial photograph
attached hereto as Exhibit A; and

Said Structures are old and a new facility will be constructed for the benefit of the
Department of Public Safety’s operation; and

By Resolution dated May 11, 2023, the Board of Public Safety resolved to demolish
the Structures.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Public Safety is authorized and directed to provide for the razing,

demolition, and disposal of said Structures under the terms and conditions which are
deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
