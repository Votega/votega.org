ORDERED:

On February 24, 2023, Dr. Romona Jackson Jones, County Commissioner for
Douglas County, was indicted on one count of Conspiracy in Restraint of Free and
Open Competition in Transactions and one count of False Statements; and

On February 24, 2023, Mr. Henry Mitchell, County Commissioner for Douglas
County, was indicted on one count of Conspiracy in Restraint of Free and Open
Competition in Transactions; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(b),
and the certified copy was received on February 27, 2023; and

The fourteen-day period following receipt of the indictment as prescribed by
O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(e) has passed; and

Pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(b), since the Attorney General of Georgia brought
the indictment against Commissioner Jones and Commissioner Mitchell, the
Attorney General shall not serve on the commission, and a retired Supreme Court
Justice or retired Court of Appeals Judge shall serve in place of the Attorney
General.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6, AND THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Honorable Leah Ward Sears, retired Chief Justice of the Supreme Court
of Georgia; Commissioner Johnny Davis of Jefferson County; and Commissioner
Carvel Lewis of Quitman County are hereby appointed as the review commission
to determine whether the above-mentioned indictment relates to and adversely
affects the administration of the duties by Commissioner Jones and Commissioner
Mitchell such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected
thereby, and to make a written report to me within thirty days with a
recommendation as to whether Commissioner Jones and Commissioner Mitchell
should be suspended from office as required by 0.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(c).

This al Way of March 2023.
