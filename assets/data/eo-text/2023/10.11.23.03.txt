THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF STAFF

On Saturday, October 7, 2023, during the Jewish holiday of Shemini
Atzeret, terrorists launched a coordinated attack on the country of Israel by
land, sea, and air, killing over 1,200 people; and

Among those killed were at least 22 American citizens, with an
undetermined number taken hostage by Hamas militants; and

The people of Georgia mourn the American lives lost in these unwarranted
attacks and join our close ally Israel in grieving all those who were taken
from their families or injured; and

It is appropriate that the flag of the United States and the flag of the State
of Georgia be flown at half-staff in memory of those killed during these
brutal assaults.

Now, THEREFORE, IN HONOR AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE
MEMORY OF THOSE KILLED IN THESE UNWARRANTED ATTACKS, PURSUANT
TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States of America and the flag of the State of
Georgia shall be flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds from
this issuance of this order until sunset on Saturday, October 14, 2023.
