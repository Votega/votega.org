THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Douglas Frank Jamieson of
Schley County, Georgia, is appointed to serve
as a member of the Board of Directors of the
Georgia Emergency Communications Authority,
for a term of office expiring April 11, 2026, to
succeed the Honorable Peter Olson, whose term
expired June 30, 2021.

This 11 day of April 2023

BRL

GOVERNOR
