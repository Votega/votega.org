ORDERED:

On February 24, 2023, Dr. Romona Jackson Jones, County Commissioner
for Douglas County, was indicted on one count of Conspiracy in Restraint
of Free and Open Competition in Transactions and one count of False
Statements; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to Code Section
45-5-6(b), and the certified copy was received on February 27, 2023; and

An Executive Order was issued on March 21, 2023, appointing members to
the Review Commission to determine whether the indictment relates to and
adversely affects the administration of the Office of the Douglas County
Commission such that the rights and interests of the public are adversely
affected; and

By report dated April 4, 2023, the Review Commission found that the
indictment of Dr. Romona Jackson Jones does relate to and does adversely
affect the administration of the Office of the Douglas County Commission,
and that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby;
and recommended that Commissioner Jones be suspend from office
pursuant to the provisions of Code Section 45-5-6.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Dr. Romona Jackson Jones is hereby suspended from office
immediately and without further action pending the final disposition of the
case or until expiration of her term of office, whichever occurs first, as
provided in Code Section 45-5-6(c).

This Ss day of April 2023.

GOVERNOR
