THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That the Honorable Shannon Wallace is hereby appointed
Superior Court Judge of the Blue Ridge Judicial Circuit to fill
the seat created by the passage of House Bill 56 (Act Number
736) during the 2022 session of the Georgia General
Assembly, for a term of office ending December 31, 2024.

THIS 307TH DAY OF JANUARY 2023.
