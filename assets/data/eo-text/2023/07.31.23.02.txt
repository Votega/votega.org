ORDERED:

On May 27, 2023, the Honorable Raymond House, II, District Five
Commissioner on the City of Jesup Board of Commissioners (the
“Commission”), passed away, creating a vacancy in such office; and

Pursuant to Code Section 45-5-3.1(b), “[w]hen any local or general law
provides for the appointment . . . of a person to fill a vacancy in an elective
public office and such vacancy is not filled within 45 days after the vacancy
occurs, the Governor shall appoint a person to fill such vacancy” and such
person “shall serve until such time as that person would serve had the
person been appointed as provided in that local or general law;” and

Section 4.2.2 of the Amended and Restated Charter of the City of Jesup,
Georgia provides that if a vacancy occurs in the office of a Commissioner
“within twelve (12) months of the expiration of the term of that office, the
Mayor and remaining Commissioners shall appoint a successor for the
remainder of the term of the vacated office of Commissioner;” and

Commissioner House’s term of office as District Five Commissioner expires
December 31, 2023, and the Commission has not appointed a successor to
fill the remainder of Commissioner House’s term.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-3.1 AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the Honorable Joseph P. Ierardi is hereby appointed as District Five
Commissioner on the City of Jesup Board of Commissioners and shall serve

GOVERNOR

This 31° day of July 2023.
