FLAGS ON ALL STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED TO HALF-STAFF

Mrs. Eleanor Rosalynn Carter, former First Lady of Georgia and
First Lady of the United States, passed away on November 19, 2023;
and

During her time as First Lady of Georgia from 1971 to 1975, and
alongside Georgia’s 76 Governor Jimmy Carter, she helped expand
access to mental health care in the State and worked to promote
childhood immunization against preventable disease; and

As First Lady of the United States from 1977 to 1981, she established
an official Office of the First Lady in the White House and served as
honorary chair of the President’s Commission on Mental Health,
among many other acts of service to the country; and

The Carter Foundation will continue to promote Mrs. Carter’s
celebrated work as a mental health advocate; and

For generations to come, the Rosalynn Carter Institute for
Caregiving, located on the campus of her alma mater, Georgia
Southwestern State University, will continue her efforts in providing
resources to caregivers; and

It is fitting and proper to remember and honor Mrs. Rosalynn
Carter’s life and dedicated service to citizens of the State of Georgia
and the United States of America by lowering the flags throughout
Georgia to half-staff.


Now, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF MRS. ELEANOR ROSALYNN CARTER, PURSUANT
TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

ORDERED: That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia
be flown at half-staff on all State buildings and grounds from
November 27, 2023, through sunset on November 29, 2023, the day
of Mrs. Carter’s interment.

This 21st day of November 2023.

hae

GOVERNOR
