EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Catherine Jones McWhirter
of Oconee County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as a member of the State Board of
Cosmetology and Barbers, for a term of office
expiring November 27, 2026, to succeed the
Honorable Van Dupree Council, whose term
expired September 22, 2023.

This 27th day of November 2023
