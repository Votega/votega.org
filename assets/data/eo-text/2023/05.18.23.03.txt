ORDERED:

The State of Georgia and local governments entered into the Memorandum
of Understanding Concerning National Distributor and Johnson & Johnson
Opioid Settlements (the “MOU”); and

The MOU provides for the establishment of a Government Participation
Mechanism (“GPM”) that shall consult with, and provide recommendations
to, the Trustee of the Georgia Opioid Crisis Abatement Trust regarding the
allocation of funds received by the State pursuant to National Distributor and
Johnson & Johnson opioid settlements; and

Pursuant to the MOU, a GPM shall be established as described in the Notice
of Filing of Eighth Plan Supplement Pursuant to the Fifth Amended Joint
Chapter 11 Plan of Reorganization of Purdue Pharma L.P. and its Affiliated
Debtors, In re: Purdue Pharma L.P., et al, Case No. 19-23649-RDD, Dkt. 3121
(Bankr. S.D.N.Y. July 8, 2021), Exhibit G at 11 (the “Purdue Bankruptcy
Plan”); and

The Purdue Bankruptcy Plan provides that the State and the State’s local
governments shall have equal representation on the GPM and the State shall
appoint a non-voting chairperson to the GPM.

Pursuant to the MOU, each member of the GPM and the non-voting
chairperson shall be appointed for a term of three years.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE MEMORANDUM OF
UNDERSTANDING CONCERNING NATIONAL DISTRIBUTOR AND JOHNSON
& JOHNSON OPIOID SETTLEMENTS AND PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY
VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the aforementioned GPM is hereby established as the Georgia Opioid
Settlement Advisory Commission (“GOSAC’).

IT IS FURTHER

That GOSAC shall be comprised of eight members and one non-voting
chairperson.

IT IS FURTHER

ORDERED: That the following four persons are appointed to serve as the State’s
representatives on GOSAC, each for a term of office expiring May 18, 2026.

The Honorable Xavier Crockett
The Honorable Cassandra Price
The Honorable Gary Sisk

The Honorable Grant Thomas

IT IS FURTHER

ORDERED: That the Honorable David Dove is appointed to serve as the chairperson of
GOSAC, for a term of office expiring May 18, 2026.
IT IS FURTHER

ORDERED: That the State’s local governments are requested to appoint four

representatives to GOSAC, including one representative who is appointed
by the Georgia Association of Community Service Boards as required by
the MOU, and notify the chairperson of such appointments.
