EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

That the Honorable Lawton C. Heard, Jr. is hereby appointed
Superior Court Judge of the South Georgia Judicial Circuit to
fill the seat created by the passage of House Bill 624 (Act
Number 746) during the 2022 session of the Georgia General
Assembly, for a term of office ending December 31, 2024.

THIS 27TH DAY OF MARCH 2023.
