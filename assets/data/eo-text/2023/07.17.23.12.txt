EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable John Ralph Thomas of
Whitfield County, Georgia, is appointed to

Representative on the State Board of the
Technical College System of Georgia, for a

succeed the Honorable Joe W. Yarbrough,
whose term expired June 30, 2023.
