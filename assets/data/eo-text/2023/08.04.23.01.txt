EXECUTIVE ORDER

County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as
Director of Health Strategy and Coordination
pursuant to Code Section 31-53-4(a), for a

Thomas, who resigned.

Sections 31-53-4(d) and 31-53-5 shall be
