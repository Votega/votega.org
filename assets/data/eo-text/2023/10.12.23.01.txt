THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

UPDATING THE GEORGIANS FOR REFUGE,

ACTION, COMPASSION, & EDUCATION COMMISSION MEMBERSHIP

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

Pursuant to Executive Order 02.26.19.01, the Georgians for Refuge, Action,
Compassion, & Education (GRACE) Commission was created to leverage
the knowledge and community resources of an alliance of public and private
sector members to seek to eliminate human trafficking in our state; and

Former co-chair of the Commission, Michael Register, accepted a role as the
Director of Public Safety for Cobb County and is no longer with the Georgia
Bureau of Investigation (“GBI”); and

Given the GBI’s substantial role in fighting human trafficking in Georgia, it
is appropriate that the current GBI director, Chris Hosey, serve as co-chair
of the GRACE Commission.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That GBI Director Chris Hosey shall replace former GBI Director Michael
Register as co-chair of the GRACE Commission and shall serve alongside

existing co-chairs First Lady Marty Kemp and State House Speaker Pro
Tempore Jan Jones.

This | 2H day of October 2023.
