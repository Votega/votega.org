THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING, SANFORD STADIUM, IN ATHENS-CLARKE
COUNTY, AND IN CHATHAM COUNTY ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

WHEREAS: Mr. Frank W. “Sonny” Seiler, famed Savannah attorney, DGD, and legendary
figure of the University of Georgia passed away on Monday, August 28, 2023; and

WHEREAS: Mr. Seiler served as patriarch of college sports’ most famous mascot — the
University of Georgia’s English Bulldog — since his first bulldog, Hood's Ole Dan,
took the field at the 1956 home opener against Florida State as Uga I; and

WHEREAS: Mr. Seiler demonstrated his nearest duty by dedicating his Uga line as the
University of Georgia mascots from 1956 to present day as Ugas I-XI, establishing
a tradition of unbridled excellence; and

WHEREAS: Mr. Seiler was a “Double Dawg” graduate of the University of Georgia; he was
awarded the Distinguished American Award by the UGA Chapter of the National
Football Foundation and received the UGA Alumni Association’s National Alumni
Merit Award, demonstrating a life-long commitment to the idea that there is no
tradition more worthy of envy, no institution worthy of such loyalty as the
University of Georgia; and

WHEREAS: Mr. Seiler honorably served as First Lieutenant in the United States Army and as
an officer in the Georgia National Guard, 230" Field Artillery; and

WHEREAS: _ It is fitting and proper to recognize Mr. Seiler’s life by lowering flags to half-staff.
Now, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS MARK OF RESPECT FOR THE MEMORY
OF MR. FRANK W. “SONNY” SEILER, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED
IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

ORDERED: _ That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be flown at

half-staff at the State Capitol, Sanford Stadium, in Athens-Clarke County, and in
Chatham County on Saturday, September 2, 2023.
