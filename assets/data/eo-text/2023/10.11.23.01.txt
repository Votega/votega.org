There currently exist at the University of Georgia, Clarke County, Athens,
Georgia, five support buildings identified as #4150, 4151, 4152, 4153, and
4155 (collectively, the “Buildings”), located at 2325 South Milledge Avenue;
and

The Board of Regents’ Policy 9.8.2.3 DEMOLITION authorizes the
Chancellor or Vice Chancellor for Facilities to declare buildings, structures,
and other improvements no longer advantageously useful to the University
System of Georgia. Said Buildings are included within this policy; and

It has been determined by Ms. Sandra Lynn Neuse, Vice Chancellor for
Facilities of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, that
said Buildings can no longer be advantageously used by the University of
Georgia or the University System of Georgia and should be removed.

NOW, THEREFORE, BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia is hereby
authorized to demolish and remove said Buildings from the University of
Georgia, Clarke County, Athens, Georgia.

IT IS FURTHER

That the appropriate staff of the University of Georgia, acting under the
approval of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, is
hereby authorized to demolish said Buildings and to have all building
materials removed from the premises by public works contract; all said
demolition and removal to be performed as expeditiously as possible.
