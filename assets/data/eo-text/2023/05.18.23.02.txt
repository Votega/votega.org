EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On April 5, 2023, Executive Order 04.05.23.02 was issued
suspending Mr. Henry Mitchell, Douglas County District 1
Commissioner, from office; and

Pursuant to Code Section 45-5-6(d)(1), there being no general or local
law, ordinance, or resolution governing the appointment of a
replacement officer, the Governor is authorized to appoint, for the
duration of the suspension, a replacement for the Douglas County
District 1 Commissioner.

Now, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6(D)Q@),
AND THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Honorable Clarence Ricky Dobbs, Jr. of Douglas County,
Georgia, is hereby appointed to fill the temporary vacancy in the office
of the Douglas County District 1 Commissioner.

This 18th day of May 2023
