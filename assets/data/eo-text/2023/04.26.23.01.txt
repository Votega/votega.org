EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Charles Paul Efstration, III
of Gwinnett County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as a member of the Georgia Child Support
Commission, for a term of office expiring
January 13, 2025, to succeed the Honorable
Bonnie Rich, whose term expired

January 9, 2023.

conde

GOVERNOR
