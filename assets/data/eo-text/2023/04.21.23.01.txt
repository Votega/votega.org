ORDERED:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 8.98 acres of improved real property
(hereinafter the “Property”) in 8th G.M. District, Plat of Lot 2C, Dogwood Tract,
Chatham County, Georgia, acquired by virtue of a Quitclaim Deed dated June 23,
2009, recorded in Deed Book 354Q, pages 720-724, in the office of the Clerk of
Superior Court of Chatham County, and filed with the State Properties
Commission Real Property Record as RPR# 10652; and

Said Property is in the custody of the Department of Agriculture and is commonly
known as the Savannah Farmers Market; and

The Property is improved with structures identified as “Buildings 3 and 4” (BLLIP
ID # 8233) and totaling 26,362 square feet (hereinafter, collectively, the
“Structure”) as shown in the aerial photograph attached hereto as Exhibit A; and

Said Structure’s trusses have failed and exceeded their serviceable life making the
Structure beyond reasonable repair; and

By letter dated March 31, 2023, the Commissioner of the Department of Agriculture
requested authority to demolish the Structure.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

The Department of Agriculture is authorized and directed to provide for the razing,

demolition, and disposal of said Structure under the terms and conditions which are
deemed to be in the best interest of the State of Georgia.
