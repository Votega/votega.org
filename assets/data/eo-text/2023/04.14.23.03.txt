THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Jerry Bruce of Lumpkin
County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as a
member of the Criminal Justice Coordinating
Council, for a term of office expiring

July 1, 2024, to succeed the Honorable Rachel
Davidson, who in accordance with

O.C.G.A. § 35-6A-3 is no longer be eligible to
hold the seat for which she was appointed.
