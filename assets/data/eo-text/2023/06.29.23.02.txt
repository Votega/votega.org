GEORGIA DEPARTMENT OF COMMUNITY SUPERVISION

Georgia Department of Community Supervision — To provide funds of $186,718
to cover costs associated with crime suppression efforts.

GEORGIA DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Georgia Department of Defense — To provide funds of $102,388 to cover costs
associated with civil unrest response efforts.

GEORGIA DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC SAFETY

Georgia Department of Public Safety — To provide funds of $2,079,993 to cover
costs associated with the crime suppression and security efforts.

GEORGIA DEPARTMENT OF REVENUE

Georgia Department of Revenue — To provide funds of $836,019 to cover costs
associated with the implementation of Senate Bill 56 (2023 Session) and the State’s
contribution to the Employee’s Retirement System of Georgia on behalf of qualifying local
tax officials.

OFFICE OF THE GOVERNOR

Office of the Governor — To provide funds of $5,741,003 to cover costs associated with
disputes over water usage in the Apalachicola-Chattahoochee-Flint River Basin and the
Alabama-Coosa-Tallapoosa River Basin, counsel fees, and litigation expenses.
