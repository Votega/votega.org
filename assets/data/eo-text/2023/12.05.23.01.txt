ORDERED:

The State of Georgia holds title to approximately 32.485 acres of unimproved real property
located in Land Lots 101, 102, and 106, 1st Land District, 418th GMD, Walton County
(“Property”) as shown on the survey attached hereto as Exhibit “A”; and

Said Property is a portion of the 1,943.04 acres of real property located in Land Lots 44, and
73, 1st District, Walton County, Georgia, and Land Lot 16, 20th District, Morgan County and
Land Lots 44, 73, 74, 75, 76, 101, 102, and 106, ist Land District, 418th GMD, Walton County,
and Land Lots 1, 2, 3, 14, 15, 16, 30, 31, 32, 33, and 34, 19th Land District, 282nd GMD,
Morgan County acquired by virtue of a Limited Warranty Deed dated September 2, 2022,
being recorded in Deed Book 5204, Pages 170-216 in the office of the Clerk of Superior Court
of Walton County, and Deed Book 701, Pages 956-1002 in the office of the Clerk of Superior
Court of Morgan County, and on file with the State Properties Commission Real Property
Records as RPR # 12780, which Property is under the custody of the Georgia Department of
Economic Development; and

By Official Action dated August 16, 2023, the Georgia Department of Economic
Development requested that custody of the Property be transferred to the Technical
College System of Georgia; and

By Official Action dated March 30, 2023, the Technical College System of Georgia
requested custody of the Property for TCSG-397 QuickStart EV Training Center-Rivian.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That custody of the above-defined Property located in Walton County, Georgia is hereby
transferred from the Georgia Department of Economic Development to the Technical
College System of Georgia.
