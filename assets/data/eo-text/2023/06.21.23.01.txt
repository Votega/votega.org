THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Robert Eddie Ausband, Jr.
of Henry County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as the Tenth Congressional District
Representative on the State Board of the
Technical College System of Georgia, for a
term of office expiring June 30, 2023, to
succeed the Honorable Trey Sheppard, who
now serves as an At-Large Representative on
the State Board of the Technical College
System of Georgia.

This 21st day of June 2023

Bl he

GOVERNOR
