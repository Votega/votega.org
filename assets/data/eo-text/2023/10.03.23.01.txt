EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Rhonda D. Bennett of
Lumpkin County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as a member of the Council on American
Indian Concerns, for a term of office expiring
October 3, 2026, to succeed the Honorable
Joseph B. Jones, whose term expired

January 6, 2023.
