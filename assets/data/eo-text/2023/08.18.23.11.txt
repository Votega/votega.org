EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Curtis Lee Jones, Jr. of
Spalding County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as Georgia’s Compact Commissioner on
the Military Interstate Children’s Compact
Commission, for a term of office ending at the
pleasure of the Governor, to succeed the
Honorable William Hardin, who resigned.

This 18 day of August 2023

Bel

GOVERNOR
