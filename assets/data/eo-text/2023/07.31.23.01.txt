ORDERED:

On July 12, 2023, Mr. Earl Jackson, Mayor of Sparks, Georgia, was indicted
on two felony counts of Theft by Taking, and one felony count of Theft by
Conversion; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to Code Section
45-5-6(b), and the certified copy was received on July 13, 2023; and

The fourteen-day period following receipt of the indictment as prescribed
by Code Section 45-5-6(e) has passed.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Attorney General Chris Carr, Mayor Jason Weaver of Screven, and
Mayor Travis Wimbush of Blakely are hereby appointed as the review
commission to determine whether the above-mentioned indictment relates
to and adversely affects the administration of duties by Mayor Jackson such
that the rights and interests of the public are adversely affected thereby, and
to make a written report to me within fourteen days with a recommendation
as to whether Mayor Jackson should be suspended from office as required

by Code Section 45-5-6(c).
