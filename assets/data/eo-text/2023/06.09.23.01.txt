WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED:

There currently exist at Georgia Southwestern State University, Sumter County,
Americus, Georgia, a pool and surrounding structure known as the Deriso Pool
#0033 (the “Pool and Structure”), located at 800 Georgia Southwestern State
University Drive; and ,

The Board of Regents’ Policy 9.8.2.3 DEMOLITION authorizes the Chancellor or
Vice Chancellor for Facilities to declare buildings, structures, and other
improvements no longer advantageously useful to the University System of
Georgia. Said Pool and Structure is included within this policy; and

It has been determined by Ms. Sandra Lynn Neuse, Vice Chancellor for Facilities
of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, that said Pool and
Structure can no longer be advantageously used by Georgia Southwestern State
University or the University System of Georgia and should be removed.

NOW, THEREFORE, BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE
OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia is hereby authorized
to demolish and remove the Pool and Structure from Georgia Southwestern State
University, Sumter County, Americus, Georgia.

IT IS FURTHER

That the appropriate staff of Georgia Southwestern State University, acting under
the approval of the Board of Regents of the University System of Georgia, are
hereby authorized to demolish the Pool and Structure and to have all Pool and
Structure materials removed from the premises by public works contract; all said
demolition and removal to be performed as expeditiously as possible.
