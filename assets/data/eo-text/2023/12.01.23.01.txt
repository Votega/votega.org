FLAGS ON STATE BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS
ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF STAFF

The Honorable Sandra Day O’Connor passed away on Friday, December 1,
2023; and

Appointed by President Ronald Reagan in 1981, Justice O’Connor was the
first woman to serve as an Associate Justice of the United States Supreme
Court; and

In 2009, Justice O'Connor was awarded a Presidential Medal of Freedom
due to her lifetime of dedicated public service to her state and our country;
and

In accordance with United States Flag Code, it is fitting and proper to fly the
flag of the United States of America at half-staff in honor or and to recognize
the passing of an Associate Justice of the United States Supreme Court.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia be
flown at half-staff on all state buildings and grounds from this date until the
date of Justice O’Connor’s interment.

This 1st day of December 2023.
