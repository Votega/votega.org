ORDERED:

On February 24, 2023, Mr. Gregory Baker, Tax Commissioner for Douglas
County, was indicted on one count of Conspiracy in Restraint of Free and
Open Competition in Transactions; and

The indictment was transmitted to the Governor pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-
5-6(b), and the certified copy was received on February 27, 2023; and

The fourteen-day period following receipt of the indictment as prescribed
by O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(e) has passed; and

Pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(b), since the Attorney General of Georgia
brought the indictment against Commissioner Baker, the Attorney General
shall not serve on the commission, and a retired Supreme Court Justice or
retired Court of Appeals Judge shall serve in place of the Attorney General.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6, AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That the Honorable Leah Ward Sears, retired Chief Justice of the Supreme
Court of Georgia; Tax Commissioner Kevin Payne of Floyd County; and Tax
Commissioner Alicia Hester of Thomas County are hereby appointed as the
review commission to determine whether the above-mentioned indictment
relates to and adversely affects the administration of the duties by
Commissioner Baker such that the rights and interests of the public are
adversely affected thereby, and to make a written report to me within thirty
days with a recommendation as to whether Commissioner Baker should be
suspended from office as required by O.C.G.A. § 45-5-6(c).
