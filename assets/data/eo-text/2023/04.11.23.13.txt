THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED:

That the Honorable Vernica Marie Crockett of
Coffee County, Georgia, is appointed to serve as
a member of the Board of Directors of the
Georgia Emergency Communications Authority,
for a term of office expiring April 11, 2026, to
succeed the Honorable William Wright, whose
term expired June 30, 2021.
