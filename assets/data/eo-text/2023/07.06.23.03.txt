THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

Code Section 12-2-21 sets the composition and _ residency
requirements for the Board of Natural Resources, providing in part
that “[n]o more than two members shall come from the same
congressional district;” and

Three members of the Board of Natural Resources currently reside
in the Fifth Congressional District; and

Code Section 45-5-1 provides that “[a]ny Governor appointed seat of
an executive branch board, commission, or council shall be filled by
appointment of the Governor as provided by law when the seat
becomes vacant upon term expiration or when declared vacant by the
Governor pursuant to this Code section.”

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTIONS 12-2-21 AND
45-5-1, AND THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE
STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the seat on the Board of Natural Resources occupied by James
Nicholas Ayers is declared vacant by operation of law.

Bh

GOVERNOR
