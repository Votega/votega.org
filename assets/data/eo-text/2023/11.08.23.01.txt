THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

RENEWAL OF STATE OF EMERGENCY FOR INFLATION

On September 12, 2023, due to the ongoing economic hardships
caused by rampant inflation, I issued Executive Order 09.12.23.01,
declaring a State of Emergency for Inflation; and

On October 6, 2023, I renewed the State of Emergency for Inflation
until November 11, 2023, by issuing Executive Order 10.06.23.01;
and

Code Section 38-3-51 vests the Governor with the power to renew any
state of emergency for a period not to exceed thirty (30) days; and

Code Section 45-12-22 vest the Governor with the power to suspend
the collection of taxes, or any part thereof, due to the state until the
next meeting of the General Assembly but no longer; and

In the event the Governor has declared a state of emergency, Code
Section 45-12-22 vests him with the power to suspend the collection
of state motor fuel taxes, subject to ratification by the General
Assembly at its next meeting; and

On October 26, 2023, I issued a proclamation convening the General
Assembly in Special Session beginning on November 29, 2023; and

Continued action by the State of Georgia is necessary to ensure the
restoration of Georgia’s social and economic welfare by responding
to the ongoing effects of inflation in the State; and

I have determined the state of emergency created by inflation
persists in the State of Georgia, and that it is necessary and
appropriate to renew the State of Emergency for Inflation for
eighteen (18) days, until the General Assembly convenes in Special
Session.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 38-3-51, AND
THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the State of Emergency for Inflation declared by Executive
Order 09.12.23.01, and renewed by Executive Order 10.06.23.01,
which is set to expire on Saturday, November 11, 2023, at 11:59 P.M.,
shall be renewed for eighteen (18) days.

IT IS FURTHER

That the State of Emergency for Inflation shall terminate on
Wednesday, November 29, 2023, at 11:59 P.M., unless otherwise
renewed or terminated via subsequent Executive Order.

IT IS FURTHER

That the terms of Executive Order 09.12.23.01, as renewed by
Executive Order 10.06.23.01, are hereby adopted by reference.

IT IS FURTHER

That if one or more of the provisions contained in this Order shall be
held to be invalid, in violation of the Georgia Constitution, in
violation of Georgia law, or unenforceable in any respect, such
invalidity, violation, or unenforceability shall not affect any other
provisions of this Order, but, in such case, this Order shall be
construed as if such invalid, illegal, or unenforceable provision had
never been contained within the Order.

IT IS FURTHER

The Office of the Governor may continue to issue guidance on the
scope of this Order as needed through communication media,
including social media, without need for further Executive Orders.
IT IS FURTHER

That all provisions of this Order shall become effective upon

signature.
