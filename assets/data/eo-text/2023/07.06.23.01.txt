THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING AND IN CRISP COUNTY

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

ORDERED LOWERED TO HALF-STAFF

Deputy Tyee Browne with the Crisp County Sheriff's Office was killed
in the line of duty on Wednesday, July 5, 2023, while conducting a
traffic stop; and

Deputy Browne graduated from the police academy in September
2022 and had served with the Crisp County Sheriff's Office since his
graduation; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Deputy Browne’s service to Crisp
County and the State of Georgia by lowering the flags on the State
Capitol building and in Crisp County to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF DEPUTY TYEE BROWNE, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia
be flown at half-staff on the State Capitol building and in Crisp
County on Monday, July 10, 2023, the day of Deputy Browne’s

interment, until sunset.

This (p= day of July 2023.
GOVERNOR
