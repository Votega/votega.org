ORDERED:

On August 14, 2023, Mr. Shawn Still, Senator of Senate District 48 in the Georgia
State Senate, was indicted on one felony count of Violation of the Georgia
Racketeer Influenced and Corrupt Organizations Act, one felony count of
Impersonating a Public Officer, two felony counts of Forgery in the First Degree,
two felony counts of False Statement and Writings, and one felony count of
Criminal Attempt to Commit Filing False Documents; and

A certified copy of such indictment was transmitted to the Governor on August 16,
2023; and

Pursuant to GA. Const. Art. II, § III, § I, no sooner than 14 days after receipt of a
certified copy of a felony indictment of a member of the General Assembly, the
Governor shall appoint a review commission composed of the Attorney General,
one member of the Georgia State Senate, and one member of the Georgia House of
Representatives; and

The review commission shall determine if the indictment relates to and adversely
affects the administration of the member’s office and whether the rights and
interests of the public are adversely affected thereby.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO GA. CONST. Art. II, § ITI, { I AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA IT IS
HEREBY

That Attorney General Christopher Carr, Senator Steve Gooch of Senate District 51
in the Georgia State Senate, and Representative Chuck Efstration of House District
104 in the Georgia House of Representatives are hereby appointed as the review
commission to determine if the indictment of Senator Shawn Still relates to and
adversely affects the administration of his office and that the rights and interests
of the public are adversely affected thereby, and to make a written report to me of
such determination within 14 days.

This 15 day of September 2023.
