THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

FLAGS ON STATE CAPITOL BUILDING, IN BRYAN, LONG, AND TATTNALL COUNTIES,
AND AT ALL DEPARTMENT OF CORRECTIONS LOCATIONS THROUGHOUT THE
STATE OF GEORGIA ORDERED LOWERED TO HALE-STAFF

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On Sunday, October 1, 2023, Correctional Officer Robert D. Clark,
42, was killed in the line of duty; and

Officer Clark began his career with the Georgia Department of
Corrections at Smith State Prison in April of this year; and

It is fitting and proper to recognize Officer Clark’s service to the State
of Georgia by lowering the flags on the State Capitol Building, in
Bryan, Long, and Tattnall Counties, and at all Department of
Corrections locations throughout the State of Georgia to half-staff.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN HONOR OF AND AS A MARK OF RESPECT FOR
THE MEMORY OF OFFICER ROBERT D. CLARK, PURSUANT TO THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF
GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the flag of the United States and the flag of the State of Georgia
be flown at half-staff on the State Capitol Building, in Bryan, Long,
and Tattnall Counties, and at all Department of Corrections locations
throughout the State of Georgia on Friday, October 6, 2023, the day
of Officer Clark’s interment, until sunset.

Pil

GOVERNOR

This 4th day of October 2023.
