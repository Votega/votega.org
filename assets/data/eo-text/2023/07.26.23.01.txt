ORDERED:

On July 20, 2023, Donald J. Trump filed an Amended Petition for Writs of
Mandamus and Prohibition (the “Petition”) against the Honorable Robert
McBurney, in his official capacity as Judge of the Superior Court of the
Atlanta Judicial Circuit, in case number 2023-CV-382670; and

The Georgia Department of Law declined representation of Judge
McBurney; and

The Petition seeks equitable relief against Judge McBurney in his official
capacity as a Superior Court Judge of this State; and

Mr. T. Joshua R. Archer of Balch & Bingham LLP is an attorney duly
admitted to the State Bar of Georgia; and

Mr. Archer has agreed to represent Judge McBurney pursuant to Code

Section 45-15-70 at a rate mutually agreed upon with the Office of the
Governor.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-15-70 AND THE
AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS
HEREBY

That Mr. T. Joshua R. Archer of Balch & Bingham LLP is hereby appointed

as Special Counsel to assume control and responsibility for the
representation of the Honorable Robert McBurney in all matters related to

the Petition.
