THE STATE OF GEORGIA

EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED: That the Honorable Linda Hall Nicholson of
Cherokee County, Georgia, is appointed to serve
as a member of the Georgia Council for the
Arts, for a term of office expiring June 30, 2026,

term expired August 13, 2022.
