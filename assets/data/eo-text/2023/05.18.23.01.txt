EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

WHEREAS:

WHEREAS:

ORDERED:

On April 5, 2023, Executive Order 04.05.23.01 was issued
suspending Dr. Romona Jackson Jones, Chairperson of the Douglas
County Board of Commissioners, from office; and

Pursuant to Code Section 45-5-6(d)(1), there being no general or
local law, ordinance, or resolution governing the appointment of a
replacement officer, the Governor is authorized to appoint, for the
duration of the suspension, a replacement for the Douglas County
Commission Chairperson.

Now, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO CODE SECTION 45-5-6(D)(),
AND THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS GOVERNOR OF THE STATE
OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Honorable Phil D. Miller of Douglas County, Georgia, is
hereby appointed to fill the temporary vacancy in the office of
Douglas County Commission Chairperson.

This 18th day of May 2023

Bib

GOVERNOR
