EXECUTIVE ORDER

BY THE GOVERNOR:

ORDERED: That the Honorable Gladys P. Wyant of
Hall County, Georgia, is appointed to
serve as the Chairperson of the Georgia
Council for the Arts, for a term of office
expiring June 30, 2024, to succeed the
Honorable J. Barry Schrenk, whose term
expired June 30, 2022.

This 14th day of August 2023

Bll

GOVERNOR
