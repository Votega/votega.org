ESTABLISHING THE GEORGIA US250 COMMITTEE

The 250th anniversary of the founding of the United States of
America will occur on July 4, 2026; and

Georgia was one of the thirteen colonies that declared independence
on July 4, 1776, and Georgians made significant contributions to
American independence; and

The United States Congress established the U.S. Semiquincentennial
Commission in 2016 to provide for the observance and
commemoration of the 250th anniversary of the founding of the
United States; and

The U.S. Semiquincentennial Commission has encouraged states to
establish state-level commissions to plan and coordinate activities
commemorating the 250th anniversary of our nation’s founding
within each state; and

The State of Georgia should recognize its unique role in the founding
of our nation by undertaking commemorative activities that honor
Georgians’ role in the American Revolution and enhance the
understanding of Georgia’s history and the history of the United
States of America.

NOW, THEREFORE, PURSUANT TO THE AUTHORITY VESTED IN ME AS
THE GOVERNOR OF THE STATE OF GEORGIA, IT IS HEREBY

That the Georgia US250 Committee (the “Committee”) is established
and shall serve as the State’s representative to plan and coordinate
activities commemorating the 250th anniversary of the founding of
the United States of America.

That the Committee shall develop and execute a plan for the
celebration, commemoration, and observance of the 250th
anniversary of the Declaration of Independence, the Revolutionary
War, and the founding of the United States of America through civic,
cultural, and historical education, events, and programming.

IT IS FURTHER

That the Committee shall coordinate and engage with the U.S.
Semiquincentennial Commission, other local, county, and state
commissions, and public and private partners to fulfill its purpose.
IT IS FURTHER

That the Committee may solicit, accept, use, and dispose of
donations, funds, and gifts, in compliance with any applicable laws
and regulations, to support the goals and purposes of the Committee.
IT IS FURTHER

All departments and agencies of the state shall, upon request of the
Committee or the Governor, provide requested services,
information, and staff support for the council, notwithstanding any
other law to the contrary.

IT IS FURTHER

That the Committee shall consist of four members, as follows:

1. The Governor’s designee;

2. The President of the Georgia Historical Society, or his or her
designee;

3. The Chair of the Board of Directors of the Georgia Humanities
Council, or his or her designee; and

4. The Commissioner of the Georgia Department of Economic
Development, or his or her designee.

IT IS FURTHER
That the Governor’s designee shall serve as chair of the Committee.

IT IS FURTHER

ORDERED:

ORDERED:

Members of the Committee shall receive no compensation for their
services, except that any members who are state officers or
employees may be reimbursed for expenses incurred in the
performance of their duties by the agency or department in which
they serve as an officer or employee.

IT IS FURTHER

That this Order shall be effective upon signature and shall remain in
effect until December 31, 2026.
