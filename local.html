Georgia Local Government

Your local elected officials, city councils and county board of commissioners, plus the public meetings, agendas, and minutes for Georgia counties and cities. Each are aggregated from their official governmental site. Cities are grouped under their county, you may search to jump to a place.

Or browse by subject: see where {%- assign topics = site.data.topics.topics | sort: "order" -%} {%- for t in topics -%} {%- if forloop.first -%}{%- elsif forloop.last -%}{% if topics.size > 2 %},{% endif %} and {% else %}, {% endif -%} {{- t.phrase | default: t.name | downcase -}} {%- endfor -%} are appearing on local agendas across every jurisdiction we cover.

More Georgia counties and cities are being added. Data is aggregated from each official government website. Open a place to see its sources, when it was last checked, and the most recent meeting on file (marked “older than usual” if the data is behind).

What the badges mean. Some places show a badge when we detect certain topics on their recent published meeting agendas:
Data centers on agenda A data-center project came before this government recently — a rezoning, permit, incentive, or moratorium. Land use Land-use business on recent agendas — rezonings, variances, special-use permits, annexations, or major developments. ALPR / surveillance Automated license plate readers or surveillance cameras discussed on recent agendas — Flock and other vendors.

These badges come from scanning the text of the published agendas and minutes for each locality for specific topic keywords. We may not detect every variation of a topic keyword so our analysis is likely limited in scope. A badge marks discussion, not action. A badge means the subject came up at a recent meeting. It does not mean the local governing authority approved it, funded it, or plans to. The topic may have been raised by a resident during public comment, mentioned in passing, tabled, or voted down. Click through to the source to find the linked agenda/minutes to get the actual context. Our badges are meant to help you focus your search to specific official agendas/minutes.
